Pixel hero survivor

Pixel Hero Survivor is a Survivor-type game where you must survive endless waves of incoming monsters. Choose from three distinct heroes, select powerful upgrades after each attack wave, and use your hero's unique, exclusive skills to defeat the horde!

¿Cómo jugar a Pixel hero survivor?

Controls: PC Players: Control your hero on the screen using the WASD keys or the mouse. Mobile Players: Use the virtual joystick on the screen via touch control. Skills: Tap the skill icon to cast your character's ability.
