Block Shooter: Shoot the Blocks!

Destroy blocks with an ever-growing arsenal of powerful weapons. Earn coins, unlock new guns, and upgrade them to deal even more damage. Level up each weapon with unique upgrade cards. Once it reaches Level 10, it keeps firing automatically while you use to another weapon. Build the ultimate arsenal and destroy blocks faster than ever.

¿Cómo jugar a Block Shooter: Shoot the Blocks!?

💥Core Gameplay: - Shoot from cannons to destroy blocks. - Earn coins for every block destroyed. - Unlock new cannons, each with its own unique power and shooting style. 🚀 Progression: - Every cannon has its own upgrade cards. - Collect and buy cards to level up your cannons. - Each level increases damage, fire rate, and other stats. - Upon reaching level 10, a cannon becomes passive. - Passive cannons continue to fire automatically while you control another cannon. Upgrade your arsenal and destroy massive block structures even faster! Controls: Left mouse button / Tap: that's it.
