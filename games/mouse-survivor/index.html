Mouse Survivor

Get ready for the thrilling journey of Mouse Survivor! In this dynamic 2D game, you'll dodge dangerous snakes and tackle explosive obstacles while gathering cheese to explore new areas. Stay agile and quick to thrive in this constantly changing environment, available on both mobile and desktop!

How to play Mouse Survivor? Keyboard Controls W / Arrow Up / Spacebar: Jump A / Arrow Left: Left D / Arrow Right: Right For Mobile:Scroll 🐭 Run, Jump, and Explore Infinitely Divided Worlds with the Brave Mouse!🧀 Embark on an epic journey with our fearless mouse hero in this exciting 2D endless running game! 🏃 Endless Running Adventure: Guide your mouse through a dynamic world full of ever-changing tiles. Watch out for those with lower alpha; One touch can be dangerous! Dodge, jump and slide your way through endless challenges. 🐍 Encounter Sneaky Snakes: Watch out for crawling snakes! Snakes will try to hinder your progress. Swipe right, left or up to perform daring moves and escape their cunning clutches. Can you outsmart the serpentine enemies? 🧀 Cheese Collection and Upgrades: Munch on delicious cheeses scattered along the way to increase your cheese count. Acquire new tile types by spending your hard-earned cheeses. Customize your running experience with a variety of tiles, each with its own unique features and challenges! Good Luck...
