Airplane Factory - Tycoon

Build and expand your own aviation empire in Airplane Factory - Tycoon ! Start small by producing lightweight planes, and gradually scale up to manufacturing giant airliners and advanced fighter jets. Manage production lines, upgrade your factory facilities, enhance your manager’s skills, and unlock new types of aircraft to boost your profits. Strategically grow your business to become the ultimate aircraft manufacturer!

¿Cómo jugar a Airplane Factory - Tycoon?

Controls on Desktop: - WASD or Left Mouse Click (LMB) : Move your character around the map. Controls on Mobile Devices: - Swipe : Simply swipe across the screen to move your character in any direction.
