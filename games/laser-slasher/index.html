Laser Slasher

Laser Slasher is a vibrant casual puzzle-arcade game where you command laser turrets to smash blocks with satisfying ASMR effects. Designed for stress-free relaxation, it combines intuitive one-tap controls, progressive upgrades, turret customization. Perfect for quick breaks or extended play.

¿Cómo jugar a Laser Slasher?

Objective: Destroy all blocks • How to Win: Aim/Reverse direction of lasers to eliminate all blocks. Destroyed blocks spawns buffs: +1 Count / +1 Damage / +1 Speed etc.. Gather buffs - to became stronger and finish level faster Control: on PC: - Click: Left Mouse Button (or Space) - to Reverse direction of turrets - Hold: Left Mouse button (or Space) - to Stop movement on Mobile: - Tap and Release - to Reverse direction of turrets - Tap and Hold: Left Mouse button or Space - to Stop movement
