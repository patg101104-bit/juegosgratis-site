Magic Water Sort

Welcome to Magic Water Sort a smooth and satisfying water sort puzzle that blends vibrant colors, intuitive gameplay, and the soothing process of color sorting. Tap the flasks to fill moving glasses with the right color, complete mocktail orders, and finish levels! Sit back, relax, and enjoy colorful pours as the water sort flows without time limits or pressure! Beyond classic liquid sorting puzzles, Magic Water Sort brings a cozy, café-inspired twist. You don't just sort water or colors—you prepare mocktails on a conveyor belt, watching glasses glide by as you decide when and where to pour. The pouring happens automatically, so the water sort puzzle feels smooth and effortless, letting you relax while still enjoying the sorting process.

¿Cómo jugar a Magic Water Sort?

- The game screen shows a conveyor with empty glasses, a space under the counter with empty cups, and a board of flasks filled with layers of colored liquid. - Tap a flask to start pouring liquid into the glasses on the conveyor. Liquid pours layer by layer, from top to bottom. - If a layer matches the color of a glass on the conveyor above, it flows straight into it. If not, it stays in the cups under the counter. - Glasses of different sizes move along the conveyor, and you can preview which ones are coming next so that you can plan how to sort colors. - After every pour, a new glass appears. Fill glasses correctly before they reach the exit line and complete all required mocktails to win a level in this pouring game.
