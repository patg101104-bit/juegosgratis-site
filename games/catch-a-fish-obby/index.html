Catch a Fish Obby

Catch a Fish Obby is a chaotic collector raider set in a bright OBBY world full of Fish and Brainrot Fish characters. Build a money making base with your favorite sea creatures, sneak into rival bases to steal their fish, and protect your own tank from greedy bots and other players. You can play solo or share the same device in 2 Player mode. Every fish you own generates coins while you move around the map. Use those coins to unlock stronger and rarer characters, then return to the obby track and steal more from everyone else. Run through the obby, collect and buy new fish characters for your base. Each fish generates coins over time according to its rarity tier. Spend coins on stronger fish or save for high end draws. Sneak into other bases and steal their best fish while defending your own! Use events and ticket machines to roll for Mythic and Omega fish.

¿Cómo jugar a Catch a Fish Obby?

Player 1 Move: W,A,S,D Jump: Space Buy or Steal: E Hit: F or Left Mouse Sprint: Left Shift Player 2 Move: Arrow Keys Jump: L Buy or Steal: O Hit: K Sprint: J
