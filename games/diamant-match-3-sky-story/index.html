Diamant: Match 3 Sky Story

A fun adventure with match 3 elements. Create your own soaring city in the sky An exciting adventure in the world of Diamant with match 3 elements. - Create special combinations and summon powerful helpers who will help you clear any, even the most difficult field! - Expand your island, study the blueprints and equip it with extraordinary buildings, create your own soaring city. - 8 types of helper golems, and each has its own superpower, which in addition can be developed!

¿Cómo jugar a Diamant: Match 3 Sky Story?

Line up three or more colorful gems to achieve your goals and solve challenging puzzles! Prepare to face a variety of obstacles - from solid stone blockades and pesky bees to adorable spiders, sticky webs, rushing rivers, and more! Delve into ancient riddles, discover hidden blueprints, and protect your very own floating island - an enchanting haven you can grow and expand. Earn valuable artifacts as you conquer levels and complete diverse quests to upgrade your town and decorate it with unique, eye-catching items. Call upon helpful Golem companions on your journey and become a true hero in this magical Match-3 adventure!
