Field Master

Field Master is a casual farming simulator that puts you behind the wheel of powerful agricultural machines. Experience the full farming cycle as you plow vast fields with your tractor, sow seeds with precision seeders, and carefully nurture your crops as they grow. When harvest season arrives, take control of massive combines to reap the rewards of your hard work. From soil preparation to harvest, every step is in your hands, making Field Master the perfect blend of relaxation and realism for farming enthusiasts and casual players alike.

¿Cómo jugar a Field Master?

Desktop Controls: W, ↑ (Up Arrow): Move upward. S, ↓ (Down Arrow): Move downward. A, ← (Left Arrow): Move left. D, → (Right Arrow): Move right. Q = switch vehicles E = load / unload Mobile Controls: ↑ : Move upward. ↓ : Move downward. ←: Move left. →: Move right.
