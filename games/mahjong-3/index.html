Mahjong 3

Embark on a fairytale adventure! This unique puzzle game combines the best mechanics of match-3 and mahjong. You'll be faced with a game board filled with tiles. Your goal is to clear them all! Use various boosters to complete levels and earn coins!

¿Cómo jugar a Mahjong 3?

Match identical tiles on the board to clear them. Clear the board to complete the level. Earn stars for completing levels.
