Shadow Stick - Lightning Ninja

Experience the thrill of battle in "Shadow Stick - Lightning Ninja," a 2D fighting game enriched with RPG elements. Conquer over 120 levels, facing tough bosses, customize your character, and unlock legendary gear. Play this engaging game for free online on your phone or computer.

¿Cómo jugar a Shadow Stick - Lightning Ninja?

Controls for PC/Keyboard: 1. arrow keys "left" and "right" for movement; 2. arrow key "up" - jump; 3. key "V" - normal attack; 4. key "B" - Special skill, allowing a 30% increase in attack and defense for 15 seconds; 5. key "F" - Summon dragon; 6. keys "Z," "X," "C" - skill buttons providing powerful attacks (effect depends on the skill branches chosen). Controls for mobile devices/touchscreen: 1. slider in the bottom left corner - for movement "left" and "right"; 2. other control buttons are displayed on the screen.
