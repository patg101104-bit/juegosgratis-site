Hidden Object: Street Of Secrets

Hidden Objects: Street of Secrets is an atmospheric hidden object game where you become the lead detective. A mysterious case awaits, and your insightful assistant is there to help, offering hints and guiding you to crucial details. Explore a series of picturesque and mysterious locations. Each scene is a new challenge for your keen eye, from evening-lit streets and cozy cafés to enigmatic rooms where every detail matters. Find all the hidden objects to progress. Immerse yourself in the perfect relaxing experience. Enjoy stylish graphics, a calm, detective-themed soundtrack, and leisurely yet engaging gameplay. ¿Cómo jugar a Hidden Object: Street Of Secrets?

Explore each scene: pan left and right, zoom in to examine details, and find all the items on your list. The game is fully optimized for both PC and mobile devices, so you can play anytime, anywhere.
