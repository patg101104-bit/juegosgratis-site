Crazy Emoji Blast

Match colorful emojis, chain explosive combos, and blast your way through 10 increasingly challenging levels! Swap adjacent emojis to create matches of 3 or more — trigger row blasts, column blasts, cross explosions, and total nukes. Watch out for bombs hidden on the board — match near them to unleash devastating chain reactions

¿Cómo jugar a Crazy Emoji Blast?

Tap or swipe two adjacent emojis to swap them. Match 3 in a row or column to blast them. Match 4 for a full row/column blast. Match 5 for a cross blast clearing entire rows AND columns. Match 6+ for a Total Nuke that resets the whole board with massive points. Bombs appear randomly — match emojis next to them to trigger an explosion blasting 15+ surrounding cells. Each level has a score target you must hit within limited moves. Run out of moves? Watch a rewarded ad to get 5 extra moves!
