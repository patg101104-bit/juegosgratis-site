Raven Estate

Raven Estate is a fast-paced shoot-'em-up where you become a fearless crow reclaiming a haunted estate. Blast through waves of monsters with strategically placed weapons that lock in the direction you pick them up. Every choice matters as you dodge, weave, and unleash chaos in this intense bullet-hell adventure. Can you reclaim your home?

¿Cómo jugar a Raven Estate?

Controls - Use arrow keys or WASD to move and navigate the menus - Hold Space to confirm options in the menus
