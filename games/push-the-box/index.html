Push The Box

Welcome to Push The Box, a fun and engaging game where you solve puzzles by just pushing buttons and sliding pieces. Complete different stage missions and try out harder levels as you improve! Game Features: - Use handy features like reset and undo to make your gameplay smooth. - Watch replays of your successful stages to see how you solved them. - Enjoy clean and stylish graphics that make the game a delight to play. - Earn achievements and compare your scores with others on the leaderboard. - Perfectly designed for smart pads and tablets for gaming on the go. ¿Cómo jugar a Push The Box?

Push each colored box onto a square marked with a star of the same color. Success means matching all stars with their respective boxes Desktop: Click on a box to push it one square in the direction indicated by an arrow on the box. Touch devices: Tap a box to move it in the arrow’s direction Arrow indicators: Boxes display arrows pointing the direction they'll move. Color-changing tiles: Passing over a colored dot (paint tile) changes a box's color (except black boxes) Black boxes: These remain their original color and cannot change; they cannot complete a star color requirement
