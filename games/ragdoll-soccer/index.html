Ragdoll Soccer

Enter the thrilling world of Ragdoll Soccer, where you command a stick figure footballer in exciting games. Aim to outscore rivals and become a champion, whether playing alone or with a friend, offering nonstop enjoyment on your phone or computer.

¿Cómo jugar a Ragdoll Soccer?

Play football with a stickman! A fun 2D sports game for one or two players! Control the stickman like a doll and hit the ball to score a goal against the opponent. Features of the game: - single player mode - local multiplayer for two players - Championship mode - The game has boosters and abilities! - penalty shootout mode To play with a PC, you can use: - keyboard (WASD +QE) - mouse - gamepad To play from your phone, use touch input
