¿Cómo jugar a Trend Family: Merge Arena?

🎯 The goal is to defeat the enemy army and advance further. Before the battle, strategically place units on the battlefield. ⚔️ Battle: press start - the battle takes place automatically, the outcome is determined by the placement. 🧩 Strategy: combine close and long-range combat, take into account the elements of the fighters. 🔃 Fusion: drag two identical characters to one cell to get a stronger one. 💎 Resources: get coins and crystals for victories, open chests and get daily rewards. 💪 Leveling up: improve heroes and open new ones. Good luck in battle!
