Mushroom Hunting: Forest Search

Step into the forest and start your quiet hunt! 🍄 Mushroom Hunting: Forest Search is a relaxing hidden object simulator for true mushroom lovers. Study mushroom descriptions and find them hidden among leaves and grass — but watch out for poisonous ones! 🌲 Explore birch groves, spruce forests, and overgrown meadows 🍄 Over 30 mushroom species to discover 🛒 Sell your harvest and unlock new forest areas ⚠️ Ate something wrong? Time to find the village healer! Perfect for fans of peaceful walks, hidden object games, and a relaxing escape from everyday life. No stress — just birdsong, fresh air, and the joy of a good find.

¿Cómo jugar a Mushroom Hunting: Forest Search?

Tap suspicious spots in the grass to uncover mushrooms. Read the description and decide: basket or pass? Complete villagers' quests, sell your harvest at the market, and unlock new forest locations to explore.
