Arklet

Welcome to Arklet - a cozy and meditative puzzle game where your talent for organizing turns into the art of creating harmony for the most adorable creatures. In this game, you become the caretaker of a unique ARKlet filled with charming "square" animals, each possessing its own personality, quirks, and little secrets. 🧩 Gameplay: Harmony in Every Block Your task is simple: distribute the arriving animals into cozy rooms. However, each ward is an individual. To complete a level perfectly, you must do more than just fill the space; you must ensure every animal feels completely safe and comfortable. 🐾 Consider Every Detail: Fears and Dislikes: A massive elephant might panic if placed in the same room as a tiny mouse. You will need to plan layouts so the giant doesn't even catch a glimpse of its small neighbor. Friendship and Socializing: Some animals, like meerkats or raccoons, love company, while bears or porcupines might prefer solitude in a far corner. (some of the pets are still WIP) Special Requests: Some dream of a room with a view of the lighthouse, while others need to be close to food supplies. 🌿 Relaxing Experience and Aesthetics The game features a detailed style that combines sharp geometric shapes with soft textures. You can expect: A Meditative Pace: There are no stress-inducing timers. You can rearrange room blocks endlessly until you find the perfect combination. Visual Delight: Watch fun animations - from a trembling, frightened elephant to a happy mouse munching on cheese. Progressive Growth: Gradually build up your zoo, unlocking new locations ranging from sunny piers to icy icebergs. (WIP) 🧠 Who is this game for? I made Arklet for fans of organization games and spatial puzzles. It is a project for those who want to unwind after a long day and immerse themselves in a world where everything can be put in its place, and the only reward that matters is the happy smile of a square animal. Create a perfect sanctuary where even the most diverse animals can live in peace and harmony!

¿Cómo jugar a Arklet?

The main objective is to satisfy as much as possible amount of pets on the floor by pairing them with suitable neighbors. Experiment, use your wits, attention and resourcefulness to figure out how to do it in the most profitable way. The captain is watching you and shall task you with peculiar missions on your campaign. Use mouse/touch drag&drop to sort out animals into cabins
