Mad Day 2 Special

Mad Day 2 Special throws you back into explosive action as ex-Marine Bob takes on a full-scale alien invasion. Ride, shoot, jump, and smash your way through hostile worlds as you battle evil aliens and fight to rescue Bob’s kidnapped pet octopus. Packed with speed, firepower, and attitude, this is an action runner that never slows down. This is a streamlined web version of the popular Mad Day 2 game. With fast loading, simple controls, and nonstop progression, it’s perfect for quick play sessions and long score-chasing runs alike. Customize Bob’s truck with powerful weapons, armor, and boosters, then unleash chaos on waves of enemies and epic boss fights. Collect coins to upgrade your gear, unlock stylish outfits, and improve your chances of going further each run.

¿Cómo jugar a Mad Day 2 Special?

Desktop: Press SPACE to jump and X to shoot. Mobile: Tap the right side of the screen to shoot, and the left side to jump.
