¿Cómo jugar a Italian Brainrot: Popper Crazy?

Tap on matching character groups (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to blast them! 💥 Earn points with each blast, but be careful: some characters hide mines that cost you 1 life! 🧨❤️ If your lives drop to zero, the game ends, so think strategically! 🧠⚡ Use the upgrade menu to increase your tap rewards, boost your starting lives, or buy powerful bombs! 💣⬆️ When a bomb is used, it clears all characters on the screen, gives you their points, and reduces the number of characters needed to win the level! 🔥 If you haven't cleared enough fruit yet, new ones will drop, and the battle will continue until you win! ⏳🍓 Clear all 125 levels and push your limits as the difficulty increases! 🚀🎯
