Word Search: Offline Puzzle

Swipe across the letters to find hidden words in the grid — a cozy word search and word puzzle you can play offline, with no internet. As you clear levels you climb an evolution ladder: atom, molecule, cell, fish, dinosaur, human… all the way to mind. How to play: a grid of letters and a list of words. Drag your finger to spell each word. In this word find the letters can bend around corners, not just run in straight lines, so it's a fresh twist on the classic word search. Features: • Hundreds of hand-crafted levels, from easy to hard • Fully offline — no wifi, no internet needed • Big, clear letters and a calm pace — great for adults and kids • Hints when a word just won't appear • No timer, no stress — play at your own pace If you love word games, crosswords, word find and brain teasers, this is a perfect daily brain training: it sharpens focus and grows your vocabulary while you relax on the couch, on the bus or in a waiting room. Enjoy the game!

¿Cómo jugar a Word Search: Offline Puzzle?

1. You're given a grid of letter tiles. Every letter is part of a hidden word — your goal is to find all the words and fill the whole board. 2. Press and drag your finger (or the mouse) across connected letters to trace a word. Unlike a straight-line word search, in Fillwords the path can bend around corners. 3. Release to submit. If the word is correct, its tiles lock in with color. If not, just try a different path — nothing is lost. 4. Find every word to clear the level. There's no timer and no pressure — take your time. 5. As you complete levels, you climb an evolution ladder — from atom and molecule all the way to mind. 6. Stuck on a word? Use a Hint to reveal a letter. 7. Tap any solved word to see what it means and grow your vocabulary. Tip: the game works fully offline, so you can play anywhere.
