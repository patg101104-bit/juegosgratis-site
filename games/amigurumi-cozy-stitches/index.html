Amigurumi: Cozy Stitches

Welcome to your cozy amigurumi workshop! Craft adorable knitted friends with your own hands — from sweet bears and bunnies to fantastical dragons and dinosaurs. Every toy comes to life in 4 delightful stages: - Knit the parts by tracing spirals with your finger - Stuff them with soft filling to perfect plumpness - Sew on little paws, ears, and tails - Style the face — eyes, nose, smile, and accessories Choose your yarn color and type, fulfill customer orders, display your toys in the shop window, level up your crafter skills, and complete your full collection. New quests and bonuses await you every day!

¿Cómo jugar a Amigurumi: Cozy Stitches?

1. Choose a toy from the catalog and pick your yarn color 2. Knitting: trace your finger along the spiral, looping each stitch row by row 3. Stuffing: tap the piece to fill it up — watch the gauge to hit the perfect zone 4. Assembly: press and hold each part until it's sewn onto the body 5. Decorating: place the eyes, nose, and mouth on the face, then add blush or a bow 6. Sell your finished toys, fulfill orders, and save up coins for new materials 7. Level up to unlock new categories, colors, and yarn types
