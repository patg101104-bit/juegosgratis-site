Mandala Meadow

Mandala Meadow is a relaxing mandala coloring game where you paint intricate designs, unwind, and create stunning art one tap at a time. Choose a mandala, fill each section with soft, calming colors, and watch detailed patterns bloom to life. With dozens of hand-crafted mandalas — from simple flowers to elaborate lotus, peacock, and animal designs — there's a soothing coloring puzzle for every mood. Perfect for mindfulness, stress relief, and creative relaxation. No timers, no pressure — just you, gentle music, and beautiful art

¿Cómo jugar a Mandala Meadow?

Find Your Calm, One Color at a Time! Step into a peaceful world of art and mindfulness with Mandala Meadow, the relaxing mandala coloring game. Pick a beautiful design, fill each section with soft, soothing colors, and watch intricate patterns bloom to life. From simple flowers to elaborate lotus, peacock, and animal mandalas, there's a calming coloring puzzle for every mood — no timers, no pressure, just you and your art. Perfect for relaxation, stress relief, and creative fun! Desktop Controls: Click a section: Select the area you want to color. Click & drag: Paint freely within the lines. Color palette: Click a swatch to pick your color. Brush / Fill toggle: Switch between hand-painting and instant fill. Zoom slider: Zoom in for fine detail. Undo / Reset: Fix mistakes or start a mandala fresh. Mobile Controls: Tap a section: Select where to paint. Drag to paint: Color with your finger, neatly inside the lines. Tap a swatch: Choose your color. Pinch to zoom: Get up close for intricate areas. Undo / Reset buttons: Adjust or restart anytime.
