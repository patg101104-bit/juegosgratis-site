Sprunki in Backrooms

🎮 Welcome to Sprunki in Backrooms! 🟨🚪 You’ve found yourself trapped in the mysterious Backrooms, where endless yellow corridors hide countless dangers. Your only way to escape is to collect 15 rolls of duct tape scattered throughout the maze. 🟨 Explore eerie Backrooms locations 🧻 Collect all 15 rolls of duct tape 🚪 Find the exit and escape the Backrooms! Can you escape before the Backrooms trap you forever? 👀

¿Cómo jugar a Sprunki in Backrooms?

PC Controls: Move — W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys; Jump — Space; Run — Shift; Crouch — C; Interact — E; Open Menu — Tab; Open Inventory — I; Zoom Camera — Right Mouse Button (RMB); Bonus Speed from Ads — Z; Bonus Jump Power from Ads — X. Mobile Controls: Move — Joystick; Jump — Jump Button; Run — Run Button; Crouch — Crouch Button; Rotate Camera — Right side of the screen; Open Inventory — Button in the top-left corner; Interact — Button with the "E" icon; Open Menu — Button in the top-right corner.
