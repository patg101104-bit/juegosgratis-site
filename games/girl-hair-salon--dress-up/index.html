Girl Hair Salon & Dress Up

In the game Hair and Beauty, you can experiment with the latest trends in hairdressing and fashion: 💇‍♀️ **Create stunning looks** Offer your clients a variety of hairstyles, from trendy cuts to elegant updos. 🎨 **Express yourself with makeup** Choose from a wide range of makeup products and styles to complete your clients' looks. 👗 **Dress to impress** Select from hundreds of outfits and accessories to help your clients look their best. 💎 **Unlock new possibilities** Earn rewards as you progress through the game and expand your salon's offerings. 📸 **Capture the moment** Take photos of your clients before and after their transformations to share with your friends and the world!

¿Cómo jugar a Girl Hair Salon & Dress Up?

Girl Hair Salon & Dress Up is a game where you can: - Use your creativity in hairstyling – offer your clients a variety of options, from trendy cuts to vibrant colors and elegant updos; - Experiment with makeup and choose fabulous outfits – turn your clients into real fashion icons; - Get rewards like stickers for your progress – unlock new opportunities for your hair salon and improve your skills as a stylist; - Take photos of your best works – share your masterpieces with friends and other players.
