Fantasy Fish World Mahjong

Fantasy Fish World Mahjong is a delightful Mahjong Solitaire puzzle game immersed in a vibrant underwater fantasy realm. Its core concept revolves around matching and removing identical fish-themed tiles—featuring colorful clownfish, angelfish, and other sea creatures—to clear intricate layouts against a pirate shipwreck backdrop with corals and seaweed. Unique elements include adorable aquatic tile designs, intuitive UI with hints, shuffles, and color swaps, plus timed levels for relaxed yet engaging play.

¿Cómo jugar a Fantasy Fish World Mahjong?

**Dive into the Depths!** **Plunge into the magical underwater world of Fantasy Fish World Mahjong and become the ultimate tile-matching master by clearing every last fish from the board!** **In this captivating Mahjong Solitaire puzzle game, you’ll match pairs of identical colorful sea creatures—like clownfish, angelfish, and more—to dismantle intricate layouts amid pirate shipwrecks, corals, and seaweed. Conquer over 4,000 levels, earn up to 3 stars per level (1 for completing, +1 for no hints, +1 for finishing in time), and unlock endless adventures!** **Desktop Controls:** **Mouse Left Click:** Select a free tile (unblocked on at least one side), then click its identical match to remove the pair. **? Button:** Get a hint for available matches. **💡 Button:** Shuffle tiles to rearrange the board. **🎨 Button:** Activate magic color swap for new tile visuals. **↺ Button:** Restart the level. **|| Button:** Pause/Resume gameplay. **Mobile Controls:** **Tap Tiles:** Tap a free tile, then tap its identical match to remove them. **On-Screen Buttons:** Tap for hint (?), shuffle (💡), magic (🎨), restart (↺), or pause (||).
