Kings Defense: Roguelike

Kings Defense: Roguelike — Defend the Kingdom! The kingdom is under threat! Enemies are advancing, and only your cannon can stop their onslaught. Kings Defense is a dynamic blend of classic block-breaker and roguelike, where every shot counts. How to Play? Your goal is to destroy monsters by launching chains of balls. They ricochet off walls and enemies, dealing damage. But be careful: opponents move toward the castle walls and strike back. Survive all 20 waves and defeat the final boss! Features: Unique Builds: Break open chests and choose skills — from lightning to splitting balls. Each run offers a new set of abilities. Combo System: Hitting enemies with balls fills the combo meter, granting bonuses to damage and healing. Tower Upgrades: Spend coins in the main menu to increase damage, add more balls, and boost HP. Varied Enemies: Fight ghosts, "dodging" monsters, and cunning bosses. Level up, combine skills, and become a legendary defender. Your kingdom awaits!

¿Cómo jugar a Kings Defense: Roguelike?

Aiming: Move the mouse cursor (on PC) or your finger across the screen (on mobile devices) left or right to set the trajectory of the balls. Shooting: Release the mouse button or lift your finger to launch a volley of balls from the cannon in the chosen direction.
