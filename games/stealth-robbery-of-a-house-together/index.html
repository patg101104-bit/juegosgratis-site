Stealth robbery of a house together

Stealth robbery of a house together: steal things, hide from the guards, take the loot to the van. Play with a friend on the same device. Play alone or with a friend on the same device. Your task is to take out as much furniture and valuables as possible from the house without being caught by security! The house is carefully guarded: guards are patrolling, and if you get in their line of sight, run! Use the abilities The goal of the game is to collect items from the house and take them to the van to complete the progress bar and complete the level! The more valuable the items are, the faster the scale increases.
