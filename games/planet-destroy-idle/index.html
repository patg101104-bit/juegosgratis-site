Planet Destroy Idle

Planet Destroy Idle - Smash planets to pieces! Craft powerful tools like pickaxes, swords, axes, and arrows to strike and destroy planets while collecting valuable resources. Complete quests, unlock achievements, and upgrade your tools to maximize destruction. Use the prestige system with unique perks to come back stronger and smash even bigger planets. Every hit brings progress, and every new planet opens up new opportunities for chaos. Become the ultimate destroyer and show the planets who’s in charge!

¿Cómo jugar a Planet Destroy Idle?

Use the left mouse button to interact with UI elements throughout the game — buy and craft new weapons, upgrade your existing weapons, complete quests, activate prestige, and unlock new perks and upgrades in the menus.
