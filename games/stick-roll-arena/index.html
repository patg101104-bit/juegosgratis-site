Stick Roll Arena

Challenge your skills in Stick Roll Arena ! Roll the dice to summon your stickman army and push the enemy team off the platform. Use strategy and timing to defeat your opponents in exciting 1v1 battles. Features: - Unique colorful stickmans - Satisfying push mechanics and physics - Simple one-touch controls - Addictive gameplay with increasing difficulty - Beatiful vibrant graphics Can you become the ultimate arena champion? Roll the dice and find out !

¿Cómo jugar a Stick Roll Arena?

- Tap / Click anywhere on the screen to roll the dice - Your stickmen will automatically push the enemy team - Time your rolls correctly to summon more warriors - Push all red opponents off the arena to win! Simple one-tap controls. Easy to learn, fun to play!
