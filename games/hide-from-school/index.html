Hide from School

🏫"Hide from School ": A free online game combining popular genres such as: hide and seek, escape and quests. Help a young schoolboy escape from his teachers! 🕵️Hiding and Disguise: Blend in with the walls, disguise yourself as various objects to avoid teachers' attention. Solve puzzles, open doors, find your way out of rooms - your goal is to escape! Become a real school assassin! 🎮Play Hide and Seek Online: Feel yourself among the school walls and corridors, and test your skills in disguise. This free game allows you to compete with players from all over the world! 🧩Interactive rooms and traps: Explore different rooms, go into the shadows, and use items to create the perfect trap. Only those who can disguise themselves and escape cleverly will reach freedom!

¿Cómo jugar a Hide from School?

🎯Your main task is to get out of the school and not let the evil teacher catch you. You can interact with some items(hide, pick up, activate, etc). 🕹️Control character: - On the desktop. Press the WASD keys or move the mouse with the left button pressed. - On mobile. Move your finger across the screen.
