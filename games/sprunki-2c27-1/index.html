Sprunki

Sprunki Incredibox Original – Create your own melody using a combination of different sounds! Dive into the world of Sprunki Incredibox Original, where music comes to life right before your eyes and creative freedom knows no bounds. Create your own melodies with the charismatic Sprunki characters! Game Features: 🎼 Unlimited musical possibilities: Discover hundreds of sound combinations that are perfect for you. 🧩 Complete creative freedom: Play your way—no restrictions, just mix and experiment. 🌟 Find the best combinations: Choose the ideal rhythms and melodies to craft your unique musical style. 🎧 Engaging and relaxing: Perfect for laid-back gameplay and inspiring musical experimentation. In Sprunki Incredibox Original, everything is simple and fun: drag characters, mix rhythms, and create tracks with no limits. You are the composer, and each sound combination is your own unique musical masterpiece.

¿Cómo jugar a Sprunki?

If you don't have sound on your iPhone, enable notifications with the special button on your phone❗️ 🎵 Choose a sound: Each character has their own unique sound. Simply drag a character from the bottom onto any Sprunki to add their sound to your mix. 🎶 Combine Sprunkis to create a harmonious and captivating melody. Experiment and enjoy the process! ✨ Find the secret scene: Try different character combinations to unlock hidden mixes and receive bonus effects!
