Mole Kingdom Defense

Mole Kingdom Defense is a charming tower defense adventure filled with brave little moles and endless battles! Build your ultimate mole army, recruit powerful heroes, and defend your kingdom against waves of dangerous enemies. Each mole warrior has unique abilities—from fearless fighters and sharp archers to powerful mages with devastating magic. Upgrade your heroes, unlock stronger weapons, and create the perfect strategy to defeat bosses and conquer challenging stages. With colorful graphics, adorable characters, and addictive idle progression, Mole Kingdom Defense offers endless fun for players of all ages. Game Features: • Cute and funny mole heroes • Strategic tower defense gameplay • Multiple hero classes and upgrades • Fight epic bosses and enemy waves • Idle rewards and easy progression • Beautiful cartoon graphics and animations Defend your kingdom and lead the Mole Army to victory!

¿Cómo jugar a Mole Kingdom Defense?

• Recruit brave mole heroes with different abilities. • Arrange your team wisely to create the strongest defense. • Defeat waves of enemies and powerful bosses. • Collect coins and resources after every battle. • Upgrade heroes, weapons, and skills to become stronger. • Unlock new stages and protect the Mole Kingdom from invasion. Use your strategy, strengthen your army, and become the greatest Mole Commander!
