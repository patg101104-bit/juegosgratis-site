Ragdoll Racing: Extreme Downhill!

🚀 Are you ready for extreme slopes? In this exciting game, you will control a ragdoll stickman who challenges dangerous tracks with sharp turns and steep descents. Use your agility to avoid falls and finish first among your rivals! 🏂 What awaits you in the game: - Epic races with challenging tracks full of surprises. - Realistic ragdoll physics: every fall looks funny and unpredictable. - Ability to choose and change skins - create a unique stickman! - AI opponents. Overtake all participants of the race and get to the finish line first! 🎮 Play online and for free! Enjoy the game both on mobile devices and on your computer. Show what you can do by avoiding hazards and competing with other players online. 🏅 Win and climb to the top of the leaderboard to become the best racer among all players!

¿Cómo jugar a Ragdoll Racing: Extreme Downhill!?

🎯 The aim of the game is to get to the finish line faster than your rivals by controlling the ragdoll stickman. Be careful: too high speed can lead to a fall! 🕹️ Controls: Press the character to speed up and keep control of him to keep his head up (literally!) 💡 Tip: Change your character by choosing different skins and colors in the main menu before the race
