DIY Ugly Christmas Sweater

Get ready to deck the halls with festive creativity in DIY Ugly Christmas Sweater! Join three BFFs as they design their unique ugly Christmas sweaters for an epic holiday party. Choose sweater colors and decorate them with Christmas trees, reindeer, snowmen, globes, lights, bows, and more! Complete their holiday looks with new hairstyles and festive accessories. Show off your holiday spirit and create the ultimate cozy, ugly sweater masterpiece in this fun and festive game!

¿Cómo jugar a DIY Ugly Christmas Sweater?

1. Click or tap to select sweater colors for each girl 2. Choose from a variety of Christmas decorations and place them on the sweaters 3. Mix and match patterns, icons, and details for a truly ugly look 4. Style each BFF with festive hairstyles and accessories 5. Enjoy your finished sweaters and admire your holiday creations Simple controls make it easy to play on both desktop and mobile devices.
