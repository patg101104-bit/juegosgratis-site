Snake Puzzle: Slither to Eat!

Snake Puzzle: Slither to Eat is a relaxing yet captivating puzzle game where you need to skillfully control your snake 🐍 to overcome challenging levels. 🍎 Guide the snake to eat apples 🍏 to make its body grow longer. Your goal is to make the snake long enough to reach the teleportation gate 🔮. But be careful: every move needs careful planning 🧠 to avoid getting stuck.

¿Cómo jugar a Snake Puzzle: Slither to Eat!?

Swipe to control the caterpillar as it crawls past obstacles, collects apples, and reaches the spiral gate to complete the level. Plan your path carefully to avoid getting stuck! 🍎🌀
