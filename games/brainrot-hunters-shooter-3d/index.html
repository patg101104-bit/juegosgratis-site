Brainrot Hunters: Shooter 3D

Dive into an intense battle for your farm in BRAINROTH HUNTERS: 3D SHOOTER! Your mission: defend the farm from the invasion of cunning Italian Brainrots and wily rival hunters. Hold the line, shoot back waves of meme-inducing monsters, and don't let the enemies break through! Key features: 🐗 A variety of Brainrots—each with their own habits and tactics 🔥 Stunning 3D graphics, full of chaos and adrenaline 👂 Smart enemies—they react to shots and footsteps, forcing you to keep your head down! Become a hunting legend, save the farm, and show who's boss! 🏹🔥

¿Cómo jugar a Brainrot Hunters: Shooter 3D?

Movement: - Mouse to Look around - WASD keys to Walk - Left shift (hold) to Run - Left Ctrl to Crouch - Space to Jump Weapon: - Left click to shoot - Right click to aim - R key to reload - T key to inspect weapon - Q / scrollwheel key to cycle equipped weapons Shop: - G to open Gun Shop, and G again to close Gun Shop - H to open Hunter's Supply, and H again to close Hunter's Supply - J to open Purchases Mobile controls: touch.
