Mystery Squishy Dumpling Blind Box

Mystery Squishy Dumpling Blind Box is a cute collecting game about mystery boxes, squishy toys and rare dumplings. Open 4 different box types, get random squishy dumplings of different rarity levels and try to complete the full collection. Each blind box can give you a common, rare, epic or highly valuable dumpling. The more boxes you open, the more chances you have to discover new cute characters and fill every empty slot in your collection. It is a simple, satisfying and relaxing game for fans of blind box toys, squishy fidgets, surprise unboxing, ASMR toys and collectible characters. Keep opening boxes, hunt rare finds, compare dumplings by rarity and collect them all. Become the ultimate squishy dumpling collector!

¿Cómo jugar a Mystery Squishy Dumpling Blind Box?

Choose one of 4 box types, open it and get a random squishy dumpling. Each dumpling has its own rarity. Collect common, rare and the most valuable characters, grow your collection and keep opening boxes until you find every dumpling!
