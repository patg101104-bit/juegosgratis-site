¿Cómo jugar a Summer Shore Hustle?

Run a small shop at beach, sell products, cook food, server food to customer and earn money. Controls: WASD - walk Shift - Run Ctrl - Couch E - Pick up Q - Drop F - Alt. Interaction P - Pause X - Call customer (increase the chance for the near by customer to visit you shop) that's it the Controls are been displayed in the tutorial.
