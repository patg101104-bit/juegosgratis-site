Strike Force: Action Platformer

Jump, shoot, and power up in this action-packed platformer! Dive into an exciting adventure through colorful levels filled with enemies and non-stop action! Run along the route, dodge bullets, and fight off enemies attacking from all sides — charging straight at you, dropping in with parachutes, or firing from cover. Collect ammo to stay in the fight, and make sure to blast enemy drones for powerful weapon upgrades. Unlock a roster of unique characters and grow stronger with every battle. Game features: Vibrant, eye-catching graphics Intense shootouts with a variety of enemies Explosive drone takedowns and weapon upgrades A collection of heroes with different looks Simple controls and addictive gameplay 🎮 Ready for battle? Jump in and show them what you’re made of!

¿Cómo jugar a Strike Force: Action Platformer?

📱 Mobile Controls: Left Side – Virtual joystick for movement Right Side – Buttons for: Jump, Shoot and Throw Grenade 💻 Desktop Controls: Movement – Use W / A / S / D or Arrow Keys Jump – Press Spacebar Shoot – Press J Throw Grenade – Press K
