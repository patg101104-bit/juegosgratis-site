Capybara Let's Go: Zombie Rush

Welcome to a relaxing casual game that combines merge gameplay and strategic auto battles! Start your journey with a group of adorable pets. Drag and place them on the battlefield, and merge two pets of the same level to get stronger upgraded ones. You can refresh the pet pool to get new companions and plan your layout carefully to make the most of limited space. Once your team is ready, start the battle! All pets will attack enemies automatically. As you clear foes, your team will level up and unlock random powerful skills, including damage boost, critical strike, healing and area attacks. Choose skills wisely to build your unique team. Advance through dozens of regular stages, challenge tough Boss levels and explore surprise bonus stages for rich rewards. Collect more styles of cute pets, raise and star them up to unlock greater power. Easy to pick up, fun to master! Experience endless joy with pet merging, strategic layout and exciting battles right now!

¿Cómo jugar a Capybara Let's Go: Zombie Rush?

1. Basic Setup & Controls Start a Battle: Tap/click the "Start" button to begin a new wave Deploy Capybaras: Drag and drop capybaras from your squad menu to the battlefield grid Activate Skills: Tap/click on a deployed capybara to use its special ability (when the skill bar is full) Upgrade Units: Merge two identical capybaras of the same level to create a more powerful version Pause Game: Tap/click the pause button in the top-right corner to adjust settings or take a break 2. Battle Mechanics Wave System: Enemies attack in increasing difficulty waves. Each wave introduces new enemy types with unique abilities Health System: Your camp has a base health (starts at 100). If enemies reach your camp, they'll reduce your health—game over when health reaches 0 Gold Collection: Defeat enemies to earn gold, which you can use to: Unlock new capybara units (10+ unique types available) Upgrade existing units' stats (attack, defense, speed) Purchase temporary battle buffs (damage boost, health regeneration) Energy Management: Skills require energy to use. Energy recharges over time or by defeating special enemies
