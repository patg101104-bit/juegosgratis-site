Color Cube Puzzle

Color Cube Puzzle is a relaxing logic puzzle where you need to plan every move and position shapes correctly on the board. Move shapes to the cube conveyors. If the cube color matches the shape color, the shape starts filling with cubes. When a shape is completely filled, it disappears from the level. Your goal is to fill all shapes and clear the level. There are no timers, no bombs and no rush — you can take your time and think through every move. It is impossible to lose, you can always try a different solution. As you progress, new mechanics, obstacles and puzzle elements will appear. You can also use three boosters to help you complete difficult levels. The game is perfect for players who enjoy relaxing but thoughtful puzzle games.

¿Cómo jugar a Color Cube Puzzle?
