World War Camp Company

Develop your camp and fight the invaders! In your battle camp you can upgrade your troops, buy military equipment, and improve your weapons. Buy jeeps, tanks, hire squads of fighters! Next, boldly lead your blue troops into battle! Go through all locations, defeat all opponents! Blue vs. Red in this exciting game of squad battles! - Several locations - A large number of upgrades - Different types of equipment - Interesting enemies - Several types of bosses! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do you need to do? A: It's an idle game where you need to gather troops and attack the enemy camp. Q: Is there a storyline in the game? A: There is no specific storyline, but the tasks are clear. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? What can hook players? A: There are few games with such high quality, well-balanced gameplay and fast-paced action. Q: Are there bosses in the game and how are they different? A: Bosses appear every few levels. They are big, tough enemies that mark the end of a level cycle. Mechanics & Progression Q: What currencies are there and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: There are two currencies: gold helmets and silver helmets. They are spent on upgrading troops. Q: How does the upgrading system work? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: You need to collect more helmets to upgrade the appearance and stats of your soldiers and vehicles. Economy & Customization Q: Are there captivating elements of content (weapons, vehicles, locations, skills, references to famous franchises, etc.)? A: There are references to action movies. Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you customize the appearance of the hero, location editor, car tuning, etc.? A: Yes, the player's skin can be customized. Retention & Engagement Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to better immerse yourself in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in passing without sound? A: Sound in the game is not mandatory.

¿Cómo jugar a World War Camp Company?

To send troops into battle, you need to press the "BATTLE" button next to the gate. Allied troops will automatically move out to search for enemies. Upgrade your army and weapons at the base in special places. To win, you must destroy all enemies on the level and capture the enemy flag. Control: For PC. Character control - "WASD", "arrows" or hold the left mouse button and drag the mouse in the desired direction. Shooting - the hero shoots automatically. For Mobile devices. Character control - press your finger on the screen and drag your finger in the desired direction. Shooting - the hero shoots automatically.
