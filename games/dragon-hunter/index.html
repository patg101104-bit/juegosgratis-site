Dragon Hunter

Choose your character, roll the dice and go on a fun adventure. Click on monsters - help the hero survive and defeat the legendary dragons in the clicker RPG Dragon Hunter. Luck and a strong phone glass will determine the fate of the dragon hunter. Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do you need to do? A: Idle RPG with character leveling. You need to level up your character with random skills and defeat various enemies. Q: Is there a storyline in the game? A: A simple and straightforward storyline, standard for this type of game. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? How can it hook players? A: There are few similar quality RPGs, interesting leveling up system, and a variety of enemies. Q: Are there bosses in the game and how are they different? A: Yes, they are much stronger than regular enemies. Mechanics & Progression Q: What currencies are there and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: Money is spent on leveling up skills. Q: How does leveling up work exactly? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: You need to defeat enemies and open chests. Economy & Customization Q: What engaging content elements are there (weapons, vehicles, locations, skills, references to famous franchises, etc.)? A: Mini-games, lots of weapons, and armor. Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you customize the appearance of the hero, location editor, car tuning, etc.? A: Yes, you can buy armor and weapons. Retention & Engagement Q: Are there daily rewards or something similar to incentivize players to come back? A: Yes, spins, leveling up. Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to better immerse in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in passing without sound? A: It is preferable to play with sound for a pleasant atmosphere in the game. Q: Are there mini-games within the main storyline? For example, match-three puzzles within a strategy game. A: Yes, there is chest opening. Q: Is there an achievement system? What are they and what can you get them for? A: Yes, there is a journal of tasks and achievements.

¿Cómo jugar a Dragon Hunter?

The goal of the game is to visit all locations, passing them by killing monsters on your way. Control - touch/click.
