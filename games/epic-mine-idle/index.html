Epic Mine Idle

⛏️ Dig at full speed! Break rocks and extract rare resources. But don't let your guard down: keep an eye on your health (HP) to avoid being caught off guard by a cave-in. 🔥 Hot work! ¿Cómo jugar a Epic Mine Idle?

Simple and clear controls, you need to click the left mouse button on the screen and the character will break the wall, but watch out for overheating, because if the character overheats, you will have to let him rest and cool down
