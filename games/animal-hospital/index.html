Animal Hospital

There's a queue of patients outside the reception window — but after midnight, it isn't only sick animals that come to town. Some of them are only pretending to be a cat, a dog, or a rabbit: an extra eye, the wrong coat color, a smile that's a little too human, a shadow that shouldn't be there, a voice that's subtly wrong. Your job is to tell a real patient from an anomaly using nothing but a photo and their paperwork. Let your own kind in and heal them; keep the others off the threshold. Every animal you admit has to be led to a ward and treated through mini-games — and in between, you'll need to run the generator, check the cages, and keep an eye on the security cameras. All of it without losing your mind, and without looking where you shouldn't. Every shift gets darker, the anomalies grow more numerous, and something that was only pretending to be a patient begins to roam the clinic's corridors. Play solo or in co-op with up to 4 players. Will you live to see the dawn?

¿Cómo jugar a Animal Hospital?

Controls PC: WASD — move, mouse — look, E — interact / admit, R — refuse, F — patient chart, G — records log, Q — flashlight, C — coffee to go, RMB (hold) — zoom in, 1–6 — quick phrases in co-op, Esc — pause. Cameras: walk up to the surveillance tablet and press E. Smartphone: left stick — move, swipe on the right — look, round buttons on the right — flashlight, magnifier, and coffee; decisions and all other actions — tap the buttons and prompts on screen.
