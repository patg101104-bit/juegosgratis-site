Mega Sweet Fruits - Popper

Welcome to Mega Sweet Fruits - Popper! 🍉💥 Take on 125 exciting levels filled with colorful fruits, plan your moves, and test your skills. 🎯🧩 But be careful! ⚠️🍒 Some fruits hide bombs that can cost you a life. Every move matters! ⏳❤️ Upgrade your abilities: increase your tap power, boost your starting lives, and use powerful pomegranates to clear the screen in one strike! 🍊🚀 Each level gets more challenging, keeping the fun fresh and intense. 🏆🔥 Unlock 9 mysterious achievements, grow stronger, and prove your mastery! 🎖️🔮 With 8 supported languages (English, Russian, French, Turkish, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese), you can share the same thrill with players worldwide. 🌍🗣️

¿Cómo jugar a Mega Sweet Fruits - Popper?

Tap on groups of matching fruits (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to pop them! 🍇💥 Earn points with every pop, but watch out: some fruits hide bombs that cost you 1 life! 💣❤️ If your lives drop to zero, it’s game over—so think strategically! 🧠⚡ Use the upgrade menu to boost your tap earnings, increase starting lives, or buy powerful pomegranates! 🍎⬆️ When used, a pomegranate clears all fruits on screen, gives you their points, and reduces the required fruit count to win the level! 🍊🔥 If you still haven’t cleared enough fruits, new ones will drop, and the challenge continues until victory! ⏳🍓 Conquer all 125 levels and push your limits as the difficulty rises! 🚀🎯
