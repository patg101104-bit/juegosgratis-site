Big Wild Bucks: Voxel Buck Hunter

A fast first-person voxel hunting game. Raise your rifle, line up the shot, and take down trophy bucks across changing treks — from pine valleys to ember woods, murky hollows, and frozen basins. Shoot a doe and you’re busted, so aim true. Upgrade rifles and ammo, collect rare coats, fill your trophy room, and chase higher scores with combos, critter bonuses, and bird rounds. Scope in, hold steady, and bring home the bucks.

¿Cómo jugar a Big Wild Bucks: Voxel Buck Hunter?

How to Play Bag is a first-person hunt against the clock. Bag enough bucks to clear each trek — if you shoot a doe, you’re busted. Desktop Move mouse to aim (cursor locks in-game) Left click / Space — shoot Right click / Shift — hold to scope R — reload Esc / P — pause · M — mute Mobile Drag to aim · tap to shoot Hold scope button to zoom · reload button to chamber shells Tips Bucks score cash and trophies; does end the run Combos, distant shots, and big racks pay more Critters are bonus points (no penalty) Spend cash in the shop on better rifles and ammo Clear treks to unlock new biomes and rare coats
