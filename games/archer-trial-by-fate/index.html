Archer: Trial by Fate

Archer: Trial by Fate is a dynamic top-down action game. You control an archer who fights through dozens of rooms filled with monsters and bosses, challenging fate itself. What awaits you: * Dozens of unique abilities: ricochet, split arrows, meteors, orbs, ice and fire arrows, ghostly companions, and much more. Every run is a new combination of skills. * Battles against a wide variety of enemies and bosses, each with its own behavior, attacks, and weaknesses. * Progression between runs: unlock new heroes, upgrade your gear in the Forge, and invest coins into permanent stat improvements. * Randomized room and wave order, so no two attempts are ever the same. Can you clear every stage and defeat the final boss?

¿Cómo jugar a Archer: Trial by Fate?

To control your character, use the virtual joystick or the WASD keys / arrow keys on your keyboard. Shooting is automatic: when the hero stands still, he aims at the nearest enemy and fires on his own. Move to dodge, stop to attack.
