Home Design: Decorate House

Welcome to renovation game, where all your dreams come true! Renovate and decorate your home and solve challenging puzzles with this relaxing match 3 design game! If you ever dreamed of having a mansion of your own, now’s your chance in this redecor game! Move into your mansion with tons of rooms, gardens, swimming pools, playgrounds, and decorating this house! Everything you can imagine you’d find in a house decor we got it here! Home Design: Decorate House is not just a home decoration and matching game: •A combination of match 3 game puzzles plus home decorating gameplay brings you an overwhelming fun gaming experience! •Design, decorate, unlock new rooms, plenty of house areas to renovate, never get bored! •All mansion makeover needs is a brilliant mind of a talented designer like you! Decorate the library, kitchen, garden, tennis court, swimming pool and many more house areas to come! Help her renovate dream house to make it the best manor in town in this puzzle game!

¿Cómo jugar a Home Design: Decorate House?

Complete levels by playing match 3 and earn brushes. On these brushes you can repair and decorate your home.
