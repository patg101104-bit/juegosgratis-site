SnowTail Peaks: 3D bubble pop adventure

Classic bubble shooter but in a glossy 3D world. Drag to aim, bounce shots off the side rails, and match colorful bubbles to clear the board before you run out of shots. Line up clusters, use bank shots to reach tricky spots, and watch unmatched bubbles fall away after each pop. Power-ups like Bomb, Rainbow, and Lift help you dig out of tight spots as stages get tougher. Clear every bubble to win — run out of shots and the level ends.

¿Cómo jugar a SnowTail Peaks: 3D bubble pop adventure?

How to Play Drag to aim — The glowing line shows where your bubble will go, including bounces off the lane rails. Release to shoot — Match 3 or more same-color bubbles to pop them. Use bank shots — Bounce off the side rails to reach bubbles behind obstacles or in hard-to-hit corners. Clear falling bubbles — Bubbles that aren’t connected to the top fall away after a match. Clear the whole board to win the stage. Watch your shots — You only have a limited number of shots. Run out before the board is clear and you lose. Swap bubbles — Tap the launcher area to swap your current bubble with the next one. Power-ups (unlock as you progress): Bomb — Arms an explosive next shot that blasts nearby bubbles. Rainbow — Arms a shot that clears one color from the board. Lift — Pushes the bubble stack away from you, buying you more time.
