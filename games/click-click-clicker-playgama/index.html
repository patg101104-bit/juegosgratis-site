Click Click Clicker

<strong>Click Click Clicker</strong> defines the idle clicker genre, offering engaging gameplay where players accumulate wealth by clicking a button. As you advance, you can unlock different buttons, cursors, themes, and music. This free game is available on <strong>mobile devices and computers</strong>, providing fun for all.

¿Cómo jugar a Click Click Clicker?

Click Click Clicker is as true to the clicker genre as it gets. You’re clicking a button to click another button. Click the button, upgrade your abilities, and get shiny new buttons for your hard work. Like many clicker games, you start with one humble click. With each click, you generate cash to buy upgrades. Those upgrades include an increase in your passive ability to earn money, the amount of cash you earn per click, or the number of cursors you have. Click Click Clicker is all about getting shiny new things with minimal effort. You can customize everything in this game: backgrounds, music, cursor, and buttons. But you have to put in the work to unlock them first! Keep clicking or coast along on your idle earnings until you get there.
