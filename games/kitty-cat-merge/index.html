Kitty Cat Merge

It’s raining cats! 🐾 Tap, drop, and merge adorable kitties to create bigger, fluffier breeds in this addictive merge game. Simple to play, impossible to put down, and packed with nonstop kitty chaos! 😺🎉

¿Cómo jugar a Kitty Cat Merge?

Tap and drag to aim where your cat will land, then release to drop it. Merge matching cats to create bigger, fluffier kitties and earn points. Keep merging, manage your space, and aim for the highest score possible!
