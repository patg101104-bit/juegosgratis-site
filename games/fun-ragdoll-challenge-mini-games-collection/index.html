Fun Ragdoll Challenge! Mini Games Collection!

🌟 "Fun Ragdoll Challenge! Mini Games Collection!" is a collection of simple and funny action games with short levels of ragdoll stickmen! Cause chaos and destruction in this free 3D physical playground. 🎮 Inside you will find such popular games as: - Human Playground Mod! Web of Destruction! - Boxing Playground: Punch! - Ragdoll Strike Shooter! Shoot from a Gun! - Ninja vs. Ragdolls! Accurate Knife Throw! - Syringe Attack Playground! Infect All Enemies! 🔥 Features: - Fun and addictive gameplay: each level is a short challenge for accuracy and skill! - One-finger or mouse control: just tap or click in the right place to attack, throw or break! - Funny graphics and ragdoll physics of characters! - Play online right in the browser, from your mobile phone or computer - without registration and downloading! - Leaderboard: compete with other players and friends!

¿Cómo jugar a Fun Ragdoll Challenge! Mini Games Collection!?

🎯 Your goal is to complete all the fun mini-challenges with ragdolls! Destroy enemies, activate traps and perform tricks to win. The further you go, the more difficult and fun it gets! 😄 🕹️ Controls: On the computer 💻 — left-click on the desired point. On the phone 📱 — tap or swipe your finger to start the action. 🔥 Tips: ✅ Use the surrounding objects — they can help. ✅ Don't be afraid to fail — sometimes it's fun too! ✅ Try different tactics to complete difficult levels.
