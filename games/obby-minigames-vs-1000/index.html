Obby: Mini-Games VS 1000

Become the best out of 1,000 players and win the grand prize in the thrilling new Squid Game! Extreme challenges await you, where only the strongest survive. Here are some of them: 🔨 Savage Hammers – Dodge them before they smash you! ➕ Math Spikes – Solve equations quickly and correctly, or lose! 🟥 Falling Tiles – Don’t stand too long — the tile might collapse beneath you! 🧱 Building Madness – Dodge brick blocks building up in front of you! Climb fast or get trapped! 🎵 Dancing Tiles – The music stopped? Stand on the right color — or it’s game over! ⛰️ Deadly Climb – Reach the top of the mountain while dodging massive rolling balls! Can you survive and prove you're number one out of a thousand?

¿Cómo jugar a Obby: Mini-Games VS 1000?

🖱 LMB (Left Mouse Button) — Attack 🎮 W / A / S / D — Move ⏫ Spacebar — Jump 🔻 C — Crouch 📱 On mobile devices, controls appear on-screen.
