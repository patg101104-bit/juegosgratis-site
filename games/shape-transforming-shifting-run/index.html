¿Cómo jugar a Shape Transforming: Shifting Run?

In Shape Transforming, you're not just running a race; you're mastering the elements of land, air, and sea. As you dash through each unique terrain, you must transform into the right character to navigate obstacles and keep moving forward. Be it a car on land, an airplane in the air, or a ship in the sea – each form has its strengths and challenges!
