99 Nights In The Forest

You find yourself in a dark and mysterious forest where the only goal is to survive for 99 nights. As darkness falls, a monstrous deer emerges from the shadows to hunt you down. Your only chance of survival is to keep the fire burning, since it is the only thing that scares the creature away. During the day, you must gather resources — wood, food, and useful items — to prepare for the night. With every passing night, the challenge becomes harder: temperatures drop, the forest grows darker, and the monster becomes more relentless. The game combines horror, survival, and rogue-like elements. You need to carefully manage limited resources and constantly watch over the fire to stay alive. Immersive sounds and a tense atmosphere keep you on edge until the very end. Will you survive and make it through all 99 nights?

¿Cómo jugar a 99 Nights In The Forest?

Controls are simple and intuitive. Use the keyboard and mouse to explore, gather resources, and fight for survival. • LMB – Attack • E – Action / interact • Q – Drop item • WASD – Move character (forward, back, left, right) • TAB – Inventory • F – Eat / use food • Shift – Run • C – Crouch
