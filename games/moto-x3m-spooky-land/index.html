Moto X3M Spooky Land

Moto X3M: Spooky Land is a spooky Halloween edition of the popular bike racing series. Put on your helmet, hop on your motorbike, and ride through haunted tracks filled with creepy traps, bone-made obstacles, and eerie surprises. Speed is key – beat the clock while avoiding deadly hazards and pulling off flips to save precious time! The controls are simple – use the arrow keys to accelerate, brake, and tilt your bike for smooth landings. Each of the 32 levels introduces new spooky challenges, making the game both thrilling and tricky. For fans of off-road biking and Halloween fun, Moto X3M: Spooky Land is the perfect ride!

¿Cómo jugar a Moto X3M Spooky Land?

WASD or ARROWS Press the up arrow key to accelerate Balance your bike with the left and right arrow keys Break by pressing the down arrow key Perform flips and other stunts to gain time Don't crash
