¿Cómo jugar a Crazy Bus Station!?

This is an exciting puzzle game in which you have to drive buses in brightly colored parking lots, solving complex problems and helping passengers find their cars. The aim of the game is to match cars with passengers and clear the parking lot. The car moves away in the direction of the arrow if you click on it. The car's path must be free, otherwise it will not be able to leave the parking lot. If there are difficulties you can always use the queue sorting and car shuffling boosters.
