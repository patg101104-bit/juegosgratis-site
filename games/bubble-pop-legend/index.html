Bubble Pop Legend

Bubble Pop Legend is the best way to kill time. Aim & shoot to pop all colorful bubbles to clear the board and win coins. You'll spend hours with fun & addictive Bubble Pop Legend to keep your mind active. ▶ How to Play • Aim carefully and tap where you want to shoot • Match 3 or more bubbles of same color to pop them • Pop all colorful bubbles to win levels • Use less shots to get higher score and earn 3 stars • Plan and use epic boosters at the right time to BLAST ALL LEVELS

¿Cómo jugar a Bubble Pop Legend?

▶ Swipe to aim the shooter. ▶ Tap anywhere to shoot. ▶ Match 3 or more bubbles of same color to pop them.
