¿Cómo jugar a Picture Jam Factory?

Your task is simple: slide blocks to complete each picture. Match colors, fill shapes, and connect pieces wisely. Once finished, the picture is boxed and shipped away, clearing space for the next order—smooth and satisfying every time.
