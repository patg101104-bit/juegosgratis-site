Catoom! - Bomb Barrel

Imagine a game where, instead of the usual movement on your feet (or paws), you roll... a barrel! But simply rolling isn't enough — to gain truly incredible speed, you need to throw bombs and use their explosions for momentum. Every correct move is a combination of timing and tactics. You'll experience: Unusual movement mechanics — roll on a barrel and learn to control the chaos of explosions. Lots of enemies — for classic use of explosions. Destructible environments — explode tiles and carve your way through. This isn't just an arcade game; it's a test of reaction, ingenuity, and the ability to find solutions, where physics itself becomes your ally.

¿Cómo jugar a Catoom! - Bomb Barrel?

Tap and release the screen to throw a bomb at the tapped location. PC: WASD - movement Touchscreens: Drag the area to reveal a virtual joystick for movement. Explode bombs near your barrel to accelerate it. Roll the barrel yourself to maintain momentum after the explosion.
