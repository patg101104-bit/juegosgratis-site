Sneaker Factory!

You're the owner of a sneaker factory! Will you be able to beat the competition and top the city ranking? 🔥 Fulfill orders – create sneakers on request: choose colors, apply labels and pack batches. 📦 Sorting in the warehouse – put the identical sneakers on the shelves. 🚚 Truck delivery – upgrade in driver mode: avoid obstacles and take the goods to the warehouse! 🏆 Leaderboard – Compete with other factories, earn a reputation and become No. 1! Vivid graphics, dynamic gameplay and the excitement of production are waiting for you! Test your speed and business skills – start the game right now!

¿Cómo jugar a Sneaker Factory!?

PC Management: - By clicking on the buttons During cargo delivery: - Left Arrow - Brake/Reverse gear - Right Arrow - Gas - Space Bar - Jump Management for Mobile devices: - By clicking on the buttons During cargo delivery: - Left arrow button - Brake/Reverse - Right arrow button - Gas - Up arrow button - Jump The level depends on the amount of reputation. Reputation can be earned by building improvements, sorting, order fulfillment, and shipping.
