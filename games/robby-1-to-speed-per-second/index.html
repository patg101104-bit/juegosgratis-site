Robby +1 To Speed Per Second

In this game, your speed increases every second. There is no speed limit. Become the fastest and pass the tests at lightning speed. There are 4 locations in the game. Each has its own unique challenges. Unlock the pets. Try to get everyone together. You can purchase accessories for your character in the store.
