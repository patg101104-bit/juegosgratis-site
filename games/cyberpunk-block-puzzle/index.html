Cyberpunk Block Puzzle

Cyberpunk Block Puzzle is a stylish logic game set on a neon circuit board. Slide glowing blocks across the grid to deliver them to matching colored connectors. Each block moves only along its axis — you'll need to think several moves ahead. 70+ levels with increasing difficulty: walls, ice blocks, and locks on grids from 5×5 to 8×9. Earn chips, climb the hacker ranks from Noob to Hack Guru, and take on a fresh daily challenge every day. A smart hint system keeps you moving, while neon glow effects and juicy animations reward every move. Easy to start — hard to put down.

¿Cómo jugar a Cyberpunk Block Puzzle?

Slide your way through the neon grid! Your goal is to move all glowing blocks into their matching colored connectors on the edges of the board. Plan each move carefully — blocks only slide along one axis and can block each other's path. Clear the entire board to complete the level. Desktop Controls: - Mouse Click & Drag: Grab a block and slide it along its axis. - Release: Drop the block into position. Mobile Controls: - Touch & Drag: Tap a block and swipe to slide it horizontally or vertically. - Release: Lift your finger to drop the block into place. Tips: - Think ahead — moving one block may open or block the path for another. - Stuck? Wait a few seconds and the built-in hint system will highlight your next move. - Earn chips by completing levels and climb the hacker ranks from Noob to Hack Guru. - Come back daily for a unique new puzzle challenge.
