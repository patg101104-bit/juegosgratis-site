Obby: Mine Crasher

🔨 Obby: Mine Crasher Hit the anvils with a hammer, gain power and smash the stone walls! For each destroyed block, you get cups (trophies). Spend them on pets, which enhance the impact on the anvils: the more powerful the satellite, the faster the walls break down and further you go through the obby levels. Hitting the anvil → improving strength 🔹 Walls to splinters → Cups to pocket 🔹 Cups → pets → even more power Are you ready to become the ultimate dungeon destroyer? Come in and start smashing! 🔥 Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do you need to do? A: It's an idle game where you need to hit the anvils, increase your strength, and break walls. Q: Is there a storyline in the game? A: There is no storyline, but the goal is to level up, become stronger, earn the best pets, many trophies, and power. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? What can hook players in? A: The pleasant visual style of the game, daily rewards, a wheel of fortune, pets, and a lot of progression. Q: Are there bosses and how are they different? A: Instead of bosses, there are walls that become more challenging with each broken wall. Q: What currencies are there and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: The key currency is trophies, which can be used to spin a pet box, buy character skins, and unlock new areas. Strength can be used to unlock weapon skins. Mechanics & Progression Q: How does leveling up work exactly? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: You need to hit the anvil and increase your strength. The higher the character's strength, the higher the attack power, and the faster the walls break. Economy & Customization Q: Are there in-game purchases? What can be bought and what does it affect? Does making purchases speed up game progress? A: Yes, there are VIP statuses that significantly speed up progress. There is an option to disable ads. There is a doubling of strength. Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you customize the appearance of the hero, location editor, car tuning, etc.? A: Yes, you can fully customize everything about your character, including weapons. Retention & Engagement Q: Are there leaderboards? How to access them? What types are there (weekly, monthly, all-time, etc.)? A: Yes, there are leaderboards for broken walls and regenerations of all time. You can access them by breaking walls and progressing in the game to activate regeneration. Q: What are the engaging content elements (weapons, cars, locations, skills, references to famous franchises, etc.)? A: There are many skins and pleasant graphics. Q: Are there daily rewards or something similar that encourages players to come back? A: Yes, there are rewards for time spent in the game. Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to immerse yourself better in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties with gameplay without sound? A: The music is pleasant and complements the gameplay.
