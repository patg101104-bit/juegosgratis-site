NSR Street

NSR is an underground street racing game focused on speed, style, and competition. Race through street circuits, master drifts to build nitro, and rise through the ranks in both single-player and real-time multiplayer races. Explore the open-world hub, drive around, live events, and join races whenever you want. Drifting is central to how you play NSR. Link smooth drifts to build boost, use nitro at the right time, and take corners carefully to stay in front. Each race rewards your driving skills, not chance. Earn coins, upgrade your car’s performance, and customize your ride just the way you like. With over 50 cars at launch and lots of ways to personalize like changing body parts, paint, wheels, decals, neon lights, stance, tuning, and more every car is unique. Race on over 50 famous street circuits, try daily challenges, and test yourself in real-time online races with up to six players. Whether you play solo against AI, explore the open world, or compete with others for the top spot, NSR gives you a true street racing experience with no damage penalties only speed, skill, and style. Get ready to hit the streets.

¿Cómo jugar a NSR Street?

Take control of the underground streets and show everyone you have what it takes to become the top racer. Sharpen your high-speed turns and test your drifting skills as you take on every corner in NSR. Steer: Arrow Keys ← → or A / D Accelerate: Up Arrow or W Brake / Reverse: Down Arrow or S Drift / Drift Brake: Space (hold while turning to initiate and control drifts) Shift to Nitro Tips: - Hold Space while steering to initiate a drift. - Shift to Nitro - Control your speed and angle to maintain longer drifts. - Practice finding the perfect line on each track.
