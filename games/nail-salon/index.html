Nail Salon

Nail Salon is a creative and fun nail beauty art experience where you transform clients’ nails into dazzling works of beauty. Step into a vibrant salon, welcome your customers, and unleash your artistic skills to create stunning makeovers. Express your creativity with endless designs and colors, making each client’s nails truly shine. 💅✨

¿Cómo jugar a Nail Salon?

In Nail Salon, you start by painting nails using a stencil to guide your design. After painting, you’ll file the nails into a perfect shape, and then choose an adhesive jewel to add the final decorative touch. As you progress, you’ll unlock more options, from detailed stencils to marble designs and more.
