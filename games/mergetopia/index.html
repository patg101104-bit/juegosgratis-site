Mergetopia

🌍 Mergetopia A delightful 2D casual merge game where evolution happens one merge at a time! 🐾 Dive into a Realm each filled with popular sea animals characters waiting to be discovered, merged, and evolved. Your goal? Build and discover the Creatures — one bubble pop and one merge at a time!

¿Cómo jugar a Mergetopia?

PC/Desktop: Use the left mouse button to click, drag and merge Mobile: tap to click, hold and drag to merge
