Cats Block Puzzle

Just drag and drop cats onto the puzzle board and see if you can make them all fit perfectly! 🧩 Each puzzle has its own unique shape and gets more challenging as you play, which will make your brain think differently. With every puzzle you solve, it gets more challenging. Will you be able to fit all the kitties? 😼 As you master each puzzle, you can collect adorable new cats to personalize your gameplay! PUZZLE CATS FEATURES: PLAY WITH CUTE CATS - Enjoy a variety of adorable cats like cute cats, fat cats, and even some angry cats! 😺 - Try to fit all the cats in the puzzle without upsetting them! FUN BLOCK PUZZLES - Challenge yourself with engaging block puzzles, but now with cute cats instead of regular blocks! 🐾 - Arrange the blocks to fit the board and move to the next level! COLLECT NEW CATS - Solve puzzles and level up 🎉 - Collect new cats as you progress in levels! - Dress up your cats with cute costumes! TRAIN YOUR BRAIN - Improve your thinking and spatial reasoning with these shape-based puzzles 🧠 - Puzzles become tougher as you advance, keeping the challenge fresh!
