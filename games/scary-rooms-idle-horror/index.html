Scary Rooms: Idle Horror

Horror Room: Scary Hotel Tycoon" is a simulator of the owner of a theme park, where visitors come to experience thrills. The player will have to develop his establishment, improving the rooms of fear, buying new decorations and equipment, as well as hiring actors and staff. The goal of the game is to scare as many visitors as possible and make their stay in the park unforgettable. Scare all your customers! Buy new creepy rooms! Monitor the condition of the rooms, clean, repair the equipment. Buy cool decorations in the Horror style! Hire scary actors, choose the most popular and famous Horror characters. Come up with new ways to scare customers. Track reviews of your establishment! Earn even more money for the ability to scare! Become the coolest owner of the Horror Room! FEATURES: • Interesting scenarios of panic rooms! • A large variety of actors! • The ability to decorate the establishment! • Opportunity to clean, repair, rescue frightened clients!

¿Cómo jugar a Scary Rooms: Idle Horror?

Improve rooms by hiring new actors, buy creepy decorations, hire assistants. Serve customers to make a profit. Conduct timely cleaning of rooms to maintain customer satisfaction levels. Control: For PC. Character control - "WASD", Arrow keys or hold down the left mouse button and drag the mouse in the desired direction. Selection of clients - the hero selects clients automatically, to do this you need to come close to the client. For Mobile devices. Character control - press your finger on the screen and drag your finger in the desired direction. Selection of clients - the hero selects clients automatically, to do this you need to come close to the client.
