Obby Space Challenge: Starships

Obby Space Challenge: Starships is an exciting game where you get to fly starships. Collect energy capsules, earn trophies for your flights, and unlock new starships. Build a collection of the best companions, acquire the ultimate ship, and set out to explore all the locations in this cosmic world! • Collect energy capsules! • Earn trophies for your flights! • Acquire companions! • Switch starships! • Unlock new locations! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do you need to do? A: The game is about collecting capsules and flying a spaceship to earn trophy points. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? What can hook players? A: The game offers a variety of pet and spaceship skins, great graphics, an achievement system, and tasks to engage players. Mechanics & Progression Q: How does leveling up work exactly? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: The more energy the character collects, the more trophies they will receive after the flight. Economy & Customization Q: What currencies are available and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: The game features energy and trophies as currencies. Trophies can be used to purchase pets and spaceships to speed up energy acquisition. Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you customize the appearance of the hero, location editor, car tuning, etc.? A: Yes, the game offers many pet and spaceship skins that come with enhancements. Retention & Engagement Q: Are there daily rewards or something similar to incentivize players to return? A: Yes, the game offers rewards that players receive based on their time spent in the game. Rewards are refreshed daily. Q: Is it advisable to play with sound to better immerse oneself in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in playing without sound? A: Music complements the game, but it is not necessary for gameplay.

¿Cómo jugar a Obby Space Challenge: Starships?

PC Controls: WASD - Move your character Space - Jump Right Mouse Button - Camera control Mobile Controls: Joystick - Move your character Hold your finger on the screen - Camera control
