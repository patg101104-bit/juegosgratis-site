Underground Heroes

Rescue, Deploy, Conquer! Dive into an action-packed rescue strategy game where every pull of the hook changes the course of the battle! In Hook Heroes, you control a powerful hook to rescue the underground heroes trapped beneath the battlefield. Time your swings, aim carefully, and pull up warriors, archers, mages, and more! Each rescued hero joins your side and fights to destroy enemy forces and destroy their towers. 🎯 Game Features Build your army of underground heroes Real-time battles with enemy waves and towers Upgrade your heroes and dominate the battlefield Fast-paced, addictive gameplay with strategic depth Can you rescue enough heroes to crush the enemy tower? The fate of the battlefield is in your hands! 🪝 Play now and become the true Hook Master!

¿Cómo jugar a Underground Heroes?

Use the hook to capture heroes underground to bring them to the battlefield. Improve your hook's features to get more heroes underground. Remember, each hero has its own unique features. Also, as you complete each arena, the next one becomes much more difficult. Don't forget to strengthen your heroes.
