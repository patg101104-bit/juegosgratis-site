Build a Go-Kart

Build your karting empire from scratch — from simple straights to complex tracks with boosts and risky turns. Design unique tracks using modular blocks: combine elements, optimize your route, and find the perfect balance between speed and profit. Every ride earns money — and your track design determines how much you make. 🏁 Race & Earn Drive your own tracks, earn income for every segment, and refine your strategy. 🧱 Flexible Building Straights, turns, boosters, and dangerous sections — build a track that works for you. 💰 Economy & Progression Earn currencies, unlock new blocks and skins, and expand your building area. 🔁 Prestige System Reset your progress to grow faster and unlock new opportunities. 🎁 Daily Rewards Come back every day and claim bonuses to boost your progress. Build, test, and optimize your tracks — and turn every race into profit. Can you create the perfect track? Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do players need to do? A: It's an arcade game where players need to create their own track by placing various blocks. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? How can it engage players? A: The game is about Obby. Players can race on their kart on the track, earn money for each segment, and use the earnings to unlock new blocks, expand their territory, and improve the track for even greater profits. Mechanics & Progression Q: How does leveling up work exactly? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: Racing on the track, earning money for each segment, using the money to unlock new blocks for greater profits, expand their territory, and improve the track. Economy & Customization Q: What currencies are there and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: In the game, there are diamonds and money as currencies. Both currencies can be spent on purchasing new blocks and skins. Q: Are there in-game purchases? What can be bought and what does it affect? Does making a donation speed up game progress? A: Yes, there are in-game purchases. Players can buy diamonds, which are then used to purchase blocks. Donations speed up game progress. Q: Are there engaging content elements (weapons, cars, locations, skills, references to famous franchises, etc.)? A: In the game, there are Obby and skins for Obby. Retention & Engagement Q: Are there daily rewards or something similar that encourages players to return? A: Yes, the game offers daily rewards and rewards for time spent in the game. Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to better immerse in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in passing without sound? A: Music complements the game but is not necessary for gameplay.

¿Cómo jugar a Build a Go-Kart?

Build your track by placing different blocks. Drive your kart along it, earn money for each segment, and use that income to unlock new blocks, expand your area, and improve your track for even higher profits. Controls: PC: WASD — move, Space — jump/exit kart, E — interact, mouse — camera. Mobile: virtual joystick — move, jump and interact buttons, touch controls — camera.
