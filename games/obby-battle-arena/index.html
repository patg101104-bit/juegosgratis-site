Obby: Battle Arena

Exciting third-person shooter with beautiful graphics, where exciting battles, cool guns, and a real battle arena await you!

¿Cómo jugar a Obby: Battle Arena?

In the game, you can choose the game mode and maps on which you will play. There are many skins and additional accessories for the character. Controls in the game on PC: WASD - movement 1, 2, 3, 4 - change weapons Shift - fast run (weapon is hidden) R - reload F - hide weapon (increases movement speed) LMB - shoot RMB - aim Esc - game menu Tab - leaderboard
