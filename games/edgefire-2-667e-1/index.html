EdgeFire 2

EdgeFire 2 is a large—scale and multifaceted game universe offering a deep reinterpretation of the classic formulas of the genre. If you are familiar with the unique atmosphere of the iconic Counter-Strike 1.6, have you followed the constant evolution of the gameplay in CS:GO and its successor CS 2, enjoy the intense and mobile battles in Standoff 2 or immerse yourself in any other of the numerous cult projects of the genre — you will find in this game the magic of the classics, recognizable at first glance. EdgeFire 2 offers its players a unique and balanced fusion of nostalgic "old school" and bold new ideas, where genuine tactical depth and the need for team synergy are seamlessly combined with an exciting, dynamic and visually spectacular action game. This combination creates an ideal, carefully calibrated space for infinitely diverse competitive battles, where each match becomes a new story.

¿Cómo jugar a EdgeFire 2?
