Mavy The Fish Mom

Mavy is a devoted fish mom who takes care of her children by cooking food for them. It's an Idle farming game.

¿Cómo jugar a Mavy The Fish Mom?

You are starting with basic equipment, then upgrading it later when you can afford the money, which can be collected from the fish. You can buy fish eggs to drop more valuable coins. After buying the egg, equip it, then lay it on the nest and wait for your little child to hatch
