CubeGate

Puzzle-platformer inspired by portal-style test chambers. Move through clean sci-fi rooms, pick up glowing cubes and place them on pressure plates to power doors, bridges, and elevators. Every room is a mini-logic task: sometimes one cube isn’t enough, sometimes the order matters, and sometimes you’ll have to jump first, then power the gate. Use your movement and your head: combine precise jumps with smart cube placement, watch where the cables go, and follow the energy lines to the exit. Short levels, clear goals, instant restarts — perfect for quick sessions. Can you activate every gate and escape the chamber?

¿Cómo jugar a CubeGate?

Goal: place cubes on the marked plates, power the logic gates, and open the doors to the next area. Action order, power distance, and precise jumping matter. W, A, S, D, Q, Z Arrow Keys — move E, Left Mouse Button — pick up / put down cube Space, Right Mouse Button — jump Shift — sprint Escape — menu (restart level, sound on/off) Tips: Floor markings and highlights will show where to place the cubes. If you get stuck, open the menu and choose restart, then try a different sequence. When carrying a cube, plan your route in advance. For distant platforms, sprint first and jump from the edge.
