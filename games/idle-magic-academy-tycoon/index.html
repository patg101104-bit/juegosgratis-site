Idle Magic Academy Tycoon

Idle Magic Academy Tycoon is a charming management simulator that puts you in charge of a growing academy of sorcery. You design classrooms, recruit skilled Magisters, and gather powerful artifacts to shape each lesson. As headmaster, you guide students from simple theories to advanced combat spells, expanding your academy into a legendary center of magic. Manage your academy wisely, constantly improve the quality of education, and transform your institution into a legendary center of arcane mastery! Develop your Academy’s infrastructure, collect Arcanum Spheres, and research magical technologies. Build your own center of magical education — step by step, classroom by classroom, artifact by artifact [Core Gameplay & Story] Q: What is the game about? What do players do? A: This is an idle tycoon game set in a magical academy. You build and upgrade various facilities, train students, and generate profits. Q: What is the game's hook? What makes it stand out to players? A: The game features a polished, beautiful design along with various features like daily quests, tasks, and achievements to keep players engaged. [Mechanics & Progression] Q: How does the progression system work? What can be done to level up faster? A: Success requires strategic thinking to upgrade the right functions and academy facilities. This increases your profit, which can then be reinvested into further upgrades. [Economy & Customization] Q: What currencies are available and what can they be spent on? A: Gold coins are used for upgrading academy facilities and expanding features, such as adding more student slots, increasing training speed, and improving the quality of education. Diamonds can also be purchased to speed up construction. Q: Are there in-app purchases? What can players buy, and how do they affect gameplay? A: Yes, players can purchase diamonds and an option to remove ads to speed up their progress. [Retention & Engagement] Q: Are there daily rewards or similar mechanics to encourage player return? A: There are daily rewards as well as free hourly chests that contain mages or in-game currency. Q: Is it recommended to play with sound for immersion, or will players face difficulties without it? A: The sound design is high-quality, but it is not required to progress in the game.

¿Cómo jugar a Idle Magic Academy Tycoon?

The introductory tutorial will familiarize you with the core game mechanics, including purchasing and upgrading rooms. To buy or upgrade a room, click on the area where the room is located. In the window that opens, you will see information about the cost as well as a purchase button. If the room has already been purchased, a management window will appear with various content options depending on the room type. To upgrade the content of the selected room, choose the desired item and click the “Upgrade” button. Access to all main game mechanics is available from the main screen.
