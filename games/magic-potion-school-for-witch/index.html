Magic Potion School For Witch

Magic Potion School for Witch is an enchanting match-3 puzzle game where young witches cast spells with a wand to connect and pop numbered magical bubbles and shapes like triangles and squares in a bubbling cauldron, mastering potion brewing through increasingly challenging levels in a whimsical wizarding academy.

¿Cómo jugar a Magic Potion School For Witch?

Brew Magical Potions! Enroll in Magic Potion School for Witch, the enchanting puzzle adventure where you play as a young witch wielding a magic wand to cast spells as bouncing balls that strike and reduce numbered magical bubbles and shapes in a bubbling cauldron. Clear all enchantments before time runs out to brew potions successfully and conquer challenging levels in wizarding school! Desktop Controls: Mouse: Aim with the wand using the mouse, click to cast spell and launch magic ball. Mobile Controls: Touch: Drag to aim the wand, tap to cast spell and launch magic ball.
