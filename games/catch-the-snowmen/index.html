Catch the Snowmen!

🎅🌨️ Touch the winter magic in the game "Catch the Snowmen!" 🎁🎅You can feel like a real Santa Claus, creating nice and cute snowmen. Have fun creating a huge crowd of cute snowmen 🌲🎄Meet New Year's toys at each level that can be hung on a magical Christmas tree! 💪🔥 Each completed level allows you to improve the abilities of your brave snowmen and acquire new heroes in your team. Who are you - Santa Claus or the Grinch? 🎅👊Collect magical items and unlock unique abilities to face the real challenge of the game - the Giant and Evil Santa as the boss ✨🎉The more snowmen you have, the higher your place in the player ranking. Be the first! ❄️

¿Cómo jugar a Catch the Snowmen!?

Recruit new snowmen by collecting gifts. Enemies below your level can be attacked. If the enemy is higher than you, run away! At the end of the level you can get a New Year's toy. Decorate the Christmas tree! Computer control: WASD or arrow keys. Phone controls: Tap the screen and a virtual joystick will appear.
