Tangram Triangle

Tangram Triangle is a captivating mobile puzzle game that reimagines the classic tangram tradition with triangular blocks, challenging players to drag and rotate colorful pieces—blue, yellow, red, and orange—to perfectly fill irregular silhouettes on a grid. Its core idea revolves around spatial reasoning and problem-solving, blending accessibility for all ages with progressively tougher levels and a time-attack mode for high-score chases, making it uniquely addictive through clean visuals, hundreds of puzzles, and family-friendly brain-teasing fun.

¿Cómo jugar a Tangram Triangle?

Master the Tangram Challenge! Dive into Tangram Triangle, the ultimate mobile puzzle where you drag and rotate colorful triangular pieces—blue, yellow, red, and orange—to perfectly fill irregular silhouettes on the grid. Your goal is to complete each level by fitting all available pieces without overlaps or gaps, unlocking progressively tougher puzzles, stars for perfection (like 3/3), gifts, and high scores in time-attack mode! Mobile Controls: Drag: Select and move any piece to the grid. Two-Finger Rotate: Pinch or rotate to adjust piece orientation. Tap: Select a piece; double-tap or use rotate button (if available) for quick spins.
