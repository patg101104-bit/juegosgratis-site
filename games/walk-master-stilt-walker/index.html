Walk Master: Stilt Walker

Ready for fun and a challenge? Walk Master: Stilt Walker is an arcade walking simulator where you'll test your agility! You start out clumsy, but the further you go, the cooler you become. Stumble through forests and fields, level up your skills and become a real stilt master. Unique levels and encounters with crazy creatures await you in Walk Master: Stilt Walker!

¿Cómo jugar a Walk Master: Stilt Walker?

💻 On PC: Hold the spacebar or left mouse button — the stilt lifts. Release — it lowers. Step by alternating presses! 📱 On smartphone or tablet: Hold your finger on the screen — the stilt lifts. Release — it lowers. Tap rhythmically to walk and keep your balance!
