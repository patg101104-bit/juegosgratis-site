Brainrot Hide and Seek Classic

Everyone has played hide-and-seek at least once. With each level, changing locations will keep you busy. In the game, you have to move quickly and skillfully before you get caught. Each level becomes more difficult, requiring you to have faster reaction speed, agility, and the ability to plan your movements.

¿Cómo jugar a Brainrot Hide and Seek Classic?

Your task is to move around the location so that the Seeker, who has a certain viewing radius, does not find you. Remember that the Seeker's movements are chaotic, so always try to keep your distance.
