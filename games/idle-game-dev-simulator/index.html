Idle Game Dev Simulator

Become the Ultimate Game Developer! Start from a small garage and build your way up to a legendary game studio in Idle Game Dev Simulator — the ultimate idle tycoon for every gamer and creator at heart! Create hit games, upgrade your studio, hire the best talent, and research cutting-edge technologies to dominate the gaming industry. Every tap brings you closer to success… or another flop. CREATE. INNOVATE. DOMINATE. Mix genres, choose themes, and pick the perfect platform to craft the next big hit. Strategy, arcade, or adventure — your creativity is the key to success. Analyze reviews, track ratings, and evolve your studio to become a true legend of game development. UPGRADE YOUR STUDIO. GROW YOUR EMPIRE. From a dusty laptop in your garage to a sleek high-tech office buzzing with talent — unlock new rooms, features, and upgrades. Automate production, research advanced mechanics, and push your limits to the top of the charts!

¿Cómo jugar a Idle Game Dev Simulator?

• Tap the center of the room to open the action menu — start developing your next masterpiece. • Experiment with genre, theme, and platform combinations to discover hit formulas. • Use the Research tab to unlock new content and boost your creative power. • Buy upgrades from the right panel to improve efficiency and production speed. Grow your team, build your empire, and show the world who’s the true Game Dev Legend!
