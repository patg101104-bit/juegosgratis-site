Block Battle - Noob vs Pro

Fight and build in the World of Blocks! Develop your abilities, defeat huge Bosses! Become a Legend in the player rankings! Survive the Battle of Blocks! This game in the style of the popular Mine combines sandbox elements and dynamic battles in block arenas, allowing you to show your creativity and dexterity. 🌍✨ • Arena battles ⚔️🏆: Take part in exciting battles in specially created arenas. Fight with rivals. Use your skills and tactics to become a real champion! • Construction and creativity 🛠️🎨: In sandbox mode, you can create your own unique worlds using a variety of blocks and materials. Your imagination has no limits! • Bosses and challenges 🐉💪: Test your strength by fighting huge bosses that are waiting for you in different corners of the world. Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What needs to be done? A: The game is in the style of Minecraft. Mechanics & Progression Q: How does leveling up work exactly? What needs to be done to level up faster? ¿Cómo jugar a Block Battle - Noob vs Pro?

Level up by collecting crystals, attacking enemies, cutting down trees. Enemies are below your level, you can attack them. If the enemy is above your level - run away! Upgrade your abilities between rounds. Computer controls: WASD or arrows. Phone controls: Touch the screen and a virtual joystick will appear.
