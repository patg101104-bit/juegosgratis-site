Save King

Save King is a fun pull-the-pin rescue puzzle game where your mission is to save the King, protect the royal family, and rebuild the kingdom. Each level gives you a tricky situation with pins, enemies, traps, and treasure you must pull the right pin at the right time to win. Use your brain, make smart moves, collect rewards, and upgrade your castle as the story continues.

¿Cómo jugar a Save King?

Look at the full puzzle first 👀 See where the King/Queen/Princess, enemies, treasure, and danger are. Pull pins in the correct order 🧷 Your main job is to pull the pins to create a safe path and stop danger. One wrong pin can ruin the level. Defeat enemies & avoid traps ⚔️🔥 Use the puzzle logic to keep enemies away and avoid risky situations while saving the King. Collect coins & treasure 💰 Finish levels to earn rewards. Rebuild and upgrade your castle 🏰 Use your collected rewards to rebuild the castle and progress through more chapters.
