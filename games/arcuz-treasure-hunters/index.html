Arcuz Treasure Hunters

"Arcuz Treasure Hunters" is a Fantasy JRPG game with treasure hunting theme, where players explore underground caves, decipher maze-like machenics, challenge powerful enemies, collect rare treasures, and assemble a strong team with diverse abilities through hiring various companions.
