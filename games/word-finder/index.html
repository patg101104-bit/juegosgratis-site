Word Finder

🐹Playing Word Finder 10 minutes a day sharpens your mind and prepares you for your daily life and challenges!🐹 Would you like to relax, exercise your brain, and expand your vocabulary-all at the same time? With Word Finder, the brand NEW & incredibly addicting word game! Word Finder is a beautiful and immersive word search game with a shape-shifting twist. Once you start playing, you just won't be able to put it down! Take your daily medication and ease your mind while enjoying all the fun from Word Finder! FEATURES ● A CLUE FOR EACH PUZZLE. Use it to find the related words in each puzzle ● SHIFTING TILES. Each puzzle shifts as you find words. ● LOTS OF LEVELS. Play over 6000 levels with more coming soon. ● EARN POWER-UPS. Use the Spyglass, Light Bulb, or shuffle when you get stuck. ● COLLECT BONUS POINTS. Earn rewards for finding extra words.

¿Cómo jugar a Word Finder?

● Swipe over letters to connect them in the right order to create words. ● Find all related words to clear the level.
