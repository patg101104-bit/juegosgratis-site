OIL RIG Driller

Drill Tycoon is a top-down idle tycoon set in a sun-baked desert oil field. Sink your rigs, haul the crude to the refinery, stack the barrels in your yard and sell them to the trucks rolling in on the loading road. Reinvest every dollar: faster rigs, a bigger depot, and a hired crew that keeps drilling while you are offline. Unlock gas, gold and finally diamonds, each with its own rig, plant and price. What sets it apart from the usual idle game: your crew works to real demand instead of just the nearest target, and your rigs wear down and need maintenance, so the field never quite runs itself.

¿Cómo jugar a OIL RIG Driller?

Move with WASD or the arrow keys on desktop, or drag anywhere on the screen on mobile. Walk up to a rig to drill it, carry the load to the matching plant to refine it, then drop the barrels at the depot. Trucks help themselves and pay at the office, so stand on the green cash ring to collect the money. Step onto an upgrade pad to buy it: new rigs, new plants, more backpack space, faster machines and extra workers. Keep your rigs running by fetching wrenches from the workshop. Goal: unlock all 19 upgrades and build the richest oil empire.
