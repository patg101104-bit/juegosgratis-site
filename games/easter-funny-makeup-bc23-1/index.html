Easter Funny Makeup

Eliza and her niece are getting ready for Easter in the most creative way—by designing festive face art! Use cheerful symbols of the season like Easter eggs, bunnies, flowers, butterflies, and rainbows to craft a bright and joyful spring look.

¿Cómo jugar a Easter Funny Makeup?

Choose your favorite decorations and apply them to the character’s face. Mix and match symbols to create a unique design. When you’re done, save your artwork as a PNG image!
