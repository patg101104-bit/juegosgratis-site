Brainrot Merge: Drop Puzzle

Brainrot Merge: Drop Puzzle is a wildly absurd merge puzzle game where quirky creatures evolve into chaotic meme-powered icons. Drop and combine animals to unlock hilarious AI-generated characters, each more bizarre than the last. With endless levels, unexpected surprises, and brain-zapping humor, every merge brings you one step closer to the ultimate meme monster.

¿Cómo jugar a Brainrot Merge: Drop Puzzle?

- Drop Brainrots into the puzzle box. - Merge two of the same Brainrot to evolve them. - Unlock new Brainrot creatures and climb the evolution ladder. - Keep dropping, keep merging, and discover the funniest meme ever.
