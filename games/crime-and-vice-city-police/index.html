Crime and Vice City Police

Switch to different cars and drive around the city with a bunch of other cars and the police. - Many different locations: Urban city, hills, crash test tracks, etc. - Online Multiplayer! - Tuning system. - Destruction of the car. There will be other cars on the city highway that can be broken! Complete deformation of the transport and realistic collisions. Arrange a crash test of cars and watch their destruction in slow motion. All the destruction of cars has a realistic simulation.

¿Cómo jugar a Crime and Vice City Police?

Controls: TAB - pause menu (or Escape) Moving W (forward), S (backward), A (left), D (right). Spacebar - handbrake. Shift - nitro. C - change camera. R - reset car. K - restore car. B - slow down time. N - switch car. For mobile devices, use the game interface.
