Unstack Tower

Unstack Tower is an addictive puzzle game available on Android that challenges players to dismantle a tower of colored cubes in a specific sequence. This game combines the mechanics of a traditional board game with modern gameplay, providing hours of fun. Players must carefully unstack the cubes without causing the tower to collapse, which requires strategy and quick reflexes. The game features various materials, including glass and wood, adding an extra layer of complexity to each level.

¿Cómo jugar a Unstack Tower?

Use clicks or taps to place or drop blocks. You may be able to rotate or position pieces for optimal placement. When blocks of the same color group together (usually in clusters of 3 or more), they vanish, freeing up space and earning points. If blocks are placed unevenly or stacked too loosely, the tower may collapse, leading to game over.
