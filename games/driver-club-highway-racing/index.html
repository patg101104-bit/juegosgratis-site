Driver Club: Highway Racing

Driver Club: Highway Racing is a high speed driving game where you dodge traffic, overtake vehicles, and put your reflexes to the test. Race through a variety of dynamic environments Unlock and drive a range of powerful cars Smooth, responsive controls for an immersive experience Stunning visuals that enhance the thrill of the ride Push your limits and dominate the highway leaderboard Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do you need to do? A: It's a racing game in the traffic racer genre. You need to overtake other cars to pass the section as quickly as possible. There is also a racing mode where a strong opponent is added to this process. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? What can hook players? A: There are not many high-quality and realistic races like this one. Q: Are there bosses and how are they different? A: There are faster opponents in the Race mode. Mechanics & Progression Q: What currencies are there and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: Money is earned for victories and can be spent on new cars and upgrades for them. Q: How does leveling up work exactly? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: You need to win races and upgrade your car. Economy & Customization Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you customize the appearance of the hero, location editor, car tuning, etc.? A: Yes, you can change the color of cars and rims. Change rims. Retention & Engagement Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to better immerse yourself in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in passing without sound? A: The sound is done excellently, but it is not necessary for the game.

¿Cómo jugar a Driver Club: Highway Racing?

Controls W / up arrow key = accelerate S / down arrow key = brake A / left arrow key = move left D / right arrow key = move right C = change camera view P = pause
