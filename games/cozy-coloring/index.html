Cozy Coloring

Discover a world of vibrant colors and calm creativity with our color-by-number game. Choose beautiful pictures, find the right colors, and fill in each image step by step. It’s a simple and enjoyable way to relax, take your mind off daily worries, and build your own collection of colorful artworks. The game offers pictures for every mood and age: cute images for kids, beautiful illustrations for adults, animals, flowers, nature, food, patterns, mandalas, and much more. Each coloring page is divided into numbered areas — simply choose a color and tap the matching sections. The game is perfect for a short break, relaxing after school or work, a peaceful evening, or family time. You don’t need any drawing skills — everyone can create a beautiful finished picture. There is also a creative mode where you can color each image in your own style! Color by number, create beautiful images, and enjoy calm creativity anytime.

¿Cómo jugar a Cozy Coloring?

1. Choose a picture from the collection. 2. Tap a color in the palette. 3. Find the animated areas for that color. 4. Color the zones with one tap. 5. Use hints if you can’t find small details. 6. Complete the picture and view your finished artwork.
