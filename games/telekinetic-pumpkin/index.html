Telekinetic Pumpkin

Telekinetic Pumpkin is an arcade game where an experiment aimed at creating a magnificent, oversized pumpkin for Halloween takes an unexpected turn, resulting in the creation of a telekinetic pumpkin. You’re a Pumpkin that teleports and moves objects with its mind! Destroy drones by smashing them, and collect power-ups to stay alive. Select your upgrades and try to survive as long as possible.

¿Cómo jugar a Telekinetic Pumpkin?

Double-click on a location to teleport. Click and drag anything to move it with your mind (boxes, enemies, or power-ups). Every 35 seconds, you can select a random upgrade or ability to help you survive longer.
