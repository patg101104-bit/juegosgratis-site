Burger Restaurant Simulator 3D

Lead your own burger adventure! Dive into the exciting world of restaurant management, where you will cook delicious dishes, expand your business and delight customers in this exciting simulation game. From frying juicy burgers to managing staff, every decision you make is important on the way to creating a real culinary empire! 🔑 Key features: 🍔 Cook and serve: Create a variety of burgers, crispy fries, juicy nuggets, refreshing drinks and aromatic coffee. 💰 Burger tycoon: Manage finances, upgrade equipment and hire employees. ✨ Expand and decorate: Use profits to create a unique interior. 🚀 Develop a network: Open new branches with car services! ⏰ New horizons: Launch a trendy cafe or food truck!

¿Cómo jugar a Burger Restaurant Simulator 3D?

Desktop: WASD - move; E - interact; Mouse - camera rotate; M - shop; J - daily tasks; H - daily reward; Tab - show/hide cursor; P - pause; Open a restaurant and create a unique design. Take orders and cook in a realistic kitchen simulator. Discover new dishes: milkshakes, desserts, snacks. Improve the kitchen, hire chefs and train staff. Happy customers = tips + reputation!
