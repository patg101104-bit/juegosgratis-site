Obby: Survival Island

Shipwrecked… and the island shows no mercy. 🌴🔥 Welcome to Obby: Survival Island — where survival meets intense obstacle challenges! 🌊 Escape rising waters 🔥 Cross dangerous lava paths 🌉 Jump across broken bridges ⚡ Dodge deadly traps 🏝️ Reach the rescue point… if you can Each stage gets harder. Every jump matters. One mistake… and it’s back to the start. Do you have what it takes to survive the island and conquer every level? Only the best players escape.

¿Cómo jugar a Obby: Survival Island?

Survive the island by completing obstacle stages, collecting resources, crafting useful items, and helping others along the way. Reach the end of each level without falling or getting eliminated to win. PC: Move using Arrow Keys or W A S D. Mobile: Move using the on-screen joystick. Collect. Craft. Help. Survive.
