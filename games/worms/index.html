Worms

Worms Puzzle is a fun and relaxing puzzle game where you help cute, colorful worms find their way across small tile boards. Just swipe to move, collect dots, and reach the goal — it’s easy to learn but gets more interesting as you play! With bright visuals and bite-sized levels, it’s perfect for quick breaks or winding down anytime.

¿Cómo jugar a Worms?

Use the arrow keys on your keyboard or simply swipe on the screen to move the worms. Guide them across the tiles, collect all the dots, and reach the goal to complete each level. Plan your moves carefully as puzzles get trickier with every stage!
