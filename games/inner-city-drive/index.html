Inner City Drive

Weave through neon traffic at 390 km/h, one mistake and it's over! Inner City Drive drops you into a rain-soaked neon city at night, behind the wheel of a car built for one thing: surviving the storm at impossible speed. Dense oncoming and same-direction traffic floods every lane — thread the gaps, launch off ramps over gridlock, and blast through tunnels while lightning cracks across wet, reflective streets. The hook is the near-miss combo: shave past traffic as close as you dare and your combo climbs, stacking speed and score multipliers the longer you hold your nerve. Play it safe and the combo bleeds away, every run is a gamble between going faster and staying alive. Every day brings a brand-new procedurally-generated drive, the same route for every player worldwide, reset at UTC midnight, so you're racing the whole planet on a level playing field on the daily global leaderboard. Grind XP and coins to unlock all 14 cars, each with its own speed and grip feel, knock out daily missions, and build your day streak. Instantly playable on desktop or mobile.

¿Cómo jugar a Inner City Drive?

Steer: A/D, Arrow Keys, mouse drag, or touch-and-drag. Start: Space or tap. Pause: on-screen pause button. Speed is automatic, focus on weaving through traffic and timing near-misses. Plays with keyboard, mouse, or touch.
