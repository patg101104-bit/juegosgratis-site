Idle Zoo Tycoon

Build and manage your dream zoo in Idle Zoo Tycoon! Start with a small park and grow it into a massive wildlife attraction filled with animals from around the world. Earn coins even while you're offline, upgrade habitats, unlock rare species, and expand your zoo to attract more visitors. Strategically improve enclosures, hire staff, and keep your guests happy while your zoo continues to generate profits automatically. Become the ultimate zoo tycoon!

¿Cómo jugar a Idle Zoo Tycoon?

In Idle Zoo Tycoon, the gameplay is simple and relaxing. Tap on your zoo to generate income and speed up your progress. Use the money you earn to unlock new animals and build exciting exhibitions that attract more visitors. Each new animal increases your zoo’s popularity and boosts your revenue. As your zoo grows, you can upgrade enclosures and expand your park to add even more species. The more animals and exhibitions you have, the more money your zoo generates automatically—even while you're away. Keep expanding, collecting animals, and improving your zoo to become the ultimate zoo tycoon!
