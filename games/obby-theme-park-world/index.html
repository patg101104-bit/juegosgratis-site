Obby: Theme Park World

Dive into a vibrant water park full of exciting rides and slides! Explore an open world, run around the park, discover hidden areas, and collect stars to unlock new zones and features. Ride thrilling slides, try different routes, and improve your results. The more stars you collect, the faster you unlock new attractions and levels. The game combines free exploration with simple and engaging gameplay. Perfect for fans of obby games, parkour, and relaxing gameplay with collection mechanics. Unlock new areas, explore the world, and enjoy speed and movement!
