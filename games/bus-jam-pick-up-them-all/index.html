Bus Jam: Pick up them all!

Bus Jam is an addictive traffic puzzle game where you navigate through a parking jam! Experience the rush of traffic mania as you strategically move cars out of the way to help your bus escape. Perfect for puzzle enthusiasts who love traffic escape challenges!

¿Cómo jugar a Bus Jam: Pick up them all!?

The goal of Bus Jam is to help the bus escape the parking jam. To do this, you need to move the other vehicles out of the way. The colorful buses will pick up the same color passengers. Let's help all bus pick up all passengers. Desktop Controls: Click: click on the bus select that bus in the slot to pick up passengers. Mobile Controls: Tap: tap on the bus select that bus in the slot to pick up passengers.
