Smash and Break

Smash and Break — a juicy voxel destruction sandbox where you can blow anything to pieces! 100 objects are waiting their turn: a mouth-watering burger, a birthday cake, a gaming PC, a helicopter, a piano, a fish tank, and even a capybara. Every object is built from thousands of voxel cubes and breaks apart for real: chunks chip off and tumble down, liquid pours out through the holes, fire spreads across anything flammable, and glass bursts into a spray of shards. No timers, no penalties, no game overs. Just you, a pile of objects, and total freedom to destroy. The perfect stress reliever!

¿Cómo jugar a Smash and Break?

• Click (or tap) an object — every hit destroys voxels • Hold — automatic weapons keep firing on their own • Rotate the object: right mouse button, two fingers, or the arrow keys / A and D • Pick a tool in the panel on the left — each one destroys in its own way • Break most of the object to unlock the next one • Collect the stars hidden inside the cubes — they give bonus XP
