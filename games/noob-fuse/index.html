Noob Fuse

Join the crazy adventures of Noob. This time he decided to take bombs, a lighter and blow up all the houses to take the cherished crystals. You will have to visit many structures from the smallest to the largest and arrange a large-scale explosion! Each house has a hidden cache in the form of a chest. Get to it and open it as soon as possible! Features of the game: + Everyone's favorite Minecraft story + Epic explosions + Cool physics + Challenging tasks

¿Cómo jugar a Noob Fuse?

RMB - put a block of dynamite / ignite with a lighter Mouse wheel - change of objects The number "1" on the keyboard - select dynamite The number "2" on the keyboard - choose a lighter WASD or arrows - movement Space - jump Control on a mobile device - game buttons on the sensor screen.
