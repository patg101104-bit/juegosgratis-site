BitBanda

BitBanda is your music studio in your pocket! No instrument skills needed: just tap the sound cards, and cheerful musicians take the stage and start playing together. All the sounds are pre-matched in rhythm and key, so playing badly is impossible. From the very first tap, you create a real track! Feel like a true DJ: control the volume of each musician, trigger solos, tweak effects (filter, echo, reverb), build your track from scenes, and arrange your mix. Hunt for secret sound combinations — they unlock bonuses and turn the stage into a real party!

¿Cómo jugar a BitBanda?

1. Tap the sound cards at the bottom — musicians will take the stage and play together. Any combination sounds great! 2. Control each musician: adjust the volume with the slider, trigger solo (🎧) or mute (🔉), and remove what you don't need. 3. Tweak the effects — filter, echo, and reverb — to give your track its own sound. 4. Fill the scenes with different sounds, arrange them into a mix, and tap "Track" to hear the whole composition from start to finish. 5. Hunt for 🔮 secret sound combos — they give bonus Notes and launch party mode. 6. Save your track or publish it to the Catalog. Listen to others' work, give likes ❤, and make remixes 🎭. 7. Earn Notes, unlock new musical worlds and accessories, complete the daily challenge, and compete for the title of Champion of the Week!
