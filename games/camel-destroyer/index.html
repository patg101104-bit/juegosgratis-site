Camel Destroyer

A camel has appeared in the city and is looking for adventures! In the game, you will have to help the camel find animals, chat with people, and explore the city. Look for hidden machines, guns, cannons, and grenades to destroy buildings and create chaos. Drive cars, operate a helicopter, and even saddle a turtle or a cow for unforgettable adventures. Ready for crazy antics and unpredictable situations? Then join the camel on its exciting journey around the city!

Desktop Move: WASD Jump: Space bar Attack: F Shoot a weapon: F Acceleration: Shift Pick up an item: E Mobile Joystick + pop-up buttons
