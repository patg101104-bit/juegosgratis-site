Arrow Rescue

Cute animals are trapped inside dangerous mazes, and only your strategy can save them! Pull arrows in the correct order to remove obstacles, unlock hidden paths, and guide every animal to safety. Each puzzle introduces new challenges that test your logic and planning. With relaxing gameplay, colorful visuals, and hundreds of handcrafted levels, every rescue is both rewarding and satisfying. Think carefully, choose wisely, and become the hero every animal is waiting for!

¿Cómo jugar a Arrow Rescue?

• Tap and pull arrows to change the maze. • Remove obstacles in the correct order. • Open a safe route for the trapped animals. • Avoid blocking the only escape path. • Plan every move before taking action. • Rescue every animal to unlock more challenging puzzles and explore new levels. Easy to learn but challenging to master—every level rewards smart thinking and careful planning.
