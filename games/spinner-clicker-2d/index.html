Spinner Clicker 2D

Spinner Clikcer 2D is an engaging progression-based game featuring a roster of 34 uniquely designed 2D fidget spinner models categorized across four distinct rarities, where players master 3 core gameplay mechanics: the Idle & Upgrades system to manually spin and permanently boost stats, the Collection system to unlock visually striking new spinners, and challenging Battle Encounters where players put their momentum and upgraded configurations to the test against scaling bosses.

¿Cómo jugar a Spinner Clicker 2D?

Browser/PC: Simply click (Left Mouse Button) on the fidget spinner repeatedly to generate spinning momentum. Use your mouse to navigate menus and purchase upgrades. Mobile: Tap repeatedly directly on the fidget spinner to rack up speed and coins. Tap on the UI buttons to upgrade stats and collect rewards.
