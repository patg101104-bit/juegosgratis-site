Sledding Hills

Sledding Hill is a whole world of sliding games. Explore different worlds: deserts, snowy mountains, and wild forests. Grab your sled and slide down the hill! * 🌎 Explore Worlds — Unlock new levels, sandy deserts, snowy mountains, and more. * 🎁 Unlock Characters & Upgrade Gear — Collect coins to upgrade your ride and personalize your adventure. * 🎳 Mini Games — Test your skills in different mini-games and place your best score on the leaderboard. * 👀 Secret Skins — Unlock secret characters and more. Stay with us, and we will bring more maps, mini-games, and more content. Subscribe to our Discord and share your thoughts and ideas.

¿Cómo jugar a Sledding Hills?

Desktop Controls: On-Screen Joystick: For push sled on start. A or On-Screen Joystick: Move left. D or On-Screen Joystick: Move right. Mobile Controls: On-Screen Joystick: For push sled on start. On-Screen Joystick: For turns.
