Collect Brainrots → Craft → Build an Empire! Your main goal is to hunt for the rarest brainrots. Find them, craft them and use them to develop your base! 💻 Computer: • WASD — Moving • E — Interaction with objects • G — Quit Brainroth • F — Enable/Disable cursor , Mobile devices: • Use the on-screen buttons on the interface.
