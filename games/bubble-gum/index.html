Bubble Gum

Blow it up and rise high! 🎈✨ Welcome to an exciting arcade game where lightness is your secret weapon! Inflate balloons, dodge obstacles, and climb as high as you can! The bigger your balloon, the more coins you earn - but watch out for spikes! How big can you grow your balloon?

¿Cómo jugar a Bubble Gum?

- Tap the screen to inflate the balloon and control its size. - Watch out for spikes! - Match the number on the lock with your balloons to open it.
