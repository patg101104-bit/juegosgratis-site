Mr Shooter

Mr Shooter, a Balloon popping desert plant on a mission... A mission to pop all the Balloons in the world! The objective is simple: Pop all the Balloons to win! Unlock and select new powerful cards as you progress through the game. With hundreds of creative and varied levels, this game will keep you entertained for hours.

¿Cómo jugar a Mr Shooter?

Tap anywhere on the screen to shoot needles from Mr. Shooter. Your goal is to hit the spinning cactus targets with your needles. Don’t hit other needles already stuck in the target or you’ll lose! Each level gets faster and trickier can you beat them all?
