Winged Wheels 2

Ready to challenge your brain and reflexes like never before? In this fast-paced action game, you'll control a car and a spaceship simultaneously. While dodging enemies and obstacles with one vehicle, you’ll be collecting coins and power-ups with the other – all in real time! Multitask like a pro: Master the art of controlling two vehicles at once Unlock new cars and spaceships with unique visuals Upgrade your abilities after each run for better performance Face random events and power-ups that shake up the gameplay Climb the global leaderboard and prove your skills This game is designed to test your concentration, dexterity, and multitasking to the absolute limit. Whether you're dodging missiles or grabbing that last coin, every second counts. Can you handle the chaos?

¿Cómo jugar a Winged Wheels 2?

PC: ← (Left Arrow): Move left with the car. → (Right Arrow): Move right with the car. A: Move left with the spaceship. D: Move right with the spaceship. Mobile: - Swipe on the left side to move the spaceship. - Swipe on the right side to move the car. Objective: Collect coins to buy upgrades and climb the leaderboard. Hitting an enemy or obstacle will cost you one health. Losing a coin also costs one health. Power Ups: - Magnetic Field: Coins are automatically attracted to your vehicle. Upgrades increase the duration. - Energy Shield: Destroys enemies and obstacles on contact. Upgrades increase the duration. - Extra Life: Grants you an additional life. - Weapon System: Automatically fires rockets at the nearest enemy or obstacle. Upgrades increase the ammo capacity.
