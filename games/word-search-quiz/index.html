¿Cómo jugar a Word Search & Quiz?

🕹️ HOW TO PLAY: 🔍 Word Search Modes (Classic, Picture, Riddle): 1. Look at the word list, picture icons, or read the riddle text. 2. Find the words hidden in the letter grid. 3. Swipe with your finger or mouse to highlight and connect the letters. ✏️ Word From Image: 1. Identify the object shown in the picture. 2. Tap the scrambled letters at the bottom to spell out the word. ❓ Quiz Mode: 1. Look at the image and read the question. 2. Choose the correct answer from the options provided. 💡 Pro Tip: Stuck on a level? Use Hints to reveal letters or remove wrong answers!
