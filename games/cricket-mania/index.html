Cricket Mania

Step up to the crease and test your reflexes in Cricket Mania, a next-generation online cricket game that combines timing, precision, and high-score challenges. If you love real cricket, stick cricket, or doodle cricket, you’ll instantly feel at home. Simple to play yet tough to master, Cricket Mania is built for players who enjoy fast matches, realistic animations, and skill-based gameplay. Every delivery tests your instincts - one mistimed shot, and you’re out! Whether you’re a casual fan or a competitive gamer, Cricket Mania delivers authentic batting fun anytime, anywhere.

¿Cómo jugar a Cricket Mania?

The goal is simple — score as many runs as possible before losing your wickets! Gameplay Flow: 1 🏃 Bowler Runs In: Watch the bowler’s motion to predict the delivery. 2 ⚾ Ball Delivery: Visual cues reveal the type — Straight, Slow, or Fast. 3 🕒 Timing Window: Press Left or Right when the ball reaches the sweet spot. 4 💥 Shot Outcome: Perfect timing = clean boundaries; late = you’re bowled!
