Escape from School 3D

🏃‍♂️ Welcome to Escape from School 3D – the ultimate blox-style school escape game full of chaos, action, and fun! Trapped in a crazy high school, you must fight your way through evil teachers, bullies, principals, and headmasters to break free. 💥 Armed with anything you can find—baseball bats, bricks, slingshots, and more—it's up to you to survive the madness and make your daring escape! 🎮 Game Features: • Action-packed school escape gameplay with hilarious chaos • Dodge and fight angry teachers, bullies, and bosses • Use funny and destructive weapons: bricks, bats, and more! • Cartoony, blox-style graphics and smooth controls • Explore multiple school zones: classrooms, hallways, gyms, and rooftops! • Fast-paced and addictive gameplay with survival elements • Easy to pick up, hard to put down – perfect for all ages!

¿Cómo jugar a Escape from School 3D?

On PC: Use the WASD keys on keyboard to control the character, and the left mouse button for the weapon. On mobile: Use the virtual joystick to control the character. Look for the elevator on the level to go through it. Collect money to buy new escape heroes.
