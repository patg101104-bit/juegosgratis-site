Draw Action Freestyle Fight

Unleash your creativity and power in Draw Action Freestyle Fight, a super fun draw-to-fight action game! Draw lines and shapes with your finger to command powerful punches, kicks, and combos against tough opponents in intense battles. Complete freedom to draw any attack you want, hilarious ragdoll physics when enemies get slapped or punched into the water, satisfying one-tap drawing mechanics, and addictive battles across different arenas!

¿Cómo jugar a Draw Action Freestyle Fight?

Your main goal is to defeat your opponent by drawing powerful attack lines — drag from your fighter toward the enemy to punch, kick, or slap them. Knock your opponent off the platform into the water to win the round. The better and faster your drawings, the stronger your attacks! PC Controls: Mouse: Click and drag from your character toward the enemy to draw an attack line, then release to strike. Mobile Controls: Touchscreen: Tap and drag from your fighter toward the opponent to draw the attack line, then release to unleash the punch or kick.
