Pregnant Mother Simulator

The simulator of a pregnant mother allows you to fully experience the whole process of pregnancy. In this anime game you have to play the role of a girl who has a loving husband and a child under her heart, who is waiting for her birth. Take care of yourself, give birth, take care of your baby and raise a healthy baby. You will feel all the difficulties of a pregnant woman's life. Take care of the mother and the child to keep health and well-being under control. Features of the game: - Brisk walking and other exercises for pregnant women for mom. - Smooth and easy operation. - A mother's game that will carry out her daily activities after pregnancy.

¿Cómo jugar a Pregnant Mother Simulator?

The goal of the game is to go through the stages of pregnancy and raise a healthy baby. Control on a mobile device: touch/swipe PC Control: Left Mouse Button
