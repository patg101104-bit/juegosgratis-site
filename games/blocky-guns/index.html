Blocky Guns

Collect colored blocks, build powerful weapons, and fight your way through waves of enemies in this blocky first-person shooter. Unlock and use different weapon types, including pistols, machine guns, rocket launchers, and grenades. Choose the right gun for each battle, upgrade your firepower, and survive intense combat across dangerous levels.

¿Cómo jugar a Blocky Guns?

Defeat enemies, collect colored blocks, and use them to create new weapons. Each weapon requires blocks of two different colors, so gather enough materials during missions to unlock stronger gear. Use your weapons wisely, keep moving, and eliminate all enemies to complete the level. On desktop, use WASD to move, mouse to aim, and left click to shoot. On mobile, use the on-screen joystick and buttons to move, aim, switch weapons, and attack.
