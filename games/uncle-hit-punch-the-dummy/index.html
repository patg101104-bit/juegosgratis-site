Uncle Hit: Punch the Dummy

Uncle Hit: Punch the Dummy is a fast-paced, casual action game where you let out your frustration on a few dummies in all sorts of scenarios. You will need quick reflexes and punches to unleash the blows it will take to knock your opponent out. Stay sharp, keep punching, and enjoy every moment of cathartic stress relief. How to Play Uncle Hit: Punch the Dummy Uncle Hit: Punch the Dummy is simple on the surface and fairly easy to play, but that is exactly what makes it so addictive. You control a floating arm wearing a boxing glove, and your only mission is to punch every dummy you see, along with other objects. Move the glove, line up your swings, and knock the targets out of the scene. Once they are all knocked out, you can move to the next level. Each level presents a new setup of dummies. Sometimes there is a single dummy sitting quietly on a bench. Other times, groups are standing together, chatting, dancing with a rat, posing like the Creation of Adam, typing at a computer, balancing on unicycles, or even a bunch of dummies carrying a casket. The variety keeps things surprising and funny as you move from one level to the next.

¿Cómo jugar a Uncle Hit: Punch the Dummy?

There is no strict progression or difficulty curve. Some levels are very simple, while others require a bit more timing. The game runs on physics, so punches can have dramatic chain reactions. You might knock two or three dummies aside with one well-placed swing. There is no timer, no pressure, and no limit to how long you can play. The focus is pure fun. Punch the dummies, enjoy the chaos, and move on to the next scene! Click on the screen to punch the annoying dummies.
