Red Hide Ball

Passionate about brain teasers? Want to challenge your logical skills? Then take a look at Hide Ball a logic puzzle game in which you should hide good balls from dreadful and harmful monsters. Red Hide Ball is an amusing logic game that will test your IQ and raise your intellectual abilities to a new level. Plan every step, estimate a possible outcome and build tactical strategies. Solve logical puzzles, pass lots of interesting levels and win the game!

¿Cómo jugar a Red Hide Ball?

A puzzle board is presented with a configuration of balls, blockers, pathways, and monsters. You tap or swipe objects often dragging or tapping using the minimal necessary moves. Hide “good” balls by positioning them behind safe zones or behind obstacles. Predict monster movements or blocked paths so the good ball isn’t captured. Once the good ball is secure or the goal for the level is achieved, you move on to the next puzzle.
