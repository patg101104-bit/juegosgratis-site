Mahjong Tower: Puzzles

Mahjong Tower: Puzzles are a mix of "puzzle" and "three-in-a-row" genres in a mahjong setting. An exciting puzzle game for MAHJONG and THREE-IN-A-ROW fans! More than 60 levels of varying difficulty. SOLVE THEM ALL! Combine mahjong tiles to remove them from the board or create special tiles that will help you clear the board even more effectively. Solve fascinating puzzles in the "Puzzles" game mode. Clear the board from mahjong tiles in the "Board Clearing" game mode. Score as many points as you can in the endless "Arcade" game mode.

¿Cómo jugar a Mahjong Tower: Puzzles?

Choose one of the three game modes: -"Puzzles" - solving spatial puzzles with a limited number of tiles; - "Board Clearing" - clearing the board from mahjong tiles in limited time; - "Arcade" - is an endless mode with increasing difficulty, where you need to combine mahjong tiles on the board for as long as possible, without allowing them to be too much. COMPUTER INPUTS: - LEFT MOUSE BUTTON - drop the tile on the board; - "R" - restart current level; - "H" - show hint for current puzzle; - "ESC" - open and close the pause menu. MOBILE INPUTS: - All controls are performed by TAPPING and/or MOVING YOUR FINGER across the screen.
