Ragdoll Playground: Break Him

Welcome to Ragdoll Playground: Break Him! Control a ragdoll, smash it into dangerous traps, and score points. Crash into walls, jump into spikes, and fly across the arena! Place traps with your mouse to cause maximum damage. The more damage your ragdoll takes, the more traps you unlock!

¿Cómo jugar a Ragdoll Playground: Break Him?

PC: Use the mouse to place traps and control the character. Left-click to perform actions. Mobile: Use your fingers on the screen.
