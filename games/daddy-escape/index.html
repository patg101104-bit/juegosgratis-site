Daddy Escape

Get ready for an Epic Puzzle adventure like no other! Daddy Escape - Pull the Pin is here to challenge your wits, strategic thinking, and problem-solving skills. Help Daddy make his daring escape by pulling the right pins and overcoming a series of mind-bending obstacles. Who is Daddy? Well, he's not your ordinary dad! Daddy is an adventurous and fearless explorer who always finds himself in tricky situations. Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is to guide Daddy through a maze of perilous traps, ingenious contraptions, and devious puzzles. Can you lead Daddy to safety?

¿Cómo jugar a Daddy Escape?

Your goal is to safely navigate Daddy through each level to the exit door. But beware, there are pins blocking his way! Tap and pull the pins in the right order to clear a path for Daddy. Use your cunning to avoid traps, outsmart enemies, and collect valuable treasures along the way.
