Emily's Hotel Solitaire

Dive into the atmosphere of a light summer adventure in Emily’s Hotel Solitaire! What are you going to do when you go on a journey to a tropical island? Will you spend your time in a lounger, enjoying the hot sun and cold drinks, or become a master surfer? Our heroine Emily has arrived at this heavenly place to make her honorable grandfather Douglas Kirby’s dream to build a hotel of his own come true. Forget about your problems and immerse yourselves in the magical atmosphere of a tropical island and a challenging game of Emily's Hotel Solitaire! Build your own hotel together with Emily, as every victory in Emily's Hotel Solitaire will let you get closer to your dream of creating the best hotel in the world! Plant green parks and picturesque flowerbeds, make sure that the rooms are cozy and also think about the menu of the Chinese restaurant! Every Star earned on the game levels allows you to select what to decorate a once deserted territory — lay the carpets and place soft little sofas. Or maybe decorate the walls with a picture gallery?

¿Cómo jugar a Emily's Hotel Solitaire?

Choose cards that are one higher or one lower in value than your drawn card to remove it from the playing field. To complete a level, you need to remove all cards from the playing field without running out of cards from your supply.
