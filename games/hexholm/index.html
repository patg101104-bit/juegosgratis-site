Hexholm

Hexholm is a fast, turn-based hex strategy game. Start from a single province and carve out a kingdom — recruit troops, claim the map hex by hex, and crush rival AI factions before they overrun you. Every battle is a tight tactical puzzle: manage your gold, position your units, and time your pushes to expand without overextending. Take on 100 hand-picked campaign stages, fight for the top of the global leaderboard in the daily challenge, or jump into a quick random skirmish. Climb the worldwide rankings for total stages cleared and daily high scores. No fluff, no waiting — just clean, snappy strategy you can play in a few minutes or sink hours into.

¿Cómo jugar a Hexholm?

1. Tap one of your territories, then pick a unit from the bottom bar and place it on an empty hex inside your land. 2. Tap a unit to see where it can go — highlighted hexes show valid moves. Tap one to move, or tap an enemy to attack. 3. Capturing neutral and enemy hexes expands your territory and raises your income. 4. Each turn you earn gold based on the land you hold. Spend it on stronger units — and merge matching units to upgrade them. 5. Tap END TURN when you're finished; the AI factions then take their turns. 6. Eliminate every rival faction to win the map. Slipped up? Use UNDO to roll back a turn.
