Crystalia

A vibrant block puzzle game. Place pieces on the board so that 3 or more blocks of the same color line up next to each other — and watch them vanish in a satisfying explosion. Simple rules, endless depth, and a fierce battle for the top spot on the leaderboard.

¿Cómo jugar a Crystalia?

Step 1. A block appears in the bottom panel. Rotate it if needed and drag it onto the board. Step 2. Place blocks so that 3 or more of the same color stand next to each other — horizontally or vertically (diagonals don't count). Step 3. Matching blocks disappear with an explosion, and you earn points. The longer the chain, the bigger the bonus. Step 4. Running out of space? Use boosters: 💣 Bomb — blasts 1 block and all its neighbors. ➖ Laser — clears an entire row or column. 🔀 Shuffle — reshuffles the blocks on the board. Step 5. The game ends when there's no room left for new blocks. Your score is automatically sent to the leaderboard — try to break into the top! Pro tip: don't rush to fill the board. Leave "corridors" open for long chains — that's how mega-combos and record-breaking scores are made.
