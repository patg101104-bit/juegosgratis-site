Marble Color Sort

Get ready for the most satisfying color sorting puzzle! 🌟 Welcome to Marble Color Sort, a vibrant, 3D brain teaser where physics meets fast-paced puzzle logic! Your mission is simple but challenging: sort every colorful marble from the top trays into their matching boxes at the bottom.

¿Cómo jugar a Marble Color Sort?

🔴 Tap to Drop: Release marbles from the top trays and watch them tumble with realistic physics! 🟡 Ride the Conveyor: Marbles land on the ever-moving circular conveyor belt. Plan your moves carefully! 🟢 Match & Pack: Guide the marbles into bottom boxes with 3 slots of the same color. 🔵 Don't Run Out of Space: If the conveyor is full of marbles and there are no matching boxes available, you lose! Think ahead and drop wisely.
