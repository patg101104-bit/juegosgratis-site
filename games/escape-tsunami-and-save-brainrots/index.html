Escape Tsunami and save Brainrots

Escape Tsunami and save Brainrots! Escape the tsunami, upgrade your base, rescue, and steal Brainrots. Can you catch all the legendary Brainrots? Are you ready for the wildest race of your life? This exciting game offers an incredible mix of action and collecting! Your main goal is to escape the giant tsunami that's relentlessly pursuing you. How to play? 🎮 Control your character, accelerate, and collect funny creatures along the way—Brainrots. The more you catch, the more you earn! But be careful: hesitate, and the wave will completely overwhelm you. Development and leveling up 💪 Earn coins and invest them in upgrades! Level up your Brainrots to make them more profitable. Expand your base by unlocking new slots. Legendary Goal ✨ Rumors of legendary creatures circulate among the many Brainrots... Will you be the lucky one to solve this mystery? Only the fastest and luckiest will reach the final! Join the chase, dodge the waves, complete your collection, and become the world champion of "Escape Tsunami and save Brainrots"! 🏆 [Core Gameplay & Story] Q: What is the game about and what do you do? A: You must survive a tsunami, rescue brainrots, and upgrade your base. Q: Is there a storyline? A: There isn't a formal story, but the objective is clear: escape the tsunami, save brainrots, and improve your base. Q: What is the game's hook or unique feature? A: The game features obbies and rare brainrots. Q: Are there any particularly engaging elements? A: The game includes rare and funny brainrots. [Mechanics & Progression] Q: How does the progression system work? A: You earn coins from brainrots and invest them to increase your running speed. You can also level up your brainrots to make them more profitable and expand your base by unlocking new slots. [Economy & Customization] Q: What currencies are available and how are they spent? A: There are two currencies: money and diamonds. They can be spent on speed boosts, packs, and the battle pass. Q: Are there in-game purchases? A: Yes, you can purchase diamonds and a battle pass. [Retention & Engagement] Q: Are there daily rewards to keep players coming back? A: Yes, logging in every day grants you diamonds, money, or a brainrot. Q: Is it better to play with sound? A: The music complements the experience well, but it isn't required to progress through the game.
