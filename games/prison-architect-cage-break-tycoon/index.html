Prison Architect: Cage Break Tycoon

At the beginning of the game you will be given an empty prison, which is not able to hold even ordinary prisoners, and there are also Bosses! They are especially dangerous! But it is in your power to make your prison better! - Build new cameras - Strengthen the doors - Add surveillance cameras - Hire security guards! - Don't forget about improvements! You can also upgrade your chief overseer - Make it faster - Improve his weapons - Carry more prisoners at the same time Once you have brought order to one prison, try doing the same in others! The game will be interesting for players of all ages. Everyone will find something interesting for themselves! This is an exciting trip for one person. The fate of the prisoners is in your hands. Great power goes hand in hand with great responsibility. Don't let me down =)!

¿Cómo jugar a Prison Architect: Cage Break Tycoon?

There are several prisons in the game. You need to stay in each prison for about 10 days. Every day you need to build fortifications, hire personnel and upgrade. At the end of each day, prisoners escape, and your task is to stop it. Defeat all the prisoners and move on to the next day, where new challenges, new prisoners and even Bosses await you! Control: - For PC. Character control - "WASD", "arrows" or hold the left mouse button and drag the mouse in the desired direction. Shooting - the hero shoots automatically. - For Mobile devices. Character control - press your finger on the screen and drag your finger in the desired direction. Shooting - the hero shoots automatically. - To interact with any activities, you just need to stand on them as a character.
