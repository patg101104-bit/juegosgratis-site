Build and Fly Obby

Build a plane from random parts — and see if it can actually fly. You are a mad inventor creating strange, funny and sometimes brilliant flying machines. Every build is an experiment: some planes wobble, some spin, and some surprisingly soar perfectly. Fly forward, dodge obstacles, collect coins and bonuses, pass through rings and gates. The farther you fly, the more parts you unlock — and the crazier your planes become. Game features: 🛠️ Free plane building ✈️ Unique physics — every design flies differently 🌍 Multiple biomes and locations 💥 Funny crashes and spectacular flights 🏆 Records and progression Experiment. Crash. Improve. The test is never over.

¿Cómo jugar a Build and Fly Obby?

Select the item in your inventory and tap on the plane to add the part Character controls: WASD or arrow keys - move Right-click to rotate the camera Space - jump Phone controls: Joystick for movement Camera rotation - swipe the screen
