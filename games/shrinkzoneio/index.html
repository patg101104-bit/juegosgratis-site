shrinkzone.io

Welcome to Shrinkzone, a lightning-fast, doodle-style battle royale where survival depends on your appetite! Start off as a tiny blob and devour food scattered across the map to grow larger and stronger. But beware — other players are doing the same. Use quick reflexes and clever movement to avoid the giants and hunt down the weak. As the shrinking safe zone forces players closer together, only the smartest and hungriest will survive. Are you ready to dominate the arena and claim the top spot?
