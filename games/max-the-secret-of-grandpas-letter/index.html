Max: The secret of Grandpa's letter

A treasure hunt quest. Meet Max, a young wolf who dreams of adventure. One day, Max receives a letter from his late grandfather, who, like any good grandfather, always loved to play riddles with his grandson. Even after he passed away. With the letter, Max receives a treasure map and mysterious clues from his grandfather's old notebook. This means it's time to pack a backpack and embark on a journey! Embarking on an exciting journey, our friendly wolf cub will face simple yet fun challenges. Max's path leads to the mysterious Island of Lost Ships, where the routes of sailors end and the adventures of true treasure hunters begin! With enthusiasm and a wagging tail, Max sets out to find his grandfather's treasure. Are you ready to unravel mysteries and become true adventurers alongside him? Let's embark on a new journey of discovery!

¿Cómo jugar a Max: The secret of Grandpa's letter?

To win - find the treasure. To do this, perform tasks and follow the instructions. Control: ASWD keys, for jumping - space, camera rotation - hold down the right mouse button and turn the mouse. Camera range - mouse wheel On the phone: mobile joystick.
