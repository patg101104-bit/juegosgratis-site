Obby: King of the Hill

👑 Welcome to “Obby: King of the Hill”! A fun online obby game where everyone races to reach the top and become the true King of the Hill! Game features: 🌀 Exciting obby challenges — run, jump, and overcome tricky obstacles. 👊 Fun player interactions — bump into rivals and knock them off the mountain. 👑 King of the Hill mode — reach the top and try to hold your position. 🎭 Funny falls and chaotic moments — every match is full of surprises. 🎨 Bright 3D graphics and simple, easy-to-learn gameplay. 🚀 Climb higher, compete with other players, and prove that you deserve the crown! Are you ready to reach the top and stay there? 🏔️👑

¿Cómo jugar a Obby: King of the Hill?

🎮 Controls (PC): W / A / S / D — move character Space — jump LMB — club attack Camera — RMB + mouse movement 📱 Controls (Mobile): Joystick — move Jump button — jump Attack button — club attack All actions are performed using easy on-screen buttons. 🚀 Progress & Power-Ups Climb the mountain, knock down rivals, and earn experience during matches. The more you play, the stronger and more stable your character becomes.
