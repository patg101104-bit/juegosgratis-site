Gun Baron

Become the boss of the arms business! You're a red panda building an empire in a world ruled by raccoon bandits. Set up production: install machines, craft pistols, assault rifles, and dynamite, sell to customers, and save up for your dream. But the road to riches is dangerous. Hostile raccoons regularly raid your base. Buy tougher weapons, upgrade your stats, and keep your paw on the trigger to protect your conveyor belts and warehouse. Key features: • Engaging production system • Dynamic real-time shootouts • Living economy: satisfy customers and defend them from raccoons • Vibrant world with signature humor • Clear goal: accumulate capital, build a rocket, and blast off! • Upgrade your skills and buy better gear Every gun sold brings you closer to that dream rocket. Every raid is a test, every dollar a step toward the sky. Run your factory, repel attacks, and become a legend. Launch Gun Baron now — your journey to the stars starts here! 🚀

¿Cómo jugar a Gun Baron?

🏭 1. Build & Optimize Place machines, keep shelves stocked. Shorter queues = bigger profits. 💰 2. Craft & Sell Make pistols, rifles, dynamite. Customers come automatically: track demand, adjust stock, grow your capital. 🔫 3. Defend Against Raids Raccoon bandits attack regularly. Buy weapons, upgrade your panda skills, defend in real-time. 🚀 4. Save for the Rocket Every dollar brings launch closer. Escape this cruel planet. 💡 Tip: Balance production and defense. Invest in character upgrades, guard your warehouse, and maintain customer reputation.
