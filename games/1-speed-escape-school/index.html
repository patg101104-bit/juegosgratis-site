+1 SPEED: Escape School

+1 SPEED: Escape School is an exciting parkour game with unique +1 to speed mechanics, where you have to escape from a school full of traps, challenges, and an evil teacher. With each step, your character becomes faster, and the levels become more difficult and dynamic. Test your reaction, agility, and speed of thinking in this exciting school adventure! The game features a variety of levels with obstacles: - moving platforms - traps - school corridors - lava ball Your task is to find a way out of the school while avoiding the evil teacher and dangers along the way. The further you go, the faster you get and the more interesting the gameplay becomes. Game features: 🔹 Unique +1 to Speed mechanic - accelerate with every step 🔹 Exciting school-themed parkour 🔹 Traps, obstacles, and secret paths 🔹 Escape from the evil teacher and parkour challenges 🔹 Simple controls and dynamic gameplay Can you complete all the levels and get out of school faster than anyone else? [Core Gameplay & Story] Q: What is the game about and what do you do? A: It is a game where you have to escape from school by navigating multiple obstacle-filled levels. You'll need to jump across platforms, dodge teachers, and avoid various traps. Q: Is there a storyline in the game? A: The main goal is for the player to successfully escape from the school. Q: What is the game's unique hook and what will attract players? A: The game features high-quality graphics, engaging obby gameplay, and deep character customization—including skins, glasses, hats, clothing, and wings. It also boasts a unique obstacle course. Q: What are the most engaging content elements? A: The game features a wide variety of interesting skins and challenging obby courses. [Mechanics & Progression] Q: How does the progression system work, and how can you level up faster? A: You can increase your running speed by using the treadmill or by gaining speed directly within the levels. [Economy & Customization] Q: What currencies are used and how can they be spent? A: There are two currencies: diamonds and money. Both can be used to purchase new skins and upgrade your running speed. Q: Are there in-app purchases? What can you buy, and does spending money speed up progress? A: Yes, the game includes in-app purchases. You can buy diamonds to unlock skins and upgrade your speed. Purchasing items does accelerate your progress. Q: Is there character customization available? A: Yes, you can fully customize your character's appearance by selecting individual pieces of clothing. [Retention & Engagement] Q: Are there leaderboards? How do you join them and what types are available? A: Yes, there is an all-time leaderboard based on a player's maximum running speed. Q: Is it better to play with sound for immersion, or will it be difficult to play without it? A: The music is a great addition and enhances the atmosphere, but it is not required to complete the game.
