Survival Tycoon: City of Zombie

Survival Tycoon: City of Zombie - Your Camp, Your Rules! You are the leader of a survivor group in the heart of a zombie apocalypse. This is a thrilling idle tycoon strategy where your goal is to protect the barrier, gather resources, and survive! A true survival simulator for those who love camp management and crushing hordes of enemies. What awaits you: ✅ Trap Defense: Protect your barrier! Research and install deadly gear - from deadly spikes to massive decoy bombs! ✅ Relocation & Progress: Don't stay still! Move between motels and farms, upgrade your camp, and unlock new zones. ✅ Scavenging Runs: Send your team for fuel. It’s a classic survival strategy - every drop of gas counts! ✅ Camp Evolution: Develop your facilities, automate your income, and become the ultimate master of the wasteland! Strengthen the barrier, gear up your scouts, and hit the gas. Become a survival legend right now!

¿Cómo jugar a Survival Tycoon: City of Zombie?

🛡 Protect the barrier: Your main goal is to prevent the zombie horde from breaking into the camp. 🏗 Place traps: Simply drag defensive devices from the bottom panel to the highlighted zones on the battlefield. 💰 Complete quests: Follow the quest list to earn resources and grow your camp's economy. 🚀 Upgrade everything: Click on buildings and objects to increase their durability, damage, or income. 🖱 Interact: Click directly on zombies and objects on the map to actively assist your survivors. Controls: Mouse: Click and drag (to install traps), left-click (for menus and actions).
