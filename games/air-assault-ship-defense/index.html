Air Assault: Ship Defense

Man a twin AA gun on a WW2 destroyer and shoot down waves of diving planes before they sink your ship. Air Assault puts you in the splinter tub of a wartime destroyer — twin barrels, open ocean, and enemy aircraft diving straight at your deck. Aim with the crosshair, manage gun heat, and cut down fighters before they reach the hull. Stages ramp up fast: denser swarms, faster dives, bombers and armored blimps on high flyovers, and kamikazes that don’t turn away. Planes shoot back. Bombs fall. One bad miss and the ship goes down. Upgrade your mount between runs, climb the stages, and hold the line as the sky fills with incoming fire. Simple controls, sharp 3D action — pure anti-aircraft mayhem in the middle of the Pacific.

¿Cómo jugar a Air Assault: Ship Defense?

You are the AA gunner on a destroyer. Shoot down every plane before they hit your ship. Desktop Move the mouse to aim (center reticle is your aim point) Hold left mouse button or Space to fire Mobile Drag to aim The gun auto-fires when the reticle is on a target Tips Watch the heat gauge — overheating locks the gun until it cools Protect hull HP; repair a bit between stages Fighters dive first; later stages bring bombers, blimps, and kamikazes Spend coins in the shop between runs to upgrade damage, cooling, and hull Pause anytime from the top-left button
