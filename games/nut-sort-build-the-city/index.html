Nut Sort: Build the City

Dive into the captivating world of «Nut Sort: Build the City» — a thrilling puzzle game where your task is to bring order to the chaos of nuts and bolts! This isn’t just your classic color-sorting game. Every completed level brings you closer to building the city of your dreams. Solve puzzles, earn nuts, and watch houses and parks rise before your eyes. 🏙️ **BUILD CITIES**: Complete levels, earn resources, and construct 14 unique cities. 🧩 **ENGAGING LEVELS**: Thousands of puzzles with varying difficulty will keep you entertained. 🎁 **BONUSES**: Unlock special bonus levels after every 5 regular ones. 💡 **HINTS**: View a level solution or get an extra bolt if you're stuck. 🧠 **ENJOYABLE EXPERIENCE**: Relaxing gameplay that also trains your brain. «Nut Sort: Build the City» is the perfect blend of soothing sorting and engaging strategy. Sharpen your mind and become the ultimate mayor in the world of puzzles!

¿Cómo jugar a Nut Sort: Build the City?

🎯 Your goal is to organize all the nuts by color. A level is considered complete when each bolt holds nuts of only one color. 📜 Simple rules: To move a nut, first tap the bolt you want to take it from, then tap the bolt you want to place it on. You can only move the topmost nut from a bolt. A nut can be placed only on top of another nut of the same color or on any empty bolt. 🏗️ Victory and building: For each successfully completed level, you earn valuable nuts as a reward. Use them to construct buildings and develop your city! 💡 In-game help: If you’re stuck, use a hint or add an extra bolt to the field to make things easier. Good luck!
