Schoolboy Runaway: room escape

Skuf dad won't let you go for a walk, can you escape room? Make first stealth room escape! Schoolboy kid wants to runaway, not learn lessons, so go escape room! "Schoolboy Runaway: room escape" is an exciting 3D first-person quest where you are a schoolboy kid and strict parents do not let you go outside. 👨👩‍🦱 Dad and mom are constantly giving you tasks, like cleaning toys or washing the floor, but schoolboy patience is running out! It's time to search hidden objects in house and escape room! 🏃‍♂️ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Please write your questions and ideas for new levels!

¿Cómo jugar a Schoolboy Runaway: room escape?

💻 PC: WASD - Walk Mouse - Camera Rotation Left mouse button - Interaction 📲 Phone: Joystick in the left half of the screen - Walk Swipe in the right half of the screen - Camera rotation Click on objects - Interaction
