Badlands Hero

Embark on an epic journey in the game "Badlands Hero", where danger lurks around every corner and adventure awaits at every step. As you play, you will: - Complete levels - Earn experience points to unlock new characters - Collect gold to upgrade your characters and weapons - Unlock new types of weapons - Defeat bosses

¿Cómo jugar a Badlands Hero?

Controls for PC/Keyboard: - Movement: Use the W, A, S, D keys or left mouse click to move your character . Controls for Mobile Devices/Touchscreen: - Movement: Tap and drag on the screen to move your character.
