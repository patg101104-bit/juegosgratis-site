¿Cómo jugar a Hex Block Puzzle Master?

Drag hexagonal blocks onto the grid using your mouse or touch controls. Place each block carefully to fill the available spaces. Blocks cannot be rotated, so plan your moves strategically. Complete the grid to solve the puzzle and progress to more challenging levels. Use logic and smart placement to achieve the best results.
