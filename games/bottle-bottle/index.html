Bottle Bottle

Bottle Bottle is a casual jumping game that is easy to play but challenging to master. In the game, you control a bottle and help it jump from one platform to another. With simple tap controls, the bottle flips and jumps forward. Perfect timing and rhythm are the key to landing safely and going further. Clean visuals and smooth animations make the gameplay relaxing and fun. Try again and again to beat your best distance!

¿Cómo jugar a Bottle Bottle?

Tap the screen once to make the bottle jump. Tap the screen again while in the air to perform a second jump. You can jump up to two times in a row.
