Super Fruit Runner - Hyper Casual

In this hyper casual runner game 🚀, you control fast-moving fruits and dodge obstacles to reach the finish line 🍏. With 15 unique levels 🗺️ and 12 special achievements 🎖️, the fun never ends! Thanks to 7 language options — English 🇬🇧, Russian 🇷🇺, French 🇫🇷, Turkish 🇹🇷, Spanish 🇪🇸, German 🇩🇪, and Italian 🇮🇹 — everyone can enjoy the game 🌍. Destroy fruits by hitting knives 🗡️ and earn Yakut to buy powerful upgrades 💎, including: Increase starting fruit count 🍎 Increase Yakut earnings 💰 Extra reward for hitting green platforms ➕ Reduce loss from hitting red platforms ➖ Use your speed, reflexes, and strategy 🧠💨 to clear all levels, unlock every achievement 🎖️.

¿Cómo jugar a Super Fruit Runner - Hyper Casual?

1️⃣ Start the Game! 🚀 Run your fruit forward 🏃‍♂️, dodge obstacles, and complete levels! 🍏 Every step requires speed and focus! ⚡ 2️⃣ Avoid obstacles! ❌ Try not to hit the knives — but if you do, you’ll earn Yakut. 3️⃣ Watch the platforms! 🟩 Green platforms give you extra rewards ➕, 🔴 red platforms reduce your fruit count ➖. 4️⃣ Use your Yakut! Between levels, upgrade your abilities 🎯 to start stronger. 5️⃣ Complete the goals! 📜 Collect all 12 special achievements and finish every level to become the master of the game! 🏅 💡 Tip: Balance your upgrades ⚖️ to survive longer and maximize your Yakut earnings! 🔥
