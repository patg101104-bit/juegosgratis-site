Locked in Grandma’s Basement 2: Revenge - Horror Escape

Locked in Grandma’s Basement 2: Revenge – Horror Escape is the chilling sequel to the cult classic point-and-click horror game. After managing to escape once, Grandma has found you again — and this time, she’s locked you in an even darker, more twisted place. Explore eerie rooms, solve cryptic puzzles, and uncover cursed objects and grotesque clues. Use your logic and courage to find tools, unlock hidden paths, and perform a disturbing ritual to escape. Every item has a purpose, every detail tells a story — and Grandma is always watching. Will you manage to escape again, or will you become her next ritual offering? Perfect for fans of horror, escape rooms, and point-and-click adventures.

¿Cómo jugar a Locked in Grandma’s Basement 2: Revenge - Horror Escape?

This is a classic point-and-click adventure with a first-person view. You don’t move around freely — instead, you explore each scene from a fixed perspective. Click on objects to examine them, collect useful items, and use them in the right places to solve puzzles and progress through the story. Every interaction matters, so pay attention to details and think logically to escape.
