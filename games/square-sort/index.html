Square Sort

The game involves swiping colorful cubes in any direction to clear the board. The objective is to sort the squares by color, and the game gets progressively harder with each level. The user interface is simple and easy to use, with visually appealing graphics that are pleasing to the eye. The game has a relaxing soundtrack that complements its challenging gameplay. Square Sort offers a great way to exercise your brain while having fun, and it's perfect for those who enjoy puzzle games. The game is free to download and play, with no in-app purchases or ads to interrupt the gameplay.

¿Cómo jugar a Square Sort?

Swipe to Move: Swipe the colorful cubes in any direction to move them across the board. Match Colors: Align cubes of the same color in a row or column to clear them from the board. Limited Moves: Each level has a set number of moves. Plan your swipes carefully to maximize efficiency. Progressive Difficulty: As you advance through levels, the complexity increases with more colors and larger grids.
