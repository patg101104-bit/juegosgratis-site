Squad Assembler: Merge

Assemble a cool squad and see who wins! Combine items to improve the fighters' equipment! Combine weapons and armor, buy loot boxes with new weapons! You can create cool, unique characters, each of which will have certain skills! Buy parts for your fighters, change their arms, torso, head! Create real cyborgs to defeat the enemy! Merge armor and weapons, increase the levels and characteristics of items. Hire cool heroes who will help you in tough battles. Defeat powerful bosses, look for treasure chests, capture enemy outposts! Game features: - Unique gameplay that combines merge, strategy, fighting and sandbox! - Combine items, weapons and armor - Multifunctional character editor, you can assemble a unique character that will only be in your squad! - A variety of different weapons, from bats and knives to grenade launchers, flamethrowers and plasma guns! Collect your fighters and win! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do you need to do? A: Idle merge game where you need to create a squad from different components and progress through levels to defeat enemies. Q: Is there a storyline in the game? A: There is no specific storyline, but the objective is clear: gather a squad and defeat enemies. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? What can hook players in? A: An interesting mechanic of collecting and upgrading the squad in parts. Q: Are there bosses, and how are they different? A: Yes, bosses are very tough, requiring a strong squad to defeat. Players also have heroes at their disposal. Q: What currencies are there and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: Money is spent on chests from which parts for squad members are obtained. Mechanics & Progression Q: How does leveling up work exactly? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: Open chests, get squad member parts, combine them, and level up the squad. Economy & Customization Q: Are there in-game purchases? What can be bought and what does it affect? Does donating speed up game progress? A: Yes, they allow speeding up squad gathering and significantly strengthening it. Q: Are there engaging content elements (weapons, vehicles, locations, skills, references to famous franchises, etc.)? A: There are references to action franchises (the very first hero is Shredder from Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles). Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you customize the appearance of the hero, location editor, car tuning, etc.? A: Yes, the entire game is built around squad customization. Retention & Engagement Q: Are there daily rewards or something similar that encourages players to return? A: No. Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to better immerse in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in playing without sound? A: Yes, but it's not mandatory. Q: Are there mini-games within the main storyline? For example, match-three puzzles within a strategy game. A: Mini-game based on merging and squad collection. Q: Is the number of levels clear? How many are there? (skip if unclear) A: Yes, it is indicated in the UI. Q: Are there achievement systems? What are they, and what can you get them for? A: No.

¿Cómo jugar a Squad Assembler: Merge?

To win the level, you need to defeat the enemy squad. To increase the level of items - combine 2 identical items. Controls: To buy a loot box - click the button with the image of the loot box and the price tag. To open the loot box - click on the loot box in the inventory. To create a fighter, drag the item to the equipment cell and click the "Create" button To send the squad into battle - click the "TO BATTLE" button
