¿Cómo jugar a Match Story: Weapons?

Match Story: Weapons is a fun 3-match puzzle game. Your goal is to carefully observe the weapons on the board and match 3 of the same kind. - Select weapons from the board and place them into the box. - When you get 3 identical weapons together, they will match and disappear. - Try to clear all matches before the timer runs out. - As you progress, the levels become more challenging with more weapon types and complex arrangements. Think strategically, act fast, and become the ultimate weapon master!
