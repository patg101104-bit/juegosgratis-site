Froggy Hop

Froggy Hop is a fun and addictive arcade platformer where you launch your frog into the sky! Bounce off lily pads, spring pads, turtles, whales, dodge tricky traps, and collect coins and gems as you go. Upgrade your frog’s abilities to jump higher, bounce farther, and unlock cool new skins. How far can you hop?

¿Cómo jugar a Froggy Hop?

Click at the right time to bounce over lily pads, turtles, spring pads and dodge obstacles by bouncing over them. Desktop Controls: Use the left mouse button to launch the frog. Mobile Controls: Tap on the screen to launch and bounce the frog.
