Drone Dash - Time Challenges!

**How to Play** Welcome to **Drone Dash - Time Challenges** – a fast-paced arcade puzzler where quick thinking and precision bouncing are the keys to victory! You’ll control a zippy little drone that *dashes* between walls and platforms like a high-tech pinball. But this isn’t your typical platformer – no jumping, no gravity drops. Your drone glides and ricochets from surface to surface, smoothly darting through tight spaces full of traps and twists. Your mission? **Collect as many bullets as you can before time runs out!** The more you grab, the more stars you’ll earn (up to 3 per level). Once you collect the minimum required, a glowing crystal spawns back at your starting point – reach it to complete the level. But watch out! Hit a trap or run out of time, and it's back to the start. Levels are short, intense, and packed with surprises. New traps and tricky tiles will keep you on your toes as you dash your way to perfection. Fast reflexes and smart movement make all the difference! **Controls:** 🖱️ On PC: Just click to bounce your drone in the direction of your choice! 📱 On Mobile: Just tap to bounce your drone in the direction of your choice! Ready to test your reflexes and grab those stars? Dive into the dash and beat the clock!
