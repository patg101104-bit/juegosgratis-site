Roll to Riches

Roll to Riches is a colorful 3D arcade simulator where you train your rolling wheel, race as far as possible, earn Cash and Trophies, collect pets, unlock new wheels and trails, claim rewards, rebirth for stronger progression, and work toward new worlds.

¿Cómo jugar a Roll to Riches?

Train on the treadmill to gain Energy, then enter races to roll as far as possible and earn Cash and Trophies. Use your rewards to buy upgrades, unlock wheels and trails, collect pets, claim daily and playtime rewards, and rebirth to progress faster. Desktop: use WASD or Arrow Keys to move, drag the mouse to rotate the camera, press E to interact, and press Space or tap during races to build speed. Mobile: use the virtual joystick to move, drag the screen to rotate the camera, tap the Interact button near stations, and tap during races to build speed.
