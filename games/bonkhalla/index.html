Bonkhalla

Choose one of 6 heroes and battle endless waves of monsters across three unique biomes: Forest, Desert, and Graveyard. Collect experience, level up, and combine 12 weapon types with upgrade tomes to unlock powerful evolutions: the Sword transforms into a Holy Blade, the Fireball into Inferno, and Lightning into the Thunder God! Over 30 items, 30 quests, 10 challenges with unique rules (one-on-one with bosses, no healing, glass cannon) and a shop with permanent upgrades — every run will feel unique. Survive 10 minutes, defeat the final boss, and try to endure the Final Storm!

¿Cómo jugar a Bonkhalla?

Weapons attack automatically — focus on moving and dodging enemies. Collect experience crystals — upon leveling up, choose a new weapon, upgrades, or tomes. Bring a weapon to level 5 and pick the right tome — and you'll unlock a powerful evolution! Smash pots and chests for loot, activate shrines for temporary buffs. Defeat mini-bosses and the final boss before time runs out — otherwise the Final Storm begins! Earn silver and upgrade your heroes in the shop between runs. Complete quests and challenges for unique rewards.
