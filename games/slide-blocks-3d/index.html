Slide Blocks 3D

Slide Blocks 3D is a relaxing yet brain-teasing sliding puzzle. Colorful pictures are cut into blocks and scrambled across the board — slide the pieces to put every picture back together before the timer runs out. Levels grow bigger and trickier as you progress. Earn coins, claim daily rewards, and climb the leaderboard!

¿Cómo jugar a Slide Blocks 3D?

Drag pieces with your mouse or finger to slide them across the board. Reassemble every picture in its frame to complete the level before time runs out. Stuck? Use boosters: Hint highlights the right spot, Undo reverts your last move, and Magnet snaps a piece into place. Finish levels to earn coins, beat your best score, and climb the leaderboard.
