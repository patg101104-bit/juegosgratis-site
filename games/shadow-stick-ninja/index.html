Shadow Stick Ninja

Shadowstick Ninja is a physics puzzle game with a dark, minimalist style. In this game, precision is more important than speed. You play as a silent shadow ninja with only a few arrows. In each level, enemy ninjas hide behind obstacles and structures, waiting for you to take them out. Your goal is to eliminate every enemy. The way you do it is up to you! Pull back, aim, and release to shoot arrows with the right trajectory. Pick from different arrow types and solve each level your own way: - Regular arrows for clean, direct hits - Bomb arrows to cause explosive area damage and break through obstacles - Balloon arrows that attach to enemies and lift them into the air, exposing them for easier shots There isn’t just one right answer. You can play smart and efficient, or try out new ideas and experiment as much as you want. Lift an enemy into the air with balloons. Use lots of bombs just to see what happens. Try odd angles, mix up different moves, or come up with wild solutions. Every arrow counts. If you run out of ammo, you’ll need to rethink your strategy and give it another shot. Plan your moves, try bold ideas, and enjoy every physics-based takedown.

¿Cómo jugar a Shadow Stick Ninja?

Desktop Controls: - Click and drag with your mouse to aim your shot. - Release the mouse button to fire the arrow. - Change how far and at what angle you drag to control the power and direction, similar to using a slingshot. Mobile Controls: - Tap and drag with your finger to aim your shot. - Release your finger to fire the arrow. Arrow Selection: - Choose your arrow type from the icons at the top left of the screen before you shoot. - Each arrow type has limited ammo, so pick carefully. - You can finish levels with any mix of arrows, depending on your strategy. Objective: - Take out all enemy ninjas with the arrows you have. If you run out of arrows before beating the level, try again with a different approach.
