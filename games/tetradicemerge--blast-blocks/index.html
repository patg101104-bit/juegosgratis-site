¿Cómo jugar a TetraDice–Merge & Blast Blocks?

- Your goal is to create combinations using the provided figures. - You get points for successfully collected combinations and perform special tasks. - The more matches - the more points. - In adventure mode, you need to complete levels by completing assigned tasks. - In classic mode, your goal is to score the maximum number of points before the game ends. Controls PC LMB (left mouse button) - selects, rotates and moves figures. Mobile Touch (tap) - rotates the figure. Dragging with your finger - moves the figure on the playing field.
