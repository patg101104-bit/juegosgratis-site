Obby: Climb Up and Slide Down on Minecarts

Welcome to an exciting game where you climb upward on carts, collect money, and upgrade your ride! Make your way through various levels and push your progress higher and higher. Each run earns you money that can be spent on a brand-new cart. The higher you climb, the more challenging the track becomes—but the rewards grow as well. Unlock new zones, explore diverse locations, and get stronger with every attempt. Adorable pets will accompany you on your journey. Collect a wide variety of pets, level them up, and use their unique abilities to complete levels faster and more efficiently. Compete with friends for the best score, improve your skills, and prove that you are the ultimate rising racer!

¿Cómo jugar a Obby: Climb Up and Slide Down on Minecarts?

🎮 On PC ● WASD – Move ● Mouse – Camera control 📱 On Mobile ● Use the on-screen controls ● Drag anywhere on the screen – Camera control
