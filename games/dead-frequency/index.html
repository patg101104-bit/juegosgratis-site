DEAD FREQUENCY

Defend the Signal Tower! A fast-paced tower defense game set in a vibrant red wasteland. Protect your crystal-powered signal tower from waves of enemies using upgradeable stats like damage, speed, bounce, and diamond yield. Level up your defenses, manage resources, and survive as long as you can!

¿Cómo jugar a DEAD FREQUENCY?

Objective: Defend your crystal-powered signal tower from waves of enemies. Upgrade your stats, collect resources, and survive as long as possible in a hostile red wasteland. Gameplay Overview: Enemies approach your tower in waves. Your goal is to stop them before they destroy it. Use earned currency (₮) to upgrade your character’s Damage, Max Health, Speed, Bounce, and Diamond Yield. The longer you survive, the stronger the enemies become—so keep upgrading!
