Road Madness

If you like race cars and guns, you will love this game! You can upgrade and unlock more destructive cars, the further you get. Enjoy epic effects and power ups while hunting the new bosses and get achievements for it!

¿Cómo jugar a Road Madness?

Shoot enemy cars and upgrade your gear to survive in higher levels. Avoid obstacles and gather power ups from destroyed special enemies. Defeat bosses at the end of levels to progress further.
