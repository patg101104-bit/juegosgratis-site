King Rugni Tower Defense

A classic Tower Defense game with lots of enemies and territories to conquer. Join King Rügni on his epic adventure against hordes of elementals. Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do you need to do? A: It's a tower defense game where you need to place and upgrade towers that shoot at enemies. Q: Is there a storyline in the game? A: There is no storyline. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? What can hook players? A: The game features a cool character - King Rugni, several maps with different themes like forests and winter settings, great design, music, and effects. There are different types of enemies with various attack styles - some walk directly on the path while others fly straight to the target. Q: Are there any currencies in the game and what are they used for (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: Every defeated enemy rewards us with gems used for building new towers. There is also gold earned by winning levels, which is spent on upgrading existing towers and purchasing new ones. Mechanics & Progression Q: How does leveling up work exactly? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: To level up faster, you need to win levels by defeating enemies and completing all waves. A: No. Q: Are there daily rewards or something similar to encourage players to return? A: No. Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to immerse oneself better in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in passing without sound? ¿Cómo jugar a King Rugni Tower Defense?

Build towers and raise your defense system against enemies. Keep at least one of your 10 lives to complete the levels. Beware the final bosses of each zone. If they make it through the level alive, you'll be doomed.
