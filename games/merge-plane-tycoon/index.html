Merge Plane Tycoon

Merge Plane Tycoon is a fun and addictive tycoon game where you build your own air empire by merging planes and upgrading them into powerful aircraft ✈️⚡ Start with small planes, merge identical ones, and unlock faster, stronger, and more advanced jets 🚀 Each upgrade boosts your income and expands your airline business 💰 Manage your runway, discover new plane types, and watch your fleet grow automatically even while you’re offline ⏳ Simple controls, satisfying merge mechanics, and colorful visuals make Merge Plane Tycoon perfect for casual and idle gameplay 🎮 How far can you grow your air empire and dominate the skies? ☁️👑

¿Cómo jugar a Merge Plane Tycoon?

Drag a plane and drop it onto another plane of the same type using your mouse or touch controls. When they match, the planes merge into a stronger one with higher value and performance. Keep merging, unlocking new planes, and expanding your runway to earn more money and grow your air empire
