Geometry Dash: Infinity

The game "Geometry Dash: Infinity" is an exciting arcade where you have to navigate through challenging and interesting obstacles and mazes, earning points in the process. Think you have the reaction time and agility of a cat? Well, let's find out! Enter the game and try to score more than anyone else.

¿Cómo jugar a Geometry Dash: Infinity?

Playing the game is extremely simple. Just tap on the screen, and your spacecraft will fly upward. Release your finger, and the spacecraft will descend. When you lose (and you will definitely lose :)), you can check your position in the global rankings.
