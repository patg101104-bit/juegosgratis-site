Slasty: Candy Tower

Meet “Slasty: Candy Tower” - a breathtaking action-arcade where cute hungry monsters sit on the crazy-spinning platforms, and you shoot them with a candy gun to feed them up! Try to make your candy-shots precise to achieve unbelievable combos, set records and compete with other players to become the best! Feed a monster simply by tapping on the screen at the right time to make a candy shot. Complete tower-levels with various platforms, but beware, they are spinning faster and faster! Open new levels with new towers. Each tower has different mechanics, style, and platforms. Game works without internet, just download and play wherever you want. Play at any time, in any place, your progress will not be lost. Features: - Control the Hero on the tower and shoot monsters with candies - Dive into awesome toon environment - Make accurate shots, combos and set high scores - Don’t rush but be precise to win - Feel the challenge of the game - Compete with players around the world and set the highest score - Simple one-touch controls - Plain and clear UI

¿Cómo jugar a Slasty: Candy Tower?

Each game level consists of several stages which gradually become more difficult. To complete the stage you need to feed all of the chewbrick-monsters with a candy from your cannon. Feed a monster simply by tapping on the screen or pressing the space bar at the right time to make a candy shot - and you'll feed the chewbrick.
