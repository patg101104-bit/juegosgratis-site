Papa Cherry Saga

Welcome to the world of sweets! Papa Cherry Saga is a colourful match-3 puzzle where you collect cookies, marshmallows, chocolate, and other treats. Swap pieces to make a row of 3 or more of the same kind. Match 4 in a line to create a Striped Cookie. Match 4 in a T or L shape to make a Bomb. Match 5 in a row to get a Rainbow Bomb that clears all pieces of one color. Combine power-ups together for devastating effects. Charge up the Chef by activating power-ups on the field, and he will reward you with a bonus. Hundreds of levels with varied goals: collect specific sweets, break obstacles, guide gingerbread cookies to the bottom. Use boosters like the Glove, Rolling Pin, Lollipop, and Undo to beat the toughest challenges.

¿Cómo jugar a Papa Cherry Saga?

Drag a piece toward an adjacent one to swap them. If the swap creates a match of 3 or more identical pieces in a row or column, they are collected and new ones drop in. Match 4 in a line to create a Striped Cookie that clears an entire row or column. Match 4 in a T or L shape to make a Bomb that explodes in a 3x3 area. Match 5 in a row to get a Rainbow Bomb that clears all pieces of one color. Combine power-ups together for even stronger effects. Activate power-ups on the field to charge the Chef — once full, he sends a bonus power-up to the board. Use the boosters at the bottom of the screen — they do not cost a move. The Glove removes any single piece. The Rolling Pin clears one row and one column. The Lollipop removes all pieces of a chosen color. Undo rewinds the last move.
