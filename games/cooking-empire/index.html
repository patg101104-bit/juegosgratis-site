Cooking Empire

🍔 Cooking Empire is not just a cooking game, it’s a real explosion of flavors and speed! 🍟 Start frying, boiling, baking and serving dishes from all over the world with one click! Are you ready to become a chef the whole world will remember? 🍳 👨‍🍳 Turn an ordinary kitchen into the epicenter of culinary fever! Cook tom yum, serve juicy burgers, cook foie gras and surprise guests with exotic recipes. The goal is simple: create your own culinary empire, where every visitor is a happy gourmet! 🔥 ✈️ Travel through the flavors of the planet! Discover the aromas of Paris, the fire of Beijing, the warmth of Rome and the taste of Moscow. Dozens of cities, hundreds of restaurants and thousands of recipes are waiting for you - from classics to hidden delicacies! Want to bake, fry, boil and experiment? Welcome to Cooking Empire!

¿Cómo jugar a Cooking Empire?

🍰 Cook anywhere, anytime! -Free, fun, and always at hand -Turn any break into a culinary adventure! 🌍Many cities and dream restaurants! -From pizzerias to sushi bars -More than 100 dishes: from ice cream to seafood -Master the world's cuisines and become a culinary legend! 🎯1000+ levels of challenge and fun! -Each level is a new culinary battle -Mini-games, events, festivals and seasonal restaurants -Participate in monthly events and get cool prizes -Create combos and earn super bonuses! 🍴 Manage your culinary universe -Serve dishes that gourmets have never dreamed of -Improve products, speed up cooking, expand the kitchen -Decorate restaurants and attract even more customers!
