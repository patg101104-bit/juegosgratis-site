Mine Mining Islands: Skyblock Village!

In this game you will plunge into the exciting world of mining adventures! - Become the mayor of your village: upgrade your village. Discover new opportunities and bargain with the residents! - Variety of islands: be the discoverer of new islands! Each island opens up new opportunities for your exciting adventure! - Upgrade your character: Upgrade your character's skills, making him faster, luckier and stronger. - Competitive spirit: Get resources and sell them to become the richest player in this game! And can you become the best in this game? Embark on an exciting adventure with your favorite characters! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What needs to be done? A: Mining adventures in the style of Minecraft. Players need to unlock islands and gather resources. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? What can hook players? A: A well-developed game in the style of Minecraft, with an achievement system, plenty of tools for resource gathering, and character progression. Q: Are there any currencies and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: The game features gold as currency, which can be used to upgrade better tools for resource gathering, exchange resources, level up characters, and unlock islands. Q: Are there engaging content elements (weapons, vehicles, locations, skills, references to famous franchises, etc.)? A: References to the Minecraft world. Mechanics & Progression Q: How does character progression work? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: Gather more resources to sell and earn coins, which can be used to level up characters and tools. After unlocking an island, players can place statues and upgrade them to earn passive income. Economy & Customization Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you customize the appearance of the hero, location editor, car tuning, etc.? A: Yes, the game offers skins for your avatar, which is displayed in the corner. Retention & Engagement Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to better immerse yourself in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in passing without sound? A: Music complements the game, but it is not necessary for gameplay. Q: Are there achievement systems? What are they and what can be obtained for them? A: The game features achievements that can be obtained during gameplay. For example, for a certain number of blocks destroyed or for unlocking the first island.

¿Cómo jugar a Mine Mining Islands: Skyblock Village!?

PC controls: W, A, S, D or ↑, ←, ↓, → — move. The space bar is a jump. Mouse movement is camera control. Left mouse button — destruction of blocks Management for mobile devices: The joystick on the left is moving. The button on the left with the image of the tool is the destruction of blocks. The button on the right is jump. Swipes on the right side of the screen — camera control.
