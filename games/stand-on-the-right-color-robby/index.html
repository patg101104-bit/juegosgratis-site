Stand on the Right Color, Robby!

"Stand on the right color, Robby!" is an addictive arcade game where your goal is to reach the finish line by jumping on colored tiles. You need to react quickly when the color tiles change randomly. You have limited time at each stage. Be vigilant and react instantly so as not to fall and get ahead of your rivals. Your reaction, accuracy and skill will be put to the test. In this exciting game you have to fight for first place on your way to the finish line. Earn in - game currency and unlock unique Robls skins, items and effects - this will add excitement and variety to the gameplay. Are you ready to take on the challenge and complete all the tiles to the finish line in this incredible game? Defeat your opponents, reach the top of the leaderboard and prove that you are the best!

¿Cómo jugar a Stand on the Right Color, Robby!?

Your goal is to reach the finish line. Computer control: W, A, S, D, Right mouse button - character control Space, Left mouse button - jump Mouse - camera control Mobile controls: Left joystick - character control Button on the screen - jump Right joystick - camera control
