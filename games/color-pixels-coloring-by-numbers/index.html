Color Pixels - Coloring by Numbers

Immerse yourself in a delightful realm of art and tranquility with Color Pixels - Coloring by Numbers. Easily turn black-and-white images into colorful creations by following the numbered instructions. This free online game offers a fun way to unwind, whether on a phone or computer, allowing everyone to achieve beautiful results.

¿Cómo jugar a Color Pixels - Coloring by Numbers?

Your task is to transform black-and-white images into vibrant works of art by coloring them by numbers. You can play on both computers and touch devices like phones and tablets. Controls: 1. Select a category from the gallery, then choose a picture to get started. 2. Zoom in or out: Use the slider at the top of the screen, pinch gestures, or your mouse wheel to focus on details. 3. Pick a color: Choose the desired color from the palette at the bottom of the screen. 4. Color by numbers: Find the corresponding areas and bring the picture to life with color. 5. Use boosters: Speed up the coloring process with special tools and achieve the best results.
