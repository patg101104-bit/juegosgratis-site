Hexa Word

Hexa Word is a cosy word-association puzzle. Each level gives you a few themed groups and a hex board full of word tiles — pick a group, tap the words that belong to it, and watch each cluster light up. The catch: some words look like they could fit two groups, so sorting them out is the real puzzle. Eight themed packs (Everyday, Cosmos, Wild World, Kitchen & Food, Travel & Places, Mystery & Crime, Fantasy & Myth, Sports & Games), endless generated levels, and Hint / Reveal / Shuffle helpers when you're stuck. No timers, gentle music — a relaxing brain-tickler you can play one-handed.

¿Cómo jugar a Hexa Word?

Tap a coloured group at the top to select it, then tap the word tiles that belong to that group. Correct words lock in instantly and the group lights up; wrong taps gently bounce. Clear every word into its group to solve the level and move on. Hint — places one correct word for you. Reveal — completes a whole group. Shuffle — rearranges the board. Out of gems? Watch a short ad to top up. Controls: touch (tap) on mobile, mouse click on desktop. Portrait orientation. No timers — play at your own pace.
