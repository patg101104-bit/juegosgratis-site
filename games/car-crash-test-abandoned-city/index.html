Car Crash Test: Abandoned City

Immerse yourself in an adrenaline-pumping world of destruction and speed! Bimka is an abandoned city with destruction online is a game where you control cars that break down with stunning realism. Doors, hoods, and wheels can all fly off with a powerful impact! Explore a vast open world filled with ramps, presses, and other tools for epic destruction. Create unique cars by changing the color of the body, details, and adjusting the size and alignment of the wheels. Meet other cars speeding along the roads and compete with them in spectacular crashes. Enjoy crazy jumps, collisions, and complete freedom of action! Realistic car destruction Customization: color, wheels, and style Vast open world Unique crash test sites Destroy, build, and race - create your own chaos on the road!

¿Cómo jugar a Car Crash Test: Abandoned City?

Controls: WASD - steer the car Space - brake hard Shift - enable nitro C - switch camera N - change car R - put car back in service K - repair car B - slow down time Tab - open pause On mobile: use the buttons on the screen. Goal: drive, crash, tune - do whatever you want!
