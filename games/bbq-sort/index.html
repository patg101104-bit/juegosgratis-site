BBQ Sort

Welcome to the sizzling excitement of BBQ Sort Step up to the grill and show off your matching prowess in this delectable puzzle adventure. Start with chill jazz and a few orders, but as you grow, expect huge, time-limited orders. Special requests will need your careful attention and unique cooking skills. Prepare for dynamic challenges and have fun!

¿Cómo jugar a BBQ Sort?

- Sort & Match: Tap and move 3 identical BBQ skewers onto the same grill to complete an order and clear the grill for the next match! - Strategic Moves: Think ahead! Each level presents unique sorting challenges and a limited time and space. Plan your placements carefully to achieve your goals. - Sizzling Power-Ups: Stuck on a tricky arrangement? Unleash awesome boosters to clear obstacles and heat up the action!
