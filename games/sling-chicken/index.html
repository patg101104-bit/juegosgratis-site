Sling Chicken

You start in a barn and climb upward by attaching to wooden rungs (pegs). From each rung you pull back and release the chicken like a slingshot to fling it to the next peg higher up. The track is endless and gets harder as you climb: Spinning saws guard some rungs Wood boards block half the lane (with a gap you must fly through) Other obstacles appear at higher heights: slanted beams, diamonds, moving pills, stone blocks, spinning wheels Golden eggs float along flight paths as bonus pickups. Your main score is height in meters — how high you’ve climbed. A simulated rival chicken climbs alongside you, gaining meters over time. Each match lasts 90 seconds. Win by ending with more meters than your rival. If you die mid-match, your score freezes at your peak height and the match ends shortly after; you still win if you’re ahead. Outside matches you can earn coins and rank points (RP), unlock cosmetics, and play Quick Match or the Coop Climb ladder (5 rivals in a row).

¿Cómo jugar a Sling Chicken?

Core loop Start on a rung in the barn. Drag backward from the chicken to stretch its wings (slingshot tension). Release to launch upward. Land on the next peg to stop and sling again. Repeat to climb higher. Collect golden eggs by flying through them (shown as coin pickups in the HUD). Avoid hazards — especially spinning saws and solid obstacle hits.
