Jurassic Merge: Dino Evolution

"Jurassic Merge: Dino Evolution" is an exciting game where you can create and upgrade dinosaurs by merging them to reach their maximum power! 🦖💥 Engage in epic battles, develop your creatures, and become a master strategist in this thrilling Jurassic world. ⚔️🧠 Game Features: 🔥 Dinosaur Evolution: Merge identical dinosaurs to create stronger creatures. 🛡️ Strategy: Place your army on the battlefield and use the right tactics to win. 💎 Upgrades: Earn crystals and gold to unlock new characters and enhance your creatures. ⚔️ Automatic Battles: Watch your dinosaurs fight against the enemy army. 🎮 Easy Controls: Simply drag and drop characters with the mouse or swipe on mobile devices. Free game in the style of "Elemental Monsters: Merge & Evolve" and "Dino World: Merge & Fight". Win, upgrade your dinosaurs, and become the most powerful creature on the battlefield! 🦕💪

¿Cómo jugar a Jurassic Merge: Dino Evolution?

🕹️ Drag characters with the left mouse button on PC or swipe on mobile devices. 🦖 Merge identical dinosaurs to create stronger creatures. 🛡️ Arrange your army on the battlefield for maximum effect. ⚔️ Send your dinosaurs into automatic battles against enemy armies. 💎 Earn crystals and gold for victories to unlock new characters or upgrade existing ones. 🧠 Strategize to defeat stronger enemies and progress further. Tip: 🌟 Always upgrade your dinosaurs and position them correctly on the battlefield to achieve the best results!
