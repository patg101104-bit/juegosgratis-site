Knit Loop: Yarn Jam Puzzle

Knit Loop: Yarn Jam Puzzle is a cozy puzzle game about yarn, threads and knitting. Sort colorful wool, untangle thread loops and complete relaxing levels with satisfying 3D physics. Enjoy a calm yarn sort puzzle with a soft handmade look: bright yarn, cute knitted details, smooth controls and simple rules. Move the threads, free blocked pieces and find the right order to finish each level. This game is great for fans of wool sort, yarn jam, knit puzzle, sorting games, logic puzzles and relaxing brain games. Each level becomes more interesting with new combinations, more yarn and trickier tangled patterns. Play for free, train your attention and enjoy a warm knitting atmosphere. Yarn sorting is perfect for a short break, cozy relaxation and a pleasant brain workout.

¿Cómo jugar a Knit Loop: Yarn Jam Puzzle?

Tap yarn pieces and move them to the right places. Your goal is to sort the threads, untangle wool loops and free all pieces on the level. Look at the color, order and direction of each thread. If a yarn piece is blocked, move the pieces that stand in the way first. Use logic and plan a few moves ahead to open the correct path. Complete levels one by one, build the right combinations and take your time. This is a relaxing 3D puzzle: think, sort, untangle and enjoy the cozy knitting mood. The further you play, the more interesting the tasks become. More yarn, loops and tangled pieces appear, so plan every move carefully.
