Rumbox Designer

"Rumbox Designer" is a game about creating cozy mini—rooms in a box: arrange furniture, combine objects and assemble beautiful scenes step by step. The style is a pastel isometry with simple shapes and soft shadows, so that the process is perceived as calm creativity. Collect rumboxes from objects: furniture, decor, room details. Experiment with the arrangement to find the most harmonious compositions. Unlock new sets and room themes by adding to your collection. Complete the levels/tasks and develop your rumbox studio.

¿Cómo jugar a Rumbox Designer?

Place objects (furniture, decor) in the room and move them until the composition becomes neat and cozy. If an item doesn't fit, remove it and try another one — the goal is to assemble a harmonious mini‑scene. Complete the room's assembly, receive a reward, and unlock new themes/sets for the next rumboxes.
