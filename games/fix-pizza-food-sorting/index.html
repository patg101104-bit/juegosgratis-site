Fix Pizza: Food Sorting

Enjoy a colorful sorting puzzle packed with pizza ingredients. Each pizza is covered with different food items: find the extra ingredients, collect them on matching plates, and restore the correct recipe. Look carefully, because the number of mistakes is limited. Complete increasingly busy levels, discover new recipes, and sort pizzas filled with unusual ingredient combinations. As you progress, more items appear and the object search becomes more challenging. Use Highlight, clear mistakes, or Shuffle whenever you need help. This is a relaxing game about sorting, observation, and finding objects, featuring juicy cartoon graphics, short levels, and simple controls. It works equally well for a quick break or a longer puzzle session at your own pace.

¿Cómo jugar a Fix Pizza: Food Sorting?

Study the recipe sheet and inspect all ingredients on the pizza. The recipe shows the food items that must remain. Every other ingredient needs to be removed. Tap extra ingredients and collect them on the matching plates. Each plate shows which item you need and how many pieces are required. After filling a plate, the next sorting task will appear. Do not tap ingredients included in the recipe. A wrong choice fills one mistake marker. If all available markers are used, you will need to restart the level. Helpful abilities: • Highlight reveals suitable ingredients. • Mistakes clears filled mistake markers. • Shuffle rearranges the items on the pizza. Fill every plate and leave only the recipe ingredients on the pizza to complete the level.
