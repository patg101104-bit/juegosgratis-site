Farmers vs. Aliens

Lead the animals to their enclosures, identified by green arrows and matching images. Keep them inside the green zone to prevent alien abductions! As the game progresses, it slows down at a certain point. If you don't gather the animals in time, aliens will arrive to take them. Leaving the green zone—whether it's you or the animals—will trigger a UFO to appear and abduct them. If aliens take more than two animals or capture you, it's game over. Stay fast and remain in the green zone to survive!

¿Cómo jugar a Farmers vs. Aliens?

PC: Use the mouse to control the character. Clicking the left mouse button will make the player follow the cursor. Mobile: Drag the on-screen joystick in the direction you want the character to move. Gamepad: Move the left stick to make the character run in the direction of the tilt.
