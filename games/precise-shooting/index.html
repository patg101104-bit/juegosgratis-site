Precise Shooting

Test your aim and precision in Precise Shooting, a satisfying basketball shooting challenge! Launch the ball with perfect timing and angle to score into the hoop from various tricky positions and distances. The core idea is mastering trajectory and power — one well-timed shot can earn you massive points and combos. What makes it unique? Beautiful minimalist 3D visuals, realistic ball physics with satisfying arcs, increasing difficulty with moving hoops and obstacles, and the addictive “one more try” feeling as you chase perfect shots and high scores!

¿Cómo jugar a Precise Shooting?

Your main goal is to shoot the basketball into the hoop with perfect precision. Hold and drag to adjust the power and angle, then release at the right moment to make the shot. Score as many points as possible by making clean shots — the better your timing and angle, the higher your score! PC Controls: Mouse: Click and hold on the player, drag to set power and angle, then release to shoot. Mobile Controls: Touchscreen: Tap and hold on the player, drag to adjust shooting power and angle, then release to shoot the ball.
