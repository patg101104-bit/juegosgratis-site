Geometry Arrow

Geometry Arrow The game is about an arrow in a cave with obstacles. The goal of to get to the end of the cave (to the portal) alive. Along the way, you need to dodge unique obstacles and spikes. There are 6 levels in the game, each subsequent one is more difficult than the previous one. Choose one of the 13 levels in the menu and start the game by clicking with the left mouse button or with your finger on the screen.
