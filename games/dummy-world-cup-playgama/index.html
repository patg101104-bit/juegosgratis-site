Dummies Football 2026

Get ready for an exciting football challenge with Dummies Football 2026, where you take command of a dummy representing your favorite national team. Immerse yourself in this captivating football game as you strive to score as many goals as possible before the final whistle. Choose from 32 national teams inspired by FIFA and engage in thrilling matches that will test your skills and strategies! Controls: Desktop: Right-click on the buttons, drag the character. Mobile: Tap the buttons, drag your finger. Key Features: • Dynamic matches: Drag your football player in different directions and try to hit the ball. Both the ball and the player can soar above the field and fall from great heights, perform flips, and score goals in the most unimaginable ways! • Wide selection of countries: Play as your favorite team, switch them after or before the championship, and engage in unique matches! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the goal of the game? A: Go for the football cup! Choose the country you want to play for and participate in the football championship. Progress through the stages and claim the cup! Q: How do you win / what counts as losing? A: Score more goals than your opponent within the allotted time while controlling your player. Q: What grabs you in the first few minutes? A: You can control your player's movements! Drag him in different directions and try to hit the ball to score against the opponent! You also have a wide selection of countries to play for. Mechanics & Progression Q: How many levels are there / is the structure clear? A: The championship consists of 8 stages. You will face different countries as opponents at each stage. Win and advance to the finals! Q: Does the difficulty change? A: With each new stage, losing a match becomes more perilous. If you lose, you will have to start the cup battle over! So prepare for the matches and win! Economy & Customization Q: What can you buy/improve? A: Buy a freeze! A powerful bonus that gives you an advantage. You can freeze your opponent for a while. Use this to score as many goals as possible! Q: Are there skins / customization? A: Win matches and earn coins. You can use them to unlock skins for the ball! Q: What is available from the start? A: All countries are available to choose from right from the start! Play as whoever you want! Retention & Engagement Q: Is sound necessary? A: Sound is not required, but it enhances your immersion in the atmosphere of the football match! Q: Is there onboarding in the game? What is it like? A: The game is intuitive from the first minutes! Drag the character and score more goals! You'll quickly understand what to do. Game tips To enhance your gameplay, focus on quick movements to outwit your opponent while choosing the right timing to kick the ball. Use feints to confuse the rival dummy and create scoring opportunities. Practicing your shooting and positioning will also help you rack up those goals! Controls Mouse, Touch

¿Cómo jugar a Dummies Football 2026?

Drag and drop with the mouse or touch to throw your dummy in the direction of the arrow. All you have to do is choose their favorite contending national from 32 teams (according to FIFA) and go head-to-head with the opponent team’s dummy.
