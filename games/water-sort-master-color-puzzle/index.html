Water Sort Master: Color Puzzle

Dive into a vibrant world of colorful liquids and brain-teasing puzzles! Pour, sort, and match colors across hundreds of challenging levels designed to test your logic and strategy. Every move counts as you untangle mixed liquids, unlock new challenges, and sharpen your problem-solving skills. Easy to learn but hard to master, Water Sort Master delivers a relaxing yet addictive puzzle experience for players of all ages. Can you sort every bottle and become the ultimate Water Sort Master?

¿Cómo jugar a Water Sort Master: Color Puzzle?

Tap a bottle to select it, then tap another bottle to pour the water. You can only pour water onto the same color or into an empty bottle. Keep sorting until each bottle contains only one color. Plan your moves carefully and solve every puzzle!
