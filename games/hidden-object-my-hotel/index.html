Hidden Object: My Hotel

Hidden Objects: My Hotel – A fun hidden-object game where you help the heroine restore her dream hotel! After buying the old building, she needs your help to clean up cluttered rooms, find hidden items, and bring back its lost charm. Features: 🔍 Hidden Object Hunt – Search for cleverly placed items in chaotic scenes. 💡 Smart Hints – Stuck? Use hints to spot tricky objects! 🎨 Vivid Graphics – Detailed, colorful scenes for immersive gameplay. Perfect for casual players who love renovation and puzzle games!

¿Cómo jugar a Hidden Object: My Hotel?
