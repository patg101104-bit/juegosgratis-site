Spin and Collect

Spin & Collect is the ultimate collecting game! Every spin awards coins, gems, and rare items. Complete sets to unlock powerful bonuses, win jackpots, and watch your collection grow even offline with AFK rewards. From Common to Legendary, will you be able to collect them all? Optional rewarded ads accelerate your progress without interrupting the game. Perfect for casual players and those looking to complete everything! Double Wheels: Basic Wheel for constant progression + Premium Wheel for colossal jackpots Epic Collections: Unlock items from Common to Legendary rarity Set Bonuses: Complete collections to get permanent bonuses and multipliers AFK Rewards: Keep earning coins even offline; progress is constant! Mobile Optimized: Smooth one-tap gameplay, ideal for quick sessions on the go

¿Cómo jugar a Spin and Collect?

🖱️ Click/Tap "SPIN" to spin the wheel and win rewards 📦 Inventory Button - View your collection and completed sets 🎬 Optional Ads - Watch to speed up progress 🔊 Audio Toggle - Control music and sound effects
