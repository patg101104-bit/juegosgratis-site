99 Nights in the Forest with Noob and Pro!

Welcome to "99 Nights in the Forest with Noob and Pro!" This is an exciting 2D Mine-style adventure where every night is a true survival challenge. 🪓 *Build and mine* — explore the world, gather resources, and create anything from solid bunkers to cozy houses. 🧱 *Various blocks and pickaxes* — choose tools and materials to your liking, level up, and unlock new possibilities. 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 *Character skins* — play as Noob or Pro, change your look, and stand out from the crowd. 🦌 *The main threat is the deer* from the *99 Nights in the Forest* mode! Avoid its gaze, hide, or run if it spots you. 🎮 *Play together with a friend* on the same device — survival becomes even more fun and exciting! And to make the challenge even more thrilling, there’s a ranking system — see who has survived the most nights and become the ultimate survival master!

¿Cómo jugar a 99 Nights in the Forest with Noob and Pro!?

✨ Whether you play alone or with a friend, here’s everything you need to know about the controls: 🖥️ On PC Player 1: * Move — A / D * Jump — W * Look down — S * Switch build/mine mode — Q * Use item in hand — E Player 2: * Move — ← / → * Jump — ↑ * Look down — ↓ * Switch build/mine mode — NumPad 1 * Use item in hand — NumPad 0 📱 On Mobile * Move — ← / → buttons * Jump — ↑ button * Look down — ↓ button * Switch build/mine mode — block / pickaxe icon * Use item in hand — crosshair icon
