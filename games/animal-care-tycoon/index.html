Animal Care Tycoon

Welcome to Animal Care Tycoon 🐾🏥, where the city depends on you and adorable animals need your care 💖 Build and manage your own idle animal hospital, starting from a small treatment room and turning it into the biggest veterinary center in the city 🏙️💰 Heal fluffy patients, earn coins, unlock powerful medicine, and upgrade modern equipment to save animals faster and better ⚕️✨ Become the ultimate pet doctor 🐶🐱 Treat cats, dogs, sheep, and even exotic animals like zebras 🦓 Every patient arrives with a unique problem such as colds, viruses, fractures, and more 🤒🦴 Unlock advanced tools, discover new treatments, and make sure every animal gets the love it deserves 💊❤️ Hire professional vet doctors and skilled staff to keep your hospital running smoothly 👩‍⚕️👨‍⚕️ The more rooms you unlock, the more animals you can help, and the more gold you earn 🪙🚀 Managing a growing animal hospital is a real challenge, but true animal lovers will enjoy every moment 🐾 Animal Care Tycoon is the perfect game for players who love animals and management games 🐕🎮 If you dream of running your own pet clinic, healing animals, and building a successful hospital empire, this journey is made for you 🌟

¿Cómo jugar a Animal Care Tycoon?

Control your character using WASD or the Arrow keys on PC, and use mouse clicks to interact with animals, tools, and hospital objects On mobile devices, move smoothly with the on-screen joystick and use touch controls to treat pets, collect coins, and manage your animal hospital efficiently
