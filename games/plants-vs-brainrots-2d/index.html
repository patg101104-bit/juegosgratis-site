Plants vs Brainrots 2D

🌱 Plants vs Brainrots 2D is a fast-paced action game inspired by the classic “Plants vs Zombies”, but with a completely different and chaotic twist. Brainrots appear in waves and fly across the screen. They don’t attack plants directly - their main threat is their huge amount of health. Your goal is to place plants wisely, upgrade them, and deal maximum damage to take down the toughest enemies. 🧠 The game features common, rare, and legendary Brainrots. 🎯 Your main objective is to defeat 3 legendary Brainrots with massive health pools. 🌿 Plants attack automatically, each with unique damage types and characteristics. ⚡ Simple controls, colorful visuals, and dynamic gameplay make the game perfect for both short and long sessions. 💻 Playable on PC, with smooth controls and optimized performance. 🔥 Plants vs Brainrots 2D combines strategy, action, and meme-style chaos. Jump in and see if you can defeat the legendary Brainrots!

¿Cómo jugar a Plants vs Brainrots 2D?

1️⃣ 🌱 Place plants Put plants on the field — they shoot at brainrots by themselves. 2️⃣ 🧠 Defeat brainrots Brainrots fly across the screen.
