Alpha Balls

Alpha Balls is a stimulating word puzzle game that sharpens your mind and vocabulary. Enjoy it online for free on any device. Solve puzzles to earn coins for hints, making it an entertaining and educational challenge for all.

¿Cómo jugar a Alpha Balls?

Make words from letters. The hint can show you the first letters of an answer. If you press the bin, the additional letters that mislead you will be removed. Press the ball with a tick to confirm your answer and earn coins. You can spend coins for hints.
