2048 Knights

2048 Knights is a puzzle twist on the classic 2048. Slide your knights across the board, merge matching numbers to forge stronger units, and reach the goal in as few moves as you can. Every level is hand-crafted — plus daily challenges and 60-second time trials to keep you coming back.

¿Cómo jugar a 2048 Knights?

Swipe, or use the arrow keys / WASD / the on-screen pad, to slide every tile in one direction. When two tiles with the same number meet, they merge into one worth double. Reach the Goal number shown at the top to clear the stage. Fewer moves means more stars — beat the par to earn all 3. Later packs add gates, blocks, and enemy units that change how tiles move. Stuck? Undo, reset, or spend a Hint to watch the solution.
