Mine Block Party: World of Minigames

In this game you will plunge into the exciting world of mini-games! - Competitive spirit: for every victory you receive cups and coins, which you can spend on buying new skins or improving your character. Fight for the championship and become the most skilled player! - Variety of skins: the ability to choose any skin, which includes characters from popular games, cartoons, as well as famous YouTubers. Let your character look the way you want! - Variety of modes: the possibility to choose one of three modes, in one of which you will have to try your luck and choose the right path, and in the other you will have to show your dexterity and wit! - Character upgrade: upgrade your character’s skills, making him faster and more agile in battles. Each victory brings not only pleasure, but also the opportunity to improve your hero. Can you become the best in this game? Embark on an exciting adventure with your favorite characters! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What needs to be done? A: Mini-games with block-style gameplay similar to Minecraft. Q: What is the game's unique feature? How can it hook players? A: The game offers a variety of games, unique skins, character progression, and rewards like cups and coins for each victory. Q: Are there any currencies and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: The game features coins and diamonds as currencies, which can be spent on skins and character upgrades. Q: What is the leveling up process like? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: To level up faster, players need to earn more money for purchasing upgrades by playing more and winning! Q: What engaging content elements are there (weapons, vehicles, locations, skills, references to famous franchises, etc.)? A: The game makes references to the world of Minecraft. Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you customize the hero's appearance, location editor, car tuning, etc.? A: Yes, the game offers various skins for customization. Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to better immerse in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in passing without sound? A: Music complements the game, but it is not necessary for progression. Q: Are there mini-games within the main storyline? For example, match-three puzzles within a strategy game. A: The entire game is a collection of mini-games.

¿Cómo jugar a Mine Block Party: World of Minigames?

Controls for PC: W, A, S, D or ↑, ←, ↓, → - move. Spacebar - jump. Mouse movement - camera control. Controls for mobile devices: Joystick on the left - move. Button on the right - jump. Swipe on the right side of the screen - camera control.
