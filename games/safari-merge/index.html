Safari Merge

Safari Merge is a bite-sized merge puzzle: grass sprouts onto the board, and you drag matching creatures together to combine them into the next animal up the chain. Keep the board from filling up, chain merges for combo bonuses, and unlock all 18 creatures. Each new animal comes with a real fact about how it fits into the natural world! - 18 creatures to discover, ordered along a real food chain - A genuine animal fact with every new tier you reach - Combo system that rewards fast, focused merging - Three difficulties, from easy to hard - Earn gems to unlock backgrounds and board tiles - Plays in your browser. Mouse or touch, phone or desktop

¿Cómo jugar a Safari Merge?

Safari Merge is an endless food-chain merge puzzle. Drag two matching animals together to combine them into the next creature up the food chain, starting from simple grass and rabbits, all the way up to elephants and beyond. Each new tier you discover comes with a real animal fact, so you're learning as you play. The board fills up over time as new creatures spawn in. Merge strategically to keep space open. If the board fills completely, the run ends. There's no fixed "win," so the goal is to reach the highest tier and score you can before the board fills up. Watch for combos: chain several higher-tier merges together in quick succession for a multiplied score and reward bonus. Controls: Click and drag (or tap and drag on touch devices) an animal onto a matching one to merge them. No keyboard required — the game is fully playable with mouse or touch.
