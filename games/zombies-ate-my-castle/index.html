Zombies ate my Castle

Zombies Ate My Castle is a tower defense game where you command an army of zombies to protect your fortress. Strategically deploy your undead allies, summon zombie carriers to revive fallen foes, and unleash powerful spells to turn the tide in battle. Collect coins from victories to upgrade your skills and boost your army. Can you save the zombie princess?

¿Cómo jugar a Zombies ate my Castle?

Recruit zombies, collect fallen enemies for resources, and defend your castle from every wave of attackers. Controls: Use the mouse or touchscreen to select units and press buttons.
