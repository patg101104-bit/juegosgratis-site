Revoxel 3D - Voxel RPG Shooter

Immerse yourself in a dynamic voxel-style RPG shooter with a cyberpunk atmosphere. A dark futuristic city overrun by a tech corporation awaits you, along with endless battles with robots and monsters, character development, and constant action in the spirit of a shooter RPG. Run through the streets of the metropolis, uncover a conspiracy, upgrade your weapons and equipment, mine resources, and defeat bosses. The game combines action-packed shooting, survival elements, character development, and a rich storyline about the struggle for human freedom.
