Mafia Wars

Mafia Wars is an action-packed shooting game where you play as a cowboy vigilante who fights against the corruption of mafia. Play engaging and immersive levels with various kinds of bosses. You must be careful with how you use your bullets, as saving them will come in handy in difficult battles.

¿Cómo jugar a Mafia Wars?

During battle, tap on characters to mark the location for the player to shoot at, but bear in mind that the bullet takes a bit of time to reach there. Switch between weapons when needed, and use automatic weapons when there is a swarm of enemies. Do not forget to unlock support and powerful defense barricades; you cannot win certain matches without them. Desktop Interact with the game UI to select and upgrade weapons. During battle, use a mouse click to trigger the player's weapon fire. Mobile Interact with the game UI to select and upgrade weapons. During battle use the touchscreen to trigger the player's weapon fire.
