Paint Pop 3D

Paint Pop 3D is an one-tap hyper casual shooting game that will keep you hooked for hours! Tap to paint. Hold for more fun! Stay away from obstacles and paint all slices on the ring. Features: - More than 1000+ levels to experience game play. - A lot challenging missions. - Various characters & themes. - Beautiful graphic.

¿Cómo jugar a Paint Pop 3D?

- Tap to paint. - Paint all slices on the ring to complete a level. - Be careful to the obstacles! Game is over if hit an obstacle.
