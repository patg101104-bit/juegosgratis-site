Kick Lucky Block For Brainrot

Throw lucky blocks, train your strength, and collect all the brainrots! Bring on the cheesy weather and enjoy the cheesy brainrots! Smash rare blocks, collect funny brain creatures, and bring them back to your base. But be careful — every powerful kick can trigger a huge tsunami wave! Run, jump, and survive before the water catches you. Upgrade your strength, train your legs, buy new weights, and become strong enough to kick blocks even farther. The farther you launch them, the better rewards you can discover! Features: - Kick mystery blocks and discover rare rewards - Survive giant tsunami waves - Collect brain creatures and place them on your base - Upgrade your power, speed, and training equipment - Unlock stronger kicks and reach new areas

¿Cómo jugar a Kick Lucky Block For Brainrot?

Use the on-screen buttons. Mobile phone controls: Left joystick to move the character. Right joystick to rotate the camera. Computer controls: WASD to move the character. Hold down the right mouse button to rotate the camera. Spacebar to jump. Left mouse button to attack. Q to throw a Brainrot. E to pick up or steal a Brainrot.
