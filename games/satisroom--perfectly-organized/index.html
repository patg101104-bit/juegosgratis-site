Satisroom - Perfectly Organized

Our game features relaxing mini-games for sorting, cleaning, packing and organizing - perfect for a break from everyday life. You'll have to put things in perfect order in a variety of levels, from organizing cosmetics and furniture to packing and cooking. All this is accompanied by a pleasant ASMR-atmosphere, creating an effect of complete peace. New levels are waiting for you every week to keep enjoying the process and develop your organizing skill.

¿Cómo jugar a Satisroom - Perfectly Organized?

Control is at our ultimate limit and is suitable for all ages. Touch the screen to select and drag objects, sort and organize items with just one swipe of your finger. There are hints in each level of architecture so that you can easily understand the purpose of the mini-game and enjoy nature❤️
