Sword Play: Chop enemies to pieces!

A fun action-packed sword game where all you need is to chop and slice enemies into pieces! Sharpen your blade, sword master! It's time to embark on a real ninja warrior adventure! Play the exciting "Sword Play: Chop enemies to pieces!", defeat hordes of sword masters and become the greatest samurai of all time! FEATURES OF THE GAME - Unique mechanics of slicing enemies - Variety of enemies and levels - Epic gaming experience - Action-packed missions - Hordes of ninja assassins If you like cool ninja games or knife throwing games like Hit Master 3D, Clumsy Ninja, Draw Saber, Sword Hunter, Stealth Master, Sword Play and other samurai games, you will definitely enjoy playing "Sword Play: Chop enemies to pieces!". What are you waiting for? Prove that you are the greatest sword master of all time!

¿Cómo jugar a Sword Play: Chop enemies to pieces!?

The goal of the game is to defeat all the enemies on the level. The gameplay is very simple. All you need is to swipe your finger on the phone screen, or the mouse cursor on the computer screen to cut the enemy into halves. Enemies can hit you or throw weapons at you, be careful. Slow down time and chop flying bullets. Cut barrels of explosives in half and beat off giant shurikens from your enemies! Do you have enough agility and reaction to repel attacks and cut all enemies into pieces?
