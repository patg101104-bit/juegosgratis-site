¿Cómo jugar a Domino Camp?

Use your mouse or touchscreen to interact with the domino tiles. Tap or click on a tile to select it, then match it with the current target number displayed at the bottom of the screen. Correct matches will help you clear tiles and progress through the game. Plan your moves carefully and try to complete each level using the fewest moves possible to achieve the highest score.
