Syntagma

A puzzle adventure with platformer elements. A suddenly arrived package contains a mysterious artifact, similar to a compass, and an ancient map pointing the way to five mysterious locations with abandoned temples. To activate the compass and reveal the secrets of the temples, the hero must find unique crystals, symbolizing the five elements, scattered around the world. To obtain a crystal, you can solve the puzzle only by jumping on active platforms. The correct sequence of stepping on the platforms and collecting all the keys on them opens the vault and grants access to the crystal. There are restrictions on moving across the platforms. Moving backward and diagonally is prohibited. Movement is only allowed forward, left, and right. The start of movement activates defense mechanisms and obstacles that cause damage. There are 2 difficulty levels, which can be changed in the main menu settings. After completing Level 0, the game will automatically suggest trying Level 1.

¿Cómo jugar a Syntagma?

Controls: Click on a platform with the mouse, tap for mobile, or move the hero with the arrow keys on the keyboard, or press the on-screen arrow buttons for mobile. Avoid collisions with traps. 1) You can jump over them when moving towards them, or 2) by clicking on the hero, 3) with the spacebar on the keyboard, 4) with a touch button on mobile - this makes the hero jump on the spot at the right moment and allows you to avoid a collision with an obstacle.
