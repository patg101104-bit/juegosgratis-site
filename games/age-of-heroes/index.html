Age of Heroes

Build a hero army, defend towers and conquer eras in Age of Heroes! Welcome to Age of Heroes — a world where heroes are born, armies march through eras, and every battle decides the fate of an empire. Lead an army, build fortresses, and guide your warriors to victory in epic battles from ancient times to the future. RPG elements and tower defense battles await you across eras, wars, and legendary castle sieges! Gather an army, develop heroes, protect every tower, and lead your knights through the ages — from ancient battles to futuristic warfare. Each era brings a new battle, a new tower, a new evolution. LEAD HEROES TO VICTORY You are the strategist and commander who decides the outcome of every battle. Create a squad of heroes and pets, combine the power of knights, mages, and warriors from different eras. Develop tower defense tactics and strike first. • Tactical battles and diverse game modes • Tower defense elements and fortress attacks • PvP battles between top teams • Heroes from different eras ready to fight for territories ERAS, CENTURIES AND THE EVOLUTION OF WAR

¿Cómo jugar a Age of Heroes?

Summon warriors and heroes from different eras and destroy enemy towers! Journey through all eras and centuries. Each brings a new army, unique battles, new wars and legendary heroes. • Stone Age — first battles and village defense • Middle Ages — knights, sieges and castle battles • Age of Gunpowder — fortified towers, artillery and wars • Modern Era — aviation, technology and new army evolution • Future — energy battles, towers and mechs • Space Era — heroes of interplanetary wars After completing all eras on Earth, new worlds and planets open up where army and hero evolution continues.
