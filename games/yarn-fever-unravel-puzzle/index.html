Yarn Fever! Unravel Puzzle

Yarn Fever! Unravel Puzzle is a soothing thread-sorting challenge that blends creativity with logic. Organize colorful yarns across tricky boards, using boosters like the Magic Box, New Hole, or Broom when levels get tough. Customize your setup with extra slots and boxes, and enjoy calm, vibrant visuals. With progressively harder puzzles, it’s both relaxing and rewarding for sorting and puzzle fans alike. Controls: Desktop: click Mobile: tap Power-ups / Abilities: • Clean: Clear all waiting holes to free up space. • Unlock the box: Add an extra box with spools for faster level completion. Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the goal of the game? A: If you struggle between a desire for destruction and a craving for comfort, this game is for you. Unravel knitted shapes layer by layer, filling spools of specific colors. A pleasant casual puzzle for stress relief. Q: What grabs you in the first few minutes? A: From the very first moments, the desire to solve the puzzle at once captivates, along with pleasant graphics and simple gameplay. A contemplative game where it's easy to get lost for a long time. Q: How do you win / what counts as losing? A: If you manage to distribute all the threads onto the spools without using the spare slots for them, you win. However, if you run out of free slots and the toys are not fully unraveled, unfortunately, you'll have to restart the level. Mechanics & Progression Q: Does the difficulty change? A: With each new level, the shapes become more complex, there are more colors, and the elements become trickier. You need to devise a strategy for interacting with the spools and accurately identify shades to avoid filling free slots for spools unnecessarily. Retention & Engagement Q: Is sound necessary? A: Sound adds a grounding effect to the calm visuals. ¿Cómo jugar a Yarn Fever! Unravel Puzzle?

You’ll need to collect colorful threads from various knitted objects and place them into matching colored boxes. Think carefully before placing your threads into temporary slots, and use your strategic mind to avoid filling up the available space.
