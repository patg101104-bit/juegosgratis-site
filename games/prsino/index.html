Prásino

Prásino is a small survival game set in a world drowned in trash. Your only lifeline is the trees - without them, there’s no air to breathe. Plant magic seeds to grow trees, restore barren zones, and carve a path forward. But stay alert, trash-born enemies lurk everywhere, ready to stop you.

¿Cómo jugar a Prásino?

Beware of enemies and collect magic seeds to plant trees. Manage your breath wisely, it’s your lifeline. Desktop Controls: ↑ (Up Arrow): Move Up ↓ (Down Arrow): Move Down ← (Left Arrow): Move Left → (Right Arrow): Move Right Z: Plant a Tree X: Attack / Break Loot Boxes Mobile Controls: On-Screen Joystick: Drag to move in any direction Plant Button: Tap to plant a tree Attack Button: Tap to attack enemies or break loot boxes
