¿Cómo jugar a Get a screw: puzzle!?

💻 Desktop: Press the desired color with the left mouse button. Close or move closer to the wheel. Squeeze the paintwork and lead to the sides - rotate the model. 📱 Smartphone: Press your finger on the screw to get it. Squeeze your finger and lead to the sides - rotate the model.
