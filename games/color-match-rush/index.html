Color Match Rush

Color Match Rush is a lightning-fast, one-thumb reflex arcade game built for quick sessions and high-score obsession. A glowing ring of colored segments spins at the center of the screen while colored dots rush inward toward a fixed gate at the top. Your only job sounds simple: rotate the ring so the segment under the gate matches the color of the incoming dot. Nail it and your score climbs; pick the wrong color even once and the run is over. There are no second chances — just you, your reflexes, and the next dot closing in. What makes Color Match Rush so addictive is how the pressure ramps up. ¿Cómo jugar a Color Match Rush?

Desktop: Using the cursor every click rotate the colors base Mobile: Tapping rotates the colors base. Levels: every 10 points are a new level adding another color to the game
