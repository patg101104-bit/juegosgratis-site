Tsunami Race

Tsunami Race is an exciting online adventure game where players race against a looming tsunami. Select your character, navigate through obstacles, and compete in various game modes, including dodging fruits and racing on water slides. Experience adrenaline-pumping action without downloads or registration across multiple platforms.

Tap and Hold on the screen to move character!
