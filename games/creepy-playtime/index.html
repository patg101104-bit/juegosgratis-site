Creepy playtime

Enter the unsettling realm of Creepy Playtime, where shadows hide lurking threats. Collect soda cans while avoiding detection in this suspenseful game. A haunting voice taunts your every step. Can you escape the monsters, or will they ensnare you? Play this frightening adventure for free online on PC and mobile.

¿Cómo jugar a Creepy playtime?

You woke up backstage, filled with monsters and mysteries! The locals do not like uninvited guests, so its better not to get caught by them. To get out of the backstage, you need to collect soda cans scattered throughout the backstage. But be careful, the monsters behind the scenes are very vigilant! Can you get out? If not, then you will stay there forever! Controls on PC: Movement - WASD Jump - Space Controls on smartphone: Movement - Left joystick Turns - Right joystick Jump - Button
