Mine Miner 3D: Mayor of Dream Island

Build a thriving island city and become the hero of a mining world! ⛏️ — Mine resources: dig into the mines, collect valuable materials, and sell them for coins — Grow your city: upgrade buildings, unlock new opportunities, and help the villagers — Discover islands: explore new lands and find rare resources — Power up your character: upgrade speed, strength, and luck for faster mining — Defend your city: repel monster raids and prove your power in the arena Collect riches, expand your territory, and become the best mayor in this world! ⚔️

¿Cómo jugar a Mine Miner 3D: Mayor of Dream Island?

PC controls: W, A, S, D or ↑, ←, ↓, → — move. The space bar is a jump. Mouse movement is camera control. Left mouse button — destruction of blocks Management for mobile devices: The joystick on the left is moving. The button on the left with the image of the tool is the destruction of blocks. The button on the right is jump. Swipes on the right side of the screen — camera control.
