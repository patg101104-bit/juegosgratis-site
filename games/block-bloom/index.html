Block Bloom

Block Bloom is a colorful puzzle-shooter about smart timing and sharper planning. A conveyor belt carries a stream of colored blocks across the board. Below it sits your grid of shooters — each one loaded with a color and a limited number of shots. Tap a shooter to send it into a firing slot, and it will automatically blast every matching block that rolls past. The catch: you only have a handful of slots. Send in the wrong shooter and it sits there, clogging a slot with nothing to shoot. Fill every slot with dead weight and the board jams — game over. Clear the whole conveyor and the level is yours.

¿Cómo jugar a Block Bloom?

Watch the conveyor. Colored blocks ride the belt across the top of the board. Tap a shooter in the grid below. A shooter is only tappable when nothing is blocking it. It slides into a free slot and starts firing automatically at every block on the belt that matches its color. Mind your shot counter. Each shooter has limited ammo. When it runs dry, it disappears and frees its slot. Keep your slots healthy. Slots are limited — a shooter whose color isn't on the belt just sits there taking up space. Clear every block on the conveyor to win the level.
