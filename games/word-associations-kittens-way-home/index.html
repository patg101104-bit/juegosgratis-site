Kitten's Way Home: Word Associations

Looking for a game for the evening? A puzzle that brings people together! Play against a friend on one screen or against artificial intelligence. The rules are simple, the adrenaline is high. A fun and simple word duel: guess words associated with the theme. Perfect brain training, an excellent educational game, and fun time well spent. Test yourself and your erudition. Can you guess all the associations?

¿Cómo jugar a Kitten's Way Home: Word Associations?

Take turns guessing words in Player vs. Player mode, or race against the clock in Player vs. Bot mode. Earn a random number of points for each guessed word. The player with the most points wins.
