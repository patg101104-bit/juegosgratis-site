War V: Path of the Survivor!

War Z: Path of the Survivor! - Story-driven first-person shooter. Go through the story, buy weapons, install various modules on them. A first-person action shooter. As a result of the man-made disaster, most of humanity was infected with a dangerous virus... which turns people into zombies The survivors were forced to form paramilitary groups... Who knows, maybe it's up to you to find the antidote to the virus and save humanity... - The world of the zombie apocalypse. - An exciting storyline. - Hordes of zombies from the simplest to the mutated. - A variety of weapons from the real world. - Various weapon modules and a leveling system.

¿Cómo jugar a War V: Path of the Survivor!?

Control: - WASD - movement, - LSHIFT - switch to running, - SPACEBAR - jump. - LEFT MOUSE BUTTON - shoot, - RIGHT MOUSE BUTTON - aim, - T - inspect the weapons. - R - reload.
