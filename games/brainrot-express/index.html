Brainrot Express

**Brainrot Jump** is a dynamic platform adventure in which your task is to collect the most legendary Brainrots! Jump over dangerous chasms, overcome challenging platforms and hurry to get to rare characters before anyone else. Each successful jump brings you closer to adding the funniest, strangest and most iconic Brainrots to your collection. Test your reaction and jump accuracy, and unlock more and more rare Brainrots as you progress. Regardless of whether you are hunting for your favorite characters or trying to collect a complete collection, each race will bring a lot of emotions and pleasant discoveries. **Features** * Addictive and addictive platform gameplay * Collect dozens of unique and fun Brainrots * Test your skills in challenging jumps over chasms and obstacles * Unlock rare and legendary Brainrots * Easy to start playing, difficult to become a master * Create the most complete Brainroot collection * Fast gaming sessions, ideal for short breaks * Suitable for players of all ages

¿Cómo jugar a Brainrot Express?

PC Controls WASD = move Space = jump E = interact Mobile Controls Swipe the left side of the screen = move with the joystick Tap on the right side button = jump Swipe the screen = move camera view Tap on the in-game UI = interact Touch control: Left side of the screen: Character movement Right side of the screen: Camera rotation
