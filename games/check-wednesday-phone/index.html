Check Wednesday Phone!

Check Wednesday Phone! creepy-cute game inspired by the Netflix series. Read messages, solve mysteries, explore Nevermore’s secrets and play gothic mini‑games. Enter Wednesday Addams' digital world in this dark, twisted game. Check emails from Nevermore students, change your gothic phone theme, play eerie mini‑games, and explore her secret-filled dorm. Earn coins doing tasks Wednesday would probably mock — but you’ll need them to unlock the next mystery.

¿Cómo jugar a Check Wednesday Phone!?

Tap Wednesday’s phone to begin. Read cryptic emails, change to delightfully gloomy wallpapers, earn coins, and explore her eerie room. Click on strange objects, crack passwords, and uncover secrets hidden in Nevermore. Watch for new messages—they guide the story.
