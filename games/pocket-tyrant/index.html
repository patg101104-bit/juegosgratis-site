Pocket Tyrant

Pocket Tyrant is a charming one-joystick action-strategy conquest game where you lead a tiny goblin warlord and his growing horde across seven islands. Gather resources, recruit goblins, upgrade your tools and weapons, then raid 27 kingdoms on the road to the Black Citadel. Combat is instantly readable—your army is a number, theirs is a number, and every clash is visible subtraction. There are no menus or attack buttons: simply walk into the world to harvest, build, fight, and turn cheerful pastel villages into crooked goblin strongholds. The choice is always yours: attack now, or grow stronger first.

¿Cómo jugar a Pocket Tyrant?

Move with W, A, S, D or the arrow keys. With a mouse, click and drag; on a touchscreen, press and drag anywhere to steer. Walk into trees, rocks, ore, and treasure to collect resources. Stand on camp pads to recruit goblins, improve equipment, unlock permanent upgrades, and build crossings. Enter a settlement to fight automatically; compare your warband number with the garrison number before attacking. If the enemy is too strong, retreat, gather more resources, and return—permanent progress is never lost. Conquer all 27 kingdoms across seven islands, then defeat the Black Citadel to complete the campaign. After the first victory, the Citadel returns for repeat battles that increase your Infamy leaderboard score.
