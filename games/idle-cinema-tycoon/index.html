Idle Cinema Tycoon

Idle Cinema Tycoon is a casual business simulation game where you are in charge of your very own movie theater with the aim of slowly managing an empire of theaters. Purchase cinema halls, schedule screenings, and upgrade interiors to boost profits. Curate and collect films of various genres, liaise with guests in VIP rooms, and squeeze out as much profit as you can from ticket sales and more. Strategically expand your theater and complete missions to unlock valuable rewards!

¿Cómo jugar a Idle Cinema Tycoon?

Navigate your cinema empire using the left mouse button to interact with the game's user interface. This control allows you to manage theater operations, make upgrades, and execute strategic decisions effortlessly
