Furry Kung Fu

Furry Kung Fu is a fun fighting game with cute cartoon graphics. You play as Catsei or his student Kung Fu Cat. You need to hit the opponent with a lot of punches, while dodging all kinds of objects flying at you, such as balls, comets, fire and pots. It's so much fun! Engage in an epic Kung Fu battle with your opponent. Get coins, unlock player skins and new weapons! Bright and memorable characters will keep you entertained, and simple game controls allow you to focus on battling countless hordes of pigeons, rats, aliens, crocodiles, and other animals. Can you collect weapons in battle and unlock all available maps? Two playable characters to choose from.

Two playable characters to choose from Как играть Control on the PC: by arrow buttons, on the smartphone - by swipes and taps on the screen. Swipe: left/right arrow, tap on the screen to the left/right of the player. Combo: repeatedly pressing the button in the direction where you want to apply a combo / multiple tap to the left or right of the player. Sit down: down arrow / swipe down. Jump: arrow up / swipe up. Double jump (if available for a specific skin): double arrow up / two swipe up Uppercut - squat and jump, arrow down and up / swipe down and up; Attack from above - press the down arrow / swipe down while jumping. The enemies drop weapons and lives. You can pick them up by tapping/tapping in their direction. The enemy on the ball and comet can be shot down. The rat in the window can be turned off briefly by throwing a weapon at it. Levels in the Survival game mode are unlocked as you progress through the main Campaign.
