Sky Blocks Falling

Sky Blocks Falling is an idle Arkanoid-style game where you catch falling blocks to fill platforms and earn money. Features Upgrade your speed for faster block-catching Add more platforms to increase your capacity Unlock critical hits for bonus rewards Boost your income with progressive upgrades Prestige to accelerate future progress Complete a variety of achievements Customize your experience with block skins and atmospheric themes Can you build the ultimate sky-falling machine?

¿Cómo jugar a Sky Blocks Falling?

Drag the left mouse button to move the bar. (Turn off drag mode to move the bar by just moving the mouse.)
