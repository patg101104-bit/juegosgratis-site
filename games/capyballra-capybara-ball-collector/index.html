Capyballra - Capybara Ball Collector!

Rotate the maze, roll to collect, unlock cute skins! Twist the maze with your mouse or your finger, send your capyballas rolling into the pipe. Simple one-touch controls, snappy physics, and clever level design make every run satisfying. Use coins to unlock new Capyballra skins—classic, seasonal, and rare animated variants—to show off your favorites. Perfect for quick runs or chasing full-collection completion: rotate, collect, unlock.

¿Cómo jugar a Capyballra - Capybara Ball Collector!?

Rotate the maze to get the Capyballras out of the maze and into the pipe to complete the levels! Once in a while you will have a bonus level where you can collect even more Capyballras to score more points. Earn coins to spend them on unlocking newer Capyballra designs. Desktop Controls: Use your mouse to drag around the maze to rotate it clock wise or anti-clockwise Mobile Controls: Use your finger to drag around the maze to rotate it clock wise or anti-clockwise
