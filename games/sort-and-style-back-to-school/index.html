Sort And Style: Back To School

Step into the world of Sort And Style: Back To School, a fun mix of sorting and dress-up games for girls! Organize messy desks, lockers, wardrobes, and even bento boxes across 9 exciting levels. Then, challenge your creativity in the final dress-up unit where you style your school look with trendy uniforms, shoes, hairstyles, and accessories. Perfect for fans of organizing games, makeover fun, and school fashion challenges!

¿Cómo jugar a Sort And Style: Back To School?

1. Choose a Unit – Start with cluttered spaces like study desks, lockers, or wardrobes. 2. Sort Items – Drag and drop objects into the right place, category, or design. 3. Unlock Levels – Complete organizing tasks to move to the next challenge. 4. Dress Up Fun – In the final unit, pick school uniforms, shoes, bags, hairstyles, and accessories to create your perfect back-to-school style.
