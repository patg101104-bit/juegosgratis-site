¿Cómo jugar a Jumping Bottle 3D?

🚀 In "Jumping Bottle 3D" your task is to complete all levels by throwing the bottle over obstacles. The main rule is not to touch the floor with the bottle! 📱 The controls are very simple: 1. Tap the screen to make the bottle jump and turn over. 2. Double tap the screen to make the bottle taller and spin faster. 💰 Collect as many gold caps as you can to unlock amazing new bottle skins! Milk, Coca-Cola, poison and other bottles are waiting for you. 🏆 Complete exciting levels and get cool rewards! Can you complete all the missions on the first try?"
