Zombie Rage: Merge 3D

Grandpa is eager for adventure again! Survive in a harsh zombie world. This time, Grandpa has to save the world from evil zombies. Zombies can drive and shoot! And Grandpa can destroy evil spirits! Fight the most dangerous mutants. Buy cool guns, find unique attachments, assemble your own unique gun and go into battle! Many locations, go through the whole world, from hot deserts to sea battles! Features: - Unique weapon assembly system. You can create a unique gun, install a barrel, butt, replace the handle, put a cool sight, install a silencer or compensator. - Many different enemies: zombie motorcyclists, shooters, Giants, zombies on jeeps, motorcycles. Dangerous bosses: Train with zombies, helicopters, Jeeps, Drones, etc. - Stylized graphics! - Cool special effects and sounds! - Realistic physics and spectacular destruction! Grandpa to battle!

¿Cómo jugar a Zombie Rage: Merge 3D?

To win, you need to destroy your pursuers. Controls Mobile: Touch the screen and move it to aim, shooting will start automatically. PC: Shooting: left mouse button Sight: right mouse button Sight control: by moving the mouse Weapon switching: 1, 2, 3, 4 Reload - R
