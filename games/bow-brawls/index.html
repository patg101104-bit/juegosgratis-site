¿Cómo jugar a Bow Brawls?

How to Play In Bow Brawls, survive endless waves of enemies using skill and precision. Use the joystick (mobile) or WASD (PC) to move. Aim by pulling the on-screen joystick in the opposite direction of your target—release to shoot arrows like a real bow. Enemies attack from all directions, including melee fighters and ranged shooters. Every few waves, a boss appears with unique skills like dashing, jumping over you, or launching projectiles—stay alert and adapt! Defeat bosses to earn gems, which unlock new bows and armors with different abilities. Reach higher scores to unlock new arenas and face tougher challenges. Your score is based on enemy kills, with each type rewarding different points. Climb the leaderboards by defeating as many enemies as possible. Manage your health bar carefully—you only get one life per run. Master your aim, survive waves, defeat bosses, and rise as the ultimate archer!
