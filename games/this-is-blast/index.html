This is Blast

Ready to blast your way through puzzles? Welcome to This is Blast, the exciting blast game that combines colorful puzzles, explosive chain reactions, and addictive ball blast mechanics. If you’re searching for a fun, free, and challenging puzzle blast game, you’ve found it. From the very first shot, This is Blast delivers an experience that is simple to play yet packed with depth. Aim carefully, unleash your balls, and trigger epic blasts that clear the board in a single swoop. With intuitive controls, smooth visuals, and clever puzzles, this game keeps you hooked whether you want a quick session or hours of puzzle-blasting fun.

¿Cómo jugar a This is Blast?

Aim Carefully Drag or move your aim direction and choose the best place to shoot the balls. Shoot the Balls Release the balls toward the puzzle area. Try to hit the correct blocks, balls, or targets. Create Big Blasts Match and merge similar items to create powerful blast effects. Bigger matches can clear more space. Use Chain Reactions One good shot can create multiple blasts. Plan your shot so it clears many objects at once. Clear the Level Goal Every level has a puzzle layout. Your main goal is to clear the required targets and complete the level. Use Power-Ups When Needed If the level becomes hard, use boosters or power-ups to clear difficult parts and save your game. Think Before Every Move The game is easy to play, but later levels need better strategy, timing, and smart aiming.
