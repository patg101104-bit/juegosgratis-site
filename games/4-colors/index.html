4 Colors

Get ready to challenge up to three computer opponents in this game! Strategize your moves and be the first to get rid of your cards by matching them by color or number. But be careful, if you forget to shout ""Uno"" when you have only one card left, you'll face a penalty of two extra cards! And don't forget about the action cards – they can bring a whole new level of chaos and confusion to the game!

¿Cómo jugar a 4 Colors?

Ready to make your move? Click or tap the card you want to play! If you don't have a card that matches the color or number, draw a card from the deck and try again. Remember, every move counts in this game, so choose wisely and be strategic!
