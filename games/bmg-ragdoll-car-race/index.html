BMG: Ragdoll Car Race

"BMG: Ragdoll Car Race" is a crash test simulator with plenty of destruction and obstacles that will easily destroy your car. Immerse yourself in the crazy world of ragdoll racing, where physics is the main source of fun and unexpected situations. A huge open world awaits you, filled with diverse locations, hidden routes, and challenges that will test not only your speed but also your driving skills. Complete unique levels with varying conditions—from extreme tracks to complex stunt zones—where every collision turns into a spectacular show thanks to a realistic destruction system.

¿Cómo jugar a BMG: Ragdoll Car Race?

PC Controls: WASD/Arrow Keys - Movement Shift - Nitro Space - Handbrake R - Restart Smartphone Controls: On-Screen Keys
