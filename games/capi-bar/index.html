Capi bar

Capi Bar is an idle game, business simulation and relaxing gameplay in one app. Combine fruits, save juice and gradually upgrade your bar!

¿Cómo jugar a Capi bar?

Game Features: 🥤 Merge Fruits Combine watermelons, oranges, coconuts and other fruits. The game mechanics are simple and straightforward, and the process itself resembles cute games that are pleasant to come back to over and over again. 🐹 Capybara is the main character and your assistant in business! Manage the bar with capybara. 🏪 Development and upgrades Upgrade your equipment, buy new bars, counters and interior elements. Business development is built as a calm simulator in which every improvement feels useful. , Idle mechanics Income continues to accumulate even when you are not in the game. This idle game is great for those who appreciate slow progress and love offline games. , Antistress and ASMR Bright graphics, smooth animations and pleasant sounds create a relaxing atmosphere. The project fits perfectly into the casual game format, allowing you to relax and enjoy the process.
