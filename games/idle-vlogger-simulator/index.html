Idle Vlogger Simulator

Step into the shoes of a rising digital star in this comprehensive content creator simulation. Your core gameplay loop revolves around planning, recording, and editing videos to maximize your views and subscriber count. Start by selecting trending themes like gaming, memes, or tech, then fine-tune your recording style by balancing visuals versus conversation and choosing a tone that resonates with your audience. Once the footage is captured, head into the editing suite to arrange clips, add transitions, and apply text effects to boost your video's quality and viral potential. Controls: Desktop: Click the buttons with the right mouse button. Mobile: Tap the buttons. Key Features: • Earn rewards! : Achieve special milestones to earn rewards, such as the Silver Button for reaching your first 100,000 subscribers. Just like in real life! • Upgrade your team and equipment. : Working with a team makes things easier! Hire your team and strive for popularity together! But don’t forget that equipment is important too — upgrade it! Choose your video theme! Film on topics that you enjoy the most or that are currently trending! Pick what resonates with you: video games, memes, and more. But don’t limit yourself — you can change themes whenever you want! Upgrades / Progression / Economy: • Upgrade your equipment. : Improve it with money to enhance the quality of your videos! • Upgrade your team! : Enhance your team with video coins to improve the quality of new videos! Levels / Maps / Worlds: • Old House.: Your first home. This is where you start! • Penthouse.: Level up to a penthouse! This is an elite home fit for a popular blogger! Characters / Heroes / Skins: • Cute streamer: A charming streamer who captures the hearts of viewers. A great skin if you want to take on that role! • Gaming blogger: Love video games and videos about them? A perfect skin for recording gaming content! Power-ups / Abilities: • AFK profit bonus: Allows you to earn money while in AFK mode. Relax and reap the rewards! • 2X views bonus: Doubles the views on your videos! Story / Setting: Immerse yourself in the life of a blogger. Record videos on various topics and hire your team to create content! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the goal of the game? A: You are a blogger. Create more videos, hire a team, and interact with colleagues — they will help you with advice at the start! Q: What grabs you in the first minutes? Mechanics & Progression Q: How can I level up faster? A: Create higher quality videos and upgrade your team. A: You will be able to upgrade your living space — from an old house to your own castle! Economy & Customization Q: What can you buy/upgrade? A: The game offers many upgrade options. Improve your equipment, upgrade your team — they will assist you in producing new videos, and buy a new house! Q: Are there skins/customization options? A: Yes! ¿Cómo jugar a Idle Vlogger Simulator?

Choose a video theme, adjust the sliders based on what each topic needs, and release your video to start gaining views, money, and subscribers over time. Keep creating more videos, upgrade your equipment and skills from the right-side panel, follow trending topics for better results, unlock new features, and become the top YouTuber!
