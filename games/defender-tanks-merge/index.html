Defender: Tanks Merge

Defender: Tanks Merge is a thrilling online strategy game where you create a powerful army by merging unique tanks with abilities. Face increasing waves of enemies, using auto-merge, bombs, and mines to strengthen your tactics. Play for free on desktop or mobile and enjoy hours of strategic fun!

¿Cómo jugar a Defender: Tanks Merge?

Controls: - Drag tanks together to merge - Drag unwanted tanks to the trash can - Tap ability buttons to use and select the target area - Cancel abilities by tapping any ability button or base area - Mines can't be placed near tanks—plan ahead
