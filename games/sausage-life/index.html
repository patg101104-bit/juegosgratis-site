Sausage Life

Avoid obstacles and become the last sausage standing. A parkour and survival game where you, as a sausage, must compete for survival. As you progress through the game, you'll unlock various unique skins. Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do players need to do? A: The game is a mix of parkour and survival for a sausage. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? What can hook players in? A: The game offers fun gameplay, unique skins referencing movies and games, like Harley Quinn. Q: Are there any currencies in the game and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: Yes, the game has currency - money, which can be spent on skins and rebirth. Q: What is the progression system like? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: Progress by surviving levels in the role of a sausage. ¿Cómo jugar a Sausage Life?

Drag your mouse to move. Use your mouse to navigate. Overcome various obstacles on different stages to be the winner! Unlock customizations, accesories, and skins through playing!
