Paint & Roll

Paint & Roll is a fun and satisfying color puzzle game where every move fills the world with vibrant paint 🎨✨ Roll your connected paint rollers across the board, cover every surface, and turn dull paths into colorful masterpieces 🌈🌀 Each level challenges your timing, direction, and planning skills as you glide, rotate, and paint your way to victory 🧠🎯 Simple controls make it easy to play, but mastering every level takes skill and strategy 😄🔥 Enjoy smooth animations, juicy paint effects, and relaxing yet challenging gameplay that feels great every time you roll 🎵💫 Perfect for quick sessions or long playtimes, Paint & Roll delivers a colorful experience that’s easy to learn and hard to put down 🚀🎮

¿Cómo jugar a Paint & Roll?

Tap or click to switch control between the connected rollers and guide them carefully across the platforms Plan your moves and timing to paint every surface while staying balanced. Be careful—if any roller touches the water, the level is instantly lost Smooth switching and smart movement are the key to reaching the finish and winning the level
