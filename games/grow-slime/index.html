Grow Slime

Grow Slime is a relaxing game about raising cute and unusual slimes. Buy eggs, plant them on your farm, and watch different slimes hatch. Each slime grows in its own way, goes through several growth stages, and can reward you with rare or valuable drops. Hit slimes to collect drops, sell them for coins, unlock new eggs, upgrade your farm, and discover even more unique slimes. Some slimes may grow larger than usual or produce golden drops — you never know what you’ll get this time! Create the biggest and most profitable slime farm!

¿Cómo jugar a Grow Slime?

Character controls: WASD or arrow keys - move Right-click to rotate the camera Space - jump E - get slime Phone controls: Joystick for movement Camera rotation - swipe the screen
