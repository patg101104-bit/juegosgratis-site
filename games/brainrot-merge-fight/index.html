Brainrot Merge & Fight

Brainrot Merge & Fight is a chaotic strategy brawler where you fuse quirky heroes into stronger versions and send them into battle. Every merge boosts your squad’s power, letting you build crazy combinations to take on tougher enemies. With each fight, your evolving team grows funnier, wilder, and more unstoppable—can you conquer every challenge?
