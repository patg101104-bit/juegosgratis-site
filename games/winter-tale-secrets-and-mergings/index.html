Winter Tale: Secrets and Mergings

A magical puzzle awaits you in the heart of a winter fairy tale! Together with brave heroes, you must return the magic of Christmas to the snowy forest. Uncover ancient secrets as you restore the lost enchantment. What awaits you: • Unravel mysteries: Each item you merge on the board is a step toward uncovering the forest’s main secret. • Create magic: Complete tasks, earn rewards, and use them to restore unique fairy-tale artifacts. • Enjoy the enchantment: Immerse yourself in a world of colorful graphics and animations that feel as if they’ve come straight from the pages of a storybook. • Don’t miss a day: Take part in daily events and quests to earn valuable prizes. This is an adventure where every puzzle brings you closer to a miracle. Are you ready to restore the spirit of Christmas?
