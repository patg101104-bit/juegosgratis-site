Gun Bounce Idle

Gun Bounce Idle throws you into a wild chain reaction of bullets, bounces, and nonstop destruction. Drop your weapons into the arena and watch them ricochet across the field, spraying fire in every direction while stacking up cash with every hit. Build your ultimate damage machine by upgrading power, attack speed, crit chance, and special modifiers. Unlock new guns with unique behaviors and turn simple shots into explosive chaos. The more you optimize, the more the screen fills with satisfying destruction. Level up to unlock stronger targets, complete achievements, and push your build to its limits. When progress slows, activate Prestige to reset and return stronger than ever — multiplying your power and accelerating your climb to insane damage numbers. Simple to start. Impossible to stop. How far can your bouncing arsenal go?

¿Cómo jugar a Gun Bounce Idle?

Use the left mouse button to interact with the in-game UI. 🎮 How to Play Buy powerful weapons and drop them onto the field — they will bounce, attack, and automatically earn coins for you! 💰 Earn Coins Your weapons deal damage while bouncing. The more damage you deal, the more coins you earn. ⬆️ Level Up Gain experience as you progress. Each level makes you stronger and unlocks new upgrades. 🧠 Upgrade Talents Invest your coins into powerful talents to boost damage, speed, critical hits, and income. 🔄 Prestige System Reach higher stages and reset your progress to gain permanent bonuses. Each prestige makes you much stronger and helps you progress faster next time.
