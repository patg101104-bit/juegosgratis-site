Food Sort 3D

In this game, you manage your own food court, sorting various food items into their respective categories. You'll encounter different eateries, from fast-food joints to upscale restaurants, each requiring you to sort items like burgers, sushi, hotdog, fries, pizza and desserts. The game features colorful graphics and quick, satisfying levels that keep you engaged as you expand your food

¿Cómo jugar a Food Sort 3D?

Drag and drop with a mouse is when you: Move the cursor over an item. Press and hold the left mouse button (don’t release yet). Move the mouse to drag the item to a new position. Release the button to drop it in place.
