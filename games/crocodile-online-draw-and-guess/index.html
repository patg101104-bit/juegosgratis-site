Crocodile Online - Draw and Guess

"Crocodile Online - Draw and Guess" is a cozy online party game where one player draws a word, and the others try to guess it as quickly as possible. You can play with friends in a private room or jump straight into a quick game with other players. The built-in chat helps you discuss your guesses and have fun, and the leaderboard system keeps track of your progress and shows you the best guessers. The game works directly in your browser, without downloading, supports keyboard and mouse, and is perfect for short sessions after school or work.

¿Cómo jugar a Crocodile Online - Draw and Guess?

Log in to the game, enter your name, and choose between a quick game or a private room. In the quick game, you will automatically be placed in a suitable room. In the private room, create a room and send its ID to your friends. One player will be the artist, who will see the word and must draw it on the canvas using a pencil, marker, and eraser. The other players will enter their guesses in the chat. The first player to guess the word will earn a point and help advance in the leaderboard. Rounds are automatically switched, and the time is limited by a timer.
