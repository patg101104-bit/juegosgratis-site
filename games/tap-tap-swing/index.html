Tap tap Swing

A simple casual game Touch to fly, hold to hover, and release to fall. In this game, your agility will be tested. Pass through obstacles and avoid crashing. Avoid all obstacles and don't fall to stay alive. Enjoy the beautiful music and complete the levels that challenge your adrenaline.
