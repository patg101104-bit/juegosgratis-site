Mahjong Unlimited

Mahjong Unlimited - fun classic oriental puzzle game with tiles and dragons! Let yourself relax in this mahjong game with endlessly generated levels of varying difficulty. You don't have to limit yourself to time or choose a timed game if you like a challenge! Customize the look of the puzzle to your liking or choose a child-friendly tile look for your child's educational game! Collect achievements and compete with other players! Follow the path of the dragon, beat your opponents and become a master! How far can you go? Game Features: - Infinitely generated levels - 4 difficulty modes - You can play against time or without - Customizable tile appearance and game background - Hints for difficult situations - Collect achievements with dragons - Compete with other players

¿Cómo jugar a Mahjong Unlimited?

Select two identical tiles to remove them from the playing field. You can only select tiles that are free on the left or right and are not closed at the top. Each tile appears on the playing field four times. Your task is to remove all tiles from the playing field.
