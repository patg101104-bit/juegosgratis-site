Steve parkour in mine

Jump, run, and don’t fall! Help Steve navigate dangerous levels filled with traps, chasms, and true cubic madness. The levels keep getting harder, and your reaction speed and jumping precision are your only hope. This game is perfect for both beginners and those who consider themselves masters of parkour in virtual worlds. Are you ready to show what Steve is capable of in the world of parkour?

¿Cómo jugar a Steve parkour in mine?

Use the WASD keys to control the character. Press the spacebar to jump. Use the mouse to control the camera.
