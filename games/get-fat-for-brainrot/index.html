Get Fat for Brainrot

Get Fat for Brainrot is a game where you need to gain a lot of weight for your character to break the dungeon floor and get Brainrots.

¿Cómo jugar a Get Fat for Brainrot?

Buy fat and weight in the store to break down and open new dungeons, and also collect different Brainrots for your collection.
