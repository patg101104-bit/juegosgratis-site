Lift Defender Zombie Carnage

Step into the exciting realm of Lift Defender Zombie Carnage, where your role as an elevator guardian is crucial for humanity's survival against a zombie apocalypse. Protect the last safe spaces in skyscrapers through thrilling online gameplay available on both mobile and desktop devices.

¿Cómo jugar a Lift Defender Zombie Carnage?

After the zombie apocalypse, the surviving people began to live in huge skyscraper shelters. The floors are connected only by elevators. You are an elevator guard who must not allow emerging monsters to penetrate from floor to floor. - Clear the floors - Save people - Earn money - Unlock new weapons Key game features: - simple controls - large selection of weapons - various levels - stylized zombie shooting gallery
