Mister Bullet: Arcade Shooter

ou are a cool special agent Mr. Bullet. You are sent on the most dangerous missions in the world. Only Mr. Bullet knows how to use all types of weapons perfectly. Only Mr. Bullet can sneak into the enemy base unnoticed. This time, a new threat looms over the world - the Balloon Villains! They brought a lot of equipment, soldiers, weapons, even Boss monsters! But Mr. Shooter knows what to do! Features of the game: - A huge arsenal of weapons! There are so many weapons that the enemies lose all desire to fight! Take what you like - pistols, shotguns, machine guns, grenade launchers and much more! - Stealth shooter on huge territories! Stay in the shadows so that your enemies are afraid of you! - Variability of level completion. Act stealthily or take a grenade launcher and give your enemies an unforgettable meeting! - A large number of levels! You have a long journey through the game worlds, so be ready for battle! - Many different types of enemies! Forward to the Battle!

¿Cómo jugar a Mister Bullet: Arcade Shooter?

To win - you need to eliminate all opponents on the level. Controls: PC - use the mouse to control the camera. Shooting is automatic when the sight is aimed at the enemy. Mobile - use your finger to control the camera. Shooting is automatic when the sight is aimed at the enemy.
