Fluffy Jump

The objective is to jump from platform to platform while continuously moving forward. Missing any platform results in failure, and the player’s speed gradually increases as the game progresses. The challenge lies in surviving as long as possible to achieve the highest time-based score. Key Features: Simple controls: Intuitive and quick controls. Dynamic difficulty increase: The game becomes more challenging as you progress.
