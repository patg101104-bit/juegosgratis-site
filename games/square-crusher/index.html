Square Crusher

Get ready for a fun and satisfying cube-crushing puzzle experience in SQUARE CRUSHER! In this game, you need to tap on unlocked fill cubes or boxes only. When you tap a cube or box, it falls into the crusher. The crusher breaks it and generates colorful balls. These balls fall onto the board and move toward the conveyor belt if there is space. Your main goal is to match the crushed balls with the correct front crates. When the balls pass near the crates, matching balls will automatically go inside the correct crate. Once a crate is full, the next crate in the queue moves forward. But be careful! Do not blindly tap on unlocked cubes or boxes. If you tap without planning, the generated balls may not match with the front crates. This can fill the conveyor belt quickly and create no space for new balls. Fill all crates, clear all boxes, and complete the level before the conveyor belt gets full!

¿Cómo jugar a Square Crusher?

-Tap on full cube or box. -The tapped cube or box will fall down into the crusher. -The crusher will crush it and generate balls. -The balls will fall onto the board. -If there is space, the balls will move onto the conveyor belt. -When balls pass near the front crates, matching balls will automatically go inside the correct crate. -If a crate becomes full, the next crate in the queue will move forward. -Your goal is to fill all crates and finish all boxes to win the level. -Think before you tap! If you blindly tap cubes or boxes, the crushed balls may not match with the current front crates. -If the conveyor belt becomes full and no ball can match with the front crates, the game will be over.
