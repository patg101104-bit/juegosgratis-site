Rolling Balls 3D

Rolling Balls 3D is a casual game in which you have to test your reaction and dexterity. The goal of the game is to guide the ball through an endless maze of obstacles, collecting balls along the way to get more! The bottom line is that every time you collect a ball, a clone of your ball is created and added to the maze, increasing the difficulty as you progress!

¿Cómo jugar a Rolling Balls 3D?

Swipe your finger or mouse to move the balls from side to side and avoid obstacles. Collect a lot of balls and reach the finish line to complete the level.
