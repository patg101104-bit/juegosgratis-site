Happy jelly

Dive into a delightful realm of sugary fun with Happy Jelly, a captivating online puzzle game. Align jelly candies to score points and relieve stress, all while enjoying vibrant visuals and lively tunes. It’s an engaging experience for everyone, available on both smartphones and computers. Enjoy the sweetness!

¿Cómo jugar a Happy jelly?

In the game you need to move candies so that they build a row of three or more candies and thereby they will be destroyed, destroy as many candies as possible!
