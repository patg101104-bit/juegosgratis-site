Yoga Master - Flex Running

Yoga is a great way to improve your quality of life. It is also a great way to entertain yourself in this game: Yoga Master. Yoga Master is an addictive 3D running game with different levels that will challenge even the fastest reflex. Move your character to adjust the pose and dodge the incoming obstacles while picking some money on the way. But beware! Sometimes this stack of cash is a trap.

¿Cómo jugar a Yoga Master - Flex Running?

- Tap or swipe to begin a level. - Swipe to move the character. - Adjust the pose to dodge the furniture. - Don't let anything hit you until the end.
