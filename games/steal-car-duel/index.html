Steal Car Duel

Step into a high-energy world of engines, rivalry, and fast reflexes. Steal Car Duel is a competitive action game that evolves the idea of Steal Brainrot Duel into a car-based experience filled with duels, rare collectibles, and endless upgrades! Race, collide, and steal your way to the top. Build your own garage, earn coins and tickets, and outsmart rivals to prove who rules the arena. Each match combines skill, timing, and strategy as you fight for dominance in Solo or 2 Player mode. Core Features: 40+ unlockable cars across 8 rarity tiers. Real-time stealing, dueling, and defense mechanics. Functional Weapons Shop and Market systems. Cosmetic customization with outfits, wings, and pets. Solo and local 2 Player support. Progressive income and rarity-based rewards. Game Modes: Solo Mode: Hunt for rare cars, raid rival garages, and defend your own collection. 2 Player Local Mode: Face a friend on the same device, steal their cars, block their progress, and see who builds the best garage. Rarity System: Standard – Basic models, perfect for starting your journey... Custom – Personalized versions with balanced performance and earnings! Street – Sportier cars built for strong garage. Track – Designed for tighter handling and speed for extra income. Premium – Exclusive visuals with solid performance. Hyper – Advanced power and huge income for your garage. Prototype – Experimental vehicles with rare features and extra huge income! Legend – Ultra-rare vehicles including the F117 jet, Formula car, and skull-themed retro car.
