Pow

Have you ever taken care your pet!? Feed it, clean it, play with it and watch it grow up while leveling up? This is all possible is this brand new Pow game for web and mobile. Feed, nurture and take care of your Pow. Play different games in games room and collect coins to buy food to feed your pow and buy decoration to customize your room! Also challenge your friends in 3 fun minigames!

¿Cómo jugar a Pow?

Use the mouse or touch the screen to interact with Pow and the objects. Follow the instructions on the screen. You can see the needs progression of the Pow in the vertical bars at the top of the screen for each room. Try to keep the bars full attending the needs in each room.
