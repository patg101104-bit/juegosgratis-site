Garden Pals IDLE

Grow your own garden that earns money for you—even while you're offline! Plant cute kawaii plants, grow them in the greenhouse, and combine identical ones to increase your income. Climb the prestige leagues and unlock five themed gardens. Each has its own set of plants to collect. Fill your album with collections, earn artifacts, and complete daily quests. A cozy idle farm where you can spend a few minutes or spend a few hours.

¿Cómo jugar a Garden Pals IDLE?

• Tap plants in your garden to collect coins instantly. • Buy seeds in the Seeds tab — rarer seeds grow more valuable plants. • Seeds grow in the Greenhouse on a timer, then appear in your garden. • Drag two identical plants together to merge them into a stronger one. • Spend coins in the Shop on permanent income upgrades. • Reach new levels to unlock more garden plots (up to 12). • Prestige to earn Fame Stars and unlock new themed gardens with fresh plants. • Collect every plant in a garden to earn a permanent Artifact bonus. Your plants keep earning while you're offline — come back for a reward!
