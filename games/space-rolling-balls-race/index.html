¿Cómo jugar a Space Rolling Balls Race?

How to Play 🎮 ✨ Follow these simple steps to master Space Rolling Balls Race: 🔹 Swipe or drag your finger on the screen to move the ball. 🔹 Avoid traps and obstacles that appear on the track. 🔹 Stay on the path — falling off means game over! 🔹 Collect power-ups to boost your score and progress faster. 🔹 Keep up with the rhythm — the music helps guide your timing. 🔹 Challenge yourself to beat your best distance and reaction speed!
