Mini Golf Battle

Mini Golf Battle is a fun and casual mini golf challenge where your goal is simple: hit the ball into the hole with as few shots as possible. The game combines easy controls with tricky levels full of slopes, angles, and obstacles that test your timing and precision. Each level is designed to get a little harder, so you’ll need to aim carefully, control your shot power, and plan the best path to sink the ball. It’s a relaxing yet addictive golf experience made for quick play sessions.

¿Cómo jugar a Mini Golf Battle?

Drag/swipe in the direction you want the ball to move. Pull back and release to adjust the strength of your shot. The longer you drag, the stronger the hit. Try to sink the ball into the hole in the fewest strokes possible. Watch out for walls, ramps, and barriers that can deflect your ball. Use angles to bounce around corners smartly. Fire Power: This bonus gives your ball a flaming speed boost. It’s useful for long straight shots or when you want to quickly cross a large gap. Best used when you need maximum distance in a single strike. Wing / Glide Power: This bonus allows your ball to glide smoothly as if it has wings. It reduces friction and gives your ball a longer travel path with better control. Ideal for precision shots where you want distance but also don’t want to overshoot too wildly. Spring / Bounce Power: This bonus acts like a spring-loaded push. It helps your ball bounce forward with extra force, perfect for overcoming obstacles or making tricky jumps. Best for courses with barriers, ramps, or platforms where normal shots can’t reach.
