Animal Evolution Simulator

Evolve and go from a worm to the highest stage of evolution. Eat plants and mushrooms, grow and fight predators. Choose your strategy - should you be a stealthy hunter or a mighty defender of your territory? Experience the delights of evolution, upgrade your skills and adapt to a changing world. Step over the threshold of the next stage of evolution and find out what awaits you next in the next biome! - Eat, fight predators and survive in a dangerous world! - Evolve and rise to the highest level of development! - Explore the map! - Discover new biomes! Good luck in Evolution! Controls: Desktop: WASD — move. Left mouse button to attack. Click on buttons with the mouse. Mobile: Use the virtual stick to move, jump button, attack button. Tap on buttons. Key Features: • Each new stage of evolution is special! : Every time you level up, you evolve into a new animal — stronger and larger! It's a pleasure to watch. • Animal battles!: Some animals want to win too, so they will fight! Dodge their attacks and strike back swiftly! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the goal of the game? A: Welcome to the animal world. You will fight for survival in the wild. Evolve and become stronger! Q: How to win / what counts as losing? A: Welcome to the animal world. You will fight for survival in the wild. Evolve and become stronger! Q: What grabs attention in the first minutes? A: Pleasant graphics and engaging progression. Each time you level up, you evolve into a stronger animal. You will journey from a worm to a lion and beyond! Mechanics & Progression Q: How many levels / is the structure clear? A: The game features different locations, each with unique content and visuals. Start in the forest and move to the savanna and beyond. You will encounter new animals along the way! Q: Does the difficulty change? A: Some animals can hit you hard! So level up and become stronger! Q: How to level up faster? A: Defeat more animals. Especially if you manage to overcome someone stronger than you, you will gain more experience and become stronger much faster! Q: What unlocks as you play? A: Each time you will evolve into a new, more powerful animal! You will also unlock new areas with special types of beasts! Economy & Customization Q: What can be bought/improved? A: Get a boost for enhanced attack! A useful item in the fight for supremacy! Q: What is available from the start? A: At the start, you begin as a worm. Anyone can crush you! Eat mushrooms to become stronger! Retention & Engagement Q: Is sound necessary? A: Sound in the game is not mandatory, but it will immerse you better in the atmosphere. Q: Is there onboarding in the game? What is it like? A: From the first minutes in the game, you will quickly get the hang of it and understand how to survive in this world.

¿Cómo jugar a Animal Evolution Simulator?

Control the creature, eat mushrooms and plants, attack predators and evolve. PC Management: WASD - moving Space - jump The left mouse button is an attack Right mouse button - moving the camera Mouse wheel - zoom in/out of the camera Smartphone controls: Left joystick - moving Button with a jumping man - jump The target button is an attack Swipe on the left side of the screen to move the camera
