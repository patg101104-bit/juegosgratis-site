Clicker Knights Vs Dragons

Clicker Knights vs Dragons is a pixel-art idle clicker RPG where players tap for "Hero Tap DMG" while auto-attacking foes like skeletons in progressive stages across fantasy biomes, as seen in this desert Stage 4-1 with 8.81 Total DPS. Its core revolves around addictive hero team-building and upgrades for exponential scaling against monsters and dragons, uniquely blending simple taps with spellcasting, offline progression, and epic boss raids for boundless medieval fantasy adventures.

¿Cómo jugar a Clicker Knights Vs Dragons?

Become a Legendary Knight Slayer! Assemble your team of pixel-art heroes and tap relentlessly to deliver "Hero Tap DMG" while auto-attacking skeletons and dragons across endless fantasy stages like this desert Stage 4-1. In this addictive idle clicker RPG, advance through progressive worlds by upgrading Striker DPS for exponential scaling, conquering epic bosses, and unlocking offline rewards to dominate monsters in boundless medieval adventures! Desktop Controls: Left Mouse Button: Tap anywhere on screen to deal Hero Tap DMG and attack enemies. Mobile Controls: Tap Screen: Tap anywhere to deal Hero Tap DMG and attack enemies. On-Screen Buttons: Tap upgrade icons and menus to hire heroes, level up, and activate abilities.
