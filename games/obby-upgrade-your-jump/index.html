Obby Upgrade Your Jump

Ready to jump insanely high? 😄 In Obby Upgrade Your Jump it’s super fun and simple: tap → earn Jump Points → jump higher! 🔥 More points = bigger jumps. 🐾 Unlock pets to boost your progress: they make taps stronger and give 2 or 3 points per tap depending on the pet. 🏆 Level up, beat your records, and show everyone how high you can fly!

¿Cómo jugar a Obby Upgrade Your Jump?

Tap to increase characteristics Character controls: WASD or arrow keys — movement Rotate the camera while holding the right mouse button Space — jump Phone controls: Joystick for movement Camera rotation — small swipes on the screen
