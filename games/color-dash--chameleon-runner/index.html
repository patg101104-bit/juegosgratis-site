Color Dash - Chameleon Runner

Run, jump, and switch colors to survive! Fast-paced endless runner challenge. Get ready for a fast and challenging color-changing adventure in Chameleon Runner: Color Dash! ¿Cómo jugar a Color Dash - Chameleon Runner?

⬆️ Up Arrow – Move Forward ⬇️ Down Arrow – Move Backward ⬅️ Left Arrow – Turn Left ➡️ Right Arrow – Turn Right 💰 Collect Coins to increase your score 🏁 Reach the Finish Line to complete the level ⚠️ Avoid obstacles and enemies ⭐ Try to finish with maximum coins for bonus rewards
