Last Knight:kings throne

This is a casual dodge arcade escape game. Play as a cute little character and keep escaping from chasing monsters on the random map. It perfectly combines fast-paced evasion gameplay, random buff items and fresh cartoon art style, bringing you relaxing and thrilling spare-time fun! Game Highlights Flexible free movement: Drag to control your character freely and change directions anytime to avoid incoming monsters.Random pickup buff system: Collect randomly spawned props including speed boost and monster freeze to get away from dangerous encirclement.Progressive difficulty curve: More monsters spawn and their moving speed rises continuously as your survival time extends. Game Advantages Easy risk avoidance tactics: Use terrain space to circle monsters and prolong your survival time for higher scores.No complicated rules: Pure casual design without tedious tutorials, instantly jump into matches after opening the game.Unlimited endless mode: Keep challenging your personal best score repeatedly for higher leaderboard rankings. Game Features Simple intuitive operation: Support drag control on mobile and mouse control on PC, all players can master in seconds.Compact random maps: Every round generates slightly different monster spawn positions to enrich replay experience.Lightweight instant play: Run smoothly in all browsers with zero download and installation required.
