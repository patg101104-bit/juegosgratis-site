Voxel Master

A 3D puzzle and builder in one! Assemble volumetric models from colored blocks using hints, or create your own masterpieces in sandbox mode. Two game modes: - Puzzle mode — hundreds of levels with increasing difficulty. You're shown a model and must recreate it block by block. - Sandbox — free 3D modeling on grids of various sizes. Save your creations and share them with friends.

¿Cómo jugar a Voxel Master?

Controls: - PC: left mouse button — place/remove a block, drag — rotate camera, scroll wheel — zoom, WASD — pan - Mobile: tap — place a block, swipe — rotate, pinch — zoom
