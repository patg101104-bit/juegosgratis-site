Rotate Cups

This is a rotation physics puzzle game where you control the angle of cups to try to get the balls to roll into specific cups. Rotate cups clockwise or counterclockwise as needed to turn the clear cups into ramps guiding the ball down the level. Place at least one ball in each of the blue cups. In this game you have to get all the balls into the blue cups lower down the screen. Larger blue cups can hold more than one ball in them. If there are multiple smaller blue cups you must get a ball into each of them. Levels are not timed & you can both rotate as many times as you like & pause as many times as you like. You can skip intermediary cups. On most levels you typically want to use all the cups on the way down, but if you are struggling to do so you can bump a ball off the outside of a cup or skip a cup entirely & it won’t harm you in the least provided your balls eventually get into the blue cups. Some levels have a long reach you have to make the ball roll. On those levels sometimes a somewhat rapid cup spin toward the destination is enough, but in some cases you have to do that rapid spin as the starting point & then slightly spin back quickly right as the ball is leaving to give the ball a bit more lift.

¿Cómo jugar a Rotate Cups?

Desktop: Use your mouse left click button to select on the screen & drag your mouse to rotate the cups. Drag left or down to spin clockwise. Drag right or up to spin counterclockwise. Touchscreen devices: Tap the screen with a finger & drag your finger across the screen to rotate cups clockwise or counterclockwise.
