Game of Warrior

Game of Warriors is a Strategy TD (Tower Defense) game with a unique style in its genre. Set in a magical world you will have to upgrade your defenses and mighty soldiers to survive and siege enemy territories to conquer them. The rival alliance of the evil kingdoms has pushed what remains of the human civilization to a corner of the world, isolated and forgotten, plunged into misery and trying to survive harshly. Ready the horses, hire your warriors and prepare the spears for battle, the war horn will sound again and our titans will destroy the enemy walls and towers. The age of dark empires have come to an end, it's time to recover what rightfully belongs to us, it's time for revolt, war and revenge! Features: - Tower Defense (TD) and Strategy game style. - 1500+ defensive waves. - 100+ territories to conquer. - 30+ soldiers to upgrade. - 1000+ building upgrades. - 4 different races to conquer (Goblins, Skeletons, Worgens and Orcs). Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do you need to do? A: It's a strategic Tower Defense game. You need to upgrade your soldiers, fortress, and attack other territories, turning them into your colonies. Q: What is the unique feature of the game that can hook players? A: It's a strategic Tower Defense game with over 30 soldiers to upgrade, over 1000 building upgrades, 4 different races to defeat (goblins, skeletons, worgens, and orcs), and a unique upgrade tree. Mechanics & Progression Q: Are there bosses, and how are they different? A: Yes, every 10 waves you face a well-organized army of bosses that are more challenging than regular units. Economy & Customization Q: What currencies are available and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: Yes, the game has currencies: gold and diamonds. You can use them to upgrade towers, warriors, attack lands, improve weapons, and more. Q: How does leveling up work exactly? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: Earn coins by defeating enemies and collecting coins from colonies we have conquered. Use these coins to level up your warriors and structures. Q: Are there in-game purchases? What can be bought and what is the impact? Does making a donation speed up game progress? A: Yes, there are in-game purchases where you can buy diamonds. These diamonds can be used to purchase numerous upgrades. Donations speed up game progress. Retention & Engagement Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to better immerse yourself in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in passing without sound? A: Music complements the game well, but it is not necessary for gameplay.

¿Cómo jugar a Game of Warrior?

Your task is either to defend the fortress or to attack! Release troops to attack or defend - they will fight automatically. To defeat the enemy, use various abilities and upgrade your warriors.
