Space Craft: Ship War

Explore deep space, piloting a cubic ship that can be infinitely expanded with modules. Collect new decks, melee and ranged weapons, boosters, and rare upgrades on the map, transforming your ship into a true battle station. Enemies also grow, making every encounter a dynamic battle for survival. Complete levels, upgrade your stats, unlock new skins, and experiment with builds. Vibrant 3D graphics, rich effects, and constant progression make space battles exciting and unpredictable. Features: 🚀 Infinite ship expansion with modules 🔫 A variety of weapons: melee and firearms ⚡ Boosters and boosters for dynamic battles 🛠 Upgrades after each level 🎨 Skin collection and customization 🌌 Colorful 3D graphics and numerous levels

¿Cómo jugar a Space Craft: Ship War?

PC: Use WASD keys to move your ship. Mobile devices: Use swipes to control movement. Game Objective: Destroy enemy ships by collecting modules, upgrading your ship, and surviving longer than anyone else.
