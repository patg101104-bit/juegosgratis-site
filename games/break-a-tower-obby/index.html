Break a Tower: Obby

"Break a Tower: Obby" - a demolition simulator in the spirit of the best obby modes! This unique obby mode combines dynamic gameplay with blocky sandbox elements. 🏗️ Key Features: ✅ Epic Demolition - destroy a tower floor by floor until complete annihilation! ✅ Deep Progression - increase punch strength, endurance, and demolition speed! ✅ Pet Collection - helpers boost destructive power and give unique bonuses! ✅ Rebirth System - rebirth to earn more trophies! ✅ Variety of Towers - from glass towers to reinforced concrete giants! ✅ Visual Style - in the style of obby and blocky sandbox games! 💥 What Awaits You: 🎮 Recognizable obby gameplay - intuitive controls and mechanics! 🏆 Global Leaderboard - become the world's best demolisher! 🐉 Legendary Pets - mythical creatures to aid in destruction! [Core Gameplay & Story] Q: What is the game about? What do you need to do? A: It's essentially a clicker game where you tap to break through the floors of a tower, climbing higher to improve your stats and progress. Q: What is the game's unique hook? What keeps players engaged? A: The game features extensive meta-gameplay elements, including character upgrades, daily rewards, skins, and pets that provide various bonuses. It also includes an upgrade system, a fortune wheel, and multiple towers to provide a sense of progression. Additionally, there's a mini-idle mechanic where you can train to increase your strength; as your strength grows, you deal more damage to the tower floors, which also increase in HP as you go. [Mechanics & Progression] Q: How does the progression system work? How can you level up faster? A: You need to train—specifically by doing push-ups—in a mini-idle style mechanic to increase your strength. Increasing your strength directly boosts the damage you deal to the tower floors. [Economy & Customization] Q: What currencies are available and what can they be spent on? A: You earn coins that can be spent on pets. If you get duplicate pets, you can combine them. These pets act as collectibles and provide bonuses based on their rarity, specifically boosting the coins earned from breaking tower floors. You can save these coins to purchase higher-tier, higher-quality pets. Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you change the hero's appearance? A: Yes, there are various skins available for the character. [Retention & Engagement] Q: Are there daily rewards or similar mechanics to keep players coming back? A: Yes, the game features daily rewards. Q: Is it better to play with sound for immersion, or is it playable without it? A: Playing with sound is not required, but the audio is pleasant and not repetitive. Q: Is there an achievement system? What are they and how do you earn them? A: There is a ""Trophy"" system. The stronger your character becomes, the more trophies you earn, which acts as a form of leaderboard. These trophies can eventually be exchanged to unlock access to other towers.

¿Cómo jugar a Break a Tower: Obby?

Computers: - WASD - control the character. - Space - jump. Mobile devices: - Joystick - control the character. - Button in the lower right corner - jump. - To rotate the camera, swipe the screen.
