Epic Empire: Tower Defense

Immerse yourself in a dynamic tower defense strategy game where you must defend your kingdom from waves of goblins, ogres, and epic bosses. Control three heroes simultaneously, build defensive towers, and empower them with runes and pets. Explore diverse worlds—from icy kingdoms to underground tunnels—and prove your skill in tournaments. Well-thought-out leveling, a variety of modes, and large-scale battles make the game an excellent choice for fans of tactics, base defense, and PvE challenges. ¿Cómo jugar a Epic Empire: Tower Defense?

The goal of the game is to defend the kingdom from waves of enemies by strategically placing towers and controlling your heroes to defeat all the bosses and hold the line. To interact with the game interface, use the left mouse button or tap the screen.
