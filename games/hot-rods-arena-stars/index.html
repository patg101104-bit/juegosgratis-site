Hot Rods Arena Stars

Welcome to the arena where the weak don’t survive! Take the wheel of a legendary, heavily armed Hot Rod and challenge dozens of ruthless opponents. Here, there are no rules — only speed, firepower, and your reflexes decide who becomes the champion. Blast enemies with precise shots, collect stars, and unlock powerful upgrades for your battle beast. Swap weapons, reinforce your armor, hit top speed, and leave nothing but smoke and wreckage behind! Every arena is a new challenge, where only those willing to take risks can claim victory. Ready to prove you’re the king of the road? Then hit the gas and race toward glory!

¿Cómo jugar a Hot Rods Arena Stars?

Desktop: W, A, S, D — drive your car Mobile: Movement — use the left stick (up, down, left, right) Goal: Collect stars to outscore your rivals. Use your weapons wisely — blow up enemies, trap them, and don’t give them a chance to strike back. One mistake, and your car becomes scrap metal!
