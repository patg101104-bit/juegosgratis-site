Connect Associations

A set of words hides several meaningful connections. Find four words that share a theme and merge them step by step into one group. Correct matches reveal categories, while later levels introduce fresh word sets and ice obstacles. The relaxed pace gives you time to consider every move. Hints can keep the solution moving when a connection is especially tricky. Complete levels, earn coins, and watch your overall progress grow.

¿Cómo jugar a Connect Associations?

1. Find two words that belong to the same theme. 2. Tap the first word and then the second, or drag one onto the other. 3. Keep adding related words until the group contains all four. 4. Every correct merge damages the ice. Free frozen words before using them. 5. Reveal every category to complete the level. Incorrect pairs cost one attempt. If you get stuck, shuffle the words or use a hint.
