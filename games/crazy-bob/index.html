Crazy Bob

Ready for a crazy adventure across floating islands? Take control of Crazy Bob, battle dangerous enemies, cause explosive chaos, and fight for survival in a world full of challenges. Explore floating islands, overcome traps and obstacles, complete quests, and unlock new portals to unexplored lands.

¿Cómo jugar a Crazy Bob?

HOW TO PLAY 🟥 Red button or RMB — Punch. 🟪 Purple button or R — Spin Attack. ⬆️ Spacebar — Jump. 🎮 W, A, S, D — Move. Not sure where to start? Check out the tutorial in the main menu!
