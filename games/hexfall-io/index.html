HexFall.io

Hex.io is an intense arcade survival experience 🎮 inspired by classic hex-tile challenges ⬢. Tiles collapse beneath your feet ⬇️ as you move, forcing you to plan every jump carefully 🧠. Outsmart the fall 😈, survive longer than your rivals 👥, and become the last player standing 🥇.

¿Cómo jugar a HexFall.io?

Move across the falling hex tiles and stay above the void for as long as possible. Every step breaks the floor, so plan your moves carefully. Avoid falling, survive longer than everyone else, and become the last player standing. PC controls: Use Arrow keys or WASD to move Mobile controls: Use the on-screen joystick to move
