¿Cómo jugar a Sorting Nuts and Bolts by Color: Home Repair?

Select a Nut: Click on the bolt you want to take the nut from. Move it: Click on another bolt to move the selected nut there. Sort by Color: Match nuts of the same color on one bolt.
