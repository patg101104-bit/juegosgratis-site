Supermarket Manager

Try your hand as a Supermarket Manager! Develop your store by purchasing shelves for products, which you will need to stock yourself at the beginning. Earn profits by serving customers and invest your earnings to hire staff like stockers and cashiers. Boost the prestige of your supermarket by improving the quality of the products you sell. Then, invest in more expensive shelves and fill them with a wider variety of goods. Start earning maximum profits from serving each customer and build the supermarket of your dreams!

¿Cómo jugar a Supermarket Manager?

For PC Controls = Use the left mouse button or the WASD keys to move your character For Smartphone Controls = Hold your finger on the screen and drag it in the direction you want the character to move To purchase a shelf or a cash register, stand on the corresponding areas marked on the game field To stock shelves with products, first load products from the warehouse, then carry them to the appropriate shelf In the warehouse area, you'll find a computer. Approaching it will open the store management menu. Here, you can improve product quality or hire staff
