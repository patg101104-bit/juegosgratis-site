Eternal Dance

Eternal Dance is a calm, meditative arcade game about keeping balance. Two koi — one black, one white — endlessly circle a glowing orb in a hand-painted zen pond. Drifting ink threatens the orb from the dark, and only the koi of the matching color can dissolve it. Guide their dance, protect the orb, and lose yourself in the flow. Easy to learn, hard to master — a one-touch game that trades frantic reflexes for focus and serenity.

¿Cómo jugar a Eternal Dance?

Goal: Protect the blue orb at the center. Two koi orbit it — spin them so each koi meets the ink of its own color: • Black koi dissolves black ink • White koi dissolves white ink Wrong-color koi can't stop the ink — it strikes the orb and cracks it. If the orb shatters, the dance ends. Modes: • Levels — survive until the timer runs out to clear each level. • Endless — last as long as you can; your survival time is ranked on the leaderboard. Powers (Shield, Slow, Cleanse, Heal) unlock as you progress, to help you hold the balance longer. Controls: • Desktop: hold SPACE (or ↑) to spin the koi; release to ease them to a stop. • Touch: tap and hold anywhere to spin; release to slow down.
