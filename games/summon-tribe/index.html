Summon Tribe

Summon Tribe is a simple merge-and-defense game where you combine identical units to create stronger ones to stop enemies. It’s easy, mostly for passing time, and it also supports idle play.

¿Cómo jugar a Summon Tribe?

Merge facilities, traps, and turrets to build the strongest defense. Plan carefully in limited space and defeat your enemies! Choose your skills to boost your defense and find the best combo to survive.
