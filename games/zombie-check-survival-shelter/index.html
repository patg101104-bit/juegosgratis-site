Zombie Check: Survival Shelter

Zombie Check: Survival Shelter is a tense quarantine inspection simulator set during a zombie outbreak. Survivors arrive one by one. Your job is to inspect their bodies, scan for symptoms and decide their fate: send to camp, quarantine, or eliminate. Symptoms can be hidden, suspicious, or evolve over time. One wrong decision can kill an innocent person or infect the entire camp. Use the scanner, examine bodies, unlock weapons and make choices under pressure. Each day brings new people, higher risks and tougher decisions. Will you survive long enough to reach the bunker and save humanity? "Core: A high-stakes social deduction and resource management game set during a global zombie epidemic, where players must identify and neutralize infected individuals to secure the survival of the human race in an underground bunker. Loop: Progression is driven by successful identification and correct processing of incoming individuals. Flawless daily performance (correctly determining the infected from the healthy) is rewarded with in-game currency, fostering a cycle of precision and reward. Start: A comprehensive tutorial module ensures immediate accessibility and full comprehension of the core mechanics. The high-stakes premise—total responsibility for humanity's future—provides a powerful, immediate retention hook. Playgama Fact: The game is fully integrated with Playgama SDK: instant login and cross-device saves between PC and mobile. HTML5. Stable performance regardless of internet quality. Visual: Features a stylized, cartoon art direction. Key Feature: A genre-defining blend of deduction mechanics, demanding constant vigilance from players to prevent a single missed symptom from compromising the entire bunker and ending humanity."

¿Cómo jugar a Zombie Check: Survival Shelter?

PC: Rotate the camera with the cursor AD or arrow keys - rotate the character left/right Right-click - use fists 1 - Send to camp 2 - Send to quarantine 3 - Send to liquidation Tab - switch cursor Q - put away tool E - equip tool Z - teleport to quarantine X - teleport to the inspection zone Mobile controls: Rotate the camera by swiping to the right side of the screen Rotate the character to the left side of the screen
