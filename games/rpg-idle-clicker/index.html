RPG Idle Clicker

RPG Idle Clicker is an incremental game where you embark on an adventure to slay monsters. Click to deal damage, earn gold, and unlock powerful swords to enhance your attacks. Upgrade your blades and recruit auto-attacking heroes who battle monsters every second, even while you are offline. With stunning WebGL visuals, face tougher enemies and epic bosses in this browser-based game. Whether you prefer active gameplay or idle progression, RPG Idle Clicker offers endless growth. Get ready and become the ultimate monster hunter!

¿Cómo jugar a RPG Idle Clicker?

Tap the enemy to deal damage and eliminate them. Interact with the UI buttons to upgrade sword levels, heroes, equip items, and much more.
