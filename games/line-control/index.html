Line Control

Line Control is a tactical game about routes, bases, and quick decisions. Draw lines from your points to neutral and enemy positions, send squads, and gradually take control of the map. Every level is a new logic challenge. Sometimes you need to capture free points quickly, sometimes you must defend key positions, and sometimes one smart route can change the whole match. The more bases you control, the stronger your advantage becomes. Simple controls make the game easy to start, while growing difficulty keeps every level tactical and engaging.

¿Cómo jugar a Line Control?

Your goal is to take control of all enemy points. Draw a line from your base to another point to send a squad. Capture neutral positions, attack enemy bases, and defend your own. Watch the numbers on each point: they show attack and defense strength. Do not spend all squads at once. Strengthen important positions, capture free points first, and choose routes that lead to victory faster.
