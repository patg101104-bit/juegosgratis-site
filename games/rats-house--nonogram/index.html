Rat's House - Nonogram

Build a home full of stories and coziness! 🐭✨ Amazing tenants are moving into your tower. From a shy book lover to a bold rocker or even an alien guest — they all need their perfect corner. Don't just arrange furniture — befriend them! The residents give tasks, request specific items, and share thoughts in funny dialogues. Fulfill their requests to unfold the plot and open new floors. 💬🏠 Earn Cheese 🧀 for shopping by solving Japanese crosswords (nonograms). It’s a great brain workout! What awaits you: 🎨 Turn empty rooms into design masterpieces. 🧩 Over 500 levels of varying difficulty. ❤️ 14 unique heroes with touching stories. 🌸 Bright seasonal events. You will love this game if you enjoy: 🧠 Logic: Sudoku, crosswords, pen-and-paper puzzles; 🔨 Creativity: Home design and renovation games; ☕ Comfort: Kind stories, cute pets, and relaxing gameplay without rushing. Move in all the fluffy friends and discover their secrets!

¿Cómo jugar a Rat's House - Nonogram?

The game combines puzzle solving and home decoration. Controls are intuitive and perfect for relaxing! ☕️ Solving Nonograms: Your goal is to fill in cells according to number clues to reveal a hidden picture. 🖱️ Mouse/Finger: The main tool. Use it to fill cells or mark empty spots with Xs. ⌨️ Keyboard: Press Spacebar to quickly switch between painting mode (square) and marking mode (cross). It’s very convenient! 🧐 Mistakes: Try to be accurate! Three mistakes will cost a life, but you can always use hints. House & Building: 🧀 You earn Cheese for completing levels. 🛍️ Visit the shop to buy furniture and decor for the rats' rooms. 💬 Complete quests to chat with characters and increase your House Level. Solve puzzles, save up resources, and unlock new floors with unique stories! Have a nice game! 🧀
