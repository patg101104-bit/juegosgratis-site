SquadZ

Get ready for fast-paced, action-packed battles that you can dive into at any time. This hyper-casual shooter features bite-sized levels full of non-stop excitement, where you can unlock cool heroes, collect powerful weapons, and take down enemies in a flash. With simple controls and quick missions, you can play for as little as one minute or twenty, delivering epic fun on the go. Whether you're short on time or ready to aim for a high score, this explosive shooter has something for everyone.

¿Cómo jugar a SquadZ?

On PC, use W, A, S, D or the Arrow Keys to move your character smoothly in all directions. On mobile devices, control movement with the on-screen joystick by touching and dragging it to navigate easily through the game environment.
