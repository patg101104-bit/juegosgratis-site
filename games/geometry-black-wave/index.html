Geometry: Black Wave

“Geometry: Black Wave” is a musical platformer game in which the player controls a small arrow, overcoming various obstacles and hurdles on the way to the finish line. The game features black and white graphics and an exciting soundtrack that is synchronized with the gameplay. The game offers many levels of varying difficulty, each with its own unique music. The player must react quickly to obstacles and move in the right direction to reach the end of the level. “Geometry: Black Wave” is a game that requires the player to react quickly and move accurately. It is suitable for all ages and skill levels, and can be a great way to pass the time and have fun.

¿Cómo jugar a Geometry: Black Wave?

"Geometry: Black Wave" can be tricky, but with these detailed instructions, you'll be able to master it in no time: 1. Start the game and select a level. The difficulty of the level depends on the emoji drawn on the button. 3. To move up, tap and hold the screen. The longer you press the screen, the higher the wave will rise, but until it hits the wall. 4. To move downward, release the screen. This will cause the wave to start moving downward until it reaches the ground. 7. The wave can overcome various obstacles such as spikes, blocks and other obstacles. But be careful: if the wave touches any obstacle, you will lose. 8. Ride the wave to the finish line to overcome the level and get a high score. These are the basic instructions for "Geometry: Black Wave". Don't be afraid to experiment and practice to become the best in this game.
