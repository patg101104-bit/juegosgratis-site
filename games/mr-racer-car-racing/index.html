MR RACER - Car Racing

MR Racer puts you in the driver's seat of blazing-fast supercars as you race through chaotic streets, dodge traffic, and conquer fierce racing challenges. With over 100 thrilling missions, limitless chase levels, and a full-fledged career mode, it’s time to test your reflexes and racing instincts. Customize and upgrade your rides to stay ahead of the curve. Choose from eye-catching paint jobs, cool wheels, and performance boosts to dominate every track.
