Commit

Explore an isometric world full of puzzles where every move is permanent. Take control of a curious cat that must traverse all the tiles in each level to collect food—but beware: every tile you step on disappears forever. No turning back. Only pure strategy.

¿Cómo jugar a Commit?

Controls Explanation: Use your mouse to click on the tiles where you want the cat to move. Each click makes the cat jump to that tile, and every tile you step on disappears forever. Plan your path carefully, as you cannot go back, and strategy is key to completing each level successfully.
