BOMBAMAN 3D

Dive into an exhilarating 3D journey with BOMBAMAN 3D! Tackle complex mazes, strategically use bombs to clear paths, and defeat fearsome enemies. Discover power-ups, find the secret exit, and face challenging bosses, all while enjoying an engaging solo or multiplayer adventure on your devices.

¿Cómo jugar a BOMBAMAN 3D?

🕹️ Use W A S D or Arrow keys to move 💣 Press Spacebar to place a bomb ⏲️ The bomb explodes after 3 seconds. Bombs placed side by side trigger each other 🔥 ⏳ You have 99 seconds for each level. Collect clocks from boxes to extend this time ⏰ 🔧 You can increase the number and range of bombs with various power-ups 👾 You need to hit the boss 3 times with bombs to defeat it
