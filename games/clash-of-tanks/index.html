Clash of Tanks

Clash of Tanks is real time strategy tank game. Objective is to destroy the opponent base by deploying the tanks. Each tank has its own weakness and strength, selecting the right tanks at appropriate time is best way to win the battle. Good Luck

¿Cómo jugar a Clash of Tanks?

Your objective is to destroy main defense of the enemy base. Desktop: Use mouse to drag and drop the units on the battlefield Mobile : Use touch to drag and drop the units on the battlefield
