¿Cómo jugar a Cannon Strike?

To finish a level, you need to fill all the buckets with balls. 🪣 Drop the right number of balls into each bucket to move to the next level. Watch out for moving obstacles that can block your shots! In Cannon Strike, aim and shoot as many cannonballs as you can into the basket. It's a game that tests your aiming skills with a bit of physics fun!
