¿Cómo jugar a Idle Cookie Tycoon?

🍪 COOKIE TYCOON - HOW TO PLAY 🎮 📖 COMPLETE GAMEPLAY GUIDE 🎯 GETTING STARTED Your First Steps Welcome to Cookie Tycoon! You start with nothing but a dream and a giant cookie button. Your goal? Build a cookie empire from scratch and become a millionaire! Step 1: Click the Cookie! 🍪 Tap the giant cookie button in the center of your screen. Each click produces fresh cookies. This is your primary way to earn in the early game! Step 2: Understand the Currency System 💰 Cookies = Your raw production resource Money = Your purchasing power (10 Cookies = 1 Dollar) Convert cookies to money strategically to buy upgrades and automation! ⚙️ CORE GAME MECHANICS 🖱️ CLICKING & UPGRADES Manual Production: Click the cookie button repeatedly to earn cookies Each click gives you a base amount of cookies Your click power increases as you upgrade! Upgrade Your Clicking: Navigate to the Upgrades Menu to: Increase cookies per click Unlock multipliers Boost overall production efficiency 💡 Pro Tip: Invest heavily in click upgrades early - they compound over time! 🤖 AUTOMATION SYSTEM Stop clicking manually! Purchase auto ovens that produce cookies automatically: 1. Simple Auto Oven (First Purchase) Your entry-level automation Produces cookies passively every second Can be upgraded to increase production speed Strategy: Buy this as soon as possible! 2. Auto Oven Program (Mid-Game) Regional production system Significantly faster than Simple Auto Oven Multiple upgrade levels available Strategy: Stack multiple units for exponential growth! 3. Advanced Auto Oven Program (End-Game) Top-tier automation fleet Massive passive income generation Each level dramatically increases output Strategy: This is your path to becoming a millionaire! Upgrading Auto Ovens: Each oven has multiple levels (Level 1, 2, 3, etc.) Higher levels = more cookies per second Costs increase with each level Balance purchases between new ovens and upgrading existing ones 🏪 SHOP SYSTEM Build your cookie empire with 4 different shops! How Shops Work: Each shop has a rotating progress bar on top The bar fills up over time (duration varies by shop level) When the bar completes, money is added to your account The bar immediately resets and starts filling again Shops earn money even when the game is closed! 📴 The 4 Shops: 🏪 Shop 1 - Starter Bakery Your first shop Moderate earning rate Low cost to unlock and upgrade Best for: Early game foundation 🏪 Shop 2 - Cookie House Mid-level establishment Better earnings than Shop 1 Requires significant investment Best for: Accelerating mid-game progress 🏪 Shop 3 - Advanced Dessert Shop High-tier production facility Excellent passive income Expensive but worth it Best for: Late-game scaling 🏪 Shop 4 - Ultra-Modern Cookie Complex Ultimate money-making machine Massive passive earnings Very expensive to unlock and upgrade Best for: Becoming a true tycoon! Shop Upgrade Strategy: Each shop can be upgraded multiple times Higher levels = faster bar filling = more money Balance unlocking new shops vs. upgrading existing ones Tip: Sometimes upgrading Shop 1 three times is better than unlocking Shop 2 once! 📈 INVESTMENT SYSTEM - HIGH RISK, HIGH REWARD! 🔥 Ready to gamble? The investment system lets you multiply your money... or lose it! How It Works: 1. Real Candlestick Charts You'll see a live chart with green and red candles Green candles = price going UP 📈 Red candles = price going DOWN 📉 Each investment round lasts 15 seconds 2. Place Your Bet Decide how much money to invest Choose BUY (if you think price will rise) or SELL (if you think price will fall) Wait 15 seconds for the result 3. Win or Lose ✅ Correct prediction: Your investment DOUBLES! 💰💰 ❌ Wrong prediction: You LOSE your investment! 💸 Investment Strategies: Conservative Approach: Only invest 10-20% of your total money Study the chart patterns before betting Wait for clear trends (3+ green or red candles) Aggressive Approach: Invest large amounts for massive gains Higher risk = higher reward Best when you have substantial backup funds Pattern Recognition: Look for consecutive green/red candles Consider recent trends Remember: markets can reverse suddenly! 💡 Warning: Never invest money you need for essential upgrades! Only gamble with surplus funds. 📊 PROGRESSION SYSTEM Early Game (0-1,000 Cookies) Focus: Manual clicking + first upgrades Click the cookie button constantly Buy your first click upgrades Purchase Simple Auto Oven as soon as possible Convert cookies to money strategically Goal: Unlock Shop 1 Mid Game (1,000-100,000 Cookies) Focus: Automation + multiple shops Upgrade your auto ovens Purchase Auto Oven Program Unlock and upgrade Shop 2 Start experimenting with small investments Goal: Build passive income foundation Late Game (100,000+ Cookies) Focus: Empire expansion + investments Max out Advanced Auto Oven Program Unlock Shop 3 and Shop 4 Make strategic investments Optimize shop upgrades Goal: Achieve millionaire status! End Game (Millionaire!) Focus: Maximization Max level all shops Max level all auto ovens Master the investment system Watch your cityline expand across the screen! Goal: True cookie tycoon! 🏆 ACHIEVEMENTS SYSTEM Track your progress through various milestones: Total cookies produced Total money earned Shops unlocked Maximum investment win Days played And many more! Check your stats in the Achievements panel to see how far you've come! 💡 ADVANCED STRATEGIES The Balanced Approach 50% investment in automation (auto ovens) 30% investment in shops 20% investment in click upgrades Result: Steady, reliable growth The Clicker Strategy Heavy investment in click upgrades early Manual clicking focus Slower automation adoption Result: Active gameplay, faster early growth The Passive Strategy Rush automation purchases Minimal clicking after early game Heavy shop investment Result: Offline earnings, slower start but powerful late game The Gambler Strategy Minimum viable automation Maximum investment system usage High risk, high reward Result: Either rapid wealth or bankruptcy! 🎓 TUTORIAL SYSTEM Interactive Learning: Built-in tutorial explains each system Can be opened/closed anytime Each feature explained separately No forced tutorials - learn at your own pace! Tutorial Sections: Cookie Clicking Basics Upgrade System Auto Oven Automation Shop Management Investment System Achievement Tracking 📴 OFFLINE EARNINGS Keep earning while away! Auto ovens continue producing Shops keep filling their bars When you return, collect all offline earnings Maximum offline time: unlimited! Offline Optimization: Prioritize auto ovens before long breaks Upgrade shops for maximum offline money Don't rely on clicking (you won't be there!) 🌍 LANGUAGE & ACCESSIBILITY Play in your native language!
