Simple Words

Simple word game where match 3 falling mechanics meet word game. Game is a Puzzle word game. Select letter make words and see the falling block.

¿Cómo jugar a Simple Words?

Play at your own pace in this relaxing word game that’s as beautiful as it is brainy. Rules are simple - • Tap/Click any letters to make a word • Used letters go dark • Clear a row when all its letters are used Simple rules — satisfying strategy. Try different modes: 🌞 Daily Challenge - Can you reach to the top? 🔁 Round Mode - Play at your own pace, as many rounds as you like. 🔢 Moves Mode - See how far you can go in just a few moves. 🤖 VS AI - Challenge a clever computer opponent!
