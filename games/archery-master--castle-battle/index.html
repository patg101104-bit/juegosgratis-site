Archery Master - Castle Battle

Defend your castle in Archery Master - Castle Battle! Battle orcs, monsters, and the undead in this epic archery game. Upgrade archers, use special arrows, and conquer kingdoms in action-packed tower defense warfare!

¿Cómo jugar a Archery Master - Castle Battle?

«Archery Master - Castle Battle» is an exciting archery game! Grab your bow and arrows and prepare for battle! You have a tower armed with skilled archers, and it’s up to you to defend your castle. Aim carefully with your bow and don’t miss your targets! The battle unfolds between the castle and the advancing army! Your mission is to destroy all enemies. Be warned: enemy troops may outnumber yours, their weapons are stronger, and their projectiles are more destructive! Every shot counts—use them wisely if you want to claim victory! Conquering all the kingdoms won’t be easy. You’ll face orcs, humans, monsters, and the undead! Upgrade your archers, recruit mercenaries, use different types of arrows, apply poisons, and victory will be yours! Game Features: - Unique gameplay mechanics - Simple and intuitive controls - A variety of arrows and poisons to use - A variety of enemies to battle: orcs, humans, monsters, and undead - Dozens of challenging levels - Upgradable archer bastion Controls: - Tap to fire arrows
