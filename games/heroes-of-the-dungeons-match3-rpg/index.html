Heroes of the Dungeons: Match-3 RPG

Collect legendary heroes, defeat invading monsters, and rebuild a fallen kingdom — one match at a time! A mysterious gate has unleashed evil creatures across the land, cutting the realm off from the outside world. As the Master of Hunters, you’re the last hope. Match gems to unleash powerful attacks, trigger epic combos, and use hero skills at the perfect moment. Loot treasures from battles, upgrade shops in your castle, and bring prosperity back to the kingdom! Core: A unique match-3 RPG experience where players strategically match colored gems to trigger distinct in-game actions, such as performing an attack on an enemy or healing the party. Loop: Player progression centers around extensive character development. Players must clear stages, defeat powerful bosses, and unlock chests to earn tokens, which are then used to upgrade character attributes like maximum health, attack power, and healing efficiency. Start: The game features a streamlined onboarding tutorial, ensuring high accessibility and a low barrier to entry, providing players with an immediately engaging and enjoyable experience from the outset. Playgama Fact: The game is fully integrated with Playgama SDK: instant login and cross-device saves between PC and mobile. HTML5. Stable performance regardless of internet quality. Visual: Features a high-quality Stylized / Cartoon art direction. Key Feature: A narrative-driven experience offering deep complexity, a rich cast of characters, and highly varied gameplay mechanics, focusing on the core gem-matching combat system where different colored gems trigger diverse tactical outcomes like attacks, ensuring deep replayability.

¿Cómo jugar a Heroes of the Dungeons: Match-3 RPG?

- Match 3+ tiles to attack enemies and charge your team’s skills. - Swap adjacent tiles to create combos and special blocks for massive damage. - Use hero abilities when they’re ready to target enemy weaknesses and turn the battle around. - Win the fight to earn loot and materials. - Upgrade castle shops with your rewards to boost income and strengthen your heroes for the next stages.
