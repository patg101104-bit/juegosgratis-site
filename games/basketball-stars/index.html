Basketball Stars

Basketball Stars is a cool 2-player basketball game by MadPuffers. Prepared for the 2025 basketball season. Choose your team and enter a challenging tournament. Control your player to perform awesome dunks and 3-pointers to win the game. Feel free to block and slap your opponent to knock him out and get the ball, and pay attention to the supershot bar on the top of the screen. Once fully loaded, you can use the hot special dunk from wherever you stand; it never fails. Be the best player! Controls: Desktop (1-Player): WASD or Arrow Keys to move. X/L to shoot or steal. S to pump/block. A+A or D+D (or Left/Right Arrow twice) to dash. K/Z for super shot. Desktop (2-Player): Player 1 uses WASD + B (shoot) + S (pump) + A/D dash. Player 2 uses Arrows + L (shoot) + Down (pump) + Left/Right dash. Both players press K for super shot. Mobile: On-screen touch buttons mirror the keyboard layout—tap to move, shoot, and dash with the same rhythm. Game Modes: • 1 player: 1-on-1 match against an opponent. • 2 players match: Team vs. team game with 2 players. A friend can become your ally or rival! • Quick match: A short match, perfect for a quick game. Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the goal of the game? A: It's basketball featuring famous clubs and players! Choose your team and score more points while playing as your favorite athletes. Q: How do you win / what counts as a loss? A: Score more points than your opponent. Q: What hooks players in the first few minutes? A: Enjoyable 2D graphics focusing on well-known players. You'll definitely recognize familiar champions! You can also choose your favorite players and teams for both yourself and your opponent, allowing for unique matchups! Mechanics & Progression Q: How many levels are there / is the structure clear? A: There is no set number of levels. You can play match after match in different modes and with various teams. Economy & Customization Q: What is available from the start? A: All modes, players, and teams are available right away. Play without restrictions! Q: Are there skins / customization options? A: You can choose your favorite player and team for both yourself and your opponent. Retention & Engagement Q: Is sound necessary? A: You can play without sound, but it enhances the atmosphere of the basketball match. Q: Is there onboarding in the game? What does it include? A: The game features a dedicated tutorial that explains the controls and gameplay principles.
