Chroma Trek

Immerse yourself in Chroma Trek, a breathtaking neon platformer that dazzles with vibrant visuals and innovative gameplay. Each level is a unique masterpiece, offering diverse challenges that keep you engaged and on your toes. The core mechanic of color-switching lets you seamlessly pass through various types of obstacles, adding depth and excitement to every moment. As you navigate through dynamic environments, you'll be uplifted by motivational messages from the creator, encouraging you to push forward and conquer the journey to the Finish Crystal!

¿Cómo jugar a Chroma Trek?

Objective: Navigate through a series of challenging obstacles to reach the Finish Crystal and complete the level! Controls: Move your character using one of two control schemes: A or ←: Move Left D or →: Move Right W or Spacebar: Jump Gameplay: Dodge Obstacles: Avoid traps, pits, and other hazards. Reach the Crystal: Find and touch the Finish Crystal to win the level.
