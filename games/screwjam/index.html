Screw Jam

Welcome to Screw Jam, where the challenge of unscrewing puzzles and sorting colorful screws, pins, and nuts takes center stage! Dive into an immersive world of wood and bolts, where every unscrew brings you closer to solving the ultimate screw pin jam puzzle. With each twist, you'll match colors and sort nuts and bolts to clear your path through tricky levels.

¿Cómo jugar a Screw Jam?

Custom Screw Tools: Unlock and use specialized tools to tackle harder puzzles and speed up unscrewing Multilayered Puzzles: Solve puzzles with multiple layers of screws and pins that need to be removed in the correct sequence Obstacle Course Levels: Navigate through levels with shifting mechanics like rotating platforms and sliding pins, adding complexity to each puzzle Endless Puzzle Mode: After conquering levels, challenge yourself with endless puzzles to keep the unscrewing fun going Enjoy new daily challenges with special rewards for completing jam-packed levels
