Idle Supermarket Tycoon

Embark on a journey to become a retail mogul with Idle Supermarket Tycoon. This engaging online simulation game lets you transform a modest shopping mall into a bustling retail empire. Strategically develop new shops, enhance their interiors, and beautify the mall's exterior. Your mission: serve customers, boost your reputation, and watch your profits soar. Play for free on your phone or computer and experience the thrill of managing your own supermarket kingdom

¿Cómo jugar a Idle Supermarket Tycoon?

You have a Shopping mall at your disposal, which you need to develop by acquiring new shops and outfitting them, as well as improving the exterior and interior of the entire Shopping mall. Serve customers, earn money and reputation. Invest your profits into your Shopping mall, and distribute reputation points among visitors. Assign priority customers to each shop in your mall. This way, you can maximize profits from serving them! Purchase delivery vehicles to earn additional income from each completed trip. Don't forget to monitor the corridors, as trash cans or vending machines might get clogged. If you're unsure what to do next, you can always refer to the missions. Complete them to earn valuable rewards and expand your Shopping mall, your grand Supermarket! Controls: Use the left mouse button to interact with the in-game UI Plus and minus keys = change the size of the camera
