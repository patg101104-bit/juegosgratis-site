Run The Electricity

Run The Electricity is a unique puzzle game that combines simplicity and benefits for your mind. It is designed to give relaxation, help to cope with stress and improve concentration. Instead of intense gameplay and a race against time, a calm process awaits you here: you just need to connect lines, lamps and energy sources in a closed circuit to turn on the light. Minimalistic design, smooth animation and soothing music create an atmosphere of complete tranquility comparable to yoga. This is not just a game, but a means to meditate and combat anxiety. You can play it at home, on the road, or at work to distract yourself and restore your mental balance. Smart puzzles train logic and imagination, and simple mechanics allow you to have fun from the first seconds. Discover a world of light, harmony and tranquility!

¿Cómo jugar a Run The Electricity?

Playing this game is easy and enjoyable. All you need to do is tap on the lines and turn them until you get a closed electrical circuit. For the light to come on, at least one lamp and a wire must be connected in it. As soon as this happens, the lines will shine, and you will get a feeling of completeness and tranquility. Each puzzle takes only a few seconds, but it brings a lot of fun. This makes the game ideal for short pauses, trips, or waiting. You don't have to rush: the levels are unlimited in time, and you can solve them at your own pace. With each new stage, the tasks become more interesting and diverse, training concentration and attention. Conduct Electricity will help to cope with anxiety, give positive emotions and become an excellent anti-stress remedy. Just open the game, start connecting the lines, and feel your mind calm down.
