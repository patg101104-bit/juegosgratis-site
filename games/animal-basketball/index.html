Animal Basketball

Basketball games: The Power of Moe! Ultra-healing hand-painted physical shooting party [Moe Animal Troupe Gathering] Manipulation will be serious kittens, cute chicks, naughty crocodiles! ¿Cómo jugar a Animal Basketball?

Desktop Controls: Mouse left button to drag and shoot. Mobile Controls: Touch and move on screen to drag and shoot.
