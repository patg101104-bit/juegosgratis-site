Hills Of Steel

Hills of Steel is an explosive tank battle drive with realistic physics that will captivate you from the very first seconds! Take control of your steel battle tank and race across the battlefields, destroying enemies with powerful shots. Collect rewards from defeated opponents, upgrade armor and weapons, unlock new unique tanks and challenge increasingly dangerous opponents. You will find a path from fierce battles on earth to epic battles on the futuristic Moon! Win, gain recognition and climb the career ladder to the title of the greatest marshal of all time! If you like heavy vehicles, dynamic battles and endless waves of enemies, play Hills of Steel right now and experience the real tank power! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do players need to do? A: Tank battles with realistic physics and in-game progression. Q: What is the standout feature of the game? What can hook players in? A: A variety of different tanks, various locations, high-quality graphics, unique tank upgrades, futuristic tanks. Q: Are there bosses and how are they different? A: Yes, at the end of a level, a boss in a tank can appear. It has more HP and is larger in size. Defeating it rewards more coins. Q: Are there any currencies and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: The game has currencies - diamonds and gold. Gold can be spent on tank upgrades. Diamonds can be used to purchase gold, crates, and new tanks. Mechanics & Progression Q: How does the leveling up system work? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: Play more and win, earning coins to upgrade your tank. Economy & Customization Q: Are there in-game purchases? What can be bought and what is the impact? Does making purchases speed up game progress? A: There are in-game purchases where you can buy diamonds to later purchase coins or new tanks. Making purchases significantly speeds up game progress. Q: What are the engaging content elements (weapons, vehicles, locations, skills, references to famous franchises, etc.)? A: The game features futuristic tanks and 2 different game modes. Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you customize the appearance of the hero, location editor, car tuning, etc.? A: The game offers various skins that can be purchased for all tanks. Retention & Engagement Q: Are there daily rewards or something similar to encourage players to return? A: Yes, the game provides a case to the player every 24 hours. Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to better immerse oneself in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in gameplay without sound? A: Music complements the game but is not necessary for gameplay.

¿Cómo jugar a Hills Of Steel?

To control on mobile devices, use the buttons on the screen. To control the computer, use the "A" and "D" keys or the arrows to move. Press the "Space" key to fire and the "X" key to use the booster. The buttons will remain on the screen - you can use them if necessary.
