Hoppy Bird

Get ready to flap, hop, and soar! Hoppy Bird takes the classic arcade formula to new heights with exciting new mechanics and game modes. Test your reflexes as you navigate through endless pipes, but watch out—it’s not just about flying anymore! Key Features: - 3 Unique Game Modes: Master the classic Normal mode, defy physics in Moon mode (Low Gravity), or test your limits in Hardcore mode (2x Speed, No Revive!). - Power-Up Your Flight: Collect active boosters like Shields for protection, Magnets to attract coins, and Double Score to climb the ranks faster. - Shop & Customization: Earn coins to unlock a variety of cool skins, including the stealthy Ninja, the Red Rocket, and more. - LiveOps & Rewards: Complete Daily Quests and participate in Gold Rush events to maximize your earnings. - Global Leaderboards: Compete with players worldwide and prove you are the ultimate bird pilot. Simple to play, impossible to master. How far can you hop?

¿Cómo jugar a Hoppy Bird?

Objective: Fly as far as possible without crashing! Your goal is to navigate Hoppy Bird through an endless series of pipes to achieve the highest score. Collect coins to unlock skins and grab boosters to extend your run. How to Play: - Stay Airbone: Gravity pulls you down constantly. You must flap your wings to gain height. - Dodge Obstacles: Timing is key! Pass through the gaps in the pipes. Hitting a pipe or the ground ends the game. - Use Power-ups: Collect items like the Shield (protection), Magnet (attract coins), and 2x Score to help you survive and rank up. Controls: - Desktop: Press Spacebar or Left Mouse Button to fly. - Mobile: Tap anywhere on the screen to fly.
