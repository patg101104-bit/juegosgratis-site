Knife Tower

Race up a colorful tower, throw knives into every block, and reach the top before your rival. Time each shot around the bouncing ball, build combos with precise hits, collect coins, unlock new knives, and upgrade your speed, combo range, and income.

¿Cómo jugar a Knife Tower?

Tap and hold to throw knives into the tower. Avoid hitting the bouncing ball. Land accurate shots to break blocks, create combos, and climb faster. Reach the final block before your opponent to win. Use earned coins to unlock knives and improve your upgrades.
