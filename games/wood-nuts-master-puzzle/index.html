Wood Nuts Master: Screw Puzzle

Wood Nuts Master: Screw Puzzle is a captivating online game that tests your strategic thinking by fitting screws and nuts to topple wooden blocks. Accessible on multiple platforms, this trending title offers a fun and engaging puzzle experience for those looking for a new play-to-earn option.

¿Cómo jugar a Wood Nuts Master: Screw Puzzle?

In this physics-based innovative puzzle game, your objective is to strategically place screws and nuts to fall all the wooden blocks. Think well and find best spots of empty holes to place the screws. Be careful when placing screws as you have limited empty holes and some of which may become covered by the wood blocks you released.
