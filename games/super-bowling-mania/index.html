¿Cómo jugar a Super Bowling Mania?

Drag to aim your bowling ball. Swipe forward to roll. Add spin and curve to control your shot. Knock down all pins to score strikes and spares. Compete against challenging opponents and improve your bowling skills. Unlock exciting balls, pins, and rewards as you progress.
