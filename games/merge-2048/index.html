Merge 2048

Merge 2048 is a fun and addictive merging game where your goal is to combine matching numbered blocks to reach higher values—can you unlock the elusive 2048 block? Choose between two exciting modes: Classic 2048 and Shape Mode for a refreshing twist in gameplay. As you drop blocks, plan your merges carefully to avoid filling up the container. Feeling stuck? Use the powerful TNT Block to clear unwanted tiles and create space. The game features clean visuals, smooth controls, and a satisfying progression that keeps you hooked. Track your score, unlock cool titles, and see how far you can go without hitting the red line. Perfect for quick sessions or long strategic runs—Merge 2048 is simple to pick up, but hard to master!

¿Cómo jugar a Merge 2048?

Desktop: Drag by clicking the left mouse button , and leave the button to drop the block. Mobile: Drag with touch screen and block is dropped when the drag ends.
