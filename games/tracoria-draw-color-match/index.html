¿Cómo jugar a Tracoria: Draw, Color & Match?

Carefully study the Reference Image and recreate it on your Canvas. Your goal is to match the colors, shapes, and strokes as accurately as possible. Once you are finished, click the "Check Answer" button to see your accuracy score. Achieve high accuracy to earn stars and unlock new worlds! Controls: Mouse / Touch: Click/Tap & Drag: Draw on the canvas or use the selected tool. Click/Tap Icons: Select tools (Brush, Bucket Fill, Eraser, Shapes) and pick colors from the palette. Mobile: Tap the Mini-Preview in the top-left to expand the reference image at any time for a closer look. Pay close attention to the small details! Use the Eyedropper tool to ensure your colors are 100% correct, and adjust your brush size frequently to handle both broad strokes and fine outlines.
