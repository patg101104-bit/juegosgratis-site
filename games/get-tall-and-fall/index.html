Get Tall And Fall

An exciting adventure in which you have to boost your growth with a variety of food, pets, potions and other methods. Conquer the peaks of each location, discover new worlds and take the lead among the players!

¿Cómo jugar a Get Tall And Fall?

WASD Management Space - Jump E - Start growth RMB - Rotate camera Pets give income multipliers Potions give powerups The higher the world, the more difficult it is to level up Joystick on the left - Movement The buttons on the right are Jump/Purchase
