Snake Tangle

Welcome to Snake Tangle, a thrilling and addictive puzzle game that will put your strategic thinking and problem-solving skills to the test! In Tangled Snake, Your mission is to guide a mischievous snake through a tangled mess of obstacles and help it reach its tasty prey. Snake Tangle features multiple levels of increasing difficulty. Each level presents a new maze layout, with more complex obstacles and narrower paths. As you progress, you'll face additional challenges such as moving barriers, time limits, and limited swipes, adding an extra layer of excitement and urgency to the gameplay. Snake Tangle offers an engaging and challenging gameplay experience that will captivate puzzle enthusiasts and snake game lovers alike. So, get ready to twist, turn, and strategize your way through a tangled maze in this thrilling adventure!

¿Cómo jugar a Snake Tangle?

To successfully navigate through the tangled maze, you'll need to carefully plan each move and consider the snake's current length and positioning. The snake can only move in a straight line until it hits an obstacle or the maze's boundary, forcing you to plan the path ahead and avoid getting trapped.
