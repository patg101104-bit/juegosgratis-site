Foxy Eco Sort

Join Foxy and Raccoon on their eco-mission! Sort waste, play match-3 and matching pairs to clean polluted locations. But it's not just cleaning! Uncover the conspiracy of cunning Ratty who wants to destroy the forest. Complete story chapters: track garbage trucks, expose arsonists and save your home! Features: 🌿 Waste sorting - learn to separate waste: glass, plastic, paper and organics. 🌎 Clean up locations - each cleaned area becomes more beautiful, and the rescued animals rejoice! 🦝 Complete the story line by uncovering the cunning rat's conspiracies. 🦊 Funny characters - Foxy the fox will tell you why sorting is important, and the raccoon... well, he's still lazy, but he'll get it! 😂 Funny dialogues - the characters won't let you get bored, their communication will cheer you up! Ready to save the planet? Then go ahead - Foxy and the raccoon are already waiting for you! ♻️🦝🦊 P.S. The game is made with love for nature and good jokes. Enjoy the game!

¿Cómo jugar a Foxy Eco Sort?

1. Sort Waste Correctly Drag items to their matching categories: cardboard to cardboard, plastic to plastic, glass to glass, organic to organic. 2. Play "Match-3" Combine three identical elements to clear the field. 3. Find Pairs Flip cards and match eco-themed pairs. Train your memory and help clean the forest!
