Rings Untie

Rings Untie is a simple but addictive Ring puzzle game. Do you want to train your brain? Do you love colorful games with easy gameplay with fun rotate the circle puzzle games? Both kids and adults can enjoy Rotate the Rings - a game in which you use your finger to rotate the circle to dissolve the puzzle. Save the lovely moments with your beloved people while playing this game! This puzzle game includes various types of colorful rings: D rings, S rings, C lock rings, Ring lock... and different obstacles. A lot of exciting puzzle levels in the game are there for you to explore. If you like mini-games like Rescue the Dog, Rescue the woman and Save the Girl... this is another fun game for you.

¿Cómo jugar a Rings Untie?

• Players need to rotate the circle by touching the screen to align it perfectly with the gap • The circle will display various colors or patterns that need to be aligned with the center target within a given time • Each successful match increases the score and unlocks the next, more challenging level • Different rings bring you different challenges, plan your next move to save the time • C lock rings is easy to rotate, D ring limit the range, S ring is flexible,... and more Ring locks to explore • Pay attention to the time limit, try to complete the matches within the designated time, and avoid incorrect moves • Like other rescue the dog game, you must be careful and have a good strategy to win the game
