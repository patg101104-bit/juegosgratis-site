Noob: Big Item Sorting

Do you enjoy keeping things tidy? Do you enjoy sorting things? Then this game is for you! Introducing a fun new game all about sorting and cleaning! 😻In this exciting puzzle, you'll find identical objects and match them together. Arrange the objects so that two identical ones end up next to each other and form a double. 🎁Match items in pairs to advance through the levels! Cleaning has never been so enjoyable and exciting! 🎮"Noob: The Big Object Sorting Game" is a light and calming game suitable for anyone who loves order, regardless of age or gender!

¿Cómo jugar a Noob: Big Item Sorting?

Simple and intuitive gameplay - drag and drop items and connect them at the bottom of the screen. - Match two identical items at the bottom of the screen to create a match; - Connect all the items to complete the level; - Unlock new items, avatars, and skins by completing more levels!
