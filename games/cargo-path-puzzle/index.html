Cargo Path Puzzle

Cargo Path Puzzle is a free puzzle-platformer that puts your logic and movement skills to the test. Navigate tricky levels filled with deadly voids, collapsing blocks, trampolines, ice slides, and directional blocks. Your goal: collect all the keys to activate the exit portal—then reach it to win. Each level has only one correct solution, so plan your moves carefully. One wrong step can trap you or block your progress. If you enjoy clever puzzles, single-solution challenges, and smart platforming, Cargo Path Puzzle is made for you.

¿Cómo jugar a Cargo Path Puzzle?

Move: Use the arrow keys or WASD to move up, down, left, and right. Jump: Press Spacebar to jump in the last direction moved, skipping over one tile. Goal: Collect all keys to unlock the portal and step on it to finish the level.
