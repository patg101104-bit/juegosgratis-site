Cooking Shawarma Idle Game

We are providing a stunning and engaging gameplay experience that delivers a real simulation of running a shawarma business. From managing different shawarma and fast-food items to handling customers in real time, every moment feels like you are operating your own busy food stall. Upgrade your kitchen, speed up your service, and take your shawarma shop to the next level as you grow your culinary empire. Your journey begins with a small shawarma stand, humble yet full of potential. As the Shawarma Restaurant Master, you will dive deep into the art of making perfect shawarma—from choosing the best meat cuts to marinating them with the ideal blend of spices. Every step matters, and every decision affects the final taste and customer satisfaction. Enhance your slicing knife, lavash station, and cooking speed to boost profits and keep customers happy. Add ketchup, mayo, fries, pickles, and many other extras to customize each order based on customer demands. Have you ever dreamed of cooking delicious dishes from around the world and exploring different cultures? All of that becomes possible in Our Shawarma Game!

¿Cómo jugar a Cooking Shawarma Idle Game?

Prepare and serve customer orders accurately and quickly. Customers will request different food items such as: Shawarma ,Fries,Pasta & Cold drinks Complete each order correctly to keep your customers happy and earn rewards. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Desktop Controls (Mouse) Fry Chips: Hold Right Mouse Button to fry the fries. Cut the Meat: Hold Right Mouse Button and drag to slice the meat. Fill the Shawarma: Click the Right Mouse Button multiple times on ingredients to add them to the shawarma. Serve the Order: Hold Right Mouse Button, then drag the completed item to the customer. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Mobile Controls (Touch) Fry Chips: Tap and hold to fry the fries. Cut the Meat: Tap and drag to slice the meat. Fill the Shawarma: Tap multiple times on ingredients to add them. Serve the Order: Tap and hold, then drag the completed item to the customer.
