Merge Home Ball 2048

¿Cómo jugar a Merge Home Ball 2048?

Shoot and merge matching numbered balls to create bigger numbers and reach massive combos! Aim carefully, drop the balls into the board, and combine identical balls like 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8, all the way up to huge numbers. Earn stars by completing merges and use them to renovate and decorate beautiful rooms. Unlock new furniture, wallpapers, and stylish home upgrades as you progress through the game. Plan your shots, create chain reactions, and transform old houses into cozy dream homes in Merge Home Ball 3D!
