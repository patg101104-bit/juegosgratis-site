Obby: Upgrade Your Lucky Machine!

Forget boring spin machines! - here you're not just spinning reels, you're building your own luck machine. Increase the number of slots, speed up spins, add multipliers and bonuses - every spin becomes more powerful! 🔹 Upgrade everything: Spin speed—from slow to lightning fast Number of reels—from 1 to 20 Symbol bonuses—+1 to 5 coins 🔹 Battle against the bot—your challenge! Who will collect more winnings? The winner takes all winnings from both machines—the loser gets nothing. 🔹 Money—your weapon: Earn coins, buy upgrades, compete in battles—and watch your machine become unstoppable. Perfect for those who love: Idle games with progression System upgrades Competing against bots! About: Upgrade the Spin Machine! – a game where you're not just a player, but the creator of a legendary machine.

¿Cómo jugar a Obby: Upgrade Your Lucky Machine!?

Controls for computers: WASD = move "E" or left mouse button = interact Space = jump Controls for phones: Joystick = move "E" button = interact Arrow button = jump
