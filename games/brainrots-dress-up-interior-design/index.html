Brainrots: Dress Up & Interior Design

«Brainrots: Dress Up & Interior Design» is a bright dress-up and interior design game where everyone can show creativity. Six popular heroes are waiting for you: Cappuccino Assassino, Ballerina Cappuccina, Bombardiro Crocodilo, Orcalero, Chimpanzini Bananini and Tralalelo Tralalata. Each character is unique, and you can create their own style: choose outfits, hats and accessories, mix looks and invent fashionable combinations. Then move on to interior design! Decorate rooms with furniture, hang paintings, add cozy details and create spaces to your taste. There are no limits or restrictions — only your imagination and freedom of expression. If you enjoy creative games like Toca Boca, where you can dress up characters and design rooms, «Brainrots: Dress Up & Interior Design» is the perfect choice. Experiment, create stories and bring your ideas to life!

¿Cómo jugar a Brainrots: Dress Up & Interior Design?

In «Brainrots: Dress Up & Interior Design», it’s easy to start: 1. Choose a character from six available heroes. 2. Pick outfits, hats and accessories to highlight the style. 3. Decorate the room interior: arrange furniture, paintings and details. 4. Combine styles, imagine stories and create unique designs. Controls: use the mouse to select and place items. The game is simple, fun and quickly pulls you into the creative process.
