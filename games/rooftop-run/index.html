Rooftop Run

Rooftop Run – Race Across the City! 🏃‍♂️💨 Run, jump, and slide over rooftops in this fast-paced parkour game! Dodge obstacles, smash through barriers, and escape from enemies as you race through exciting levels. Controls: Desktop: Hold the right mouse button and direct your movement. Mobile: Use your finger to direct your movement. Key Features: • Only Forward: Run forward and only forward! Don't stop. You control only the direction of movement; everything else happens automatically. But don't think this will make it easy — you'll need to make decisions on the fly! • Don't Get Caught by Enemies: They will try to grab you. Be faster and smarter! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the goal of the game? A: A true dynamic parkour experience! Run, jump from rooftop to rooftop, dive through skyscraper windows, roll, slide under obstacles, and run along walls! And of course, try not to get caught by enemies! Q: How do you win / what counts as losing? A: Try not to fall and avoid getting caught by enemies who will try to stop you! Q: What grabs you in the first few minutes? A: Insane dynamics! You run at high speed across rooftops, leap over obstacles, smash windows, roll, and at certain moments, you'll catch a stunning shot! Mechanics & Progression Q: How many levels are there / is the structure clear? A: Levels follow one after another, each presenting different obstacles in a variety of variations! Q: Does the difficulty change? A: Each subsequent level is harder than the previous one. You'll need to test your reflexes! Economy & Customization Q: Are there skins / customization options? A: Collect coins scattered throughout the levels to unlock various customization elements. Retention & Engagement Q: Is sound necessary? A: Sound is not mandatory; play however you feel comfortable. However, turning it on will help you immerse yourself better in the dynamics. Q: Is there onboarding in the game? What does it include? A: At the start, you'll be shown everything essential. You'll immediately understand what to do.
