2048 checkers

🧩 2048 Checkers is a fresh twist on the classic 2048, where numbers meet the logic and strategy of checkers! ♟️ Move numbered checkers across the board — they can capture only pieces with the same number. 💥 After a successful capture, your checker’s number doubles. 🏆 The goal — reach the 2048 checker and claim victory!

¿Cómo jugar a 2048 checkers?

1️⃣ Choose a checker and capture another with the same number ♟️ 2️⃣ After capturing, your checker becomes twice as strong 💪 3️⃣ Earn 💰 coins for every successful move 4️⃣ Use coins to: 🔸 Double a checker’s number 🔸 Remove a piece 🔸 Or shuffle the board 🔄 5️⃣ Reach 2048 and climb to the top of the player leaderboard 🌟
