Yenemy

Yenemy is a game that combines survival, farming, base-building, crafting, and large-scale clan battles. Players gather resources, build and defend castles, fight monsters and enemy clans, and work together to destroy the opposing clan’s Temple. Outside battles, players develop their own Farm Island by growing crops, producing goods, trading with ports, hiring workers, and expanding production chains. Progress in battles directly supports farm development, creating a strong connection between combat and long term progression.

¿Cómo jugar a Yenemy?

Craft weapons, fight monsters and enemy players, safeguard your clan temple and storm enemy castle. Destroy enemy clan temple to achieve victory in battle! Controls: W: move towards mouse pointer. S: move backwards from mouse pointer. A: strafe left. D: strafe right. Left Mouse Button: attack/gather. Right Mouse Button: automatically attack. Space: jump/dash.
