Tile Farm Story

The game "Tile Farm Story" is an exciting and challenging adventure that you won't be able to put down! This chip collecting game will allow you to take a fresh look at the traditional rules of mahjong and test your Puzzle Master skills! Hundreds of exciting levels, a rich history of the Stone sisters and colorful places that you will visit will help you relax and at the same time train your brain by solving many puzzles presented in the game. You won't notice how time flies! Game Features: - diverse and colorful locations in the most amazing corners of the world - hundreds of exciting levels based on the mechanics of collecting chips - many useful boosters to help you progress - a rich plot with surprising twists and turns - many unique animals with bright characters and funny animations - an opportunity to train your thinking skills and relax in the company of an exciting game that combines mahjong traditions and new mechanics

¿Cómo jugar a Tile Farm Story?

The goal of the game is to find identical chips on the field and collect them. The collected chips are collected in the cells below. When there are 3 identical chips in the cells, they will be destroyed. If all the cells are filled, the game will fail. To win, you must destroy all the chips on the playing field. Control: touch/click.
