¿Cómo jugar a Sprunki Craft - Sandbox 3D?

Level up by collecting crystals, attacking enemies, cutting down trees. Enemies are below your level, you can attack them. If the enemy is above your level - run away! Upgrade your abilities between rounds. Computer controls: WASD or arrows. Phone controls: Touch the screen and a virtual joystick will appear.
