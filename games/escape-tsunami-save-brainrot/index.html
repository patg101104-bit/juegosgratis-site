Escape Tsunami Save Brainrot

An incredible threat is looming over the city — a giant wave created by alien technologies is rapidly approaching! Only you can save the unique Brainroth creatures before they are overwhelmed by an alien tsunami. Grab them, rush away from the impending disaster and manage to deliver them to a safe zone. Upgrade your mobility — increase your speed, agility and endurance to get further away from the wave and save more creatures. 🧠 Search and collect — from the most common to the legendary brainrots, each of which has unique features and value. 🏠 Create a rescue base — take brainrots to the shelter, replenish the collection, unlock new abilities and upgrade the shelter. Become a master of escape — use strategy, quick reactions and ingenuity to outrun the alien threat. Can you outrun the UFO tsunami, save all the brainrots and become the legend of the base? Run, collect and survive — the challenge has already begun! 🛸🔥

¿Cómo jugar a Escape Tsunami Save Brainrot?

On the Computer: WASD- move. PCM - look around. The space bar is a jump. On the phone: The joystick is moving. There is a jump button at the bottom right.
