Poppy 4! Cut Monsters with Sword in Arena!

Welcome to "Poppy 4! Cut Monsters with Sword in Arena!" - an exciting action game with a sword, where you fight against an army of monsters! Get ready for an epic battle and test your agility and blade skill. Survive in the monsters' lair and become a real sword master! 🎮 A game inspired by popular projects such as "War of the Knights: Sword Arena Battle 3D", "Sword Master: Slash Enemies!" and "Swing Sword: Slash Enemies into Pieces!" Here you will find a mix of the themes of "poppy playtime", "rainbow friends", "hagi wagi", "repo" and the atmosphere of the "Dendy World"! ⚔️ Game Features: - Simple and clear controls - Stylish 3D graphics in retro dandy and repo style - Exciting gameplay with chopping enemies - Various levels and enemy characters - A large selection of sword skins - A leaderboard where you can compete with friends ⚡ Ready to join the fight? Then take your sword and start the battle with monsters!

¿Cómo jugar a Poppy 4! Cut Monsters with Sword in Arena!?

🎯 Your goal is to defeat all the monsters in the arena using your sword! Cut down your enemies and stop the monster invasion. The further you go, the harder it gets! ⚔️ 🕹️ Controls: On PC 💻 — hold down the left mouse button and move the cursor to attack. On phone 📱 — swipe your finger across the screen to strike with your sword. 🔥 Tips: ✅ Watch your enemies' movements and strike first! ✅ Combine attacks to deal more damage. ✅ Don't forget to upgrade your sword as you progress through the game!
