Hand Daily

A daily card roguelite. Every day, the same 52-card deck deals across the world. Build five-card hands, chain joker effects, climb 5 antes, build a daily streak. Five minutes per run, one chance per day. Same hand for every player. Free, no install, no microtransactions.

¿Cómo jugar a Hand Daily?

Pick 1–5 cards from your hand to play as a poker hand. Beat the ante's target score in 4 plays — discards swap up to 5 cards. Win an ante to earn coins, then buy a joker that boosts every play after. Joker effects stack (+chips, +mult, suit/rank bonuses). Clear all 5 antes to win the day. There's also an endless mode for after you finish.
