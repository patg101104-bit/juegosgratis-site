Mr.Reckless Car Chase Simulator

Run fast, crash hard, and cheat death — in style. Race through a chaotic low-poly city as you dodge police, traffic, and total destruction. Use game-changing power-ups like Slow-Mo, Freeze, Nitro to escape the impossible. One rule: don’t stop. How long can you stay free?

¿Cómo jugar a Mr.Reckless Car Chase Simulator?

Use WASD keys or Arrow Keys to move your vehicle. Press the Spacebar to engage the handbrake for sharp turns or emergency stops.
