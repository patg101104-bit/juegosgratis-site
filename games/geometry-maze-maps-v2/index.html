Geometry Maze Maps V2

Geometry Dash Maze Maps V2 is a fast-paced platformer where you control a cube through six tricky mazes. The game throws obstacles like spikes and moving platforms at you while your character speeds forward on its own, testing your reflexes. Time your jumps carefully - use the yellow boosters to reach higher spots and avoid instant-death traps. The more you play, the better your reaction time gets, so keep at it.

¿Cómo jugar a Geometry Maze Maps V2?

Speed will make it difficult for you to win the level, but that's what's challenging Desktop Controls: Jump - Spacebar Mobile Controls: Jump - tap on screen
