Cat vs Granny. Cat Simulator

Immerse yourself in madness and fun as a mischievous cat who decided to turn grandma's cozy house into a field of chaos! 🐱 Break pots, steal fish from the aquarium, throw things and scratch everything that comes under your paw. And all this is in the form of a first-person simulator with elements of humor and mayhem. Explore rooms stuffed with interactive objects and watch as grandma's patience melts with each of your pranks. How far will you go before she explodes with anger? 🐾 Game features: 🐟 Destructible objects and interactive environment 😂 Funny and unpredictable reactions of grandma 😼 Endless opportunities for mischief 🎮 Easy to learn, but addictive gameplay

¿Cómo jugar a Cat vs Granny. Cat Simulator?

The goal of the game is to complete all the tasks, while annoying Granny. PC controls: WASD - move; Shift - run; Space - jump; E - put/take; F - interact; LMB - throw/shoot. Mobile device controls: Right swipe - camera movement; Left joystick - player movement.
