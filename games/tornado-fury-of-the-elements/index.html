Tornado: Fury of the elements

In this game you take on the role of a tornado lord, who must wreak havoc and destruction in the city. Your task is to create powerful whirlwinds, destroy buildings and infrastructure, and avoid collisions with defenses and other obstacles. You will be able to improve your tornado control skills and destructive power to wreak havoc more effectively. “Tornado: Fury of the Element” is an exciting game that will make you feel like a real master of the elements. It is suitable for all fans of arcade games and simulators.

¿Cómo jugar a Tornado: Fury of the elements?

The game uses intuitive controls, which makes it easy to master the game even for beginners. On PC: W - go forward; A - go left; S - go backwards; D - go right. On mobile devices, control is done with the joystick on the screen.
