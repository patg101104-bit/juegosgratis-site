Zombie Terminator

Zombie Terminator is an intense zombie shooting survival game set in a post-apocalyptic city overrun by the undead. You play as one of the last survivors, armed with powerful weapons to fight endless waves of zombies. Combining action shooting, survival gameplay, and strategic movement, the game delivers thrilling combat and nonstop excitement. Defeat enemies, collect rewards, upgrade your weapons, and survive against increasingly dangerous zombie hordes. With immersive environments, challenging missions, and fast-paced gameplay, every battle feels epic and rewarding!

¿Cómo jugar a Zombie Terminator?

Your mission is to eliminate all zombies and survive until the end of each level. Move carefully to avoid being surrounded while aiming and shooting enemies. Defeated zombies drop coins and power-ups that can improve your weapons and survival abilities. As the game progresses, stronger enemies will appear, so stay alert and become the ultimate zombie terminator! PC Controls: ↑ (Up Arrow): Move up ↓ (Down Arrow): Move down ← (Left Arrow): Move left → (Right Arrow): Move right Space: Shoot / Fire Shift: Speed boost Mobile Controls: Joystick: Drag to move in any direction Shoot Button: Tap to attack zombies Boost Button: Tap for a temporary speed boost
