Far Orion: New worlds

The year is 2121. Technology has advanced far beyond the bounds of our wildest fantasies, but great progress demands great sacrifice... Explore other worlds in the search for treasure and limitless power! Command a team of brave heroes and lead your world to victory! Game features: - several dozen unique heroes and battle cards - dozens of missions on every world - no irritating ads - PvP arena - tower mode - clans and clan wars [Core Gameplay & Story] Q: What is the game about? What do you need to do? A: It's an action strategy game where you complete levels (missions) and fight enemies. Q: Does the game have a storyline? A: Yes, there is a story. It's a sci-fi setting in the year 2121 with advanced technology, but something went wrong, and the heroes want to save the world. Q: What is the game's unique selling point? What can hook players? A: There are many different heroes with an upgrade system, a large number of missions, a PvP arena, a leaderboard, and clan wars. You can also upgrade attack cards. Q: What are the engaging content elements (weapons, vehicles, locations, skills, references to famous franchises, etc.)? A: A huge variety of heroes, many different and interesting abilities for these heroes, an engaging story, clear objectives, and simple in-game mechanics. [Mechanics & Progression] Q: Are there bosses and how do they differ? A: There are bosses throughout the storyline, and they differ in design, attack types, and HP amount. Q: How exactly does progression work? What should you do to level up faster? A: To level up faster, you need to play well to earn enough in-game currency. The game is easy to play. Q: Are there mini-games within the main story? For example, 'match-three' puzzles within the strategy game. A: No. [Economy & Customization] Q: What are the currencies and what are they spent on (standard - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: Money is spent on upgrading heroes, upgrading cards, and buying hero cards to unlock them. Q: Are there in-game purchases? What can you buy and what does it affect? Does spending money speed up game progress? A: Yes, microtransactions will speed up game progress, as you can buy in-game currency with real money and spend that currency on upgrades and cards. Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you customize a hero's appearance, location editor, vehicle tuning, etc.? A: You can only upgrade the heroes' levels and abilities, which also changes their appearance. [Retention & Engagement] Q: Are there leaderboards? How do you get on them? What types are there (weekly, monthly, all-time, etc.)? A: There are various leaderboards depending on the different game modes, as well as a clan leaderboard. You need to play and gain levels. Q: Are there daily rewards or something similar that encourages the player to return? A: Yes, logging into the game every day provides various bonuses: cards, chests, and in-game currency. Q: Is it advisable to play with sound to better immerse yourself in the atmosphere, or will playing without sound cause difficulties? A: The music is good and complements the game, but it is not essential for completing the game. Q: Is there an achievement system? What are they and what can you get them for? A: Achievements are awarded for daily logins, reaching a certain level in a specific mode, and completing certain objectives, such as collecting 6 heroes in total. Rewards include chests, in-game currency, and cards.

¿Cómo jugar a Far Orion: New worlds?

Gather a squad of heroes and a deck of cards and complete missions. Use the money you receive to unlock new heroes and upgrade your cards. Which heroes should I take into battle? - those that complement each other and are stronger together, like a healer and a tank. What cards should I fight with? - those that can strengthen your squad of heroes, or basic cards for healing and damage to enemies How do I earn gold? - by completing missions and daily quests, and fighting in the tower and the aren
