Cubby Soccer League

Cubby Soccer League is a thrilling online HTML5 game that lets you enjoy real soccer action for free. Navigate the field, protect the ball from rivals, and aim for the goal, all while managing your stamina. It's perfect for both casual gamers and soccer fans.

¿Cómo jugar a Cubby Soccer League?

For computer: - Use arrow keys to move left and right - use "Spacebar" to move forward - Use "Spacebar" to shoot and take penalties - Use the arrow keys to control the goalkeeper. For mobile: - Use the on-screen Direction keys to move left and right - use "ball icon" to move forward - Tap the "ball icon" or the "screen" to shoot and take penalties - Tap the colors on the goal to control the goalkeeper
