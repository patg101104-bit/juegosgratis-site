Blasting Marbles

Blasting Marbles is a thrilling 2D puzzle adventure game where your objective is to strategically place the required number of marbles into the hole. Explore the game world, collecting crates to boost your marble count and discover marbles with unique abilities. Switch between marbles to split their efforts and coordinate teamwork for successful challenges. Immerse yourself in a captivating gameplay experience that combines puzzle, physics, and skill.

¿Cómo jugar a Blasting Marbles?

Your goal is to get all the marbles in the level into the hole! The number in the top left of the screen is the amount of marbles that have to get into the hole. To make a marble move, left click near it to create a blast and watch it roll! There will be boxes in the level - these are extra marbles you can unlock! Have your marble collide into the box to obtain them. The amount of balls in the box are displayed on the box. When you have multiple marbles, you can press the arrow keys or A or D to switch between which marble you want to have in focus.
