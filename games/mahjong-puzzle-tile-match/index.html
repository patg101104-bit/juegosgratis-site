Mahjong Puzzle: Tile Match

Mahjong Puzzle: Tile Match is a soothing yet brain-teasing tile-matching game where you clear the board by linking identical tiles in smart rows and columns. You study every layout, spot hidden patterns, and plan each move ahead. As difficulty rises, you test your focus, relax your mind, and sharpen your puzzle-solving instincts.

¿Cómo jugar a Mahjong Puzzle: Tile Match?

Click on horizontally or vertically adjacent tiles to make a match. Match tiles in the same row or column, even if there is empty space between them. Move matching tiles to the same row or column. Clear the board to win!
