Evolution: Survival

A survival game where you begin as a tiny cell in the primordial soup. Collect DNA by consuming food and defeating enemies to trigger evolution and choose your mutation: speed, attack, defense, poison, regeneration, or intelligence. With each evolution, the world transforms — from open ocean to the deep sea, from shallow waters to dry land. Enemies grow stronger, elite opponents emerge, and fearsome bosses stand in your way. Combine mutations to unlock secret forms: Dragon, Leviathan, Phantom, Hive Mind, and more. Collect rare stars scattered across the map and invest them into permanent upgrades that carry over between runs. Use active abilities — dash, acid spit, death cloud, traps, and cover — to survive as long as possible. How many evolutions will you conquer?

¿Cómo jugar a Evolution: Survival?

Controls: - Mouse / finger — steer your creature's movement - WASD / arrow keys — alternative controls on PC - Ability buttons — displayed at the bottom of the screen (or press keys 1–5) - Spacebar — dash (if unlocked) Tips: - Don't rush strong enemies early on — build up your mutations first - Pick up enemy drops: healing, magnet, shield, explosion - Collect rare golden stars — they grant currency for permanent upgrades
