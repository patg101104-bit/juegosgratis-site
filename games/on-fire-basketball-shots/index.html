¿Cómo jugar a On Fire Basketball Shots?

Ignite the Hoops and Go On Fire! The crowd is roaring as you chain blazing basketball shots from hoop to sizzling hoop in this addictive arcade thriller. Pull back to launch perfect dunks, build unstoppable streaks, set the ball ablaze for massive multipliers, collect coins and stars, and climb the leaderboards with sky-high scores—every swish counts in this red-hot test of precision and timing! Main Objective and Winning Method: The primary goal is to score as many points as possible by chaining consecutive successful basketball shots into moving hoops without missing. Each dunk adds to your score (with perfect swishes earning bonuses), builds your streak, and unlocks 'On Fire' mode for double points and fiery visual effects. The game ends when you miss a shot—survive longer, chain more dunks, and rack up the highest score to top the global leaderboards and earn coins for upgrades! Desktop Controls (via emulator or browser): Mouse: Click and hold on the basketball, drag downward to aim and set power (watch the trajectory guide), release to shoot. Mobile Controls: Touch Screen: Tap and hold on the basketball hanging in the net, drag your finger downward to adjust aim and strength, then release to launch the shot toward the next hoop.
