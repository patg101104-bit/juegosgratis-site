ASMR Girl: Livestream Mukbang

ASMR Girl: Livestream Mukbang is a casual food game where you are the star of a mukbang stream. You get to enjoy delicious dishes, interact with your audience, and grow your popularity with every bite, all on camera! Each meal takes you closer to becoming even more popular, so keep eating, keep engaging, and watch your virtual foodie journey unfold. How to Play ASMR Girl: Livestream Mukbang Relaxing, fun, and super engaging, ASMR Girl: Livestream Mukbang lets you enjoy cooking, eating, and styling in a way that’s not only entertaining but also rewarding. Keep streaming, earning, and upgrading to become the most popular mukbang star! Become a livestream star with your channel, serving up drinks, dishes, and endless ASMR satisfaction. There are a few livestreams that you’ll participate in to get as many likes and hearts as possible from your fans. Curate Your Stream Start with a drink, pick your glass from thin-rimmed, stemmed, and rounded, and then add ice cubes in shapes from classic squares to cute bears. Garnish with fruits like lime, kiwi, or raspberry (some unlock as you earn rewards). Choose your syrup, give it a shake, add a straw, and get ready to sip your way into livestream fame. But beware — a boring drink could mean negative feedback! Dig In on Camera Once the drinks are done, move on to food. Eat tempura, sundaes, ramen, chicken, lobster, and even mix them in one stream for bigger rewards. The more fun and satisfying the experience, the more your audience grows. Once your food is ready, it’s time to go live! Eat, chat, and interact with your viewers as you enjoy the food. Your realistic ASMR sounds will make the experience feel so satisfying that your viewers won’t want to miss a moment. Engage with fans and watch your audience grow! Go to the Supermarket Start by shopping for ingredients, then move on to cooking mouth-watering dishes! From spicy noodles to delicious seafood and even sweet treats, the possibilities are endless. Prepare a meal that will make your audience hungry for more! For some of your livestreams, you get to shop for snacks and treats like sushi, dumplings, candy, and ice cream, but don’t get brain freeze! Pay with your card, stock up, and get ready to stream again. Cook, Stream, and Engage Shop for ingredients, cook delicious meals, then go live. Eat, chat, and interact while your realistic ASMR sounds keep viewers hooked. Successful streams bring in money. Spend it on stylish outfits for your character, or unlock even more mouth-watering food to keep your audience entertained. If you’re a livestream fan, this game can get quite addictive. The more successful your streams, the more money you make. Use your earnings to upgrade your girl’s outfits, giving her a stylish and trendy appearance. You can also buy even more delicious food to satisfy your growing audience and keep the mukbang going! Controls: Desktop: Right-click on buttons, move the mouse to rotate. Mobile: Tap on buttons, swipe your finger to rotate. Key Features: • Plenty of Food: Discover a wide variety of dishes to suit every taste! Try them all and showcase them to your audience! • Interactive: Visit restaurants for delicious treats or head to the supermarket to pick out your favorite dishes. Scan items at the checkout and pay with your card! And that's not all. You can even prepare some delicacies yourself! Skewer fruits and berries and coat them in caramel glaze. A perfect dessert for your mukbang! • Decorate Your Home: Choose furniture, flooring, walls, and other details for your home! Mukbangs need to be filmed in a cozy setting! Game Modes: • Dining: Head to a café and select delicacies for your mukbang! • Minimart: Go shopping at the supermarket! Pick something tasty and showcase it on your stream! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the goal of the game? A: You play as a mukbang streamer! Eat delicacies live on stream, gain new subscribers and likes, enjoy new tasty treats, and build your popularity with your audience! Q: What grabs attention in the first minutes? A: A plethora of delicious options! You’ll find a variety of tasty dishes here, from sweets and drinks to hot fish or meat dishes. Takoyaki, onigiri, caramel-coated berries, and much more—there will definitely be plenty to eat on your stream and show to your viewers! Mechanics & Progression Q: How many levels are there / is the structure clear? A: The game flows seamlessly. Days pass one after another, and with each new day, your subscribers will grow, and you'll earn more money! Q: What unlocks as you play? A: Unlock new foods and modes. Surprise your viewers with new dishes, and of course, try them out yourself! Economy & Customization Q: What can you buy/upgrade? A: Purchase new food at the store, but you can also visit restaurants to choose treats there! You can even make drinks and some desserts yourself! Q: Are there skins/customization options? You can also change not just your character's skin but also the table or walls in your room! Diversify your background! Q: What is available from the start? A: Right from the start, you will make drinks for your first mukbang! Pour a cocktail of your choice, add selected ingredients, mix well, and see what you’ve created! Retention & Engagement Q: Are there daily tasks / a wheel of fortune / achievements / tasks? A: Yes! Spin the wheel of fortune and win prizes! You won’t be disappointed. You’re sure to get something worthwhile! Q: Is sound necessary? A: You can play as you like. But with sound, you’ll immerse yourself better in the cozy atmosphere of the game! Q: What achievements are there and how can you earn them? A: Stream and post updates, collect clips from your mukbangs, and cherish the wonderful moments! Q: Is there onboarding in the game? What is it like? A: The game is intuitive from the first seconds. It will guide you through all the mechanics and show you how to play without unnecessary text.
