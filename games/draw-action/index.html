Draw Action

Draw lines and defeat enemies effortlessly! The game is best suited for those who want to let off steam and relax now. Various attack schemes are available to you, depending on which pattern you draw. The enemy is making a desperate attack on you. Can you win without taking damage?

¿Cómo jugar a Draw Action?

Goal: defeat all enemies on the level. To win, beat the enemy until his health is reduced to zero. Or you can push the enemy out of the location so that he falls into the water and dies. To make a hit, tap on the leg or arm of the blue character and draw a pattern. Draw lines and strike enemies wisely using tactics. Do not forget to improve the characteristics of your character. Over time, more and more deadly combo attacks will be available to you. But the enemies are getting stronger, more agile and their number is growing. Become a winner in the exciting game “Draw Action" right now!
