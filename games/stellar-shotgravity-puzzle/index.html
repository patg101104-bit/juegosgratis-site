Stellar Shot-Gravity Puzzle

Stellar Shot is a physics-based space puzzle where every shot bends to gravity. Drag to aim, release to launch, and use orbiting planets, heavy masses, repulsion fields, and wormholes to curve your path into the portal. Each level rewards precision with fewer shots and smarter trajectories. From gentle intro stages to challenging late-game puzzles, Stellar Shot is easy to pick up and satisfying to master. Plan ahead, read the gravity, and find the cleanest route through the cosmos.

¿Cómo jugar a Stellar Shot-Gravity Puzzle?

Stellar Shot is a physics-based space puzzle where every shot bends to gravity. Your objective is to launch the probe and guide it into the portal by using the gravity of nearby bodies. Drag to aim (direction and power), release to shoot, and plan your trajectory around orbiting planets, heavy masses with stronger pull, repulsion fields that push you away, and wormholes that teleport you across the level. Clear each level in as few shots as possible to earn more stars and unlock further missions. Desktop Controls: Mouse: Click and drag to aim, release to shoot. R: Retry the level. H: Hint. Esc: Pause/Menu. On-screen buttons: Menu, Mute, Retry, Hint. Mobile Controls: Touch: Drag to aim, release to shoot. Tap on-screen buttons: Menu, Mute, Retry, Hint.
