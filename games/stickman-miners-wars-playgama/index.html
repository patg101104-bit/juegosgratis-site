Stickman Miners Wars

Lead your stickman king to victory in Stickman Miners Wars, where strategic decision-making is key. Command an army of miners and warriors to build your forces and triumph over opponents. Upgrade your units and yourself as you gain experience, and strategically attack enemy castles while defending your own. Will your strategy lead you to dominance? Play Stickman Miners Wars now!

¿Cómo jugar a Stickman Miners Wars?

First, you need to choose the first region you want to reclaim on the global map and press the PLAY button. After this, you will enter the level and can play as the Stickman King. To move, use the keyboard keys (arrow keys or WASD) or a joystick or mouse. To start, go to the miner purchase zone and buy a few miners. They will begin mining gold for you, and you can mine gold alongside them. Once you have accumulated enough gold, go to the warrior purchase zone to build your army. To send your army into attack, click the sword icon on the right or press the E key. Your goal is to first destroy the opponent’s towers and then their castle. But be careful, as the opponent aims to do the same to your castle. Controls: WASD / arrow keys / drag left-click = move Left-click = interact with the in-game buttons
