Flufflings: Pocket Pets

Take care of stray kittens and create a cozy home and yard for them! 🧸 Buy toys and decorations: Make your space even more appealing to the cats. 🍲 Attract cats with food: The more food and toys, the more fluffy guests you'll get! 😊 Earn coins: The happier a cat is when it leaves, the more rewards it will leave behind. 🏠 Adopt kittens: Put a collar on a cat to make it stay forever. 🔍 Learn about the cats: Hold a cat to see its characteristics. 🍽️ Fill the bowls: Simply drop food or water into the bowl. 🎾 Play with the cats: Drag and release the ball to play. 🚪 Control the flow of visitors: Click the gate to stop new cats from visiting.

¿Cómo jugar a Flufflings: Pocket Pets?

Attract Stray Cats: Leave food outside to attract cats. More food and toys will bring more cats to your yard. Earn Coins: The happier the cats are when they leave, the more coins they will leave behind. Adopt Cats: Put a collar on a cat to adopt it. Adopted cats will never leave! Interact with the Cats: Hold a cat to see its information. Fill bowls with food or water by dropping items into them. Drag and release the ball to play with the cats.
