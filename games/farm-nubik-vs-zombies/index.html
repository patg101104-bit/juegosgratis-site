¿Cómo jugar a Farm: Nubik Vs Zombies?

Relaxing gameplay with a leaderboard. Get to the top of the murders. Your main goal is to escape and get out of here. Get resources and upgrade yourself and your base so that you can open new zones. Look for a portal with which you can get out of here, simultaneously destroying the zombie helpers! The bigger the zone, the more zombies there will be!
