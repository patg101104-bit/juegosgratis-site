Skebob Gun

Welcome to a frantic open-world shooter where you decide who to be: a hero, a villain, or just have fun! Fight with friends, run, jump, shoot, and explore huge maps full of secrets, weapons, and surprises. Game Features: - Online multiplayer: play against other players in real time - Wide selection of weapons: from blasters to rocket launchers - Open world: explore maps, drive cars, and cause chaos - Various modes: duels, survival, fun minigames - Lots of skins and customization: stand out from the crowd - Physics and tricks: jump, flip, and make the most of the environment

¿Cómo jugar a Skebob Gun?

Desktop controls: WASD - move; Space - jump; LMB - shoot; R - reload; G - grenade C - change camera Mobile controls: touch/swipe.
