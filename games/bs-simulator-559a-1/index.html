¿Cómo jugar a BS Simulator?

- Open Brawl Boxes to unlock Brawlers, earn coins and gems, and improve your progress! - Upgrade your Brawlers! - Complete quests and get tokens to complete the BRAWL PASS! - Earn trophies to advance your Trophy Road and win exclusive rewards! - Log into the game every day to get daily bonuses! - Take advantage of special offers in the shop, they are updated several times a week! - Customize your profile: player icon, name, name color (if you have a Brawl Pass, you can put a gradient color!) - Play mini-games, you can earn good rewards in them!
