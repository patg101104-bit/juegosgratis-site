Escape School Duel

Escape School Duel is a fast, funny, and tense escape challenge set inside a school that does not want to let you leave. Play solo to chase your best time, or go 2 player and turn every hallway into a race for freedom. One moment you are dodging traps, the next you are trying to slip past the principal in a long corridor or outsmart a teacher waiting near the classroom. Every section has its own danger, so learning the school layout is part of the strategy. [Core Gameplay & Story] Q: What is the game about? What do you have to do? A: It's a fast-paced school escape game. Outrun crazy teachers and coaches to escape the school as quickly as possible. Q: Is there a storyline? A: The game starts with the player in class when they suddenly get the idea to break out of school. Q: What are the game's unique features? What makes it stand out? A: High-quality graphics, unexpected traps, various skins, a two-player mode, and the ability to set new high scores. Q: What are the most engaging content elements? A: Various types of traps and well-designed locations. The mechanics are simple and the objectives are clear. [Mechanics & Progression] Q: Are there bosses, and how do they differ? A: Yes, bosses try to block your path. For example, a coach will try to stop you from running through the gym. Q: Are there mini-games within the main plot? A: Yes. Once you escape the school, there are mini-games available in the yard. [Economy & Customization] Q: What currencies are there and what can they be spent on? A: There are two currencies: tickets and money. You can use them to buy new skins. Q: Is there customization in the game? Can you change the character's appearance? A: Yes, you can purchase various skins and even a pet for your character. [Retention & Engagement] Q: Are there leaderboards? How do you join them? A: Yes, there is a time-based leaderboard. Try to escape school faster than anyone else\! Q: Should the game be played with sound? A: The music complements the atmosphere well, but it is not required to complete the game

¿Cómo jugar a Escape School Duel?

How to Play? Player 1 Move: W, A, S, D Jump: Space (Press twice for double jump) Run: Left Shift Player 2 Move: Arrow Keys Jump: L (Press twice for double jump) Run: K Your goal is simple: escape as fast as possible. Watch trap patterns, pick safer routes when the staff blocks the way, and use double jumps to skip danger or recover from mistakes. Grab tickets and money on the run, then upgrade your look and come back for a faster record.
