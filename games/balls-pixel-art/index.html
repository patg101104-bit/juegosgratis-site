Balls: Pixel Art

Balls: Pixel Art is a game about knocking out pictures with balloons. More precisely, you have to disassemble the drawn picture into pixels by breaking it up with balloons. This is a very interesting, exciting process. In which you need to show ingenuity, and accurate calculation. The game has to offer: - Exciting gameplay. - Multiple images for you to smash - Nice graphics - Lots of levels to complete Ready to be the Anti-Artist? Cracking the works of art? Then onward to new victories!Balls: Pixel Art

¿Cómo jugar a Balls: Pixel Art?

The goal of the game is to break the picture, to pass the level. To do this you have a cannon, which fires a charge of 16 balls. You have a total of 15 attempts to completely eliminate the painting. Good luck!
