Rocketcar Cup

Rocketcar Cup is a fast-paced 2D arcade soccer game where rocket-powered cars battle for victory. Boost, jump, flip, and master aerial moves to score incredible goals in exciting one-on-one or team matches. Use skill, timing, and strategy to outplay your opponents, defend your goal, and become the ultimate champion. Easy to learn, challenging to master, and packed with non-stop action for players of all ages.

¿Cómo jugar a Rocketcar Cup?

Use the virtual joystick to drive your car. Press the blue button to activate Boost. Press the yellow button to perform a Hard Hit. Use W, A, S, and D to drive your car. Press Shift to use Boost. Press Q to perform a Hard Hit. Use the Arrow Keys to drive
