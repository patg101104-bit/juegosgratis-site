School Of Basketball

It is the basketball arcade game in which you must try to score baskets in all the different game modes available. A basketball school opens its doors for enthusiasts like you. Do you know where to aim the ball so that you always hit the basket? Do you know any secret strategies? You have a limited number of balls and every time you miss, you also miss one ball. However, if you never miss, on the contrary, sometimes the ball will be added. How many points will you score?

¿Cómo jugar a School Of Basketball?

Touch and drug anywhere on screen to throw a ball! You can play either arcade, time attack, and distance mode. Arcade mode gives you 10 balls to use; you must continue to score baskets although it becomes harder each time you score. Time attack mode requires you to score as many baskets within the time limit as possible; you have unlimited balls but limited time. Distance mode allows you to increase the distance of your shot each time that you score. The distance decreases every time you miss a shot, don’t let the distance reach zero; otherwise, it is game over.
