Drive & Crash Sandbox

Realistic High-Speed Car Crash Simulator Launch vehicles down massive slopes and crash them into destructible obstacles! Experience thrilling high-speed collisions and realistic crash physics like never before. Crash cars. Break parts. Feel the impact. Choose from a variety of vehicles and watch as they crumble and break apart with ultra-realistic physics and damage simulation. ¿Cómo jugar a Drive & Crash Sandbox?

Controls: Tab – Pause WASD – Drive Space – Handbrake Shift – Nitro N – Next vehicle C – Change camera R – Repair car V – Slow motion On mobile devices, use the on-screen controls. Game Objective: Just drive around and smash cars however you like!
