99 Nights In The Forest - Survival Simulator

99 Nights In The Forest - Survival Simulator - Go on outings, get food and resources, fight monsters and find the missing children! What is in the game: 3 locations filled with both peaceful creatures and hostile ones! And also chests and resources! The ability to save children by destroying secret bosses! The skin merchant's store, where you can buy various upgrades! The ability to earn achievements. For each achievement, you will receive DIAMONDS.

¿Cómo jugar a 99 Nights In The Forest - Survival Simulator?

Click on "Hike" and select a location. Attack - click when the "triangle" is over the center of the attack bar. Defense - hold to stop the heart, release to move it in the other direction.
