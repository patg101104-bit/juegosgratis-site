Flows

Flows is a cozy logic puzzle about sorting colored liquids between flasks. Tap one flask, then another, to pour the top color — your goal is to leave every flask filled with a single color (or empty). ✨ Features: • 40 hand-tuned levels with a smooth difficulty curve • Per-level time leaderboards — race the world for the fastest solve • Optional hint system (rewarded ad) • Clean minimalist visuals, calming palette • Plays in 4 languages: English, Русский, Español, Português • No accounts, no timers, no pressure — pick up and play Perfect for short coffee breaks or long focused sessions.

¿Cómo jugar a Flows?

Tap a flask, then tap another flask to pour the top-most color into it. You can pour only when the destination flask is empty or its top color matches. The level is solved when every flask contains a single color or is empty. 🎯 Tips: • Plan ahead — once a color is buried under another, you must move the top one first • Use the Hint button (rewarded ad) if you get stuck • Each level has its own time leaderboard — finish fast to climb the ranks Controls: Mouse click on desktop, tap on mobile.
