Merge Fruit Monster Defense: Tower Defense

A bizarre infection has brought huge changes to the world. Ferocious little monsters are getting ready to attack. But Tao is never scared, because all kinds of magical fruit warriors are holding the line firmly! Your goal is to protect Tao and stop monsters from eating her! Game Features Creative mixed gameplay of backpack merging, roguelike and tower defense, featuring funny fruit characters and weird zombie monsters. Simple backpack merging mechanics. You can easily experience character development, inventory sorting, silver coin management and tactical troop arrangement. Fruit crossbreeding. After upgrading fruits to Tier 4 via merging, cross them with other creatures to gain stronger combat power and funny unique looks. Hybrid monster styles. Most in-game monsters are designed with diverse crossbred visual styles. Exclusive endless challenge mode. Start with plenty of silver coins to finish quick backpack merging. Countless monsters keep spawning nonstop for you to take on extreme battles.

¿Cómo jugar a Merge Fruit Monster Defense: Tower Defense?

Control your character by touch or mouse drag to move around the map. Avoid all chasing monsters and do not get caught. The game will end immediately if a monster touches you. Collect random buff items on the map to help you survive longer: Speed boost: temporarily increase your movement speed Freeze skill: stop all monsters for a short time The game difficulty increases over time. More monsters will spawn and move faster as your survival time grows.
