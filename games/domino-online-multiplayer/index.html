¿Cómo jugar a Domino Online Multiplayer?

Getting Started Choose your game mode: - play against the computer; - engage in online duels with real players; - play with a friend by sharing a link; - enjoy a «one-screen» mode for playing with someone nearby. Select your game settings: - choose a time control mode (Bullet, Blitz, Rapid, or Classic); - enable hints if you need help with your moves. Game Rules and Gameplay Setup: - each player is dealt 7 domino tiles at the start of the game; - the player with the lowest double (except for the blank tile) starts the game. Making Moves: - players take turns extending the domino chain on the table, matching the numbers on adjacent tiles; - if a player doesn’t have a tile that matches the open ends of the chain, they must draw tiles from the bazaar (remaining tiles) until they find a matching one. Winning a Round: - the first player to play all their tiles wins the round; - the losing player counts their penalty points, which are equal to the sum of the pips (dots) on their remaining tiles; - special tiles: the 0-0 tile is worth 25 points, and the 6-6 tile is worth 50 points. Ending a Round: - if no player can make a move and the bazaar is empty, the round ends; - the player with the fewest pips on their remaining tiles wins the round. Scoring and Ending the Game: - after each round, the next hand begins; - the game continues until one player reaches 100 penalty points, at which point they are considered the loser of the entire game. Enjoy the strategic challenge of Domino Online Multiplayer and see if you have what it takes to outwit your opponents!
