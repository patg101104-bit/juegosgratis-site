Cannon Balls

Try to be the master of destruction, heat up the buildings neatly and show no mercy! In 'Cannon Balls 3D' it depends on how skillfully you place the shots so that the structures collapse as efficiently as possible. Watch your ammo, because it's limited. But don't worry. There will also come a time when you will bring the cannon to the edge of functionality. Especially when the great bombs are used. So what are you waiting for?

¿Cómo jugar a Cannon Balls?

Aiming & Shooting: On desktop/browser: Move your mouse to aim the cannon and click the left button to fire. On mobile: Drag your finger (or stylus) to aim, and tap once to shoot. Weak Points and TNT: Target weak spots in the structure (like glass or foundation blocks) to maximize collapse. Hitting TNT (when present) triggers chain reactions to clear multiple blocks at once. Bomb Power-Ups: A bomb meter fills up as you shoot shapes and earn points. When full, tap the bomb icon to switch cannonballs to bombs temporarily. You’ll normally get only three bombs per level, so use them wisely on tougher structures or large clusters.
