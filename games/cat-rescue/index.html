Cat Rescue

The newest save cat game is here! Rescue the poor kitty from all the obstacles in his way. Draw a simple line to protect the cat from the evil bees that will try everything to sting him. Cat Rescue is the best way to train your brain. Draws a line, a circle, a star, anything you like, the sky is the limit. Each level will require a different solution. It takes the sharpest of minds to complete every challenge and protect the kitty and his nap.

¿Cómo jugar a Cat Rescue?

- Touch and drag to draw. - Protect the cat with your drawing. - Beware! The larger the drawing, the fewer stars you get. - Try to get 3-stars on every level.
