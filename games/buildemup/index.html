BuildemUp

BuildemUp is an entertaining and cool game where you should put various color and shape blocks on a cargo machine in order to deliver them and finish building beautiful houses. You will enjoy the challenge of keeping all these weird blocks on the truck. As you progress in game you will earn coins witch you can buy and unlock other cool looking trucks and activate power up to help you advance more easily in levels. BuildemUp is an ideal game for all ages, providing easy to difficult mind challenges and enhancing puzzle solving skills.

¿Cómo jugar a BuildemUp?

Drag the shapes and drop them onto the truck. You must stack them in a way that when the truck moves, the shapes don’t fall off!
