Merge number up

Number Click Merge is a simple yet highly addictive number puzzle game that challenges your logic and observation skills. Tap on groups of the same number to merge them into a higher number! The more blocks you merge in one move, the more points you earn. As numbers grow, the board becomes more strategic — plan carefully to keep the combos going!

¿Cómo jugar a Merge number up?

Tap on two or more connected tiles with the same number. They merge into one tile with a higher number. New tiles fall from the top to fill the gaps. Keep merging to reach higher numbers and beat your high score!
