Knife vs Fruits

Knife vs Fruits Just Shoot It is a casual game in which you will be playing as a knife-wielding maniac who is on a mission to destroy all the fruits, vegetables, and desserts in the kitchen. The knife-wielding maniac will be thrown to the targets that are located on the kitchen floor. The targets will be rotating and the goal is to hit and stab them all. You can also get cool knives, swords, and daggers from the shop. The goal of this game is to hit all the targets and become the greatest knife-throwing maniac. To do that, you will need to hit the coins surrounding the fruits. You will also be able to buy cool knives, swords, and daggers from the shop.

¿Cómo jugar a Knife vs Fruits?

- Just tap on the screen to fling the knifes. - The fruits can spin either way—clockwise or anticlockwise—and they keep changing speeds! - Every few levels, there’s a tough ‘crazy boss challenge’. Beat the boss and earn loads of coins!
