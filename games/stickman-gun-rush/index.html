Stickman Gun Rush

Build the ultimate stickman weapon and blast your way to victory in Stickman Gun Rush! Run through exciting tracks, collect stickmen, and assemble them into massive, powerful guns. Merge your growing weapon, avoid dangerous obstacles, collect cash, and upgrade your firepower as you race toward the finish line. Every level brings new challenges, stronger enemies, and bigger rewards. Can you build the biggest gun and defeat the final boss? Features 🔫 Build gigantic weapons from stickmen 🏃 Fast-paced runner gameplay 💰 Collect cash and unlock upgrades 🚀 Merge parts to create stronger guns 🎯 Face challenging enemies and bosses 🌆 Bright, colorful 3D environments 🎮 Easy controls, satisfying gameplay

¿Cómo jugar a Stickman Gun Rush?

Swipe left and right to move your character. Collect stickmen and gun parts to build a bigger weapon. Pick up cash bundles to earn more coins. Avoid obstacles that reduce your weapon's size or power. Reach the finish line with the strongest gun possible. Defeat the final enemy to complete the level and earn rewards. Upgrade your damage, fire rate, and income to become unstoppable.
