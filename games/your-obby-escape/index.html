Your Obby Escape

Join an exciting multiplayer escape adventure. Team up with friends worldwide to discover hidden secrets, messages, prizes, and hidden paths to freedom. Choose unique character skins and pets to accompany you. Avoid AI monsters and quirky NPCs that add humor and challenge to every escape. Simple controls let you interact with doors, levers, buttons, and keys to break free. Explore colorful locations packed with traps, puzzles, and checkpoints. Enhance your journey with double jumps, dashes, gravity shifts, and size changes. Find rare surprises and unlock powerful abilities. Enter magical portals to access new escape challenges. Chat, cooperate, and experience a dynamic escape universe.

¿Cómo jugar a Your Obby Escape?

The goal: get to the end. Control: WASD - movement. Space bar - jump. TAB - pause. E - Interact. Shift - cursor. Mouse wheel - zoom in/out. On the phone: Use the game interface.
