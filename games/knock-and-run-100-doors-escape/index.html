Knock and Run. 100 Doors Escape

Become the fastest and most daring stickman in this fun arcade game! Your goal is to race through the corridors of the hotel, ring the doors, knock and slam them, arranging epic pranks. The main thing is not to get caught! Residents' reactions are unpredictable, and danger can lurk around every corner. - Dynamic gameplay — run, prank and avoid capture! - Different doors and reactions — someone will get scared, someone will get angry! - Fun challenges — show agility and speed! - Bright minimalistic style is a classic stickman action game! - Simple controls — addictive gameplay in a couple of clicks!

¿Cómo jugar a Knock and Run. 100 Doors Escape?

Control your character through touch, cursor or WASD to move and always run! Run to the end of the level to win this game!
