Help Me: Tricky Story of Puzzles

Help Me: Tricky Story of Puzzles - a casual game with logical challenges, 2D graphics, intuitive controls, tough situations, and funny scenarios for all ages. Help the character pick up the right tools while avoiding all obstacles on the way. You will be surprised at how funny the game situation will unfold after each step of your solution. Can you find a solution and help the characters? Unleash your creativity and enjoy the game. Start training your brain right now! Game Feature: - Nice 2D graphics 🎨 - Simple and very addictive gameplay 🔥 - Lots of funny situations 🤡 - Unusual game idea 🎭

¿Cómo jugar a Help Me: Tricky Story of Puzzles?

- Click on the character and start dragging the hand; - Hold and drag over various obstacles to reach items; - Choose the right item that will help the character get out of a difficult situation.
