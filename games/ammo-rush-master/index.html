¿Cómo jugar a Ammo Rush Master?

? How to Play Run through the track and pass through gates (+, -, ×, ÷) to increase your firepower. Collect stickman allies and multiply your shooting strength. Avoid traps and tricky gates that can reduce your numbers. At the end, aim your Ammo blaster and fire away to break through walls and bosses!
