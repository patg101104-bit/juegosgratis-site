Cube Sort

CubeSort is a colorful 3D tap-and-sort puzzle. Each level is a floating sculpture built from patterned tiles — cubes, spheres, stairs, arches, and hundreds of surprising shapes. Rotate it freely, find the tiles that match the open buckets, and tap them away before your tray overflows. Simple to pick up, tricky to master: gray riveted tiles block your way, hidden "?" tiles keep their pattern secret until you dig next to them, and the order you clear tiles in decides whether the tray survives. ¿Cómo jugar a Cube Sort?

Drag empty space to rotate the cube. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Tap a tile on the surface — if it matches an open bucket, it flies in. No matching bucket? The tile waits in the tray. If the tray fills up, the level fails. Fill a bucket to complete it — the next bucket opens, and matching tray tiles fly in automatically. Gray riveted tiles can never be removed. Dig around them. "?" tiles reveal their pattern when you clear a tile right next to them. Stuck? Watch a short ad to open an extra bucket or add a tray slot. Complete every bucket to clear the level. 500 levels await!
