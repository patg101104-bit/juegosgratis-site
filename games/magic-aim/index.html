Magic Aim

Magic Aim is a real-time arcade artillery game starring a chibi magician. Read the shifting wind, charge your wand, and fire arcing elemental blasts that split in mid-air to clear waves of monsters across hilly terrain. Dash for a better angle, raise an energy shield to block incoming fire, grab item drops that supercharge your next shots, then warp to a fresh layout. Unlike classic turn-based artillery games, every shot is live and fast — mastery is reading arcs and wind on the fly. Four elements (Fire, Water, Thunder, Earth) each fly differently, so swapping changes how you read the same shot. Pixel-art, juicy, and easy to pick up for a quick run.

¿Cómo jugar a Magic Aim?

Survive escalating waves of monsters and score as high as you can. Clear every monster in a layout to warp to the next, tougher one — your run ends when you're defeated. Score rewards kills, speed, accuracy and style (splits and perfect blocks). Aim toward your cursor and watch the wind gauge — wind pushes your shot mid-flight and shifts every few seconds. Hold to charge for power, release to fire; shots can split at the apex into a fan for crowd control. Raise your shield just before an enemy shot lands for a perfect block (mana refund + speed burst). Walk to reposition and grab item drops that buff your shots. Controls (desktop): • Aim — Mouse • Charge & fire — hold / release Left Mouse • Energy shield — Right Mouse or Space • Move — A / D (or ← / →) • Dash-warp — double-tap ← or → • Swap element — 1–4 • Pause — Esc Mobile: virtual joystick + drag-to-aim + on-screen fire/shield buttons.
