Road Rage

Road Rage is an action-packed racing game where players speed through traffic, dodge obstacles, and compete against rivals in thrilling bike races. Featuring fast-paced gameplay, smooth controls, and exciting challenges, the game delivers an adrenaline-filled racing experience across multiple tracks and environments.

¿Cómo jugar a Road Rage?

Controls: A / Left Arrow – Steer Left D / Right Arrow – Steer Right Down Arrow – Brake Spacebar – Accelerate G – Attack / Fight
