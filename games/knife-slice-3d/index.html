Knife Slice 3D

Flip, slice, and conquer your way through exciting obstacle-filled levels in Knife Slice 3D! Master realistic knife physics as you jump between platforms, slice through stacks of objects, collect apples, and avoid deadly traps. Time every flip perfectly to reach the portal and complete increasingly challenging levels. Explore colorful low-poly worlds filled with forests, castles, and unique environments while unlocking powerful new knives. Easy one-tap controls make the game simple to pick up, but only the most skilled players can master every level and achieve perfect landings. Whether you're looking for a relaxing casual game or a fast-paced precision challenge, Knife Slice 3D delivers satisfying gameplay that's perfect for both desktop and mobile browsers. Features 🔪 Addictive one-tap knife flipping gameplay 🍎 Slice objects and collect apples ⚠️ Dodge spinning saws, spikes, and challenging obstacles 🌲 Beautiful low-poly 3D environments 🎯 Smooth physics-based controls 🏰 Unlock new knives and explore unique worlds 📱 Optimized for mobile, desktop, and browser 🆓 Free to play with no downloads required CREDITS & ATTRIBUTIONS: • 3D Models: "Low Poly Knife Collection" by BUttERflY_Bee (CC-BY 4.0 via Sketchfab) • UI & Icons: Flaticon, Mimu Studio, EmojiOne • Sound Effects: lloydevans09, gameaudio, mlaudio (via Freesound) & Pixabay Audio • Fonts & Code: Baloo, Liberation Sans, Kino Bloom, Preview Field, Cartoon FX

¿Cómo jugar a Knife Slice 3D?

How to Play 🔪 Tap or click to flip your knife to the next platform. 🎯 Land the knife upright to keep moving forward. 🪵 Slice through objects to clear your path and score points. 🍎 Collect apples to earn rewards and unlock new knives. ⚠️ Avoid spikes, spinning saws, moving traps, and other hazards. 🏁 Reach the finish platform to complete the level.
