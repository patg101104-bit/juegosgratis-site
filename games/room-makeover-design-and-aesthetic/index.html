Room Makeover: Design And Aesthetic

Welcome to the world of cozy interior design! Each level presents a new room filled with boxes of furniture and decor. Unpack everything and place each item where it belongs to transform an empty space into a stylish retreat. Choose from a variety of design styles: soft pastels, modern elegance, bohemian chic, or trendy aesthetics. Every room is a fresh challenge for your sense of taste and creativity. No timers, no pressure. Just a calm, meditative experience of bringing beauty and order to every corner. Mix colors, experiment with arrangements, and enjoy the satisfying results. Perfect for anyone who loves organizing, interior design, or simply wants to unwind with a feel-good game. Create your dream room now!

¿Cómo jugar a Room Makeover: Design And Aesthetic?

Your goal is to turn an empty room into a cozy, stylish space. Each level gives you a new room and a set of boxes filled with furniture and decor items. Drag and drop furniture and decorations onto the highlighted spots in the room. Every item has its correct place — find it! Once all items are placed correctly, the level is complete. As you progress, rooms become more complex with additional items and more elaborate design styles to work with. Tip: pay attention to the color palette and overall style of each room. This will help you quickly find the right spot for every piece.
