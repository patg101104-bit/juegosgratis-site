My Little Farm

- A casual arcade game with simple and intuitive controls; - Gather resources to build a farm; - Chop trees to get wood; - Chop rocks to get stone; - Dig a well to get water; - Breed animals to collect fertilizer for your plants; - Build a mill; - Harvest wheat and process it into flour; - Bake bread and hire helpers.

¿Cómo jugar a My Little Farm?

- Move using the on-screen joystick; - Approach a tree to cut it down; - Approach water to extract water; - Accumulate resources to construct a building.
