Yatzy

Yatzy is the ultimate classic dice game that brings endless fun and excitement! Experience the thrill of rolling five dice to score the best combinations and outsmart your opponents. Whether you're a casual player or a Yatzy master, this game is perfect for you! Similar to games like Yacht and Yahtzee, Yatzy combines elements of strategy and luck, making it both challenging and entertaining. Whether aiming for a 🎲 Full House, a 📏 Straight, or the elusive 🏆 Yatzy (five of a kind), each roll offers a new opportunity to outscore your opponents.

¿Cómo jugar a Yatzy?

Roll five dice to score the highest total over 13 rounds. Your Goal: Get the best possible combinations each round. Roll Up to 3 Times: Choose which dice to keep or re-roll. Pick a Category: After rolling, select a scoring category — each can be used only once. Scorecard: Upper Section: Count dice by number (Ones to Sixes). Lower Section: Use combos like Full House, Straights, or Yatzy (5 of a kind). Bonus: Score 63+ in the Upper Section for +50 points. Extra Yatzy? Get 100 bonus points! Highest total wins!
