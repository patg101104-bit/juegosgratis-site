Your Obby Labyrinth

Join an exciting multiplayer maze adventure! Team up with friends to explore tricky mazes full of secrets, funny messages, cool prizes, and hidden paths! Pick awesome character skins and cute pets to join you! Dodge silly AI monsters and funny NPCs that make your journey fun! Easy controls let you use doors, levers, buttons, and keys to escape! Each maze has colorful places, sneaky traps and puzzles. Jump into magical portals and discover new maze adventures! Chat, laugh, and escape in your-styled universe !

¿Cómo jugar a Your Obby Labyrinth?

The goal: get to the end. Control: WASD - movement. Space bar - jump. TAB - pause. E - Interact. Shift - cursor. Mouse wheel - zoom in/out. On the phone: Use the game interface.
