¿Cómo jugar a Card Sort: Challenge?

Your goal is to group matching cards and clear the entire board. Move cards into the right positions, open up new combinations, and always leave yourself enough free space to keep making moves. As levels become more complex, special obstacles such as fog, bombs, chains, and locks change how you approach each puzzle. Think several moves ahead and use your buffer space wisely so you do not trap important cards. If the layout becomes too difficult, use boosters like shuffle or undo to recover and keep progressing through the challenge.
