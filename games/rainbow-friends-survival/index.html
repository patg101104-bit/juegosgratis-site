Rainbow Friends. Survival

In this intense survival game you find yourself trapped in a haunted environment where you must endure five days and five nights against relentless monster encounters. Each night brings bloodthirsty creatures that stalk the shadows, turning every step into a heart?pounding horror challenge. To succeed you must complete mission objectives quickly, perform precise actions, and stay one step ahead of the terrifying foes. Master fast?paced gameplay by learning how to hide, escape, and use the environment to avoid detection while conserving your stamina. Follow proven strategies and tips for night survival to outsmart the monsters and emerge victorious.

¿Cómo jugar a Rainbow Friends. Survival?

Desktop Controls: ↑ (Up Arrow/W): Move upward. ↓ (Down Arrow/S): Move downward. ← (Left Arrow/A): Move left. → (Right Arrow/D): Move right. Mobile Controls: On-Screen Joystick: Move in all directions by dragging the joystick.
