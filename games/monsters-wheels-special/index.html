Monsters' Wheels Special

Monsters' Wheels Special is a 2D race game created by Smokoko. In this online game you race with your own monster truck in different stages like the Industrial, Forest, Arctic, Desert and City stage. Ugrade your monster truck so you can stay ahead of the competition and equip it with rockets and other weapons to really crush it. [Core Gameplay & Story] Q: What is the game about and what are the objectives? A: It is a racing platformer where you must navigate obstacles and cross the finish line first, mastering the vehicle's physics to succeed. Q: What is the game's unique hook and what makes it engaging? A: The game features an upgrade system, a cool soundtrack, monster trucks, and diverse environments like forests, cities, winter landscapes, industrial zones, and deserts. It also offers multiple modes, including traditional races and time trials. Q: What are the most engaging content elements? A: The highlight of the game is the collection of awesome monster trucks and several beautifully designed locations. [Mechanics & Progression] Q: How does the progression system work? What is the fastest way to level up? A: Players need to win levels to accumulate gold, which is spent on vehicle upgrades. You can improve your car's durability, nitro capacity, tires, and engine performance. Q: Is the total number of levels known? A: There are 45 levels in the game. [Economy & Customization] Q: What currencies are available and what can they be spent on? A: There is gold, which can be used to buy new cars or upgrade existing ones. [Retention & Engagement] Q: Are there daily rewards or similar retention mechanics? A: Yes, the game features daily rewards to encourage regular play. Q: Is it better to play with sound on, or are there difficulties playing without it? A: While not strictly necessary for gameplay, the music and sound effects greatly enhance the overall atmosphere.

¿Cómo jugar a Monsters' Wheels Special?

Desktop: Throttle - up arrow; Brake - down arrow; Lean backwards/forward - left/right arrow Mobile: Automatic acceleration; Tilt buttons to turn left/right; Nitro button - Nitro
