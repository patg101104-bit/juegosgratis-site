Archer Ragdoll

Get ready for the exciting adventure that awaits you: • Experience the incredible ragdoll archer physics that will leave you breathless; • Feel the realistic physics of arrows and armor - each arrow loudly ricochets and bounces, creating unpredictable situations; • Discover unique arrows with unique gameplay features that will add variety to your game; • A damage system awaits you, where each part of the body receives a different level of damage, making the game even more realistic; • Upgrade your hero to become a real master archer; • Fight interesting opponents and powerful bosses for a real challenge; • Enjoy beautiful and pleasant graphics, where explosions, sparks and splashes will envelop you like “Fire”; • Participate in the general ranking of players to show your skills; • Constantly level up with daily player ratings.

¿Cómo jugar a Archer Ragdoll?

Control the archer using the mouse or by touching the screen on the left highlighted part of the screen. Hold the mouse (finger) behind the archer's back to draw the bow (no need to pull it horizontally). Keeping the bow drawn, aim and release.
