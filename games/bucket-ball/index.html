Bucket Ball

Welcome to the Bucket Ball Game! 🎉 Tap the gate to open it and start the fun. Move obstacles out of the way or use them cleverly to guide the ball into the bucket. You'll get different items to help you complete each level. Remember, as you go higher, the game gets tougher, so think before you move! Each time you finish a level, you earn 1 point and move on to a more challenging one. How many levels can you play? Press on and challenge yourself! Just click your mouse to play. Enjoy the game! 🎮
