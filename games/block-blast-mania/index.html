Block Blast Mania

In Block Blast Mania , your mission is simple: try to place colorful blocks of various shapes on the game board in such a way that complete rows are formed. A fully filled row or column without any gaps will disappear from the board, making room for new blocks. The more rows you destroy at once, the more points you'll earn. There are two game modes. In the classic mode, the game will continue until there’s no space left on the board to place one of the three shapes. In the level-based mode, you need to complete objectives such as collecting pigs. Boosters help you get through the levels.

¿Cómo jugar a Block Blast Mania?

Drag the block from the bottom to the grid to place it. You can't rotate the blocks, but you can place them in any place or order that you want. Fill the colums or rows to destroy them and get points
