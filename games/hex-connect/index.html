HEX Connect

HEX Connect is a captivating hexagonal Mahjong Connect puzzle game where players strategically link pairs of identical colored tiles on a large hex grid, with connections limited to paths of no more than two bends, challenging spatial reasoning to clear the board entirely.

¿Cómo jugar a HEX Connect?

Master the Hex Grid! Dive into HEX Connect, the ultimate hexagonal Mahjong Connect puzzle, and strategically link pairs of identical colored tiles on a massive hex grid using paths with no more than two bends. Clear every tile from the board to win and conquer increasingly challenging levels! Desktop Controls: Mouse: Click on the first matching tile, then click on its pair to connect and remove them if a valid path exists. Mobile Controls: Touch: Tap on the first matching tile, then tap on its pair to connect and remove them if a valid path exists.
