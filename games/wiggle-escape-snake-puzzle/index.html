Wiggle Escape: Snake Puzzle

Wiggle Escape: Snake Puzzle is a smart snake puzzle game. Tap snakes in order, untangle shapes, solve hundreds of levels, or create your own in sandbox mode. Wiggle Escape: Snake Puzzle is a relaxing yet challenging logic game where you untangle tangled snakes shaped like fun emoji figures. At the start of each level, snakes form a complex structure, and your goal is to tap them in the correct order to slowly free and untangle the entire shape. The rules are simple, but the puzzles require careful thinking, attention, and planning. Only snakes that are not blocked can move, so every tap matters. As you progress, levels become more complex with longer snakes, tighter knots, and trickier formations. The main game mode offers a huge number of handcrafted levels designed to challenge both casual players and puzzle fans. A standout feature is the sandbox mode. Here you can freely choose shapes, experiment with mechanics, or even draw and design your own levels from scratch. Test your ideas, create unique puzzles, and enjoy endless replayability.

¿Cómo jugar a Wiggle Escape: Snake Puzzle?

At the start of each level, snakes are tangled into a single shape. Your goal is to untangle it completely. Tap snakes one by one — only snakes that are not blocked can move. Choosing the wrong order will stop your progress, so think ahead. In sandbox mode, you can select preset shapes or draw your own levels and try to solve them.
