Color Fall

Color Fall is a captivating puzzle game where you match colors with precision by sliding pins in various directions. As the difficulty increases, strategize to navigate levels while avoiding the black color. Playable on Android and desktops, it’s a free game that doesn’t require downloads or registrations.

¿Cómo jugar a Color Fall?

In the game, you need to open paths for liquids that match the color of the truck, and you should also avoid getting black liquid into colored liquid, otherwise it will evaporate. The game has 30 interesting levels, try to complete them all!
