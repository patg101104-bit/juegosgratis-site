Kitty Grid Puzzle

Welcome to Kitty Grid Puzzle, a relaxing logic game where adorable cats are waiting for their perfect space! Each kitty has a number that shows exactly how many grid squares its room must contain. Drag across the board to create correctly sized rectangles, give every cat its own cozy room and fill the entire grid without leaving empty spaces. The puzzles begin simple but gradually become more challenging, encouraging you to think carefully, plan each move and improve your spatial reasoning. Cute cat reactions, satisfying grid-filling gameplay and clever levels make every puzzle enjoyable to solve.

¿Cómo jugar a Kitty Grid Puzzle?

Look at the number shown beside each cat. The number tells you how many grid squares that cat’s room must cover. Touch and drag across the grid to create a rectangular room. Every rectangle must contain exactly one cat. The rectangle’s total number of squares must match the cat’s number. Rooms cannot overlap or extend outside the grid. Fill every square on the board to complete the puzzle.
