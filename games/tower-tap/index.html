Tower Tap

Where every tap brings you closer to victory! In this fun and addictive puzzle game, your mission is to clear layers of colorful cubes to reach the treasure chest at the bottom. The fewer taps you use, the bigger the reward! Each tower is made up of layers filled with cubes of different colors. When you tap a cube, all connected blocks of the same color pop and disappear. But be careful! You'll need to think ahead to plan your moves and clear the tower efficiently. Every tap counts!

¿Cómo jugar a Tower Tap?

Tap Same-Colored Cubes – Touch groups of cubes with the same color to break them. The more cubes in a group, the better. Clear Tower Layers – Keep popping cubes to go down each layer of the tower. Reach the Treasure Chest – Your goal is to open the chest at the bottom by clearing all the layers above it.
