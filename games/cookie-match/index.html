Cookie Match

Cookie Match is a sliding puzzle game where you must match each cookie to its corresponding cutter. If you move the cookies off the edge of the board or into knives, they’ll be destroyed. Use the tubs of butter as barriers to keep the cookies in one piece.

¿Cómo jugar a Cookie Match?

Mouse Click or Swipe. All cookies move in the same direction at the same time. The objective of the game is to complete all cookies in any order.
