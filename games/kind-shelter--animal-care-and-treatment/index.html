Kind Shelter - Animal Care and Treatment

A relaxing ASMR animal-care game where you treat and style adorable pets. As a caring vet and stylist, you’ll examine and heal stray cats and other furry friends, bathe and groom them, remove parasites, and perform delicate treatments. After care, dress them up with cute outfits, accessories, and hairstyles. Enjoy soothing sounds, cozy vibes, and the harmony of every touch!

¿Cómo jugar a Kind Shelter - Animal Care and Treatment?

First, choose the pet you want to save and transform. Use the mouse or simply tap the screen to select tools and perform actions. Follow instructions to care for the pet’s skin, eyes, ears, and claws. For cosmetic procedures, just tap the desired tool and drag it over the corresponding area of the pet. The game controls are intuitive, allowing you to easily switch between actions and choose the necessary tools.
