Microgic 89

MICROGIC 89 is a retro tile puzzle set inside a 1980s microchip. You play BYTE, a courier who has to collect every data chip on the board, unlock the socket they open, and reach the exit before the timer runs out. 22 hand-built levels, each one testing a different machine: sliding ice floors, force belts, teleport pads, cloning machines, trap doors, live bombs and patrolling bugs. Nothing on the board is decoration - if a gadget is there, you have to use it. Play it flat in the classic top-down view or flip to 3D at any time. A full level editor is built in, so you can draw your own maps and play the ones other players have published.

¿Cómo jugar a Microgic 89?

GOAL: collect every data chip on the board, step onto the socket to open it, then reach the exit before TIME reaches zero. CONTROLS - Desktop: arrow keys or WASD. You can also click and drag toward where you want to go. - Mobile: swipe or drag in the direction you want to move. Pinch to zoom. - P pauses, R restarts the level, and any key clears the level card. WATCH OUT - Water and fire are fatal unless you are carrying the matching boots. - Ice slides you until you hit a wall. Force floors push you along their arrows. - Push a block into water to build a bridge over it. - Coloured doors need the matching key. Bombs destroy whatever steps on them. - Monsters walk fixed patrol routes - wait for the gap, then cross.
