Fun Sorting Through The Shelves

Welcome to the cozy world of logic and order! In this relaxing and addictive puzzle game, you have to search for identical objects and carefully place them on the shelves. Arrange things so that three identical items are next to each other — they will combine into a triplet and make room for new finds. Collect objects three at a time, decorating your virtual corner and turning it into an ideal space for relaxing the soul. Cleaning has never been so much fun! This light and peaceful game is suitable for everyone — regardless of age, gender and experience. If you love order, beautiful collections and a pleasant atmosphere — this game is just for you!

¿Cómo jugar a Fun Sorting Through The Shelves?

Just drag and drop the items and place them neatly on the shelves. Collect three identical objects on the same shelf to create a triplet and make room for new items. Complete the levels, clearing all the shelves and revealing your strategic wit. With each new stage, the difficulty increases, and with it the pleasure of solving problems! Unlock dozens of unique items. The more levels you complete, the more cool rewards you'll get! Simple, intuitive and incredibly addictive gameplay awaits you. Are you ready to start cleaning up?
