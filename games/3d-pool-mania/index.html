3D Pool Mania

Pool Mania is an exciting single-player billiards game with smooth controls, realistic physics, and engaging gameplay. Test your skills across various challenges, master trick shots, and enjoy a relaxing yet competitive experience with immersive visuals and customizable options.

¿Cómo jugar a 3D Pool Mania?

Drag to aim the cue stick. Adjust the power meter to control shot strength. Release to strike the cue ball. Use angles and spin carefully to line up better shots.
