Fun fishing

Welcome to Fun Fishing — a fun and addictive fishing game about catching fish, upgrading your gear, and selling your catch! Catch rare fish, sell your haul, upgrade your fishing rods, and explore different locations. 🌊 What awaits you: 🎣 Simple and enjoyable fishing 🐟 Many types of fish — from common to rare 💵 Sell fish and earn money 🚤 Boats and new areas to explore ⬆️ Gear upgrades and progression 😄 Relaxing yet addictive atmosphere

¿Cómo jugar a Fun fishing?

PC: Use WASD to move, Space to jump, Left Mouse Button (LMB) to interact, and E to interact with objects. Mobile: Use the left side of the screen to move with the joystick, and the right side of the screen to rotate the camera
