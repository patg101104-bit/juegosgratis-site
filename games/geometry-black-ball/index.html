Geometry: Black Ball

Geometry: Black Ball is an exciting arcade game based on the legendary Geometry Dash. In this game, you have to control the ball and overcome various obstacles using the unique mechanics of gravity change. The game includes 10 levels of varying difficulty, each of which is an exciting challenge and requires the player to have quick reactions and precise movements. The further you advance, the more difficult the obstacles become, adding an additional challenge to the game. In addition, Geometry: Black Ball has a shop where the player can purchase various skins for his ball. You can choose from a variety of stylish and creative skins to give your ball a unique look and enhance the feel of the game. Get ready for an exciting adventure that will test your dexterity and reaction!

¿Cómo jugar a Geometry: Black Ball?

In the game "Geometry: Black Ball" you play as a ball from "Geometry Dash". To start the game, select one of the 10 levels that have different difficulty levels displayed by emojis on the level buttons. After selecting a level, the gameplay begins. During the game, you can change the gravity of the ball by pressing the space bar or right mouse button to overcome obstacles. This will help you change the direction of the ball and overcome obstacles on the way to the finish line. Your task is to get through all the obstacles and reach the finish line of each level. Be careful and react quickly to overcome the challenges you will face. Use your skills and strategies to successfully complete each level and advance further. Have fun in an exciting adventure in the game "Geometry Dash: Black Ball"!
