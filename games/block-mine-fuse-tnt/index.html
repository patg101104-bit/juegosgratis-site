Block Mine Fuse TNT

Step into this action-packed universe and unleash your full potential by upgrading and deploying TNT, rockets, and a myriad of explosive powers upon cubic settlements. ¿Cómo jugar a Block Mine Fuse TNT?

Block Mine Fuse TNT this is a story game set in a cubic world, a wonderful world captured by Herobrine. Help Nubik the fuse defeat Herobrine with TNT on a parachute and other arsenal. Pump up and drop TNT on Herobrine. Free the world from Herobrine and its creepy buildings. Upgrade and drop TNT, rockets and all your power on cubic settlements. In the game you will find: - 31 exciting unique levels; - - storyline with a cube and a Herobrine; - various types of TNT; - Unique Survival mode, create your bases and repel attacks as much as you can; - Desiccant, Ifrit, Gast, Vardan, Creeper and other bosses; - New game +, after completing the game (all pumping remains from the last game);
