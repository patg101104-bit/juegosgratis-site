Voxel Terraform

🌍 Voxel Terraform is an exciting sandbox game. Explore a cube-based world, mine resources, craft tools and armor, fight dangerous mobs, and grow stronger every day. Build your path to survival in an open voxel world! 💥 Game Features 🔨 Craft tools and armor 🧱 Build and destroy blocks 🗡️ Battle mobs and bosses 🌍 Explore the world and biomes ✨ Simple controls and engaging gameplay

¿Cómo jugar a Voxel Terraform?

🏁 Explore the world and gather resources 🔨 Craft tools and equipment 🔥 Fight enemies and survive 🗺️ Discover new territories 💻 Controls (PC) WASD — Move LMB — Destroy Block RMB — Interact ESC / TAB — Menu 📱 Controls (Mobile) Left Stick — Move Right Side of the Screen — View and Actions
