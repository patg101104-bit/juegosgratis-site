¿Cómo jugar a Merge Royal 2048?

Hit the Target Score to Level Up! Your main objective in this game is to rack up enough points to reach the required target score for each level. You do this by combining matching cards on the board. Drag and drop two identical items together to merge them into a single, higher-level card, which earns you points! Keep merging strategically to clear the board, hit your target score, and advance to the next challenging level. Desktop Controls: Left Mouse Button: Click and hold on a card or item. Mouse Movement: Drag the held item across the board. Drop (Release Click): Release the mouse button over an identical item to merge them. Mobile Controls: Touch & Hold: Tap and hold an item with your finger. Swipe/Drag: Drag your finger across the screen to move the item. Release: Lift your finger to drop the item onto a matching one to merge them.
