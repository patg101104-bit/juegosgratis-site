Trick Your Brain Puzzle

A fun drag-and-drop puzzle adventure where every choice matters! Solve tricky situations, uncover funny secrets, and help characters escape awkward moments by interacting with objects in the correct order before time runs out.

¿Cómo jugar a Trick Your Brain Puzzle?

Tap, drag, and release objects to interact with the scene. Look for clues, remove obstacles, and find the correct solution to complete each level. Think carefully—one wrong move can lead to a hilarious failure! Complete objectives before the timer ends to win.
