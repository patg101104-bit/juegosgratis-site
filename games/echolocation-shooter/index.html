Echolocation Shooter

Shoot to see in the dark! Eliminate all the neon enemies, in the horroristic level! The only way to see in this FPS is to generate soundwave with your weapon. Survive endless waves of the wide variety of neon monster enemies, where the only way to see is to shoot. Unleash soundwave rifles, laser cannons, and grenades to light up the battlefield, upgrade your gear, and fight deeper into the horroristic horde. Every shot reveals the world—every wave pushes your limits.

¿Cómo jugar a Echolocation Shooter?

Desktop: Movement: Arrows, A, W, S, D Jump: Space Dodge: Shift Interact with environment: Left-click Generate Soundwave: Left-click Activate Weapon: Right-click Shoot: Right-click Change Weapon: 1, 2, Mouse-scroll Grenade: 3 Menu: P Mobile: Buttons
