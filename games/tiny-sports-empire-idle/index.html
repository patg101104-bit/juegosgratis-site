Tiny Sports Empire Idle

Tiny Sports Empire Idle is a sports management idle game where you build a growing multi-sport franchise across basketball, hockey, soccer, rugby, volleyball, and baseball. Earn cash, upgrade your training plans, improve your win chance, unlock new sports, recruit players, equip gear, and boost your team power. Matches are simulated automatically, so every upgrade and strategic choice helps your club grow faster. Keep expanding your sports empire with idle income, offline rewards, special events, skills, and season progress as you turn a small hoops team into a full sports dynasty.

¿Cómo jugar a Tiny Sports Empire Idle?

Build and manage your sports empire by earning cash, upgrading your team, unlocking new sports, recruiting players, equipping gear, using skills, and claiming special rewards. Matches run automatically, and your goal is to keep improving your win chance, team power, income, and league progress until your franchise becomes a sports dynasty. Desktop Controls: Use the left mouse button to interact with the game UI. Click buttons to buy upgrades, unlock sports, equip gear, recruit players, use skills, claim rewards, open settings, and switch between menu tabs. Click the Sports button to view or switch sports and check league progress. Mobile Controls: Tap buttons to interact with the game UI. Tap upgrades, sports, gear, players, skills, and event cards to manage your team. Tap and scroll panels to browse menus, claim offline rewards, and continue growing your empire.
