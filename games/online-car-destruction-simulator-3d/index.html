Online Car Destruction Simulator 3D

This is a game that features racing, destruction, and car customization in an open world! Break cars, jump off ramps, and create your own style! Immerse yourself in an adrenaline-pumping world of destruction and speed! This is a game where you control cars that break down with stunning realism. Doors, hoods, and wheels can all fly off with a powerful impact! Explore a vast open world filled with ramps, presses, and other tools for epic destruction. Create unique cars by changing the color of the body, details, and adjusting the size and alignment of the wheels. Meet other cars speeding along the roads and compete with them in spectacular crashes. Crazy jumps, collisions, and complete freedom of action await you! Realistic car destruction Tuning: color, wheels, and style Huge open world Unique crash test sites Destroy, build, and race - create your own chaos on the road!

¿Cómo jugar a Online Car Destruction Simulator 3D?

Controls: WASD - steer the car Space - brake hard Shift - enable nitro C - switch camera N - change the car R - put the car back in service K - repair the car B - slow down time Tab - open the pause On mobile: use the buttons on the screen. Goal: drive, crash, and customize - do whatever you want!
