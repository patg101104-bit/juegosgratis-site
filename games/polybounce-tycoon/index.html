PolyBounce Tycoon

PolyBounce Tycoon is an idle + arcade style ball bounce game with physics! Spawn balls, bounce them around, earn virtual cash, and unlock new arenas. Features: Arena Shapes: Play inside circles, squares, triangles, hexagons, and more. Each level changes the arena. Balls: Spawn manually or let the auto spawner (auto clicker style) do the work. Virtual Earnings: Every bounce gives you in-game cash with popup text and sound effects. The amount grows dynamically ($1.5k, $2.5m, etc.). Upgrades: Spawn new balls. Increase virtual cash earned per bounce. Speed up the auto spawner. Upgrade balls with new sprites and levels. Next Level: Unlock new arena shapes, reset upgrades, and multiply your in-game profits. Idle mechanics, bounce physics, and tycoon upgrades combined. Build your ball idle empire in PolyBounce Tycoon! (Note: All cash in this game is virtual currency and has no real-world value.)

¿Cómo jugar a PolyBounce Tycoon?

* Tap anywhere on the screen to temporarily boost the ball speed for free. * At the bottom, you’ll see 3 upgrade buttons: 1. **Add Ball** – Spawns an extra ball to increase your total earnings. 2. **Incremental Payout** – Increases the money you earn each time a ball bounces off the wall. 3. **Passive Income** – Raises the passive income you earn every second, even when idle. * In the **Shop**, you can unlock new ball skins by watching ads.
