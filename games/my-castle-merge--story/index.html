My Castle. Merge & Story

My Castle: Merge & Story is a relaxing merge 2 puzzle game set in a warm and atmospheric medieval world of renewal, mystery, and self-discovery. Merge items, restore and decorate an ancient castle, and follow the journey of a young princess who chooses a new path in life. This calm offline merge adventure combines cozy gameplay, castle restoration, and a story about starting over — far from royal expectations, closer to one’s true self. Restore and Decorate a Forgotten Castle The story begins when a young princess leaves her father’s royal court and travels to a remote medieval castle once owned by her grandfather. The reasons behind her escape remain a mystery — and uncovering it is part of the journey. The castle has stood empty for years. Time has worn it down, but its spirit remains. ¿Cómo jugar a My Castle. Merge & Story?

At its core, My Castle: Merge & Story offers intuitive and satisfying merge 2 mechanics designed for relaxation: Merge identical items to create upgraded tools and resources Discover rare medieval objects and hidden item chains Unlock new crafting possibilities as the castle grows Play at your own pace with no pressure or timers This is a cozy merge game made for calm moments and thoughtful progression.
