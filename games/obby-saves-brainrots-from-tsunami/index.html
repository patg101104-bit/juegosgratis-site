Obby Saves Brainrots from Tsunami

Welcome to Obby Saves Brainrots from Tsunami! A fast-paced escape game that blends tsunami runner, idle game, and idle tycoon mechanics into one addictive experience. Run, jump, and survive while escaping giant waves, collecting brainrots, and building your Brainrot empire. This is more than just a runner. Escape Brainrots Tsunami Obby is a true survival runner with long-term progression, idle systems, and satisfying upgrades. Escape the Tsunami A massive tsunami is coming and you must escape before it reaches you. Every second matters. 🌊 Classic tsunami obby and tsunami runner gameplay 🏃 Run fast, jump obstacles, and survive dangerous zones ⚡ Reflex-based escape game mechanics Collect Brainrots and Progress Brainrots are the main resource in this game. The more you collect, the faster your empire grows. 🧠 Collect brainrots during runs 💸 Turn brainrots into money 📈 Grow into a powerful idle tycoon

¿Cómo jugar a Obby Saves Brainrots from Tsunami?

Collect brainrots, craft them, and get the rarest brainrots by building an empire! Computer controls: WASD - to move E - interact with objects G - throw brain rot F - turn on cursor Use the UI to control mobile devices
