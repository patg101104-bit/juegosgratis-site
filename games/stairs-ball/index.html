STAIR BALL

Climb an endless staircase full of danger in Stairs Ball, a fast and minimal arcade game. You control a small ball running up colorful steps. The path looks simple, but each stair is covered with deadly spikes and tricky gaps. One wrong move and your run is over. Collect shiny rubies on your way to unlock new balls and styles. Chain “perfect” moves to build huge combos and smash your high score. The higher you climb, the faster and harder it gets it’s all about timing, reflexes, and focus.

¿Cómo jugar a STAIR BALL?

Touch and hold the screen to move the ball left or right across the stairs. Release and press again to quickly switch direction. Your goal is to keep climbing without hitting any spikes. Each stair can have black cone spikes. If the ball touches a spike, the run ends instantly. Read the pattern of spikes and move early, not at the last second.
