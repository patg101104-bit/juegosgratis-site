Candy for capybara

Capybara Wants a Candy! Match identical marmalades to create bigger candies and earn coins. Use the crane to grab delicious treats for your capybara! Game Features: 36 levels with increasing difficulty – from simple puzzles to challenging candy combinations. Endless high-score mode – compete with friends for the best candy-matching skills. Jelly physics – watch candies bounce, stick, and deform as they collide. Adorable pet – the capybara reacts to your success (happy when fed, sad when you miss).

¿Cómo jugar a Candy for capybara?

How to Play: Marmalade Candy Control: 📱 Mobile: Touch & drag to move candies – release to drop them. 🖥️ Desktop: Mouse: Click & drag, release to throw. Keyboard: Arrow keys (← → ↑ ↓) + Space to drop. Crane Control: 📱 Mobile: Slide your finger to move the crane – release to grab candy. 🖥️ Desktop: Mouse: Move cursor + click to grab. Keyboard: Arrow keys (← →) + Space to capture.
