Hex Mine 3D

Hex Mine 3D is a relaxing yet strategic puzzle game where every move matters. Explore beautiful spherical hex worlds, clear the correct tiles, and solve carefully designed levels with logic, focus, and precision. Each level presents a unique hex-tile pattern wrapped around a 3D globe. Your goal is simple: analyze the board, make smart choices, and complete the puzzle without mistakes. As levels progress, the layouts become more challenging with larger patterns, trickier tile placement, and deeper strategy. Rotate, inspect, and solve each hex puzzle at your own pace. Easy to understand, satisfying to play, and challenging to master. Features Unique 3D hex puzzle gameplay Beautiful globe-style puzzle boards Carefully designed level-based progression Simple controls and clean visual style Increasing difficulty across many handcrafted levels Relaxing but brain-teasing puzzle experience Perfect for short play sessions or focused puzzle solving
