Dead Lands Driver Open World

The world has fallen. Civilization has collapsed. Only empty roads, abandoned military bases, and endless hordes of zombies remain. Now you are one of the survivors. It no longer matters who you were or where you came from — you must survive in a brutal post-apocalyptic world. Explore a vast open world filled with deserted cities, military facilities, dark forests, and dangerous places better left unexplored. Choose your vehicle, find weapons, gather resources, and fight to stay alive. The world will never be the same again. Now you are part of the zombie apocalypse.

¿Cómo jugar a Dead Lands Driver Open World?

There are markers in the remnants of the world. They represent missions (delivery/zombie cleanup), as well as other interactions (leaving/entering car, entering garage, accessing shop etc.) Simply enter them (inside car) and/or press interaction key (outside car) to start mission or do something else. You'll see further instructions in-game. Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move Left mouse button to attack/shoot (outside car) Drag mouse while holding LMB to rotate camera (inside car), or without holding (outside car) E to interact (while being inside marker) U to use item in middle slot (outside car) I to open/close invertory (outside car) ESC to pause/unpause game
