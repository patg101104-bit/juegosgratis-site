Sworded io - Spin and Rub

Sworded io - Spin and Rub is a fast-paced arcade sword-fighting game where you’ll test your reflexes, tactics, and timing against waves of fierce opponents. Upgrade your fighter, unleash spells, and conquer battle arenas through pure skill and clever strategy. Features Massive weapon variety: swords, axes, knives, and more Impactful power-ups to shift the tide of combat Two thrilling modes, Domination and Battle Royal Spin-powered action with dynamic, fast-paced gameplay Compete and rise as the ultimate warrior

¿Cómo jugar a Sworded io - Spin and Rub?

Controls PC Controls Use WASD / arrow keys / drag left-click to move around. Mobile Controls Use the in-game joystick to move around.
