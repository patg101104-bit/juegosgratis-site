Plants vs Zombies Fusion Nightmare

Welcome to the ruins of the suburban zone, where the old rules no longer apply. You are not a gardener. You are the Chaos Dispatcher. The zombies have mutated, now moving stealthily, attacking from the sky, and hacking into your turrets. The only way to survive is to create hybrid mutants from the scraps of plant DNA. Chimeras that carry the fury of the Pea Farmer and the coldness of the Recidivist Cactus. Forget the "plant and water" tactic. Here, every hybrid is a crime against nature, and it's worth it.

¿Cómo jugar a Plants vs Zombies Fusion Nightmare?

Put hybrids and other plants on the field to protect yourself from zombies. See how much they cost and how fast they recharge. Zombies are getting cooler, so change tactics, combine plants and come up with new strategies!
