Cooking City

If you have always wanted to become a top chef in a crazy culinary world, it’s time to dive into the cooking fever! 🔥🍳 Head back to the kitchen and prepare delicious dishes for hungry customers 🍔🌭🍟 Show off your impressive cooking skills and use smart time management to overcome challenging tasks ⏳💪 Immerse yourself in an atmosphere of excitement and fun: cook in different kitchens, cafes, and restaurants, serve visitors, and master new recipes 🍲🏪 A wide variety of challenging levels and exciting tasks are waiting for you! 🎯 Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do players need to do? A: It's a cooking and restaurant simulator. Players need to prepare and serve guests as quickly as possible. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? What can hook players? A: Various dishes, the ability to upgrade the kitchen, and unlocking new establishments. Q: Are there any currencies and what can they be spent on (regular - gold, premium - crystals, etc.)? A: The game has 2 currencies: coins and diamonds. Both currencies can be used to upgrade the kitchen and purchase enhancements. Q: Are there in-game purchases? What can be bought and what does it affect? Does making a donation speed up game progress? A: Yes, there are in-game purchases. Players can buy diamonds or booster packs. Donations speed up game progress. Q: Are there engaging content elements (weapons, cars, locations, skills, references to famous franchises, etc.)? A: The game features a simple yet captivating gameplay mechanic. Q: Is it preferable to play with sound to immerse oneself better in the atmosphere, or will there be difficulties in playing without sound? A: The music complements the game, but it is not necessary for gameplay.

¿Cómo jugar a Cooking City?

Simple and addictive gameplay 🎮 -Easy to start, but hard to master 😉 Help the charming waitress cook and serve guests. -Keep an eye on the clock and make sure no dish gets burned! 🔔🔥 Amazing restaurants and diverse dishes 🍽️ -Enjoy a fun culinary journey packed with delicious recipes and new venues to unlock 🏬✨ -Cook hundreds of tasty meals including burgers, sausages, hot dogs, soups, french fries, and more 🤤 -Serve a variety of customers, from caring moms to impatient kids 👩‍👦 -Skill development and brain training 🧠 -Memorize new recipes and improve your memory 📖
