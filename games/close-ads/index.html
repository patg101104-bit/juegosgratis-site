Close Ads

A fast chaos arcade game where your only goal is simple: close ads. But every few levels, the ads become more annoying.

¿Cómo jugar a Close Ads?

PC Left Mouse Button — click close buttons Mouse Move — aim and chase ads Double Left Click — required on some levels UI Button: Restart From Level 1 — restart progress Mobile / Tablet Tap — press close buttons Double Tap — required on some levels Drag finger / move finger — follow moving ads visually UI Button: Restart From Level 1 — restart progress
