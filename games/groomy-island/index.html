Groomy Island

Groomy Island - is a brand new realistic horror-survival game in 3D Island that Your sole objective is Survive and collect all the 10 Grimace Shake. - There are many enjoyable places to explore on a large island. - Make sure to keep an eye on a MiniMap to navigate around. Core: A compelling first-person survival horror experience centered on high-stakes stealth and object retrieval. Loop: Players engage in a tense evasion-and-search mechanic, navigating environments to find hidden collectibles while actively avoiding an immediate predatory threat. Start: Instant player engagement is ensured through an immediate and intuitive 'hook,' providing compelling interest from the very first minute of gameplay. Playgama Fact: The game is fully integrated with Playgama SDK. HTML5. Stable performance regardless of internet quality. Visual: Features a highly immersive, Realistic visual design to enhance environmental dread and player connection to the experience. Key Feature: A unique, light-touch horror approach that distinguishes it from traditional genre titles, offering a novel blend of tension and accessibility for a broader audience.
