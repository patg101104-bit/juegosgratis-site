Mr.Tung Shoot Zombie

Help Mr. Tung and ensure he shoots all the zombies with the most precise shot. You must be very careful. Ballerina Cappuccino can be eaten by zombies at any time. Ballerinakapicuno is currently in prison, and to save him, you must shoot and destroy all the zombies with your arrows. Mr. Tung is the best marksman and will destroy all the zombies. Destroy all the zombies and save Ballerina Cappuccino.

¿Cómo jugar a Mr.Tung Shoot Zombie?

Playable on both mobile and PC. To shoot an arrow, click on the direction or the zombie you want to hit with your mouse. On mobile, tap the direction or the zombie you want to shoot. Touch controls are available on mobile.
