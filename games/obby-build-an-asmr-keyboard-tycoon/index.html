Obby: Build an ASMR Keyboard Tycoon

Obby: Build an ASMR Keyboard Tycoon is a fun Roblox-style simulator where you build your own ASMR keyboard and turn it into a massive money-making machine. Every key press earns cash, and your goal is to create the most profitable and satisfying keyboard possible! Build, Upgrade, Earn! Upgrade your typing speed, increase the money earned from every key press, and unlock new keyboard colors, designs, and effects. Hire workers to generate income automatically, even while you're busy exploring. Compete against other players on the global leaderboard and become the richest ASMR keyboard owner. The better your upgrades, the faster your income grows—and the closer you get to the top! Features 🎹 Build and customize your ASMR keyboard 💰 Upgrade typing speed and earnings 👷 Hire workers for automatic income 🏆 Compete on the global leaderboard ✨ Relaxing ASMR atmosphere with satisfying visuals 🚀 Become the ultimate keyboard tycoon!

🖥️ PC 🚶 WASD / Arrow Keys — Move 🦘 Space — Jump 🖱️ Hold Right Mouse Button — Rotate the camera 📱 Mobile 🕹️ Virtual Joystick — Move 👆 Swipe — Rotate the camera
