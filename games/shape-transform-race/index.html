Shape Transform Race

Shape Transform Race is a fun racing game that can be played in reaction-based 1 and 2 player game modes. You can play the game against bots or against your friend. Always be the fastest by choosing the right vehicle for the terrain! You can unlock new vehicles and characters with the coins you earn from the races. Shape Transform Race Features: Shape Transformation: Transform your character according to the terrain and proceed without slowing down. Various Tracks: Each with different difficulties, fun and full of surprises. Fast and Dynamic Gameplay: Determine your strategy, overcome obstacles with the right transformations. Fun Competition: Race against your friends or artificial intelligence!

¿Cómo jugar a Shape Transform Race?

You can use "MOUSE" or touch controls to change vehicles. For 2 player mode, you can use "A,S,D" and "J,K,L" keys for shortcuts to change vehicles. How to Play? Your character can transform into different shapes to overcome obstacles. You must change into the appropriate form according to the type of obstacle: Boat for water, car for road, airplane to move in the air, etc. Make the transformations in time to maintain your speed and leave your opponents behind! The first one to reach the finish line wins!
