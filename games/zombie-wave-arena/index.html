Zombie Wave Arena

Zombie Wave Arena is a fast top-down arcade survival shooter. Blast endless zombie hordes, earn coins, and upgrade six guns from pistol to legendary Ray Gun. Face nine unique zombie mutations, ten escalating bosses, and five rotating arenas. Grab pickups, hurl grenades, and survive on desktop or one thumb mobile controls.

¿Cómo jugar a Zombie Wave Arena?

Objective: Survive endless waves of zombies in the arena. Kill zombies to earn coins, then spend them between waves on weapon upgrades, new guns, armor, and grenades. Every 5th wave summons a powerful boss with double the HP of the last — defeat it to unlock new zombie threats and bigger rewards. The arena changes every 10 waves. There is no final level: you "win" by beating your best wave and high score — reach Wave 50 to conquer the Death King and every arena. Desktop controls: WASD or Arrow Keys — move · Mouse — aim · Left Click — shoot · Right Click — throw grenade · G — switch grenade · R — reload · 1–6 or Q — switch weapons · P / Esc — pause. Mobile controls: Left thumb — virtual joystick to move · FIRE button — shoot with auto-aim · on-screen buttons for reload, weapon swap, and grenades.
