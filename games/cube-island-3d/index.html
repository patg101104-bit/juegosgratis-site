Cube Island 3D

Welcome to the exciting world of survival and adventure! In the role of brave Steve, you have to start from scratch on a mysterious and uninhabited island. Your goal is to turn this corner of nature into a real paradise for life. Game Features: - Survival and Adventure. - Tasks and Achievements. - Construction and Development. - Resources and Crafting. - Discovery of New Islands. An exciting journey from survival to prosperity, where your creativity and strategic decisions will be the key to success. Get ready for the adventure of your life on an island of your own making!

¿Cómo jugar a Cube Island 3D?

The goal of the game is to turn a desert island into a prosperous paradise by completing tasks, constructing buildings, collecting resources and discovering new territories. The player develops the infrastructure, explores the secrets of the island and achieves a high level of survival and prosperity. Controls - Swipe/Left mouse button
