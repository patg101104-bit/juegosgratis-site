Crash Test Simulator 3d

Car Crash Test Simulator 3D is a realistic simulator of car steering and collisions. Here you can test the strength of your cars! The cars are unique, and they can all lose different details depending on the obstacles and elements you drive through. The loss of car parts is very real, so take advantage of this and check their durability. Drive on the roads at high speed and overcome potholes if you can, jump over extreme ramps and cause maximum damage. Use the truck to crush other cars. Enjoy extreme resolutions on the mountain map where you will drop your car off the mountain and smash it into a thousand pieces while watching the cars collide. Drive through the crash test map and smash the car with the help of map items. If you hit hard enough, you can make the car parts fall down, the game uses realistic enough destruction physics for fun. Perform various crash tests on the same level with different cars and thus destroy them in various ways, you can also smash cars with those shown on the maps and thereby smash cars. Features: - Unique time dilation system - Exciting gameplay - Drawn realistic 3D graphics - Realistic car deformation physics - Realistic car driving - Detailed car damage system - Cameras with a view from the first, second, third and fourth person - 7 maps with lots of cars to choose from. - Different colors of cars - Impressive ways to destroy machines Recommendations: - At a higher speed, there is more destruction of cars. - Try different ways to demolish and smash the car on the same level for your enjoyment. This game is not for those who want to practice car parking (parking cars), because on frenzied descents from ski jumps, cars can reach huge speeds and stop being controlled. A regular traffic racer will not cope with this, you need the experience of a bemidji drive lover to withstand the difficulties of a frenzied crash test. The game's maps include a variety of crash test scenarios, drift, free riding - maps with ski jumps, dotted with pits that can fit a truck, huge jumps over the abyss with spikes, narrow tunnels that you need to get into, rushing down the mountain at high speed, barrels of fuel that can explode and destroy your car, hills and slides on which you can leave not only one suspension of the car, but all 4 wheels at once during the race! The obstacles and elements for smashing cars that you will find on the map are very diverse: very long ramps where you can make incredible jumps, each of which ends in a different way, perform impressive flips, expose cars to the pressure of hammers or crushers, dodge or crash against rotating balls and sticks, or skip your car through various crushers that you will find, each of which is more dangerous than the previous one. The game also has a unique time dilation system, thanks to which you can crash your cars epically. In addition, this system allows you to observe the destruction more closely, and thanks to its drift and jumps, it becomes even more epic!
