Warrior Bee Apocalypse

Warrior Bee Apocalypse is an action and upgrade game. The warrior bee faces hordes of enemy insects to survive. Collecting diamonds to improve your items and skills.

¿Cómo jugar a Warrior Bee Apocalypse?

The main objective of the warrior bee is to eliminate hordes of enemy insects to complete the levels and collect gems to upgrade the bee. Desktop Controls: Use the WASD keys or the ARROW KEYS to move the bee. Approach enemies and she attacks automatically. 1, 2 or 3 keys to use the special items. Mobile Controls: On mobile devices tap and drag of the screen to move the bee. It will automatically attack when it is close to the enemy. To use special items, tap on them in the lower right corner.
