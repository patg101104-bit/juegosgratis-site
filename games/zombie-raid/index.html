Zombie Raid

Zombie Raid is a fast-paced survival action game where you must fight off hordes of zombies, complete dangerous missions, and upgrade your gear. The city has been overrun by monsters, and only you can clear the streets and stop the growing threat. Unlock new characters, buy and upgrade weapons, earn resources, and find the best playstyle for each challenge. You will have access to a wide range of equipment, from bats and crossbows to lightsabers and blasters. Each new mission becomes harder, and the enemies become more dangerous. Survive, upgrade your loadout, and prove that you can stop the zombie onslaught. Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the game about? What do you need to do? A: The game is about survival, where you need to fend off waves of zombies. Q: Is there a storyline in the game? A: There is no specific storyline, but the goal is clear - fend off zombies, complete dangerous missions, and upgrade your gear. Q: What is the unique feature of the game? What can hook players in? A: The game has two currencies: gold and crystals. Q: How does leveling up work exactly? What needs to be done to level up faster? A: To level up faster, players need to complete missions, upgrade weapons, enhance their character, increase their level, and watch ads to earn more gold. Q: What are some engaging content elements (weapons, vehicles, locations, skills, references to famous franchises, etc.)? ¿Cómo jugar a Zombie Raid?

Complete different types of missions: survival, cleanup, running, clown hunting, and other challenges. Destroy zombies, unlock and upgrade weapons, and earn gold and crystals. Buy new equipment, unlock characters, and improve your chances of survival. Choose the right weapon for each mission and do not let the enemies surround you. PC controls: Move left and right using the A and D keys respectively.
