Cup Quest

Cup Quest – Hero’s Journey: A Feline Hero’s Adventure Begins! Join a brave cat on an epic quest in Cup Quest – Hero’s Journey, a fresh twist on the classic balls-and-cups game. This isn't just about tossing balls into cups—it's a full-blown adventure packed with clever puzzles, physics-based challenges, and one courageous kitty determined to save the day. Your Cat, the Hero Meet your furry champion—a quick-thinking, nimble cat with a heart of gold. As danger looms and traps lie in wait, it’s up to this whiskered warrior to take on wild levels filled with spinning platforms, explosives, and tricky obstacles. Precision Meets Puzzle Each level is a test of aim and timing. Launch the ball just right, bounce it off the environment, and land it perfectly in the cup. It’s not just about power—success requires strategy, finesse, and a little creative thinking. What Awaits You - Over 200 unique levels, ranging from simple to seriously tricky - Skill-based gameplay that mixes timing, puzzle-solving, and action - Hero upgrades: unlock new feline champions and grow stronger as you progress - Satisfying animations and smooth physics that make every throw feel great - Enemies and traps: it's not just puzzles—you’ll have to outsmart swarm enemies too Become a Cup Champion Explore a magical world where every level brings new challenges. Battle your way through enemy swarms, master the art of the perfect shot, and rise as the legendary hero of the cup realm. Cup Quest – Hero’s Journey isn’t just a puzzle game—it’s a heartwarming, skill-testing adventure that’ll keep you coming back for more. Grab your paws, line up your shot, and get ready to become a legend.

¿Cómo jugar a Cup Quest?

Use the mouse to guide the balls dropping from the cup, and mouse is also used for interacting with UI elements (buttons, powerups, etc.)
