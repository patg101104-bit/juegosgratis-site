Wild Love

An adventure awaits you on a mysterious island where the heroine is in danger at every turn. Make the right decisions, try to guess who is a friend and who is an enemy. Unravel the mystery of the island! Your heart will tell you! Match your heroine's looks to your liking and go adventurous!

¿Cómo jugar a Wild Love?

It's easy to play - flip through the novel, immersing yourself in the amazing story and empathizing with the heroine. Choose what the heroine will say and do by pressing the buttons at times when the game offers choices.
