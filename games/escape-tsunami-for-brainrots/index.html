Escape Tsunami for Brainrots

Run fast and collect Brainrots! 🏃 Run from the tsunamis when they appear! 🌊 Survive and collect as many Brainrots as possible! ⚡ Upgrade your Brainrots! 💸 Upgrade your base! 🏗️ Controls: Desktop — WASD: move, left and right mouse buttons — control the camera, E — interact, Space — jump. Mobile — virtual stick — move, jump button — jump, tap for action on buttons. Key Features: • Steal brainrots!: For diamonds, you can steal brainrots from other players! • Buy VIP shelters: Purchase VIP shelters to hide from the incoming wave faster! Upgrades / Progression / Economy: • Coins.: Upgrade your character's speed and your base with coins! • Diamonds.: A valuable currency that you can use to buy character skins, special brainrots, or even steal brainrots from other players! You can watch an ad right now to get your first diamonds. Enemies / Bosses: • Blue waves.: They move slower. You have more time to run further! • Green waves.: You better hurry, they are faster and won't wait long for you to grab your brainrot. • Red waves.: Run as fast as you can!!! It rushes faster than all, and you have little time! Core Gameplay & Story Q: What is the goal of the game? A: Try to steal the most valuable brainrots before the wave catches you. You need to hurry! Q: How do you win / what counts as losing? A: If you manage to grab a more expensive brainrot and go further — consider it a victory! Become richer than other players and you can make it onto the leaderboard of the wealthiest players! Q: What grabs you in the first minutes? A: Different waves and the chance to run further! Each wave moves at a different speed, so you need to choose the best time to start. The further you go, the more expensive the brainrots, but the cost of mistakes increases. Those who are willing to take risks can gain much more! Mechanics & Progression Q: How many levels are there / is the structure clear? A: There is one large level in the game. You need to go further while dodging waves. The further you go, the more frequent the waves and the harder it is to return. Q: Does the difficulty change? A: The further you go, the more frequent the waves and the harder it is to return. The cost of mistakes increases, but the rewards will not leave you indifferent. Q: How can I level up faster? A: Steal the most expensive brainrots! They will bring you more coins, and you can upgrade your speed and run further! Q: What unlocks as you play? A: Earn more money — you can upgrade your base and place more brainrots. More brainrots mean more money! Economy & Customization Q: What can you buy/upgrade? A: Upgrade your speed and run further for more valuable brainrots! Buy VIP. You will need VIP shelters to avoid waves. Upgrade your base and place more brainrots. And if you don't want to run for a brainrot, steal it from other players for diamonds! Q: Are there skins / customization? A: You can buy skins for your character. Running further and getting richer is better with style! Retention & Engagement Q: Are there leaderboards and how do they work? A: Yes! Earn more gold and make it onto the list of the wealthiest players! You can also make it onto the list of the highest income. Q: Is sound necessary? A: You can play without sound. The game is clear from the first minutes, you can play in a way that is comfortable for you. Q: Is there onboarding in the game? What is it like? A: From the first minutes, you will immediately understand what you need to do. Convenient markers will help you not get lost.

¿Cómo jugar a Escape Tsunami for Brainrots?

Run forward to get Brainrots. Grab a Brainrot, bring it back to your base in time, and don’t get caught by the tsunami! 🌊🏃
