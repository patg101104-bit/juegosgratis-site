Mahjong 3D Match

Welcome to the 3D Mahjong adventure! Challenge your mind with speed and precision. Plan your moves, view the board from all angles and remove pieces intelligently. Beat increasingly difficult levels, play offline and relax with immersive music.

¿Cómo jugar a Mahjong 3D Match?

Tap 3 identical pieces to collect them before time runs out. Clear the board quickly to earn more stars and coins. Visit the shop to unlock 4 unique skills, complete daily events and use helpful aids like shuffling pieces and extra time to keep advancing.
