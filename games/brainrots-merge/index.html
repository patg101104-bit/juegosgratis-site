Brainrots Merge

An engaging merge-clicker autobattler. Combine characters to get a new one, periodically battle other players, and unlock all brainrots!

¿Cómo jugar a Brainrots Merge?

- Click on characters to earn currency - Drag identical characters onto each other to unlock a new one - When you're attacked, select the characters you want to send into battle
