Driverz Ed

Welcome to Driverz Ed, with your instructor Ed! Explore Ed's beautiful island, completing challenges, or just drive around and try to find all the secrets the Island has to offer! Find the hidden Puzzle Pieces and unlock every car.

¿Cómo jugar a Driverz Ed?

Explore the world, find the hidden Puzzle Pieces, unlock every car. Controls: WASD to Move Arrows to Move Mouse to Control the Camera. G - Fast Travel to Start of Races M - Shows all available missions and current records P - Pause and open settings
