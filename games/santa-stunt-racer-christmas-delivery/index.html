Santa Stunt Racer Christmas Delivery

Get ready for the ultimate festive adventure in Santa Stunt Racer: Christmas Delivery! Ride through thrilling holiday stunt tracks filled with ramps, obstacles, and challenging paths. Each level brings a new snowy track where you must drive carefully, perform stunts, and collect the Christmas gifts placed along the ramps. Your mission is simple: reach the finish line and deliver the collected gifts to the kids waiting for Santa! The faster and cleaner you complete the track, the better your delivery score. Unlock a variety of custom Santa vehicles as you progress—each with unique speed, handling, and style to help you conquer tougher levels. Master the tracks, dodge obstacles, and perform daring jumps to complete Santa’s delivery run before Christmas ends! Are you ready to race, stunt, and deliver joy? Christmas needs you! 🎅

¿Cómo jugar a Santa Stunt Racer Christmas Delivery?

Drive Santa’s car with WASD/Arrow Keys and conquer ramps, obstacles, and gift tracks. Collect gifts to the kids waiting for Santa at the finish place!
