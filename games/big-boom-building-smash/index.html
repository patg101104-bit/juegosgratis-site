Big Boom! Building Smash!

💣 Ready to dive into a world of chaos and mayhem? If you’ve enjoyed games like Captain TNT and Noob Demolisher, then our game is about to become your new favorite! Blast buildings, smash through obstacles, and create total havoc on every level! You’ll face hundreds of crazy missions where every move could lead to epic explosions and complete destruction! 🧨 ⚙️ What’s in Store: - Thrilling levels with unique challenges and missions - Realistic 3D graphics and dynamic effects - Play online on any device – from your phone to your PC - Leaderboards where you can compete with others and prove you’re the ultimate demolisher 🚀 Play solo or with friends, blow up everything in sight, and have a blast with all the destruction!

¿Cómo jugar a Big Boom! Building Smash!?

💥 Your goal? Blow up buildings with dynamite, bombs, and rockets! Tear down everything in your path and turn any structure into rubble. 🕹️ Game Controls: - On desktop: Use your mouse to rotate around objects by holding down the left button. Set your explosives in the perfect spots for maximum impact. - On mobile: Swipe your finger across the screen to choose where to place the explosives. Rotate the object and drop bombs wherever you like. 🚀 Pro Tip: Upgrade your weapons to cause even more destruction and boost your efficiency! 🎯 Blow things up, smash through obstacles, and enjoy the chaos!
