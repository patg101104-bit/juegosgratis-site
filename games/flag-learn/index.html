Flag Learn

Flag Learn is a geography quiz game that helps you truly memorize every flag, capital, and US state — not just guess them. A smart spaced-repetition system tracks each flag you've seen and brings back the ones you struggle with until you master them all. Beyond the learning mode, you'll find a whole collection of challenges: Daily Flagle, Paint the Flag, Border Guess, timed Highscore speedruns with a global leaderboard, and real-time PvP battles against friends. Clean night-sky design, satisfying progress tracking, and quick rounds make it perfect for both short sessions and serious learning.

¿Cómo jugar a Flag Learn?

Learn and master all world flags, US states, and capitals. A flag appears — type its name and confirm. Answer correctly 3 times in a row to master a flag and add it to your collection. Mistakes cost a life; lives refill over time or by watching a short ad. Master the full collection to become a true Flag Master! Try the Challenges: guess the daily flag in Daily Flagle, identify blurred flags in Blur Mode, color flags in Paint the Flag, recognize countries by their outline in Border Guess, race the clock in Highscore speedruns, or battle friends in real-time PvP. Desktop Controls: Keyboard: Type the country, state, or capital name. Enter: Submit your answer. Mouse: Navigate menus, select modes, and pick colors in Paint the Flag. Mobile Controls: Tap: Navigate menus and select answers. On-Screen Keyboard: Type your answer, then tap Check.
