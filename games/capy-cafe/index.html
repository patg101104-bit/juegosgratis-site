Capy Cafe

Capy Cafe is a cozy idle-management game where you run an adorable café as a hard-working capybara barista. Harvest fresh strawberries and tea leaves, brew fragrant teas, shake up bubble tea and boba, and blend creamy smoothies — then serve them to a steady stream of happy customers. There are no buttons to fumble with: just waddle up to a station and your capy gets to work automatically. Reinvest your earnings to unlock new drinks and stations, and hire helper capybaras to keep every corner of the cafe humming while you expand. What starts as a tiny tea stand grows into a bustling boba empire — one cup at a time. Simple one-thumb controls, satisfying progression, and the most relaxing café this side of the riverbank. Genre: casual idle tycoon / shop-management sim.

¿Cómo jugar a Capy Cafe?

Welcome to Capy Cafe! Run the coziest café in town as an adorable capybara barista. Brew fragrant teas, shake up bubble tea and boba, blend fresh strawberry smoothies, and keep your happy customers served. Harvest your ingredients, unlock new stations, hire helpers, and grow your little cafe into a bustling boba empire! Desktop Controls: - ↑ / W: Move upward. - ↓ / S: Move downward. - ← / A: Move left. - → / D: Move right. - No buttons needed — just walk up to a plant, brewer, fridge, or the cashier and pause beside it. Your capybara picks up ingredients, brews drinks, and serves customers automatically. Some stations want you to stand still while they work. Mobile Controls: - Floating Joystick: Touch and hold anywhere on the screen — a joystick appears under your paw. Drag to move in any direction. - Push for Speed: A gentle drag strolls; push the joystick further to scurry at full speed. - Auto-Serve: Step next to any station or customer and pause — harvesting, brewing, and serving all happen on their own.
