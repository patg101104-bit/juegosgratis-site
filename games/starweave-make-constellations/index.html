Starweave Make Constellations

Starweave is a breathtaking, meditative puzzle game where you restore balance to the universe one strand of light at a time. Guided by ancient celestial dust, your journey begins with a single spark. Draw glowing pathways between pulsing star nodes to weave intricate constellations. Whether you are looking to sharpen your mind with elegant geometric puzzles or simply wash away stress of the day with soothing ambient soundscapes.

¿Cómo jugar a Starweave Make Constellations?

Use mouse to click and drag to connect the stars and make constellations in the sky, use hints for the correct connection
