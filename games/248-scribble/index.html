248 Scribble

248 Scribble is a casual mobile merge puzzle game inspired by 2048, where players connect adjacent dots with matching numbers (like 2s, 4s, or 8s) horizontally or vertically on a colorful grid to merge them into higher values, aiming to achieve 248 while managing space and earning coins—its unique "scribble" art style with hand-drawn, vibrant, doodle-like tiles and intuitive touch controls sets it apart as an addictive, relaxing brain teaser.

¿Cómo jugar a 248 Scribble?

Merge to 248 Mastery! Dive into 248 Scribble, the addictive casual merge puzzle where you connect adjacent dots with matching numbers (2s, 4s, 8s, and beyond) horizontally or vertically on a vibrant grid to merge them into higher values, strategically managing space to reach the ultimate 248 tile and claim victory while earning coins. Mobile Controls: Drag/Swipe: Touch and drag between adjacent matching-number dots horizontally or vertically to connect and merge them. Undo (↶): Tap the left arrow button to undo your last move (costs 100 coins). Hint (↗️): Tap the hint button for a suggested merge (costs 75 coins). Shuffle (🗑️): Tap the shuffle button to rearrange tiles (costs 50 coins). Boost (🔄): Tap the boost button for a game-changing power-up (costs 200 coins).
