Bike Stunt Game

Bike Stunt Game is a thrilling 2.5D arcade racer where you perform insane tricks on a motorbike. Choose from three modes – Time Trial (beat the clock), Trick Challenge (earn style points), or Survival (avoid crashes at all costs). With a unique side-scrolling perspective that blends 2D gameplay with 3D depth, you'll navigate ramps, loops, and obstacles across a handful of hand-crafted levels. What makes it unique? The 2.5D view gives you depth perception for landing perfect jumps while keeping simple side-scroller controls. Unlock new bikes as you master each level. Works flawlessly on desktop and mobile browsers.

¿Cómo jugar a Bike Stunt Game?

Complete each level by reaching the finish line. Your bike moves forward automatically — just control balance and tricks. Desktop – Up/down arrows to lean forward/backward. Spacebar to jump. Hold down during jumps to perform tricks (backflip, Superman, etc.). Mobile – Tap left/right side of screen to lean. Swipe up to jump. Tap and hold mid-air for tricks. Both – Land smoothly to avoid crashing. Level ends if you wipe out. Three modes: Time Trial (beat best time), Trick Challenge (hit score target), Survival (one crash = game over). Complete all levels in any mode to win. Have fun stunting!
