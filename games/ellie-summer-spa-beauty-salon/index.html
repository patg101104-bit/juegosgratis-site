¿Cómo jugar a Ellie Summer Spa Beauty Salon?

Start the game and select the spa routine level you want to play. Follow the on-screen guidance to complete each beauty-care activity in the correct order. Use simple tap, drag, and swipe controls to interact with spa tools and beauty products. Carefully perform each treatment step such as cleaning, applying products, rinsing, and styling to progress through the level. Finish all required tasks in a level to unlock the next spa routine. Relax and enjoy the calming gameplay experience while helping Ellie prepare for the summer season.
