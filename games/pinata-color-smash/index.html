Pinata Color Smash

Smash Color Pinatas by Selecting the matching color bat. You can Select Bats on top and then the bottom bats are revealed. You can Place Extra Bats on 5 Tile Slots and use them when needed. Pinatas Color Smash is a colorful and engaging puzzle game where players aim to break Pinatas by selecting matching colored bats. The game offers a fun and casual experience suitable for all ages.

¿Cómo jugar a Pinata Color Smash?

At the top of the screen, you'll see a selection of bats. Choose one that matches the color of the Pinata you wish to break. After selecting a bat, the corresponding bat at the bottom of the screen will be revealed. You have five tile slots where you can place extra bats. Use these when needed to help break more challenging Pinatas.
